# Issue brief: Not ready to connect to other EU sectoral information systems

RI-TEC-032 · North Macedonia · **Status: Draft, rebuilt after G7 (GATE-0012) with evidence or judgements added since, for sign-off at the next G7.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0007 and GATE-0020) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0011). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end. Agent synthesis sections written for the version signed off at G7 may not reflect what was added since, unless they say so; values marked (proposed) are tier 2 ratings still to be reviewed.

**Issue definition.** Systems in other sectors are not yet ready to interconnect with EU information systems, such as the business registers interconnection system, the Internal Market Information System, the civil protection system, EU taxation systems and the own resources reporting system. **Problem mechanisms:** Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03); Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission asks North Macedonia to prepare several sectoral systems to connect with EU information systems, notably the civil protection system CECIS, the Business Registers Interconnection System (BRIS), EU taxation IT systems and the own resources reporting system [1][2][3][4]. The Commission reported steps towards the CECIS connection from 2020 to 2022 but no progress in 2023, and says in 2025 that the link is still not made, while the country plans it by 2027 [5][6][7][8][9]. Some areas show more: the Commission states that alignment with the EU Digital COVID Certificate was completed in 2021 and that the country has achieved high-level integration of EU food safety IT tools [10][11]. Most other responses are proposed, and BRIS and the Internal Market Information System also depend on EU decisions or accession [12][13]. The validated assessment is open, with priority Medium, direction of travel persistent and tractability Medium.

**What remains.** The latest Commission items still ask for the CECIS link, business register readiness, tax interoperability and the own resources system, and the responding interventions are nearly all proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals. North Macedonia states it is technically ready for BRIS, but the Commission says machine-readable data alignment is still needed. What remains is connection to those systems, noting that BRIS and the Internal Market Information System also depend on EU decisions or accession.

**Direction of travel.** The sectors do not share one trend, so this rests on those with repeated wording: CECIS from 2020 to 2025 (with 'no progress' in 2023), the business registers link from 2023 to 2025, and EU tax systems and own resources in 2024 and 2025. Some areas are better, including the 2021 COVID certificate alignment and food safety tools in 2025, which the residual label hides. The chapter scales were context only and not decisive.

**Priority.** Each requirement is repeated, and tax interoperability reaches the main findings in 2024 and 2025 and CECIS is an issue for immediate attention in 2024, but none is central to the theme as a whole and the only Reform Agenda references are railway and freight outputs in the government's text. This is a residual group spanning several chapters, so the rating is an average that would differ by sector.

**Tractability.** The sectors differ: food safety shows active use of EU tools, the CECIS link looks technically limited yet has not been finished in over five years, and BRIS requires full company law alignment and access to groups open only to Member States and EEA countries. The rating is a mid-point, not a uniform finding.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The requirement has grown by sector rather than changed in wording. The CECIS link has been raised since 2020, and the Commission moved from reporting steps towards it to no progress in 2023, an issue for immediate attention in 2024 and still needing to be done in 2025 [5][7][14][1]. Business register readiness for BRIS appears from 2023 in the same words, and the 2025 screening report adds that alignment on structured, machine-readable data is needed [15][16][2]. Preparing for interoperability with EU taxation IT systems, and the own resources IT system and reporting access, entered in 2024 and are repeated in 2025, when the own resources items are described as still pending [17][18][3][4].

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- North Macedonia states it is technically ready to interconnect with BRIS, while the Commission says alignment on machine-readable data and with EU company law is still needed, and a Commission assessment of the register against those provisions would settle readiness [19][2][25].
- The country's use of SDMX for transmitting data to Eurostat and the synchronisation of its phytosanitary information system rest on its own presentation, and independent confirmation from the Commission would be needed to show use [27][28].
- The priority and tractability ratings are averages over a residual group of sectors that differ, so sector-by-sector evidence, for example on the CECIS connection planned by 2027, would be needed to judge each link separately [9][24].
- No item shows how the EU Digital COVID Certificate alignment was used after August 2021, so later evidence of use would be needed to say more than that alignment was completed [10].
