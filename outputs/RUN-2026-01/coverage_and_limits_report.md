# Source coverage and limits report: RUN-2026-01

**Status: Draft, rebuilt after G7 (GATE-0012) with evidence or judgements added since, for sign-off at the next G7.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0007 and GATE-0020) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0011). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end. Agent synthesis sections written for the version signed off at G7 may not reflect what was added since, unless they say so; values marked (proposed) are tier 2 ratings still to be reviewed.

## Summary (agent synthesis)

The run met most of its core source requirements for North Macedonia, but with gaps that matter for particular issues. Of 34 requirements, 16 were found, 13 partly found, 3 not found and 2 remain open. The main gaps are the National Programme for the Adoption of the Acquis, for which no version from 2020 onwards could be retrieved; opening or closing benchmarks for Chapter 2, which do not exist because no cluster has been opened; dedicated monitoring of the rights of foreign workers; and the SIGMA and OECD reports, whose publishers block automated download and which were not added by hand. Of 112 registered North Macedonia sources, 99 were available and 13 inaccessible. A large share of the evidence on the country response comes from Macedonian-language government sources and is machine translated, capped at Medium confidence. The comparator pilot used public sources only, with restricted EU documents and blocked sources recorded as gaps (LIM-0028), and extended machine translation to named comparator sources (LIM-0030).

Refresh with the 2026 Enlargement Package (decision E18, 5 November 2026). Three Commission documents published on 4 November 2026 were added under the existing requirements for annual reports and the enlargement communication: the 2026 report, the factsheet (which held no in-scope claim) and the 2026 Communication. They bring the North Macedonia sources to 115, of which 102 are available, and add 155 in-scope items, none machine translated, so the share of machine-translated items falls slightly. The limit recording that the package was awaited (LIM-0036) is superseded; the exclusion of the Commission communication on preparing for a wider Union (LIM-0037) is kept, and its review point is met because the 2026 Communication repeats the regional roaming point. The refresh used the run's own rule versions and re-judged only the 41 issues the new items touch; evidence confidence, relevance and implementation statuses for the new material are proposals for the refresh G7.

## Methodological limitations found during testing (agent synthesis)

The alpha found these limitations, each recorded in the alpha learning log. Extraction missed about one in eight in-scope claims in EU sources, mostly of low importance (LIM-0025, ALN-0017), and the second-pass check may have been applied unevenly between EU and other sources (ALN-0022). The automatic quote check confirms that a quote exists at its location, not that it is cut to the claim (ALN-0013). Only exact file duplicates are detected, so two versions of one screening report were counted twice until an independent check found them (ALN-0023). Parallel agents applied rules differently where the rules were silent, and judged priority inconsistently until a calibration across all issues (ALN-0012, ALN-0026). Agent inputs that were truncated or filtered by issue tag hid relevant evidence in two places (ALN-0025, ALN-0029). In the comparator pilot, first drafts of the cases contained serious errors that routine validation did not catch (ALN-0028), sources stated few lessons, so the case-level lessons are the tool's synthesis (ALN-0031, E15), and passing gates under a standing instruction moved the discovery of a language-limit problem to the end of the pilot (ALN-0030). Since decision E12, no person has read the source documents behind the extraction or the comparator cases; the checks were made by independent agents. Some validated judgement reasons still carry stage 10 wording, such as statuses described as proposals; the values were validated at G6.
