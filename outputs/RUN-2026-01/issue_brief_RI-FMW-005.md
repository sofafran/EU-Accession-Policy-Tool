# Issue brief: Employment service capacity for EURES and labour mobility

RI-FMW-005 · North Macedonia · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0022), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0020) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0011). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** The public employment service lacks trained staff or adequate IT and data systems to take part in EU labour mobility networks and vacancy exchange. **Problem mechanisms:** Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission asks North Macedonia to strengthen the Employment Service Agency through continued training and to align legislation and the exchange of good practice with EURES and European Labour Authority rules [1][2][3]. The Commission reports that capacity building for civil servants on EURES and the European Labour Authority took place in 2023 [4][5], and the 2025 screening report states that all main IT elements for EURES are in place and that administrative capacity is satisfactory [6][7]. The capacity building and the Agency's web portal are assessed as implemented, but no source shows how the training or the portal is used, so no result is established [8]. What remains is updated EURES training after staff turnover, connection of private employment agencies and, later, ESCO mapping [9][10][11]. The validated assessment is partly_addressed, with priority Medium, direction of travel persistent and tractability High.

**What remains.** What remains is updated EURES training after staff turnover, with connection of private employment agencies and later ESCO mapping. The Commission finds the main IT elements in place and capacity satisfactory, and capacity building and IT upgrades are proposed here as implemented while further strengthening is only proposed; all these statuses are proposals.

**Direction of travel.** The chapter scale was context only. The key recommendation to strengthen the Employment Service Agency is nearly unchanged in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, which sets the default, and the 2025 report says the previous recommendations were not implemented. Training took place in 2023 and the screening report calls capacity satisfactory, but no earlier rating exists to show a rise from.

**Priority.** Agency capacity is named in the key recommendation in three reports, so the requirement is repeated. It is not central now, because the Commission finds capacity satisfactory and the IT elements in place.

**Tractability.** The remaining work rests mainly with one agency, does not depend on other countries and builds on training and IT delivered before. Past capacity building is reported by the Commission, not only by the government.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The key recommendation is nearly unchanged from 2023 to 2025: in each report the Commission asks the country to strengthen the capacity of the Employment Service Agency by continuing training, and to align legislation and the exchange of good practices with EURES and ELA regulations [1][2][3]. The 2025 wording differs only in spelling out the two acronyms, and the 2025 report states that the previous recommendations were not implemented [12]. The February 2025 screening report adds more specific points: it finds the IT elements in place and capacity satisfactory, but asks for the 2014 to 2015 twinning training to be updated, for private employment agencies to be given access and adequately connected, and for ESCO mapping at a later stage [6][7][9][13][10][11].

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- No source shows the EURES training or the Agency's web portal being used, so independently checked data on vacancy exchange or online service use would be needed to judge results [5][8].
- The planned upgrade that would let the Agency take data from private employment agencies and act as the single point for EURES exchange is not shown as delivered, and an Agency or Commission report on that specific function would settle it [14].
- Whether private employment agencies have been connected to the system is not reported, and a later Commission assessment of EURES readiness would show it [13][10].
- The planned strengthening of the Agency's capacities and the EU-funded analysis of harmonisation needs are only proposed, and evidence that they were carried out would be needed to update the gap [15].
