# Issue brief: Customs IT systems not aligned with EU electronic customs

RI-TEC-029 · North Macedonia · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0012), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0007) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0011). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** National customs IT systems are not yet aligned with the EU electronic customs strategic plan, and upgrades such as the New Computerised Transit System phases, export and import control systems are delayed or understaffed. **Problem mechanisms:** Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03); Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission asks North Macedonia to keep consolidating and upgrading its customs IT systems in line with the Union Customs Code work programme and the electronic customs strategic plan, to upgrade its transit system to Phase 6 and to recruit IT staff, and states that alignment on accession is critical to EU financial interests and security [1][2][3][4]. After delays reported in 2023 and 2024, the Commission states that the New Computerised Transit System Phase 5 was completed and launched in January 2025, and notes good progress [5][6][7]. Phase 6, the export system and the import control system remain Reform Agenda steps due between 2026 and 2027 [8][9][10]. The screening report rates alignment between low and partial, with remaining systems costed at about EUR 34.5 million [11][12]. No usage data exist for Phase 5. The validated judgements are priority High, direction of travel improving, unresolved gap partly_addressed and tractability High.

**What remains.** NCTS Phase 5 is live, but the 2025 screening rates alignment between low and partial, and Phase 6, the export system and ICS2 are proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals. What remains is development of the remaining systems under the strategic plan, costed at about EUR 34.5 million, with full alignment planned for 2026 to 2030.

**Direction of travel.** NCTS Phase 5 preparation started by 2021, delays were reported in 2023 and 2024, and the system launched in January 2025, with the Commission noting 'good progress' on IT systems in 2025. The Commission's recommendation to consolidate systems repeats each year, but the stated result changed. The sub-area wording matches this issue, yet the chapter scale was context only and not decisive.

**Priority.** E-customs steps appear in the Reform Agenda with a stated payment provision for Phase 6, and the customs IT recommendation recurs from 2021 to 2025, in the main findings in 2024 and 2025. The Commission calls alignment on accession critical to EU financial interests and security, so the issue is central to Chapter 29. Some Reform Agenda items carry a theme_doubtful flag because their wording stresses efficiency, not connection to the EU.

**Tractability.** One capable agency leads, and it has delivered NCTS Phase 5 after delay and kept Phase 4 running satisfactorily. The limits are IT staffing, the planned 15 experts not yet shown as hired, cost, and an EU-driven timetable to 2030.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The core recommendation has barely changed: from 2021 the Commission asks the country to consolidate and apply its customs IT systems in line with the strategic plan modules and ensure their upgrade, and from 2024 it adds the Union Customs Code work programme and continued legal alignment [13][14][15][16][1]. The specific ask moved with events: implement Phase 5 in 2023 and 2024, then upgrade to Phase 6 in November 2025 [17][18][2]. In 2024 the Reform Agenda set payment conditions for Phase 6, the export system and the import control system [19][20][21], and staff recruitment became a recurring ask [22][3].

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- No source gives usage or performance data for Phase 5 since its launch, so transit declaration and processing statistics would be needed to show it working in practice [6].
- The Reform Agenda's own text gives a 2 to 4 per cent time reduction for Phase 6 while the payment condition gives 2 to 5 per cent, and the signed Reform Agenda annex would settle which applies [8][19][26].
- The plan to hire 15 more IT experts and the 2024 upgrade of the economic operators module are not shown as done, and customs staffing figures or a later Commission report would settle this [28][29].
- The draft Law on Single Window for Trade, needed for the new national single window, is not reported as adopted, and its publication would settle whether that system can proceed [30].
