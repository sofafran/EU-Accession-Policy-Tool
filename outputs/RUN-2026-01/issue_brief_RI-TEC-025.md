# Issue brief: Electronic identification and trust services law not aligned

RI-TEC-025 · North Macedonia · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0022), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0020) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0011). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** The law on electronic identification and trust services is not fully aligned with eIDAS and the European Digital Identity Regulation, including the Digital Identity Wallet, and implementing rules are pending. **Problem mechanisms:** Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01); Missing implementing rules (MEC-02).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align its law on electronic identification and trust services with the revised EU Digital Identity Regulation, including a national Digital Identity Wallet, and the Reform Agenda ties a payment to an updated law [1][2]. Parliament adopted amendments transposing eIDAS 2.0 on 27 June 2025, and the Commission considered the Reform Agenda step achieved while finding the law not fully aligned [3][4][5]. The amendments are validated as adopted; a machine-translated law text says they apply only from 1 June 2027 [6]. The 14 by-laws and the wallet remain proposed, with some by-laws tied to December 2027 [7]. The 2019 framework is validated as in_practice, with narrow use. No outcome is recorded. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is High and tractability is High.

**What remains.** The law was amended in June 2025 and the Commission counted the step as achieved for Reform Agenda purposes, while saying the law does not fully align and asking for further efforts in its 2025 report; the intervention statuses used here are themselves proposals. What remains is full alignment of the partially harmonised provisions, adoption of the 14 by-laws, which no item shows as adopted, and actual application. The machine-translated law text, capped at Medium confidence, says the amendments apply only from 1 June 2027.

**Direction of travel.** The Commission moved from 'yet to align' (2022) and 'further delayed' (2023) to treating the law as harmonised with the 2014 regulation and asking for the 2024 revision (2024), then to amendments transposing eIDAS 2.0, with 'some progress' for e-government and further efforts required (2025). Sources conflict on whether alignment with the 2014 regulation already existed in 2020 (screening report and 2023 strategy against the 2022 and 2023 reports), which affects the start point but not the trend. The chapter scale is context only and was not decisive; the judgement rests on the issue's own items.

**Priority.** The updated law is a Reform Agenda payment condition, and eIDAS alignment was raised by the Commission in each report from 2022 to 2025. It is the legal base of the electronic identification theme. The rating rests on these requirements and not on the number of items, most of which are provisions of one law.

**Tractability.** The remaining work is mainly legal drafting led by one ministry, with a gap analysis by SIGMA and a by-law roadmap already available, and the ministry met the June 2025 law deadline. Earlier delays in 2022 and 2023 and the staged by-laws, some tied to the wallet in December 2027, are the main limits.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The Commission's wording has moved from basic alignment to the revised regulation. In 2022 it said North Macedonia had yet to align with the eIDAS Regulation, and in 2023 that alignment was further delayed [8][9]. In 2024 it treated the law as harmonised with the 2014 regulation and asked for alignment with the revised 2024 regulation, including a Digital Identity Wallet, which the Reform Agenda made a payment condition [10][1][2]. In 2025 it recorded some progress through the eIDAS 2.0 amendments but said further efforts are required for full alignment [11][12].

## Conflicting evidence

- The 2022 report says national legislation has yet to align with eIDAS, while the screening report says the by-laws of the first half of 2020 established a legal environment harmonised with eIDAS. [8][13]
- The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2023 screening report says the legal environment has been harmonised with eIDAS since the first half of 2020. [9][13]
- The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2023 public administration reform strategy says the 2019 law on electronic documents, identification and trust services is aligned with European legislation on digitalisation. [9][14]
- The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2024 report says national legislation is harmonised with the 2014 eIDAS Regulation, and the list of amendments to the law in [15] shows none between the two reports. [9][10]

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Sources conflict on whether the law was already aligned with the 2014 regulation before 2024, with the screening report and a government strategy against the Commission's 2022 and 2023 reports [13][14][8][9]; a Commission statement on the earlier alignment status would settle it.
- No source up to the freeze date reports any of the 14 by-laws as adopted [7], so official gazette records or a later Commission assessment are needed.
- The 1 June 2027 application date rests on a machine-translated law text capped at Medium confidence [6], so the official text or an English Commission source is needed to confirm it.
- No outcome evidence shows use of electronic identification or trust services by citizens, so usage data are needed to judge any result.
