# Issue brief: Not ready for EU justice, home affairs and border information systems

RI-TEC-030 · North Macedonia · **Status: Draft.** Prepared at stage 12 for sign-off at G7. It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0007) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0011). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** The country lacks the systems, technical adaptations and funding to connect to EU justice and home affairs systems such as ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac. **Problem mechanisms:** Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03); Insufficient resources (MEC-09).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission's 2023 screening report sets out what North Macedonia needs to connect to EU justice and home affairs systems: an analysis of the means to implement ECRIS, significant technical adaptations and funding for the Visa Information System, and an IT structure compatible with Eurodac by accession [1][2][3]. It found no functional system yet to share criminal records [4]. Frontex prepared a Masterplan for interoperable IT systems, but it is validated only as proposed, as no source shows the systems being built [5][6]. The Commission reports that the roll-out of CT SIENA increased opportunities for counter-terrorism exchange, judged to have worked, but this narrow outcome says nothing about readiness for EU large-scale systems [7]. The country's request for Eurodac access before accession has no basis in EU law [8]. The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is insufficient_evidence. Priority is Medium and tractability is Low.

**What remains.** Responding interventions are proposed or lack a clear status, and these statuses are themselves proposals; part of the requirement falls due only at accession, and Eurodac access before accession has no legal basis, so 'open' does not by itself mean late. What remains is a functional criminal records system with ECRIS analysis done, the technical adaptations for the Visa Information System, an IT structure compatible with Eurodac on accession, and the funding for them.

**Direction of travel.** The requirements on ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac come essentially from one 2023 screening report, so there is no repeated or changed wording to compare. The 2021, 2022 and 2024 items concern a Frontex biometric plan and do not show whether readiness moved. The chapter scale was context only and not decisive.

**Priority.** The systems matter for Chapter 24, but support is thin: one screening report states the requirements and no item shows a benchmark, Reform Agenda condition or repeated key recommendation. This is central with thin support.

**Tractability.** Progress depends on the EU: the Commission says EU law gives candidates no Eurodac access, and the country reports it cannot build to Eurodac without the technical specifications and struggles to secure funding. The Frontex plan offers a route for border systems, but no item shows it being carried out.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* No change can be traced. The requirements on ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac come essentially from one 2023 screening report, so there is no earlier or later Commission wording on the same points to compare [1][2][3]. The same report also asks for adequate funding of the border sector, including IT systems in line with EU standards [9]. Commission items from 2021, 2022 and 2024 describe the Frontex biometric plan rather than restating a requirement, so they do not show whether the requirement has moved [10][5][6].

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether readiness has improved since 2023 is insufficient_evidence, because no later Commission item repeats the screening requirements; a later Commission assessment of ECRIS, Visa Information System and Eurodac readiness would settle it.
- The status of the planned biometric registration system following the Eurodac model is insufficient_evidence, as the only item dates from 2021 [10]; a later Commission or Frontex report would settle it.
- No source shows whether the Frontex Masterplan is being carried out or funded [6], so implementation and budget reporting on the planned systems is needed.
- Whether a functional criminal records system and the ECRIS analysis now exist is not shown after 2023 [4], so Ministry of Justice or Commission reporting is needed.
