# Issue brief: Public sector posts closed to EU citizens beyond the public authority exception

RI-FMW-003 · Moldova · **Status: Draft, prepared at stage 12 for sign-off at G7.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** EU citizens are barred from public sector posts other than a short list of exemptions, rather than only from posts involving the exercise of public authority and safeguarding the general interests of the State. **Problem mechanisms:** Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The issue is whether EU citizens are barred from public sector posts beyond those that involve the exercise of public authority and the safeguarding of the general interests of the State, which are the only posts that may be reserved for nationals. From 2023 to 2025 the Commission found that EU citizens had no access to posts in public administration bodies [1][2][3]. Government Decision No. 566/2025 allows technical, specialist and auxiliary posts to be filled by persons without Moldovan citizenship under employment contracts [4]. The Commission's 2026 report confirms that the new legislation opened these posts to EU citizens, while civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals [5]. No source reports EU citizens actually recruited to the opened posts, so the change is shown in law, not in practice. The Government's own account still excludes public functions that expressly require Moldovan citizenship [6], and it plans an analysis, including the case law of the Court of Justice, before deciding which further functions or posts can be opened [7][8]. What remains is opening civil servant posts to EU citizens, except those involving the exercise of public authority. The unresolved gap is partly addressed and the direction of travel is improving, legal only. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

**What remains.** Government Decision No. 566/2025 opens technical, specialist and auxiliary posts to persons without Moldovan citizenship, which the Commission confirms in 2026, and the Government plans an analysis, in the light of case law of the Court of Justice, of which civil service functions can be opened; its own account still excludes public functions that expressly require citizenship. What remains is opening civil servant posts to EU citizens, except those that involve the exercise of public authority and safeguarding the general interests of the State.

**Direction of travel.** The Commission found that EU citizens had no access to posts in public administration bodies in the 2023 report and the 2023 analytical report, and repeated the finding unchanged in the 2024 and 2025 reports. The 2026 report records that new legislation opened certain technical, specialised and auxiliary posts to EU citizens while civil servant posts remain reserved to nationals, so the improvement is legal only and partial.

**Priority.** The finding is repeated in the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports and concerns a core rule of the chapter, but it sits in the body text: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on public sector posts. Partial legal progress is recorded in 2026, so the route to High for a core requirement with no progress does not apply.

**Tractability.** The change is a national legal matter with no dependence on other states, and the 2025 Government decision shows that categories of posts can be opened. The remaining step needs a review of civil service functions against case law, which the Government has so far only planned.

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The 2023 analytical report stated that employment in the public administration is reserved for Moldovan citizens [9]. The 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports repeated, in identical words, that EU citizens "still" have no access to posts in public administration bodies [1][2][3]. The 2026 report changes the finding: it records that certain technical, specialised and auxiliary positions have been opened to EU citizens and narrows the remaining restriction to civil servant posts [10]. The finding sits in the body of the reports throughout; the evidence contains no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on public sector posts.

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- It is not known whether any EU citizen has been recruited to the technical, specialist and auxiliary posts opened in 2025; recruitment data from the public employers or the State Chancellery would show whether the legal change works in practice [5].
- The Government's planned analytical note on EU citizens' access to public functions, due in August 2026, is not in the evidence; the note itself would show which civil servant functions the Government considers open and on what reasoning [8].
- The scope of the "public functions that expressly require Moldovan citizenship" is not set out in the evidence; a list of those functions in the Law on the public function or its implementing acts would show how far the restriction goes beyond the public authority exception [6].
- Whether the Commission would accept a partial opening limited to non-civil-servant posts is not stated; a future report or screening document setting out the Commission's test for civil servant posts would settle it [10].
