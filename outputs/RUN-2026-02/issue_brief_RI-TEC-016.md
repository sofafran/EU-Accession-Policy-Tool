# Issue brief: Open data and re-use of public sector information incomplete

RI-TEC-016 · Moldova · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0029), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** Institutions do not regularly publish and update datasets on the open data portal, and legislation is only partly aligned with the Open Data Directive and related EU data rules. **Problem mechanisms:** Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01); Poor transparency or unreliable data (MEC-07).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission has found that no authority was responsible for open data policies and that Moldova was not yet aligned with the Open Data Directive, and the 2024 Growth Plan Communication asks Moldova to strengthen implementation of the European data strategy rules, including the Open Data Directive [1][2][3]. Parliament adopted Law No. 109/2025 on open data and the reuse of public sector information in May 2025, partially transposing the Directive, followed by methodological rules in Government Decision No. 563/2025, and the ministry created an open data policy service with two staff [4][5][6][7]. The law and rules are adopted, but no source shows their effect on data publication. Civil society states that adoption of the law and continued efforts to implement it have strengthened proactive access to information, but it gives no measure of datasets published or re-used, so success cannot be shown [8]. The only count is the Government's figure of 1,268 datasets on the portal against a target of 1,500 by 2027, and the World Bank found in 2024 that the portal is still lacking in use [9][10]. The Government itself says the portal needs updating and completing with data, and the draft law on data and data governance has been suspended until 2027 [11][12]. What remains is full alignment with the Open Data Directive and related EU data rules, and regular publication and updating of datasets by institutions. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving, legally and institutionally only. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

**What remains.** The law on open data and re-use, its methodological rules and a two-person policy unit address the Commission's findings on alignment and on the absence of a responsible authority, but the law only partly transposes the Directive. The government itself says the data.gov.md portal needs updating and completing with data, the World Bank finds the portal still lacking in use, and the draft law on data governance has been suspended until 2027. What remains is full alignment with the Open Data Directive and related EU data rules, and regular publication and updating of datasets by institutions on the portal.

**Direction of travel.** The chapter scale was used as context only, since this is part of the chapter; the issue's own items show change. The 2023 report and Analytical Report and the 2024 report find that no authority is responsible for open data policies, and the 2025 report finds Moldova not yet aligned with the Open Data Directive; the 2026 report records a law partially harmonising with that Directive, and the government reports a dedicated open data unit created in the economy ministry under Law No. 109/2025. The improvement is legal and institutional only: across all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files the only measure of publication is the Government's count of 1,268 datasets on the portal, a single figure with no trend, and the World Bank found the portal still lacking in use in 2024.

**Priority.** The Commission repeats the finding on open data in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, and the 2024 Growth Plan Communication refers to implementing the Open Data Directive, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on open data. Open data is a defined part of the data theme rather than its centre.

**Tractability.** Recent legal progress is recorded, with a law, methodological rules and a responsible unit in place, and the portal already exists. Against that, capacity is thin, with two posts in the ministry and one at the e-Governance Agency for the portal, and publication depends on many institutions, including local authorities, whose capacity the government lists as a challenge.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The 2022 Opinion contains no item on open data in the evidence. The Commission's analytical report of February 2023 and its 2023 and 2024 reports state in almost identical words that no authority has responsibility for open data policies [13][14][1]. The 2024 Growth Plan Communication asks for stronger implementation of the Data Governance Act, the Data Act, the Open Data Directive and the rules on high-value datasets [3]. The 2025 report moves the finding from institutions to law, stating that Moldova is not yet aligned with the Open Data Directive [2]. The 2026 report records that in July 2026 Moldova adopted a law partially harmonising with the Directive [15]. No key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition addresses open data.

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether institutions are publishing and updating datasets more regularly is not measured, and a time series of datasets on the portal, ideally checked independently, would settle it [9][10].
- The Commission dates the adoption of a partially harmonising law to July 2026 while Government and civil society sources date Law No. 109/2025 to May 2025, and the Commission's reference would need to be identified to know whether a later act exists [15][4].
- Whether the list of high-value datasets and the technical norms on publication have been approved is not reported, and their adoption would show whether the remaining transposition is advancing [20].
- Whether the draft law on data and data governance resumes after its suspension until 2027 is not known, and its adoption or withdrawal would settle alignment with the related EU data rules [12][21].
