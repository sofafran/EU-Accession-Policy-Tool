# Issue brief: Digital tools in company law not fully aligned

RI-TEC-035 · Moldova · **Status: Draft, prepared at stage 12 for sign-off at G7.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** Company law is not fully aligned with EU rules on the use of digital tools and processes, such as online formation, electronic filing and director checks. **Problem mechanisms:** Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission asks Moldova to align its company law with the EU acquis on the use of digital tools and processes, which covers matters such as online company formation and electronic filing [1]. Law No. 175/2021 allowed documents for registering legal entities to be submitted electronically with an electronic signature [2], and the Commission reported in 2024 that a qualified electronic signature had been introduced for filing documents with the business register [3]. In practice, however, online registration ran by e-mail as a provisional solution because the Public Services Agency's systems were delayed [4][5]. The new State Register of Legal Entities, planned for July 2025, was launched in December 2025 and enables registration of all legal forms of entities [6][7]. In February 2026 the platform offered online applications such as name reservation and register extracts [8], and no source yet shows company formation and filings running fully online at scale. The Government itself considers the EU directive on digital tools in company law only partially transposed [9], and a law on digital tools and processes in company law is planned for December 2026 [10]. What remains is full transposition of these rules, with company formation and filings fully online through the new register. The unresolved gap is partly addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is Medium and tractability is High.

**What remains.** The latest EU requirement, from 2024, is further alignment on the use of digital tools and processes in company law. Electronic signature filing and the new register are implemented, but the draft law on digital tools in company law is still planned, online company registration ran by e-mail as a provisional solution, and in February 2026 the new platform offered online only name reservation and register extracts (machine translated). What remains is full transposition of the rules on digital tools and processes, with company formation and filings fully online through the new register.

**Direction of travel.** The Chapter 6 scale is context only, since this is part of the chapter. The 2023 report and the 2023 Analytical Report found company law not aligned on digital tools and processes, the 2024 report repeated the recommendation while recording a qualified electronic signature for filing with the business register, and the 2026 report records the new State Register of Legal Entities, launched in December 2025, which enables registration of all legal forms. The Government's own assessment (machine translated) is that the directive on digital tools in company law is only partly transposed.

**Priority.** Digital tools in company law appear in the 2023 recommendation and among many elements of the 2024 key recommendation on company law, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found them in no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition and in no 2025 or 2026 item repeating the recommendation. It is a defined acquis requirement but a narrow part of Chapter 6; the Reform Agenda step on the new register is counted under the base registers issue.

**Tractability.** The remaining work is mainly legal and within the Government's control: a draft law on digital tools in company law is planned and the new register is in place. Against that, the register slipped from a planned July 2025 launch to December 2025 and online company registration still ran by e-mail as a provisional solution, so delays are the main risk (Government figures, machine translated).

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The 2023 analytical report listed gaps in alignment with the company law acquis, including the more recent EU laws on digital tools and processes [11]. The 2023 report stated that Moldova needed to align with the acquis on digital tools and processes and on cross-border operations [12]. In 2024 the key recommendation on company law asked Moldova to continue alignment on a long list of areas, among them the use of digital tools and processes, while the report recorded the qualified electronic signature for filing with the business register [1][3]. The 2025 and 2026 reports in the evidence do not repeat the recommendation on digital tools; the 2026 report records the launch of the new register instead [7]. The requirement has not appeared in a benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition.

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether the planned law on digital tools and processes in company law will be adopted by December 2026, and how fully it transposes the directive, is not known; the adopted law and a table of concordance would settle it [10][9].
- It is not shown that companies can now be formed fully online through the new register; data from the Public Services Agency on online registrations, and whether the e-mail route has been closed, would answer it [8][5].
- The draft amendment to the concept of the State Register of Legal Units had been sent to the Government in 2026; the adopted decision would show what further functions the register is to provide [13].
- The Commission's view of alignment after 2024 is not stated; a future report or screening document assessing the digital tools rules would show whether the requirement is still considered open [1].
