# Issue brief: Readiness for electronic exchange of social security information

RI-FMW-010 · Moldova · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0029), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** The institutions lack the IT and human capacity to exchange social security information electronically with Member States (the EESSI system) and to use electronic health insurance data. **Problem mechanisms:** Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission asks Moldova to develop the IT infrastructure, systems, databases and administrative and financial capacity needed for full participation in the Electronic Exchange of Social Security Information (EESSI) system and the EHIC scheme [1][2]. In February 2026 Moldova designated the e-Governance Agency as the EESSI National Access Point and the National Office of Social Insurance and the National Health Insurance Company as liaison bodies, adopted a law setting the legal basis for EU social security coordination rules and EESSI interconnection, and approved an EESSI Action Plan [3][4][5]. These steps are adopted, but no source reports the access point or liaison bodies working or any data exchanged, and Moldova is not connected to EESSI. The Government reports that the information systems of the employment agency and the social insurance house still need to be adjusted for EESSI, that the technical solution and its financing will be chosen only after technical documentation arrives from the Commission, and that 3 of 8 planned IT staff at the health insurance company work on the EHIC and interconnection with European systems [6][7][8]. What remains is the IT infrastructure, interconnected systems, staff and funding for full participation in EESSI and for electronic exchange of health insurance data. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving, organisationally and legally only, as no technical connection is reported. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

**What remains.** The access point, liaison bodies, legal basis and action plan are in place, but the Government reports that the employment agency and CNAS systems still need adjusting for EESSI, that the technical solution and its financing will be chosen later, and that only 3 of 8 planned IT directorate staff work on the health insurance card and interconnection with European systems. What remains is the IT infrastructure, interconnected information systems, staff and funding for full participation in EESSI and for electronic exchange of health insurance data.

**Direction of travel.** The 2023 analytical report found that authorities struggle with administrative capacity and data exchange in international cases, and the 2025 key recommendation asked Moldova to develop IT infrastructure for EESSI. In 2026 the Commission reports that the e-Governance Agency was designated as the EESSI National Access Point with two liaison bodies, a law setting the basis for EESSI interconnection was adopted and an EESSI Action Plan approved, while the 2026 key recommendation still asks for the IT infrastructure and capacity. The improvement is organisational and legal only, as no technical connection is reported.

**Priority.** Electronic exchange is needed to apply social security coordination at accession, and it is named in the key recommendations of the 2025 and 2026 reports. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found EESSI in five Commission items, all from 2025 and 2026, and in no key recommendation repeated across three reports, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition.

**Tractability.** The 2026 designations and action plan show that organisational steps can be taken, and the Government reports the interoperability platform as technically ready for cross-border exchange. Connection, however, depends on technical documentation from the Commission, an investment not yet decided, several institutions and a new employment agency system funded externally.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The 2022 Opinion contains no item on electronic exchange of social security information in the evidence. The Commission's 2023 analytical report found that national authorities struggle with administrative capacity and data exchange in international cases, leading to delays [9]. The 2025 report turned this into a key recommendation to develop IT infrastructure for the EHIC and EESSI and administrative capacity in view of joining the future EESSI Network, alongside concluding more social security agreements [1]. The 2026 report broadens the request to technical and organisational measures, administrative and financial capacity and resources, and IT infrastructure, systems and databases for full participation in EURES, EESSI and the EHIC scheme [2]. No Reform Agenda payment condition or benchmark addresses the issue.

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether Moldova has connected to EESSI depends on the technical solution and investment still to be decided, and a Commission or e-Governance Agency report of live exchange through the National Access Point would settle it [7][3].
- Whether the employment agency and social insurance systems have been made interoperable with EESSI is not shown, and evidence that the new employment agency system and the adjusted social insurance system are in use would be needed [6][13].
- The Government states that data exchange takes place exclusively through the MConnect platform, while the data protection authority finds that state bodies still access personal data outside it, and an independent audit of data flows would show which account holds [11][10].
- Whether the health insurance company has filled its planned IT posts for the EHIC and European interconnection is not known, and later staffing data would settle it [8].
