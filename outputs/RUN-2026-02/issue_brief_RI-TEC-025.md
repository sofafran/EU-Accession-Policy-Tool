# Issue brief: Electronic identification and trust services law not aligned

RI-TEC-025 · Moldova · **Status: Draft, prepared at stage 12 for sign-off at G7.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** The law on electronic identification and trust services is not fully aligned with eIDAS and the European Digital Identity Regulation, including the Digital Identity Wallet, and implementing rules are pending. **Problem mechanisms:** Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01); Missing implementing rules (MEC-02).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The EU asks Moldova to align its law on electronic identification and trust services with EU requirements, now including the European Digital Identity framework (eIDAS 2), and to make its national wallet interoperable with the European Digital Identity Wallets; the Reform Agenda expects eIDAS 2 alignment, an EU-aligned national wallet and at least 20 e-services in the EVO application by December 2026 [1][2][3]. Moldova adopted Law No. 124/2022 on electronic identification and trust services, amended it in 2025 to open the market to EU qualified trust service providers, and joined the EU Digital Identity Cooperation Group [4][5][6]. The Commission reports that December 2025 amendments fully aligned the rules on qualified trust service providers with the Digital Identity Regulation and that Moldova joined the EU third country trusted list for advanced electronic signatures and seals [7]. A first national wallet was launched in the EVO application in June 2024, and work on a wallet aligned with the EU framework began in September 2025 [8][9]. The law transposing eIDAS 2 is still being drafted, and implementing orders on registering wallet-relying parties and certifying wallets are planned for December 2026 [10][11][12]. Capacity is thin, with one ministry employee covering information technology, information society and trust services [13]. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving, legal only. Priority is High and tractability is High.

**What remains.** The Commission asked in 2024 for interoperability with the European Digital Identity Wallets, and the Reform Agenda expects alignment with eIDAS 2 and an EU-aligned national wallet by December 2026. In 2026 the law transposing eIDAS 2 was still being drafted, a set of implementing orders on the wallet framework was planned, and the national wallet was under development since September 2025. What remains is the eIDAS 2 law and its implementing acts, and a national digital identity wallet interoperable with the European Digital Identity Wallets.

**Direction of travel.** The Chapter 10 scale is context only. The Commission found the 2022 law partially aligned with eIDAS in 2023, found full alignment with eIDAS 1.0 in 2025 with the European Digital Identity framework to follow, and in 2026 found an advanced level of alignment after the December 2025 amendments on qualified trust service providers. The improvement is legal, and sources conflict on when Law No. 124/2022 entered into force: 10 December 2022 in the 2023 report, 1 January 2025 according to the Government.

**Priority.** Alignment with EU electronic identification requirements was part of the 2024 key recommendation, and alignment with eIDAS 2 with an EU-aligned national wallet is a step of the Reform Agenda due by December 2026. It is central to the electronic identification theme.

**Tractability.** Moldova moved from partial to advanced alignment between 2023 and 2026, with amending laws in 2025 and technical assistance under EU4Digital for eIDAS 2. Staffing is thin, with one ministry post on trust services and the capacity of the ministry and regulator named as needs, which may slow the remaining implementing acts.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The evidence holds no Commission item on this issue from the 2022 Opinion. In 2023 the Commission found the new law broadly aligned with the relevant acquis but only partially aligned with the eIDAS Regulation [14][15]. In 2024 the requirement became a key recommendation to align legislation with EU electronic identification requirements, and the Commission added that further work was needed for interoperability with the European Digital Identity Wallets [1][2]. The 2025 Reform Agenda set a December 2026 step on eIDAS 2 alignment and an EU-aligned wallet, and the 2025 report found full alignment with eIDAS 1.0, with eIDAS 2.0 to follow [3][16]. By 2026 the Commission described an advanced level of alignment and noted a national strategy to align with eIDAS 2.0 [17][18]. The 2023 report and the Government give different dates for the law's entry into force, 10 December 2022 and 1 January 2025 respectively [19][20].

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether the law transposing eIDAS 2 will be adopted by the Reform Agenda deadline of December 2026 is open; its adoption, and the Commission's assessment of the step, would settle it [10][3].
- It is not clear whether the December 2025 amendments the Commission reports are Law No. 117/2025 or a separate act, since the Government still describes an eIDAS 2 law as being drafted; the published acts would settle it [7][25][10].
- Whether the new national wallet is interoperable with the European Digital Identity Wallets in practice is not shown; results of cross-border interoperability tests would settle it [2][9].
- The date Law No. 124/2022 entered into force differs between the Commission and the Government; the Official Gazette text would settle it [19][20].
