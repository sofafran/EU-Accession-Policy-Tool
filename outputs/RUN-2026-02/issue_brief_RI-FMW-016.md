# Issue brief: Host-language tuition for EU workers and their children

RI-FMW-016 · Moldova · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0029), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** Not raised in EU material: arrangements to teach the official language to the children of EU workers, and to EU workers and their family members, in line with Directive 77/486/EEC on the education of the children of migrant workers, are not yet in place or resourced. **Problem mechanisms:** Legal or regulatory gap (MEC-01); Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The issue is not raised in EU material. It concerns teaching the official language to the children of EU workers, and to EU workers and their family members, in line with Directive 77/486/EEC on the education of the children of migrant workers. The Commission's 2026 report says only that the law on free movement and residence of EU citizens aims to align with the acquis on the education of children of migrant workers, with no finding on tuition [1]. In its 2025 accession programme, in machine-translated text, the Government states that the Ministry of Education and Research will create opportunities to learn Romanian for EU and European Economic Area citizens and their family members through the national programme for learning Romanian, and that children of EU citizens enrolled in national schools will be offered Romanian courses in their schools [2][3]. A new edition of the programme for 2026 to 2028, with national Romanian language study centres, was to be drafted in 2025, and a mechanism for paying the teachers is planned [4][5]. The Government adds that these actions could require additional teaching posts [6]. All of these are plans: no source reports the new programme adopted, courses provided or funding allocated, and no outcome has been assessed. What remains, on the Government's own account, is putting in place and resourcing host-language tuition for EU workers, their family members and their children. The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is new. Priority is Low and tractability is Medium.

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether the 2026 to 2028 edition of the national programme for learning Romanian has been adopted, and whether it includes courses for EU citizens and their children, would be settled by the adopted programme [4].
- Whether schools are offering Romanian courses to children of EU citizens is not shown; enrolment and course data from the Ministry of Education and Research would show it [3].
- No source reports funding for the teacher payment mechanism or for extra teaching posts; budget lines or staffing decisions would settle it [5][6].
- Whether the Commission will treat host-language tuition as part of alignment with the acquis on the education of migrant workers' children is not known; a Commission finding on Directive 77/486/EEC in a later report or screening would settle it [1].
