# Issue brief: Operational cybersecurity capacity of public institutions weak

RI-TEC-023 · Moldova · **Status: Validated at G7 (GATE-0029), internal output.** It uses only judgements validated at G6 (GATE-0024) and, for comparator material, G6b (GATE-0028). Sections marked *Agent synthesis* are written by the tool from that material and add no new judgement. Numbers in square brackets refer to the evidence annex at the end.

**Issue definition.** The national incident response centre and state institutions lack staff, expertise and secure infrastructure, so government services and digital infrastructure remain exposed to attacks. **Problem mechanisms:** Weak administrative capacity (MEC-03); Insufficient resources (MEC-09).

## In brief (agent synthesis)

The Commission has asked Moldova to make its new national cybersecurity authority operational, and the Reform Agenda set the step of making the National Agency for Cybersecurity fully operational with a national CERT by December 2025 [1][2]. Moldova established the Agency in December 2023, launched it in February 2024, and brought the law on cybersecurity into force in January 2025 [3][4][5]. With EU funding, seconded experts and training, the Agency had 32 staff by December 2025, and the Commission reports that it became operational as the single point of contact and national CERT, with an incident management platform in use [6][7][8][9][10]. The Commission records 38 incidents handled and 52 vulnerability notifications in 2025, and judged the Reform Agenda step satisfactorily fulfilled [11][12]. Gaps remain in the Agency's own account: supervision was only partly carried out for lack of staff and a complete framework, its security monitoring system was still being configured, its incident playbooks were 70% complete, and it lacks qualified IT specialists [13][14][15][16]. The Government reports that cyber incidents rose by over 30% between 2021 and 2023, and the Commission notes security vulnerabilities in the border police information system [17][18]. The Agency's reports are machine translated. The unresolved gap is partly addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

**What remains.** The Reform Agenda step on making the Agency fully operational with a national CERT is judged fulfilled, but the Agency's 2025 report says supervision was only partly carried out for lack of staff and a complete framework, its SIEM and incident playbooks were incomplete, and it lacks qualified IT specialists. The Government reports that cyber incidents rose by over 30 percent between 2021 and 2023, and the 2026 report notes security vulnerabilities in the border police information system. What remains is enough qualified staff and tools for the Agency and CERT to supervise providers and protect state systems.

**Direction of travel.** The Chapter 10 scale is context only. In 2021 the national CERT was pending approval of its mandate, in 2024 and 2025 the Commission asked for further effort on the operational capacity of the new Cybersecurity Agency, and the Reform Agenda found it short of staff, IT resources and legal authority. In 2026 the Commission reports the Agency operational as single point of contact and national CERT, with 32 staff by December 2025 and an incident platform in use, and judged the related Reform Agenda step satisfactorily fulfilled.

**Priority.** Operational capacity of the Agency was named in the 2024 and 2025 reports and in a Reform Agenda step, but the Commission judged that step satisfactorily fulfilled, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no 2026 Commission recommendation on the Agency's capacity. A fulfilled condition with no repeat of the requirement in 2026 does not on its own make the issue High, although it stays central to the cybersecurity theme.

**Tractability.** The Agency went from its launch in 2024 to an operational CERT in 2025, with substantial EU and bilateral support in experts, equipment, software and training. The Agency names recruitment of qualified specialists and limited own resources for equipment as its constraints.

*These reasons are shown as validated at G6. Where a reason says that implementation statuses were still proposals, it was written at stage 10; those statuses were validated at the same gate.*

## The EU requirement and how it has changed

*Agent synthesis:* The Commission's wording has moved from a missing institution to its operational capacity and then to fulfilment. In 2021 it found that a national CERT foreseen by the 2019 to 2024 information security strategy was pending approval of its mandate [19]. In 2023 it described the Information Technology and Cyber Security Service as the government's incident response team [20]. In 2024, after the Agency was set up, it called for further effort to ensure its operational capacity, and in 2025 it found progress but said further efforts were needed [1][21][22]. The Reform Agenda then made the Agency's full operation and a national CERT a step, noting that the Agency lacked staff, IT resources and legal authority [2][23]. In 2026 the Commission judged the step satisfactorily fulfilled and described the Agency as further strengthened, without repeating a recommendation on its capacity, while pointing to vulnerabilities in a specific state system [12][24][18].

## Open questions (agent synthesis)

- Whether the Agency reaches its planned staffing and recruits qualified IT specialists would be shown by staffing figures after 2025 [6][29].
- Whether the Agency fully supervises essential and important service providers, which it reports doing only in part, would be shown by audit and supervision records corroborated by an independent source [13].
- Whether the national incident and crisis response plan is drawn up and the State Register of Cyber Incidents built would be shown by the approved plan and the register in operation [30][31].
- The Government's claim of substantial progress towards a resilient cyber ecosystem is not confirmed independently, and incident trends after 2023 from an independent source would settle it [25][17].
