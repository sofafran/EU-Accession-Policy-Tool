COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION 
of 29.9.2026 
approving the third release of funds to the Republic of Moldova under the Reform and

THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, 
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, 
Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2025/535 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 
18 March 2025 establishing the Reform and Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldo va1, 
and in particular Article 19(3) thereof, 
Whereas: 
(1) Regulation (EU) 2025/535 established the Reform and Growth Facility for the 
Republic of Moldova (‘the Facility’), which aims to provide Union support to the 
Republic of Moldova (‘Moldova’) for the period 2025 -2027. This support takes the 
form of non -repayable support and loans for the delivery of reforms promoted by the 
Union, in particular inclusive and sustainable socio -economic reforms and reforms 
concerning the fundamentals of the enlargement process, aligned with Union values, 
as well as investments to implement Moldova’s Reform Agenda. Both the reforms and 
investments comply with the general principles of Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 
2025/535.  
(2) The Commission approved the Reform Agenda of Moldova on 7 May 2025 2 and its 
multi-annual work programme on 8 May 2025 3. The Reform Agenda constitutes a 
prioritised set of targeted reforms and investments in Moldova and serves as an 
overarching framework to achieve the objectives of the Facility. Pursuant to Articles 
14(1) and 14(3) of Regulation (EU) 2025/5 35, the Commission amended the Reform 
Agenda on 23 June 20264 following a reasoned request made by Moldova.  
(3) Pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, the Commission concluded a 
Facility Agreement with Moldova on 9 May 2025 for the implementation of the 
Facility setting out the obligations and conditions for the disbursement of the Facility. 
The Facility Agreement entered into force on 4 June 2025.

(4) The Facility Agreement is complemented by a loan agreement, as required by Article  
8(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535. A Loan Agreement was concluded with Moldova 
on 21 May 2025, and entered into force on 5 June 2025.  
(5) In accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, twice per year Moldova is 
to submit a duly justified reque st for the release of funds in respect of fulfilled 
payment conditions related to the quantitative and qualitative steps as set out in its 
Reform Agenda. 
(6) On 11 March 2026, the Commission approved the second release of funds under the 
Reform and Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova 5 after its assessment 
concluded that 24 out of the 26 steps were satisfactorily fulfilled. For two steps that 
were not satisfactory fulfilled, a grace period of 24 months started as of 31 December 
2025, in line with Article 17(2) and 14(5) of the Facility Agreement respectively. 
(7) In accordance with Article 19(3) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, EUR 209 434 792.92 
has been released in the first two releases of funds under the Reform and Growth 
Facility for the Republic of  Moldova, of which EUR 17 292 781.06 has been released 
in the form of non-repayable financial support and EUR 192 142 011.86 in the form of 
a loan. In accordance with the Loan Agreement concluded between the Union and 
Moldova pursuant to Article 15 of Regu lation (EU) 2025/535, an amount of EUR 
3 795 976.34 from the first two releases of funds was used to clear the pre-financing of 
non-repayable financial support and an amount of EUR 42 177 514.80 was used to 
clear the pre -financing of the loan. An amount of  EUR 24  300 000 of the non -
repayable financial support and EUR 270  000 000 of the loan support were released 
for the benefit of Moldova as pre-financing. 
(8) In accordance with Article 19(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, on 10 July 2026 
Moldova submitted a request for the payment of a part of the third instalment under 
the Reform and Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova. The request was 
accompanied by a series of documents evidencing the satisfactory fulfilment of 18 out 
of 20 steps, composed of 17 out of 18 steps due by June 2026 and one out of two steps 
originally scheduled by December 2025 under the 24 -month grace period. The present 
Implementing Decision provides  the breakdown of the amounts to be released in the 
form of a loan and the non-repayable support to be provided under the Neighbourhood 
Investment Platform (NIP). 
(9) In accordance with Article 8(3) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, disbursement of Union 
support under the Facility is done in accordance with the terms of the Facility 
Agreement and of the Loan Agreement. In addition, disbursements are conditional on 
Moldova’s compliance with the preconditions set out in Article 5(1), the general 
payment conditions set out in Article 10(3), and the satisfactory fulfilment of the 
payment conditions se t out in the Reform Agenda, as referred to in Article 10(1) of

Regulation (EU) 2025/535. Pursuant to Article 19(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535,

(10) The steps underpinning the request relate to various reforms set out in the Reform 
Agenda in the areas of private sector development, connectivity and digital 
infrastructure, economic governance, social capital, green transition, e nergy security 
and efficiency, and fundamental rights. 
(11) Pursuant to Article 19(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, the Commission assessed the

instalment, as set out in the Reform Agenda. One step originally due by December 
2025, under a 24 -month grace period, was also satisfactorily fulfilled. The 
Commission assessment is included in the Annex to this Decision. 
(12) Moldova has also confirmed that it has not reversed any measure related to the steps 
which it had previously satisfactorily fulfilled.  
(13) The envisaged assistance complies with the conditions and procedures set out by the 
restrictive measures6 adopted pursuant to Article 215 of the Treaty on the Functioning 
of the European Union. 
(14) In accordance with Article 1 of the Loan Agreement, the loan support is composed of 
two components: the Allocated Percentage (25%), which Moldova is required to invest 
in projects approved under the NIP, and the remaining part of the loan.  For the portion 
of the loan support allocated to investment projects to be approved under the NIP, 
Moldova will provide the Commission with an Engagement letter in accordance with 
the procedure set out in Article 20 of the Facility Agreement. 
(15) In accordance with Article 6(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, an amount of non -
repayable financial support is provided by the Union for projects to be approved und er 
the NIP. 
(16)  In accordance with Article 13(3) of the Facility Agreement and Article 6(5) of the 
Loan Agreement, pre -financing of the non -repayable financial support and the loan 
support should be cleared by being proportionally deducted against the pa yment of the 
instalments. Since EUR 24  300 000 of the non -repayable financial support and EUR 
270 000 000 of the loan support were released for the benefit of Moldova as pre -
financing, for this third request of funds  an amount of EUR 2 846 982.25 of the 
payment should be utilised to clear the non -repayable financial support part of the pre-
financing and an amount of EUR 31 633 136.10 of the payment should be utilised to 
clear the loan support part of the pre-financing.

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:  
Article 1 
Authorisation of the release of the non-repayable support for the Republic of Moldova 
(1) Based on the assessment of the payment conditions, the non -repayable financial 
support component of the third request for release of funds under the Reform and 
Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova shall be set at a gross amount of EUR 
15 816 568.05.  
(2) In accordance with Article 13(3) of the Facility Agreement, an amount of  
EUR 2 846 982.25 shall be utilised to clear the non -repayable financial support part 
of the pre-financing. 
(3) The net amount of EUR 12 969 585.80 shall be released in accordance with the terms 
of the Facility Agreement.  
(4) The amount set out in paragraph 3 shall be made available for investment projects to 
be approved under the NIP. 
Article 2 
Authorisation of the release of the loan support for the Republic of Moldova 
(1) Based on the assessment of the payment conditions, the loan support component of 
the third request for release of funds under the Reform and Growth Facility for the 
Republic of Moldova shall be set at a gross amount of EUR 175 739 644.99. 
(2) In accordance with Article 6(5) of the Loan Agreement, an amount of  
EUR 31 633 136.10 shall be utilised to clear the loan support part of the pre -
financing. 
(3) The net amount of EUR 144 106 508.89 shall be released in accordance with Article 
8 of the Loan Agreement. 
(4) In accordance with the Loan Agreement, the net amount shall be split into two 
components: EUR 36 026 627.22, corresponding to the Allocated Percentage (25%), 
to be made available for investment projects to be approved under the NIP; and EUR 
108 079 881.67, representing the remaining portion of the loan.  
(5) The Commission shall disburse to the Republic of Moldova the amount of EUR 
108 079 881.67 corresponding to the remaining portion of the loan referred to in 
paragraph 4.
