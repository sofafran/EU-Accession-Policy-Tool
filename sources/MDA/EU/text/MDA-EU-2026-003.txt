COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION 
of 11.3.2026 
approving the second release of funds to the Republic of Moldova under the Reform and 
Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova 
THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, 
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, 
Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2025/535 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 
18 March 2025 establishing the Reform and Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova 1, 
and in particular Article 19(3) thereof, 
Whereas: 
(1) Regulation (EU) 2025/535 established the Reform and Growth Facility for the 
Republic of Moldova  (‘the Facility’), which aims to provide Union support to the 
Republic of Moldova  (‘Moldova’) for the period 2025-2027. This support takes the 
form of non -repayable support and loans for the delivery of reforms promoted by the 
Union, in particular inclusiv e and sustainable socio -economic reforms and reforms 
concerning the fundamentals of the enlargement process, aligned with Union values, 
as well as investments to implement Moldova’s Reform Agenda. Both the reforms and 
investments comply with the general pr inciples of Article 4 of Regulation (EU) 
2025/535.  
(2) The Commission approved the Reform Agenda of Moldova on 7 May 2025 2 and its 
multi-annual work programme on 8 May 2025 3. The Reform Agenda constitute s a  
prioritised set of targeted reforms and invest ments in Moldova and serve s as an 
overarching framework to achieve the objectives of the Facility.  
(3) Pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, the Commission concluded a 
Facility Agreement with Moldova on 9 May 2025  for the implementation of the 
Facility setting out the obligations and conditions for the disbursement of the Facility. 
The Facility Agreement entered into force on 4 June 2025. 
(4) The Facility Agreement is complemented by a loan agreement, as required by Article 
8(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535. A Loan Agreement was concluded with Moldova 
on 21 May 2025, and entered into force on 5 June 2025.

(5) In accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, twice per year Moldova is 
to submit a duly justified request for the  release of funds in respect of fulfilled 
payment conditions related to the quantitative and qualitative steps as set out in its 
Reform Agenda.  
(6) In accordance with Article 19(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, on 30 January 2026  
Moldova submitted a request for the  payment of a part of the second instalment under 
the Reform Agenda . The request was accompanied by a series of documents 
evidencing the satisfactory fulfilment of 24 steps . T he second instalment of the 
approved Reform Agenda  includes 29 steps, out of which 3 steps were fulfiled ahead 
of schedule and were included in the fi rst release of funds . The present Implementing 
Decision provides the breakdown of the amounts to be released in the form of a loan 
and the non -repayable support to be provided under the Neighbourhood Investment 
Platform (NIP). 
(7) In accordance with Article 8(3) of Regulation (EU) 202 5/535, disbursement of Union 
support under the Facility is done in accordance with the terms of the Facility 
Agreement and of the Loan Agreement. In addition, disbursements are conditional on 
Moldova’s compliance with the preconditions set out in Article 5 (1), the general 
payment conditions set out in Article 10(3), and the satisfactory fulfilment of the 
payment conditions set out in the Reform Agenda , as referred to in Article 10(1) of 
Regulation (EU) 2 025/535. Pursuant to Article 19(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, 
following its assessment , the Commission conclude s that Moldova complies with the 
preconditions and the general payment conditions. 
(8) The steps underpinning the request relate to various reforms set out in the Reform 
Agenda in the areas of private sector development, economic resilience, economic 
governance, social cohesion, green transition, energy, and fundamental rights. 
(9) Pursuant to Article 19(2) of Regulation (EU) 202 5/535, the Commission assessed the 
request for release of funds from Moldova and concluded that it has satisfactorily 
fulfilled 24 out of 26 of the remaining quantitative and qualitative steps included in the 
second in stalment, as set out in the approved  Reform Agenda.  The Commission 
assessment is included in the Annex to this Decision. 
(10) Moldova has also confirmed that it has not reversed any measure related to the steps 
which it had previously satisfactorily fulfilled.  
(11) The envisaged assistance compl ies with the conditions and procedures set out by the 
restrictive measures4 adopted pursuant to Article 215 of the Treaty on th e Functioning 
of the European Union. 
(12) In accordance with Article 1 of the Loan Agreement, the loan support is composed of 
two components: the Allocated Percentage (25%), which Moldova is required to invest 
in projects approved under the NIP, and the remaining part of the loan.  For the portion

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:  
Article 1 
Authorisation of the release of the non-repayable support for the Republic of Moldova 
(1) Based on the assessment of the payment conditions, the non -repayable financial 
support component of the second request for release of funds under the Reform and 
Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova shall be set at a gross amount of EUR 
19 011 834.32.  
(2) In accordance with Article 13(3) of the Facility Agreement, an amount of  
EUR 3 422 130.18 shall be utilised to clear the non -repayable financial support part 
of the pre-financing. 
(3) The net amount of EUR 15 589 704.14 shall be released in accordance with the terms 
of the Facility Agreement.  
(4) The amount set out in paragraph 3 shall be made available for investment projects to 
be approved under the NIP. 
Article 2 
Authorisation of the release of the loan support for the Republic of Moldova 
(1) Based on the assessment of the payment conditions , the loan support component of 
the second request for release of funds under the Reform and Growth Facility for the 
Republic of Moldova shall be set at a gross amount of EUR 211 242 603.58. 
(2) In accordance with Article 6(5) of the Loan Agreement, an amount of  
EUR 38 023 668.64 1  shall be utilised to clear the loan support part of the pre -
financing.
