COMMISSION IMPLEMENTING DECISION 
of 27.8.2025 
approving the first release of funds to the Republic of Moldova under the Reform and 
Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova 
THE EUROPEAN COMMISSION, 
Having regard to the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, 
Having regard to Regulation (EU) 2025/535 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 
18 March 2025 establishing the Reform and Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova 1, 
and in particular Article 19(3) thereof, 
Whereas: 
(1) Regulation (EU) 2025/535 established the Reform and Growth Facility for the 
Republic of Moldova  (‘the Facility’), which aims to provide Union support to the 
Republic of Moldova  for the period 2025-2027. This support takes the form of non -
repayable support and loans for the delivery of reforms promoted by the Union, in 
particular inclusive and sustai nable socio -economic reforms and reforms concerning 
the fundamentals of the enlargement process, aligned with Union values, as well as 
investments to implement the Republic of Moldova’s  Reform Agenda. Both the 
reforms and investments comply with the genera l principles of Article 4 of Regulation 
(EU) 2025/535.  
(2) The Commission has approved the Reform Agenda of the Republic of Moldova on 7 
May 2025 2 and its multi -annual work programme on 8 May 2025 3. The Reform 
Agenda constitute s a  prioritised set of tar geted reforms and investments in the 
Beneficiary and serve as an overarching framework to achieve the objectives of the 
Facility.  
(3) Pursuant to Article 8(1) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, the Commission has concluded 
a Facility Agreement with the Republic of Moldova  on 9 May 2025  for the 
implementation of the Facility setting out the obligations and conditions for the 
disbursement of the Facility. The Facility Agreement entered into force  on 4 June 
2025. 
(4) The Facility Agreement is complemented by a loan agreement, as required by Article 
8(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535. A Loan Agreement has been concluded with the 
Republic of Moldova on 21 May 2025, and entered into force on 5 June 2025.   
(5) In accordance with Article 19 of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, twice per year the 
Beneficiary is to request a release of funds in respect of fulfilled payment conditions 
related to the quantitative and qualitative steps as set out in their Reform Agendas.

(6) The Republic of Moldova has requested on 15 July 2025 by letter the release of funds 
in respect of payment conditions that were fulfilled during the period since the entry 
into force of Regulation (EU) 2025/535 on 18 March 2025  and until 30 June 2025. 
The present Implementing Decision provides the breakdown of the amounts to be 
released in the form of a loan and the non -repayable support to be provided under the 
Neighbourhood Investment Platform (NIP). 
(7) In accordance with Article 8(3) of Regulation (EU) 202 5/535, disbursement of Union 
support under the Fac ility is to take place in accordance with the terms of the Facility 
Agreement, and the Loan Agreement . In addition, disbursements are conditional on 
the Beneficiary’s compliance with the preconditions set out in Article 5 (1) of 
Regulation (EU) 2025/535 and with the general payment conditions set out in Article 
10(3) of Regulation (EU) 202 5/535, and on the satisfactory fulfilment of the payment 
conditions set out in the Reform Agenda , as referred to in Article 10(1) of Regulation 
(EU) 2025/535.  
(8) Pursuant to Article 19(2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, following its assessment the 
Commission concluded that the Republic of Moldova complies with the preconditions 
and the general payment conditions. 
(9) Pursuant to Article 19(2) of Regulation (EU) 202 5/535, the Commission assessed the 
request for release of funds from the Republic of Moldova  and concluded that it has 
satisfactorily f ulfilled four out of four of the payment conditions related to the 
quantitative and qualitative steps as set out in its request and in its Reform Agenda.  
The Commission assessment is included in the Annex to this Decision. 
(10) The envisaged assistance is to comply with the conditions and procedures set out by 
the restrictive measures 4 adopted pursuant to Article 215 of the Treaty on the 
Functioning of the European Union. 
(11) In accordance with Article 1 of the Loan Agreement, the loan support is composed of 
two components: the Allocated Percentage ( 25%), which the Republic of Moldova  is 
required to invest in projects approved under the NIP, and the remaining part of the 
loan. 
(12) In accordance with Article 6( 2) of Regulation (EU) 2025/535, an amount of non -
repayable financial support is provided by the Union for projects to be approved under 
the NIP. 
(13) For the portion of the loan support allocated to investment projects to be approved 
under the NIP, the Republic of Moldova is to provide the Commission an Engagement 
letter in accordance with the procedure set out in Article 20 of the Facility Agreement. 
(14) Therefore, the release of funds, in the form of non -repayable financial support and 
loan support, for the Republic of Moldova  under the Reform and Growth Facility for 
the Republic of Moldova should be approved. 
(15) In accordance with Article 13(3) of the Facility Agreement and Article 6(5) of the 
Loan Agreement, pre -financing of the non -repayable finan cial support and the loan 
support should be cleared by being proportionally deducted against the payment of the 
instalments. Since EUR 24 300 000.00 of the non -repayable financial support and

HAS DECIDED AS FOLLOWS:  
Article 1 
Authorisation of the release of the non-repayable support for the Republic of Moldova 
(1) Based on the assessment of the payment conditions, the non -repayable financial 
support component of the first request for release of funds under the Reform and 
Growth Facility for the Republic of Moldova shall be set at a gross amount of EUR 
2 076 923.08.  
(2) In accordance with Article 13(3) of the Facility Agreement, an amount of  
EUR 373 846.16 shall be utilised to clear the non -repayable financial support part of 
the pre-financing. 
(3) The net amount of EUR 1 703 076.92 shall be released in accordance with the terms 
of the Facility Agreement.  
(4) The amount set out in paragraph 3 shall be made available for investment projects to 
be approved under the NIP. 
Article 2 
Authorisation of the release of the loan support for the Republic of Moldova 
(1) Based on the assessment of the payment conditions , the loan support component of 
the first request for release of funds under the Reform and Growth Facility for the 
Republic of Moldova shall be set at a gross amount of EUR 23 076 923.08. 
(2) In accordance with Article 6(5) of the Loan Agreement, an amount of  
EUR 4 153 846.16 shall be utilised to clear the loan support part of the pre-financing. 
(3) The net amount of EUR 18 923 076.92 shall be released in accordance with Article 8 
of the Loan Agreement. 
(4) In accordance with the Loan Agreement, the net amount shall be split into two 
components: EUR 4 730 769.23, corresponding to the Allocated Percentage ( 25%), 
to be made available for investment projects to be approved under the NIP ; and EUR 
14 192 307.69, representing the remaining portion of the loan.  
(5) The Commission shall disburse to the Republic of Moldova  the amount of EUR  
14 192 307.69 corresponding to the remaining portion of the loan referred to in 
paragraph 4.
