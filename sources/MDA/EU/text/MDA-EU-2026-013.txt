indicative priorities.
EU GROWTH PLAN FOR MOLDOVA
In October 2024, the Commission proposed a Growth Plan for the 
Republic of Moldova, worth up to €1.9 billion for the period 2025-2027. 
Based on a more-for-more approach, the implementation of reforms 
determines the amount of funding that Moldova receives. It is the largest 
ever stand-alone EU financial support package for the country. It is 
expected to mobilise, an additional €2.5 billion of new investments from 
international financial institutions and the private sector.
The plan will boost Moldova’s economy, accelerate reforms and 
benefit Moldovans directly by enabling better roads, energy security 
infrastructure, modernised schools, new hospitals, support for small 
businesses, or the protection of Moldova’s cultural heritage. 
Supporting the country’s socio-economic convergence is essential for Moldova’s EU accession.
To this date, Moldova has unlocked €661 million under the Growth Plan, while implementing key reforms.

GLOBAL GATEWAY
The EU’s Global Gateway strategy supports investments and mobilises 
the private sector and international financing institutions, towards more 
resilient economies. It delivers concrete benefits for people in areas such 
as transport, energy, environment and climate, water and sanitation 
and digital development.
In addition to the support in grants, in 2021-2025, the EU has mobilised 
€1.7 billion for investment projects in Moldova. 
Ongoing investments include: 
Providing energy efficiency improvements to healthcare and education institutions 
across the country, including improvements of heating systems and installation of photovoltaic 
systems. 
Rehabilitating the Valcinet-Ungheni-Chisinau-Cainari rail corridor
as part of the EU-Ukraine Solidarity Lanes initiative. 
Development of European Standard Gauge railway line between Ungheni and Chisinau
boosting trade and economic development and contributing to Moldova’s physical integration into 
the EU rail network.
Improving insulation of buildings 
installing more energy efficient lighting, heating, cooling and ventilation systems, integrating 
renewable energy sources and introducing efficient energy management practices. 
Road transport infrastructure
building connections within Moldova and bringing the country closer to the EU. 
In 2025, over 9,000 Small and Medium Enterprises benefited from EU support 
through the EU4Business project. Support is provided through enhanced access to finance, 
business development services and strengthened business enabling environment.

TRADE
The EU is Moldova’s main trading partner. In 
2025, the EU accounted for 55.9% of Moldova’s total 
trade in goods (69.8% of total exports and 51.3% of 
total imports). Total EU Foreign Direct Investment 
reached €2 billion by 2024.
The Deep and Comprehensive Free Trade Area is 
an integral part of the Association Agreement, in full 
effect since 2016.  
Moldova is part of the Single Euro Payments Area 
(SEPA) since October 2025, which, via participating 
banks, lowers transaction costs for businesses and 
citizens. It also deepens Moldova’s integration into 
the European financial system and stimulates cross-
border trade and investments.

EU SOLIDARITY WITH MOLDOVA
• In response to the 2025 energy crisis and related price increases, the 
EU and Moldova agreed on a two-year Comprehensive Strategy 
for Energy Independence and Resilience aimed at eleminating 
Moldova’
s dependence on Russian energy and fully integrating 
Moldova into the EU energy market. 
•
 This support came in addition to the extensive budget support 
provided for the energy sector in Moldova between 2021 and 2025, 
which helped compensate households for rising gas and electricity 
bills.
•
 In light of the impact of Russia’
s war of aggression against Ukraine on 
Moldova, the EU has mobilised over €93.5 million of humanitarian 
assistance to support vulnerable Ukrainian refugees and families in 
Moldova.
•
 Moldova has participated in the Union 
Civil Protection Mechanism since 2023, 
strengthening its cooperation with the EU 
on disaster preparedness and emergency 
response.
