=== PAGE 3 ===
Problems caused by lack of interoperability
➢ Inefficiency of the public service provision process
➢ Low quality of the decision-making process
➢ Low administrative performance
Ineffective interoperability, why?
➢ Resistance from data owners – reduced data vailability
➢ Data inconsistency and incoherence
➢ Redundancy of collected data
➢ Technical incompatibility of IS – low connectivity
➢ Bilateral technical dependencies
➢ Lack of standards for ensuring data protection
➢ Rigid legal framework
