Executive Summary: 
The implementation of the Republic of Moldova Digital Transformation Strategy  (MDTS) is a 
priority for the Government of the Republic of Moldova in order to align with the European and 
international standards of a modern and innovative digital society. The  MDTS was adopted by 
Government in September 2023 and with an aim of supporting Moldova’s efforts in achieving the 
UN Sustainable Development Goals and aligning with existing European policies and standards 
in the digital field. 
To support efforts to implement the MDTS, several initiatives were undertaken in 2023, including 
the establishment of the National Digi tal Transformation Council (NDTC). One of the main 
objectives of the strategy is the digitalization of public services, and the results achieved in 2023 
show significant progress in this respect: both the increase in the percentage of digitalized public 
services and the number of requests for e-services for the business environment (70%). 
To ensure a secure digital environment for citizens, various legislative and institutional updates 
have been made. One of the successes of the Ministry of Economic Development and 
Digitalization (MDED) in this regard is the establishment of the Agency for Cyber Security. This will 
ensure an appropriate legal and institutional framework in the field of cybersecurity. 
Partnerships and collaborations with donors and external organi zations have played a crucial 
role in supporting digital transformation efforts, helping to access the necessary resources and 
expertise. In 2024, efforts to implement external assistance projects will continue, with a focus 
on coordination and alignment with strategic objectives. 
The main challenges identified concern the low availability of financial and human resources. In 
addition to these challenges, institutional rigidity sometimes hampers and slows down 
digitalization efforts. 
2024 will be dynamic year, in which the digital transformation process of the Republic of Moldova 
will continue. In addition to the projects to digitalize society as a whole, governance elements 
and programs detailing the strategy’s action plans will be finalized.

1 Introduction:  
 
The Republic of Moldova Digital Transformation Strategy  2023-2030 (MDTS) represents a 
commitment of the Government of the Republic of Moldova to align the Republic of Moldova with 
the European standards of a modern and innovative digital society.  
This strategy has been developed on the basis of the principles set out in the United Nations 
2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, indirectly contributing to the achievement of all the 
Sustainable Development Goals, and directly to Goal 9 – Industry Innovation and Infrastructure. 
The Strategy is also aligned with the Digital Compass 2030 , the Digital Decade , and the 
Association Agreement of the Republic of Moldova with the European Union, thus ensuring a 
framework for aligning the country with existing policies at European level.  
At national level, this strategy aligns itself with the National Development Strategy “European 
Moldova 2030” – which is the country’s long-term strategic vision document, and which includes 
the country’s national and international commitments. Along also exists with the National Plan 
“Building European Moldova” 20 Government Priority Actions. Although the objectives and 
efforts undertaken through the strategy will lead to the indirect achievement of several priority 
actions of the Plan, the priority action directly targeted in the Strategy is Action 5 – Digitalization 
of Public Services. 
This report is an essential element of the implementation effort of the MDTS, as it identifies and 
summarizes the main strengths and achievements in the field of digitalization, as well as the 
main challenges and barriers encountered in the implementation process.  
While the Strategy was approved in September 2023 and was only in force for 3 months in 2023, 
this report will identify both initiatives and procedures that have been undertaken by the 
institutions responsible to operationalize the implementation of the strategy, as well as the result 
indicators that can be measured for 2023.The conclusions identified in this report will be used in 
the design and implementation of public policies in the field of digital transformation for the 
Republic of Moldova.

2 Overview of progress:  
2.1 Review of Objectives and Appraisal of Progress:  
 
The implementation of the MDTS will enable the Republic of Moldova to become an innovative 
and inclusive digital society with advanced digital skills, robust digital infrastructure, pro -digital 
governance, and a business community that maximizes digital opportunities. To achieve this 
vision, the Strategy includes a set of 6 general objectives, detailed in 62 sub -objectives 
(sometimes called priority directions) to support the achievement of 33 result indicators.  
For this annual implementation report, the review of progress includes: 
• quantitative elements, such as measurement of result indicators (those indicators that 
can be measured for 2023); 
• qualitative elements, such as key measures in the field of public policies, development 
of implementation program s, organization of governance elements related to the 
strategy, etc. 
 
2.1.1 Analysis of Progress: 
2.1.1.1 Operationalization of the Strategy 
To support efforts to coordinate and implement the Strategy, the National  Digitalization 
Transformation Council (NDTC) was established on 27 November 2023 by order of the Minister 
for Economic Development and Digitalization. The NDTC has the role of coordinating the 
implementation of the Strategy and approved programs to achieve the MDTS’ vision and strategic 
objectives. The first NDTC meeting took place on 11 December 2023, where the following steps 
in the implementation of the Strategy were agreed: 
• Development of sectoral implementation programs to support the implementation 
process; 
• Consultation of the programs with the NDTC; and, 
• Establishment of sectoral working groups to support the NDTC in coordinating the 
implementation of the programs. 
 
2.1.1.2 Strategy implementation activities 
One of the main objectives of the Strategy, which is also found in the National Plan “Building 
European Moldova” is to digitalize public services and increase their adoption among individuals 
and businesses. Since the adoption of the Strategy in September 2023  through the end of 2023  
2023, the percentage of digitalized public services increased from 38% to more than 50%. The 
services were digitalized with the support of the Electronic Governance Agency, implementing 
the Government Services Modernization Project (MGSP), implemented with the support of the 
World Bank Group.  During 2023, through the project, 41 government to citizen p ublic services 
predominantly were  digitalized: civil status documents, cadastral documents, criminal records, 
apostille services, documents from the national archive , extracts from registers, and others. In 
addition, 4 public services for businesses were also digitalized.

At the end 2023, 70% of the requests for business services (G2B) were online. Two (2) out of three 
(3) entrepreneurs prefer to use online services and only 30% of requests are still in-person. Out 
of 3.7 million requests for various services for business, 2.48 million were provided online and 
1.1 million were in -person. MDED ensures the transparency of the digitalization process of 
services by proactively publishing data on the Ministry’s website and updating this data quarterly. 
In addition to the successes in   digitalizing public services, Moldova joined the Digital Europe 
Programme on 19 February 2024. Moldova is eligible for 4 of the 5 components of the Programme: 
Artificial Intelligence, Digital Skills, Digital Transformation, and Connectivity. Private, public, and 
civil society  entities in the Republic of Moldova can apply with innovative initiatives to receive 
funding. The Programme has a total value of 7.5 billion euros divided among member countries. 
Moldova will benefit from an exemption of 95 % of the annual financial contribution as a member 
of the Programme.

Based on the data existing at the time of the preparation of this report, some successes in 
achieving result indicators can be observed at 2023 level, namely: 
• The share of population with 100Mbps connectivity  – a value reported at the end of

To improve the quality of the monitoring, reporting and evaluation process, a set of activities will 
be carried out in 2024 to finalize governance arrangements around the strategy implementation 
process. These activities include, but are not limited to: 
• Detailing the result indicators and developing the “indicator passport”  allowing for 
continuous monitoring, as well as the development of related explanatory notes for a 
better understanding and interpretation of the data. 
• Establishment of the Strategic Delivery Unit (SDU) within the eGovernment Agency. The 
SDU will be responsible for the implementation, monitoring, evaluation efforts, ensuring 
effective coordination between the relevant actors involved. In its work, the SDU will work 
in coordination with the NDTC, MDED, and eGA, to ensure a harmonious and coordinated 
implementation process. 
• Mechanisms for identifying and reporting bottlenecks in the implementation of  the 
strategy, as well as methods to identify solutions to remove bottlenecks, will be detailed. 
These mechanisms will be used by the SDU both to inform MDED and eGA about 
potential bottlenecks, but also to generate possible solutions. 
Also, in 2024, the NDTC will continue its work.  In this regard, MDED  (acting as the NDTC 
Secretariat) and the SDU will coordinate and ensure the regular meeting of the NDTC to discuss 
the progress and problems identified in the implementation of the MDTS.

2.2.2 Institutions 
The establishment of the National Agency for Cyber Security (ASC) is a major success of the 
Ministry of Economic Development and Digitalization in 2023. The ASC will support efforts to 
implement the strategy and fulfil the following functions to ensure a secure digital development 
framework: 
• Identifying and recording service providers; 
• State supervision and control of compliance by service providers with the provisions of 
the law; 
• National and international cooperation and information exchange on cybersecurity; 
• Single national point of contact; 
• National cyber incident response team; 
• Methodological and regulatory guidance function; and, 
• Research and Development.

accessible, secure and inclusive digital environment”.

3 Partnerships and Collaborations:  
The Government of the Republic of Moldova enjoys external support and assistance in the 
process of digital transformation of the country. The impact of the collaborations and 
partnerships in digital transformation is of major importance and relevance.  Most of the 
successes achieved in 2023 for digital transformation were delivered through partners with the 
Government and partners.  
 
As implementation of the MDTS continues,  coordination of digital transformation projects and 
programs with external partners is a priority for the Government. In this respect, the Government 
of the Republic of Moldova together with its partners will continue the efforts to identify, prioritize 
and allocate resources efficiently in order to maximize their efficiency in achieving the objectives 
of the Strategy.

4 Lessons learned and opportunities 
The MDTS envisions Moldova as a regional leader in capitalizing on technological progress for the 
well-being of citizens. Achieving this vision requires close and coordinated collaboration 
between all key actors: the private sector, the public sector, civil society, and partners. 
The detailed analysis of the actions carried out in 2023 revealed a number of challenges as well 
as learning opportunities for the future: 
 Lessons learned Opportunities  
1 Further coordination with 
external partners is needed to 
unlock financial resources. 
The cost of digitalizing public services is higher than the 
existing financial resources available for Government . The 
expertise and support provided by external partners are 
crucial. 
In 2024, MDED and its external partners will cooperate to 
prioritize needs, and to ensure that the financial resources 
allocated are geared towards achieving impact, through the 
NDTC and sectoral working groups. 
2 Overcoming institutional 
rigidity for more effective 
interactions with business 
and citizens is an integral part 
of the transformation. 
Both the public and private sectors are relatively slow in 
adapting practices that reduce work and increase labor 
productivity. A clear example is the  use of corporate 
stamps: even though they are no longer mandatory . There 
has also been  reluctance to capitalize on data from 
interconnected information systems and slow adoption  of 
electronic signatures. 
The government will continue to invest in interoperable 
platforms and e -government solutions to facilitate access 
to information and public services.  
3 Human resources are critical 
to Moldova’s success in the 
digital transformation 
process. 
The “brain drain” phenomenon, which has been 
characteristic of the Republic of Moldova in the last 30 
years, negatively affects the public and private sector s’ 
capacity to innovate and modernize. Access to a highly 
skilled and trained workforce is critical to achieve the  
MDTS, but competition in the labor market is strong for such 
resources. 
MDED and its partners will identify opportunities to 
capitalize on the most important existing resource – 
talented human resources – for the most important projects 
in 2024. Increased attention also be put on upskilling and 
resource sharing of existing resources across digital 
initiatives.

Digital transformation is a complex and long -lasting process that requires continuous efforts, 
perseverance, and adaptability from all parties. Much of the next efforts to implement the MDTS 
will be concentrated in the following areas: 
• Detailing the three sectoral programs. These programs will serve as annual action plans 
for digital transformation efforts for the coming years in the Republic of Moldova. 
• Developing inclusive governance structures and processes to support an accelerated 
pace of implementation for digital projects. These include: operationalization of the 
working groups of the NDTC; intensify collaboration between government institutions, as 
well as proactive collaboration with the private sector and civil society to support the 
implementation of the strategy. 
• Doubling efforts to reduce bureaucracy, with a focus on digitalizing and simplifying 
government services, to serve the needs of an inclusive, technological , and modern 
state. 
• Operationalizing the ASC to support the security of the country’s digital infrastructure 
and increase the country’s resilience in an increasingly complex digital world. 
2024 will certainly be a dynamic year, full of many activities aimed at supporting Moldova’s digital 
future. Proactively addressing the challenges identified and taking advantage of the lessons 
drawn are essential to ensure the success of this transformati on. MDED and its partners will 
continue to work throughout 2024 to identify opportunities for improvement and turn these 
lessons into opportunities to accelerate Moldova’s digitalization process.
