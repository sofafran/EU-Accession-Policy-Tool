General Position 
–
 
Opening Statement of North Macedonia
 
for the Political Meeting of the Intergovernmental Conference
 
on the accession of North
 
Macedonia to the European Union
 
Brussels, 19 July 2022
 
1.
 
On behalf of the citizens and the Government of the 
Republic of North Macedonia, it is an 
exceptional honour for me to have the opportunity to address, in the Macedonian language, 
today's long awaited and well
-
deserved intergovernmental conference opening the 
negotiations for the accession of North M
acedoni
a in the European Union.
 
2.
 
Today, North Macedonia begins the last stage of its long journey towards full membership of 
the European Union. This intergovernmental conference operationalizes the Council decision 
of March 2020 and opens the process of admission
 
of North Macedonia and its citizens into 
the big European family, into our common home, the European Union, as our multi
-
decade 
strategic goal.
 
3.
 
North Macedonia formally applied for membership in 2004 and gained candidate country 
status in 2005. Ever since
 
October 2009, the European Commission has recommended to open 
accession negotiations with North Macedonia, and the Council confirmed its 
recommendations with a unanimous decision in March 2020.
 
4.
 
This day is historic for us. We are taking the crucial step on the way to achieving the three
-
decade generational dream of the vast majority of our citizens. Dozens of our state leaders, 
political representatives, the civil sector, academic and scientific 
workers, businessmen, 
journalists and media workers, and thousands of state officials have tirelessly worked to make 
this dream a reality. They all have a part in what we have achieved so far. We dedicate this 
day to all of them, we thank them for their su
pport and efforts, and we pay respect for 
all the
 
work they have done.

5.
 
Enlargement is undoubtedly one of the most successful policies of the Union. The process of 
unification of the European continent began with the accession of 13 countries from Central 
and Southeast Europe, and it will not be completed without the integration of the countries of 
the Western Balkans into the EU. The enlargement policy most directly contributes to the 
maintenance of peace and stability in our region, to the improvement of 
democracy, the rule 
of law and the protection of human rights and freedoms, and to the strengthening of the 
competitiveness of our economies. North Macedonia made a strategic decision to join the 
Union, because we are deeply convinced that it is our future
, to which there
 
is no other valid 
alternative.
 
6.
 
North Macedonia shall continue to invest and contribute to the common future, as a partner 
that the EU can rely on in good times and in bad. Russia’s aggression against the 
independence, territorial integrity
 
and sovereignty of Ukraine has clearly shown that nothing 
is given once and for all and that all threats to the peace, stability and security on the continent 
must be jointly addressed.
 
7.
 
Civilizationally, geographically and historically, North Macedonia be
longs to the European 
family. We have a representative cultural, historical and natural heritage and a vibrant cultural 
scene that will enrich the diversity of the Union with our membership. As a multi
-
ethnic and 
multi
-
confessional functional democracy, we
 
are building a society for all. Through the Ohrid 
Framework Agreement and subsequent constitutional amendments, North Macedonia created 
an advanced model that ensures equality in the realisation of the cultural, linguistic and 
identity rights of the commu
nities, making it an example of a successful unitary multi
-
ethnic 
state on the European continent.
 
8.
 
North Macedonia does not have an open territorial dispute with any neighbour, and fully 
implements its obligations from the Stabilisation and Association Pro
cess. We constantly and 
decisively promote good neighbourly relations and regional cooperation, and constructively 
and proactively participate in all regional organisations and initiatives.

17.
 
North Macedonia is ready to fully adopt the EU acquis in its internal legal order and to build 
efficient administrative and judicial capacities to ensure effective application and 
enforcement. By signing the Stabilisat
ion and Association Agreement (SAA) in 2001, as the 
first country in the region, North Macedonia assumed the obligation to gradually align its 
legislation with the EU acquis. Starting from 2006, North Macedonia adopts and updates the 
National Programme for
 
the Adoption of the Acquis, achieving already now a high level of 
compliance of around 45%. Accession negotiations will only be an additional incentive to 
deepen and accelerate the integration into the EU Single Market and the full transposition of 
the ac
quis into the national 
legal and institutional system.
 
18.
 
The conclusion of the SAA was of exceptional importance for the European integration of 
North Macedonia. The SAA established a formal political dialogue framework, at bilateral 
and regional level, a fr
ee trade zone with the EU, and also ensured economic, social, 
educational, scientific, technological, energy, environmental and cultural cooperation, through 
continuous legal harmonisation and administrative capacity building. The SSA will remain in 
force 
until North Macedonia accedes to the Union.
 
19.
 
Through the SAA, EU has become the main trading partner of North Macedonia. The total 
trade exchange increased by 744% from 2002 to 2021. Macedonian economy is structurally 
inextricably linked to the EU economy, 
not only in terms of trade but also in direct foreign 
in
vestments and business ties.
 
20.
 
All relevant institutions in North Macedonia are fully prepared to contribute to the efficient 
and effective implementation of the screening and to continue further the al
ignment of the 
national legal framework with the EU standards. Today's opening of the accession 
negotiations contributes to the renewal of the collective enthusiasm in the administration, 
most directly encourages reforms and will increase the confidence of
 
the society in the 
purposefulness and the certainty of the accession process.
