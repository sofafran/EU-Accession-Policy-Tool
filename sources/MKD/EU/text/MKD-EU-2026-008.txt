GROWTH PLAN FOR THE WESTERN 
BALKANS
Under the Reform and Growth Facility for the Western 
Balkans, €750.4 million in non-repayable grants and 
favourable loans are allocated to North Macedonia for the 
implementation of this Reform Agenda. As of November 2026, 
€142.1 million has been released in recognition of progress in 
implementing reforms. 
North Macedonia’s Reform Agenda addresses critical 
issues and sets clear targets for progress across key policy 
areas. The successful implementation of these reforms 
will significantly enhance its public finance management 
and public administration, digital transformation and 
green transition, human capital development, business 
democracy judicial integrity, fight against corruption and 
organised crime.

Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance
In 2021-2027, the EU has allocated over €670 million of financial and 
technical assistance to North Macedonia under IPA III. Assistance is 
provided in the form of non-repayable grants. This includes:
•
 €50 million for the transport sector
.
•
 €80 million Energy Support Package to support vulnerable 
families and SMEs in North Macedonia in dealing with rising energy 
prices.
•
 €18 million to boost the 
effectiveness and efficiency of public 
administration. 
•
 Over €20 million to accelerate business environment reform and 
transformation
.
•
 €67 million via the 
IPA III 2025-2027 multiannual action for rule of law, good 
governance, education and European Integration.
Global Gateway in North Macedonia
In the framework of Global Gateway, the EU has identified 
investments in sustainable transport, clean energy, 
environment and climate, digital future, human capital and 
the private sector. These investments aim to support a green 
and digital transition and bring the region closer to the EU’s 
single market.  
For projects in North Macedonia, since 2021, the EU has 
provided €665 million, expected to mobilise €1.7 billion in 
total investments. This includes:
Constructing of 23.4 km of railway line 
and electrification of the Kumanovo border railway to the 
border with Bulgaria, providing direct access to modern 
means of transport to 350,000 people.
Increasing the capacity of the Bogdanci wind park 
by up to 15 MW, which will produce 46 GWh of electricity 
annually, meeting the energy demand of 22,000 households 
with clean energy, and avoid 152,000 tonnes of CO2 
emissions each year. 
Constructing a wastewater treatment plant 
for Skopje, expected to serve 625,000 people and mitigate  
pollution of the Vardar River.
ONGOING EU SUPPORT FOR NORTH MACEDONIA
The EU is the largest provider of financial assistance to North 
Macedonia. This support is provided through channels like the 
Instrument for Pre-Accession Assistance (IPA), part of the EU’s Global 
Gateway initiative.

CLOSE TRADE & INVESTMENT LINKS 
The EU is North Macedonia’s main trading partner. In 
2025, the EU accounted for 49.5% of North Macedonia’s 
total trade in goods (48.2% of total exports and 50.1% of 
total imports). Total EU Foreign Direct Investment reached 
€4.4 billion by 2024.
Progressive trade liberalisation and mutual duty-free 
access for most goods has been taking place since 2004 
and is currently based on the EU – North Macedonia 
Stabilisation and Association Agreement (SAA).
FOREIGN, SECURITY AND DEFENCE 
POLICY COOPERATION
North Macedonia fully aligns with the EU Common Foreign and 
Security Policy statements and restrictive measures. In addition: 
•
 The EU and North Macedonia adopted their Security and 
Defence Partnership in November 2024.
•
 North Macedonia continued to actively participate in EU 
crisis management missions and operations
 under the Common Security 
and Defence Policy.
•
 In 2025, North Macedonia received €15 million through the third bilateral 
assistance measure to strengthen defence capabilities under the European 
Peace Facility
, bringing total EPF support to €38 million.
•
 The Balkans Medical 
Task Force, in which North Macedonia participates, 
benefits from a €6 million 2022 support under the European Peace Facility.
•
 The EU supports North Macedonia in strengthening its resilience 
to hybrid threats, including cyber threats and foreign information 
manipulation and interference.
PEOPLE TO PEOPLE CONTACTS

under
