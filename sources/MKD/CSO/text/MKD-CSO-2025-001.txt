Two Decades of Impact: Serving Democracy in a Changing Digital World 
Bardhyl Jashari, Executive Director 
 
In 2024, Metamorphosis Foundation proudly marked twenty years of commitment to democracy, 
digital rights, and civic empowerment. What began as a small, locally rooted initiative has evolved into 
a regional leader, defending truth, advancing civic participation, and shaping digital in a world where 
technology opens new possibilities for progress, while also demanding thoughtful action and shared 
responsibility. This journey has been shaped by the dedication, creativity, and conviction of our 
donors, partners, and staff who believe deeply in our mission and make its impact real every day. 
This milestone year was not only a moment for reflection, but also a period of strategic renewal. We 
launched our Strategy 2024 –2028, shaped through broad community consultation and grounded in 
the belief that people must be at the center of the digital tr ansformation. In an era of accelerated 
technological change, we remain committed to helping individuals navigate the complex and rapidly 
evolving digital landscape — to feel secure, protected, and competent in their online environments. 
At the same time, Metamorphosis is also pushing back against a rising fear we often encounter — the 
fear of technology itself. In a world of rapid change, it’s natural to feel overwhelmed. But when fear 
turns into avoidance, it risks cutting people off from the very tools that can empower them,  protect 
their rights, and amplify their voices. We believe technology should be understood, not feared — and 
that everyone deserves the chance to adapt, to learn, and to feel confident navigating the digital 
world. If we leave people behind, we don’t just risk widening divides — we risk sliding toward a future 
shaped by exclusion, manipulation, and disempowerment. The future doesn’t have to be dystopian. 
With active engagement, the right skills, protections, and choic es, it can be fair, free, and human -
centered. 
Our efforts in 2024 spanned from countering disinformation during national elections to driving 
institutional transparency, building civic resilience, and advocating for rights-based digital governance. 
But we know that empowering citizens is not enough: we must also be actively engaged in shaping 
the policies, standards, and systems that govern how technologies are developed, deployed, and used 
— ensuring they serve the public interest. 
Throughout this report, you’ll find evidence of our impact, innovation, and enduring partnerships. 
Whether leading regional fact-checking coalitions, amplifying civic voices, or championing responsible 
digital transformation, Metamorphosis continues to ope rate at the intersection of technology and 
democratic values — with integrity, vision, and a commitment to ensuring that no one is left behind.

Summary 
In 2024, the Metamorphosis Foundation continued to build on two decades of work dedicated to 
fostering democracy, human rights, and responsible digital transformation in North Macedonia and 
beyond. Following a comprehensive evaluation of the previous strategy’s implementation, we 
collaboratively developed a new mid -term Strategy for the period 2024 –2028. This inclusive and 
participatory process enabled us to integrate diverse perspectives, ensuring that the new Strategy was 
responsive to evolving challenges and reflective of the shared values and goals of our broader 
community. Our Strategy served not only our internal needs but also addressed our beneficiaries – 
especially youth, decision-makers, local, national, regional, and international partner CSOs, journalists,  
and the general public.  
Throughout the year, Metamorphosis maintained its commitment to partnering with both 
governments and citizens to advance democracy . We upheld responsible innovation as a guiding 
principle, constantly exploring and implementing creative solutions to enhance civic engagement and 
support democratic processes. 
The year 2024 held special significance for us, as we celebrated the 20 th anniversary of the 
Foundation’s establishment. To mark this milestone, we organized the traditional e -Society.mk 
conference under the theme "Digital Transformation for Life" . Once again, we gathered experts, 
policymakers, and civil society representatives to deliberate on the advancements and challenges of 
digitalization, emphasizing its impact on societal development and quality of life. A memorable 
moment at this celebration was the inaugural presentation of the "Ilija Županoski" Award, introduced 
to honour outstanding contributions to combating disinformation. 
Throughout 2024, Metamorphosis Foundation continued to apply a human rights -based approach 
to capacity building, targeting both public authorities responsible for ensuring good governance and 
rights holders advocating for a democratic and inclusive society. We prioritized ensuring that ongoing 
technological development and future digital transformations protected human rights online and left 
no one behind. Special focus was placed on reducing the digital divide among citizens by equipping 
them with essential digital skills and ensuring their participation in decision-making processes related 
to digital development.  
Our efforts to counter disinformation and foreign information manipulation and interference 
remained continuous. Combining approaches such as fact -checking, watchdog and investigative 
journalism, and media monitoring, we worked to expose and debunk manipul ations across North 
Macedonia and the Western Balkans region. Beyond strengthening our partnerships with civil society 
and journalist organizations, we actively advocated for the government to develop comprehensive 
policy against disinformation, upholding our belief in a whole-of-society approach. These efforts were 
recognized internationally, as the Executive Director, Bardhyl Jashari,  was elected as a member of  
the new Governing Board of the European Fact -Checking Standards Network (EFCSN),  further 
bonding our leadership in strengthening fact-checking practices and promoting media accountability. 
In 2024, we reshaped and focused our programmes on three key areas: Responsible Digital 
Transformation, Social Accountability, and Media and Information Integrity . We provided

PROGRAMME HIGHLIGHTS IN 2024 
 
• In 2024, engaging young audiences (ages 14 –29) remained a central focus for Meta News Agency 
and Portalb. One of the most notable activities was the series of workshops which Metamorphosis 
Foundation organized together with the International Center for Jou rnalists. More than 50 young 
journalists and students participated in the workshops on subjects like data journalism and 
countering disinformation. In 2024 for the first time in North Macedonia, Metamorphosis together 
with ICFJ organized a workshop on pre-bunking. The best 6 participants on the workshops were 
invited for a one-month internship at Metamorphosis media, and out of them three got the chance 
to attend a weeklong study tour in Washington DC. For young journalists at the beginning of their 
career, visiting newsrooms like Voice of America, Washington Post, as well as organizations like ICFJ 
or the Philip Merril School of Journalism is an endeavor they will never forget which will guide them 
in their media careers. 
• In February 2024, European Union Ambassador in North Macedonia David Geer, along with 
ambassadors from Germany, the Netherlands, Sweden, and Italy visited the Metamorphosis 
Foundation to express solidarity following recent threats and smear campaigns against its staff. 
They emphasized the vital role of fact -checkers in safeguarding democracy and ensuring public 
access to accurate information. Ambassador Geer stated: " There should be no place for incitement 
of hatred or spreading false accusations in the media”. 
• In November 2024, the Metamorphosis Foundation hosted the 20th annual international 
conference, e -Society.mk: "Digital Transformation for Life". The event convened experts, 
policymakers, and civil society representatives to discuss advancements and challen ges in 
digitalization, emphasizing its impact on societal development and quality of life. A notable feature 
of this year's conference was the inaugural presentation of the "Ilija Županoski" award, recognizing 
significant contributions to combating disinformation. The winners of the award were the Institute 
for Media and Analytics – IMA and the journalist Ardit Ramadani. Additionally, the conference 
included presentations from individuals wi th disabilities, highlighting how artificial intelligence 
technologies are enhancing accessibility and inclusion.  
• In 2024, the Metamorphosis Foundation conducted a pioneering study analyzing the influence of 
state-allocated funds on the impartiality of online media portals during the parliamentary elections. 
The research monitored 30 selected news portals, examining 13,645 election -related articles 
between April 15 and May 10, 2024.  Findings revealed a significant correlation: portals receiving 
substantial funding from a particular political coalition exhibited less impartial reporting, often 
favoring the funding coalition.  Additionally, Meta.mk's investigative journalism uncovered that 
political coalitions allocated a total of €811,000 for advertising across more than 150 online portals 
during the parliamentary elections. In November 2024, Meta.mk published another investigative 
report revealing that out of 150 online portals receiving state funds for the parliamentary election 
campaign, 40 lacked an impressum, indicating unclear ownership. This underscores the need for 
transparent allocation of public funds to media outlets to maintain journalistic integrity and public 
trust. Metam orphosis Foundation also organized a two-day event  titled "Money, Media, and 
Elections," bringing together experts, politicians, and civil society representatives to examine the 
interplay between finances, media, and politics during election processes. This significant 
expenditure raised concerns about transparency and accountability in the allocation of state funds

"Deepfake or Reality" at the "KOTUR" café and cinema. The event aimed to educate young people 
on identifying deepfakes— synthetic media created using artificial intelligence that can manipulate 
videos, audios, and images to mislead viewers. Participants engaged in discussions and quizzes to 
enhance their ability to recognize and critically assess such content. Speakers included Bojan 
Stojkovski, editor-in-chief of IT.mk, Ivan Čorbiev, a professor from FINKI, and Meri Jordanovska,

Meta.mk - Independent News Agency  
In 2024 Meta News Agency celebrated one decade of existence! It 
officially started working in November 2014 and established a solid 
reputation of a reliable source of objective news on current events in 
Macedonian and English, as well as other Balkan langu ages, whose 
contents is republished by over thirty domestic and foreign media 
outlets. Meta.mk provides a professional news service, and its reporting 
is used as a reference both by the media and decision makers, including 
the highest representatives of the international community.   
Meta News Agency in 2024 published more than 7000 articles in total out 
of which 6000 in Macedonian language and the rest in English, Albanian 
and BCS. Its website had more than one million pageviews and 680,000 
users.

Portalb.mk – News Portal in Albanian Language 
The news portal Portalb.mk was developed in April 2012, and over the 
years grew into one of the leading and most credible sources of 
information in Albanian language from North Macedonia. Its reputation 
based on strict implementation of journalistic standa rds is 
complemented with the popularity as one of the most influential online 
media among decision makers and general audience, with fan base of 
over 230,000 social networks followers.   
In a very segregated media environment, Portalb contributes to 
increasing the social cohesion by providing an important linguistic bridge 
towards the Albanian -speaking communities by reporting on issues 
affecting citizens of all ethnicities in the country and translating contents 
produced in Macedonian and other languages.  
In 2024 Portalb published more than 8000 articles, receiving 1.5 million 
pageviews and about 650,000 users on its website.
