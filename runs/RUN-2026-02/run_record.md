## Run-specific settings

These settings apply the country-neutral procedures to this run. Every statement about the country is checked at stage 1 against registered EU sources before analytical use.

- **Accession context (to be confirmed at stage 1):** candidate status granted in June 2022; accession negotiations opened in June 2024; screening of the acquis carried out in 2024 and 2025; the rollout plan records that the Commission reported on 13 October 2026 that the internal market cluster (Cluster 2, which contains Chapter 2) was opened, so opening benchmarks may exist and closing benchmarks may be set; main instruments are the Growth Plan for Moldova and the Moldova Reform and Growth Facility with its Reform Agenda; the latest Commission report is the 2025 report, with the 2026 report due in the Enlargement Package on 4 November 2026; whether a Rule of Law Report country chapter exists for Moldova is to be checked. None of these points is used analytically until confirmed from registered EU sources.
- **Chapter 2 boundary:** Chapter 2 is read in its EU meaning: the rights and access of EU and foreign workers in the country, access to the labour market, equal treatment, EURES, the European Labour Authority, coordination of social security, the European Health Insurance Card, and anti-corruption measures the Commission ties specifically to Chapter 2. Emigration, brain drain and the diaspora are in scope only where the source directly links them to those issues. Nationals' access to EU labour markets and general labour-market policy are out of scope. Moldova has a large share of citizens holding a second (mostly Romanian, so EU) citizenship; this is noted as context only and is not a reason to widen the boundary.
- **Territorial coverage (approved at G0, G0-D03, LIM-0044):** the Transnistrian region is outside the effective control of the constitutional authorities. Relevant evidence about it is recorded as sources state it, but Moldova's implementation performance is not judged where the government does not exercise effective control. The autonomous territorial unit of Gagauzia is part of Moldova and is assessed normally where relevant.
- **Digital skills:** not a theme area; included only where directly linked to an in-scope technology or AI recommendation.
- **Folders and IDs:** sources are filed under `sources/MDA/<family>/` and evidence under `evidence/MDA/`. Source IDs take the form `MDA-<family>-<year>-<sequence>`, for example `MDA-EU-2025-001_EC_Moldova-Report-2025.pdf`. Reform issue IDs are shared and carry no country code.
- **Country names as printed:** the EU uses "the Republic of Moldova" and "Moldova". The official language is named "Romanian" since the 2023 constitutional amendment; earlier texts may say "Moldovan" or "the state language". Ministries have been reorganised several times since 2020 (for example labour and social protection, economic development and digitalisation); quotes keep names as printed and actor records list earlier names under `former_names`.
- **Evidence already held:** 13 Moldova sources were registered in the alpha's comparator step (RUN-2026-01, role `comparator`): MDA-CSO-2023-001, MDA-EU-2024-001, MDA-EU-2025-001, MDA-GOV-2020-001, MDA-GOV-2021-001 (inaccessible), MDA-GOV-2021-002, MDA-GOV-2024-001, MDA-GOV-2024-002, MDA-GOV-2024-003, MDA-GOV-2025-001, MDA-GOV-2026-001, MDA-IO-2024-001 and MDA-IO-2024-002.
