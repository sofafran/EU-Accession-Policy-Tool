## 1. What the run produced

RUN-2026-01 was an alpha product and methodology test covering North Macedonia, Chapter 2 (Freedom of movement for workers) and the cross-cutting technology and AI theme, with evidence from 1 January 2020 to the freeze date of 29 October 2026. It ran from 29 October to 2 November 2026.

- **Sources:** 112 North Macedonia sources registered (99 available, 13 inaccessible) and, in the comparator pilot, 54 (43 available, 9 inaccessible, 2 excluded).
- **Evidence:** 4,408 North Macedonia items, of which 3,949 are in scope, and 580 comparator items; every quote verified word for word.
- **Structure:** 48 approved reform issues (14 Chapter 2, 34 technology), 12 problem mechanisms, 716 interventions, 968 links.
- **Judgements:** 11,072, all validated except one analyst override (RI-TEC-017 priority, set to High at G6).
- **Comparator pilot:** nine cases on three issues (RI-FMW-001, RI-TEC-028, RI-TEC-036) in five countries.
- **Outputs (signed off at G7):** a scope overview, 48 issue briefs, a coverage and limits report, and three comparator briefs with nine draft policy options and one warning, which stay internal drafts.

The main finding is persistence. 33 of 48 issues have a persistent direction of travel, none of the 48 gaps is closed, and no Chapter 2 issue is improving. The comparator pilot showed what neighbouring countries tried, not what worked: no case shows a reform that raised platform use or changed EU workers' access.

## 3. Error patterns and how often they occurred

**Extraction.**
- *Missed claims* were the main weakness. The G3 check found 14% of in-scope claims missed in the sample, about 1% of them of high importance (GATE-0004). In full extraction, the second pass added 13.6% more items on non-EU sources against 2.7% on EU sources. A later recheck of four EU sources found 37 missed claims against 272 items, 8 of them of higher importance (LIM-0025, ALN-0017, ALN-0022).
- *Quotes holding two claims* were the most common serious error: 12 of the 15 firm serious errors (2.1% of items) at G3, and 44 split at the EU second pass.
- *Quotes cut too early or too late* passed the automatic check, because it confirms only that a quote exists at its location (three cases found by hand, ALN-0013).
- *Lost text:* pages with a heading over an image kept almost no text, and content was missing in five sources that mattered (ALN-0021).
- *Rule drift:* parallel agents applied silent rules differently (ALN-0012). Two refinement loops cut the sample from 1,054 to 725 items before full extraction.
- Type accuracy (99.6%) and page references (no errors found) were reliable throughout.

**Classification and linking.**
- Of 391 sampled classifications, 351 were correct, 34 arguable and 6 wrong; 82 of 100 out-of-scope decisions were correct.
- Of 75 proposed contradictions, 37 were confirmed, 33 were apparent and 5 were not contradictions.
- A merge step defaulted non-EU findings to the wrong chain link (99 corrected), and 31 groups of identical sentences were classified differently.
- Two versions of one screening report were counted as separate sources until stage 9 (ALN-0023).
- At G7 the output writers found that some screening report items typed as EU requirements are North Macedonia's own statements (for example MKD-EU-2025-003-0019 to 0023), and that two item summaries name the wrong instrument (run_notes 2026-11-02).

**Judgement.**
- *Priority:* proposed group by group, 21 issues were rated High. A calibration across all issues reduced this to 18, which exposed a working test stricter than the rules (ALN-0026). Two issues moved from improving to persistent because they rested on adoption alone.
- *Truncated inputs:* an input cap hid the newest evidence on six interventions until an agent reported it (ALN-0025).
- *Comparator drafts:* the first drafts of the cases had 24 serious errors in about 480 claims, mostly claims of absence, values not following from reasons, and lessons going too far (ALN-0028). An issue-tag filter caused wrong claims of absence (ALN-0029). A helper brief applied a stricter success test than the rules (ALN-0032).
- *At G7:* validated reasons still carried stage 10 wording, and seven differences between sources were not logged as conflicts. Seven judgements were flagged for re-review (ALN-0033).

**Overall pattern.** Errors were rarely of fact or reference. They came from scope boundaries, absence claims, consistency between parallel agents, and wording that outlived its stage. Independent agent checks caught most of them before or at the gate, and routine validation caught almost none.

## 8. Scaling readiness

The alpha shows that the chain, the gates and the outputs work end to end for one country, one chapter and one theme in five days. Before the tool covers more chapters, clusters or countries, these must change.

**Must change before scaling**
- **Sources:**
  - A manual or permitted retrieval route for blocked publishers (ALN-0006).
  - A near-duplicate check (ALN-0023).
  - A text-density check in conversion (ALN-0021).
  - A tested acquisition tool (ALN-0008).
- **Languages:** a decision on reviewers who read the national languages, or an explicit acceptance that most country-response evidence stays at Medium confidence (ALN-0007, LIM-0018). This becomes harder with each new country.
- **Consistency between parallel agents:**
  - Shared worked examples and rules for silent cases (ALN-0012).
  - A merge step for taxonomy drafts (ALN-0019).
  - Calibration across issues for priority (ALN-0026).
  - Same-sentence consistency checks (ALN-0024).
  - These failures grow with volume and with the number of agents.
- **Agent inputs:** no truncation, and searches across the whole country for claims of absence (ALN-0025, ALN-0029).
- **Assurance:**
  - Independent agent checks before each gate worked (ALN-0016, ALN-0024, ALN-0028) and should be standard.
  - But no person read the sources behind the extraction or the comparator cases (E12), and one person validated all analytical judgements (E13, ALN-0027).
  - Before outputs leave the alpha, decide who besides the analyst reviews analytical judgements, and add a small human spot check when a new country or language is added (ALN-0016).
- **Final wording before validation:** reasons written for output, not for the next stage (ALN-0033).

**Can scale as it is**
- The taxonomy and mechanism model.
- The three separate judgements.
- The ID and versioning discipline.
- Quote verification.
- The generated review and analysis pages.
- The output builder, once it has tests.

**Comparators**
- Case-level transferability (ALN-0031) should become a standard judgement only after a second pilot.
- Comparator evidence on what worked is thin across the region, so comparator work is best limited to issues where outcome evidence exists or can be commissioned.
