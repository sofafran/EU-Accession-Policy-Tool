# Run notes: RUN-2026-01

The raw working diary for the run, kept by the agent. The threshold is low: note any problem with the method, recurring error, technical difficulty or idea as soon as it appears. Each note has a date, the stage, and an example (evidence item or source ID).

Notes are not curated and are not shown on the review page. When a note proves material, especially for scaling, the agent proposes a row in `alpha_learning_log.csv` that cites the note's date rather than copying it.

- **2026-10-29, before stage 0 (pilot reconfiguration).** Two risks to watch at G3 and G6, with no examples yet. First, Chapter 2 is a small chapter and its section in each Commission report is short, so the evidence base for (a) may be thin, especially in the RES and CSO families; the G3 sample may struggle to fill every family from Chapter 2 alone. Second, the Commission's standard preparation and progress wording rates whole chapters, so it gives only weak support to direction of travel for technology and AI issues that sit inside other chapters; the judgement rules (0.1b) now treat the scale as context only for such issues. Revisit both at G3 and G6.
- **2026-10-29, stage 0 (tools).** Built and tested the seven helper tools (40 of 40 tests passed). Points for later gates: `export_review.py` only builds G0 to G2 packages so far, because there is no data yet to test later sheets on; they must be added before G3. The environment needed system packages (Tesseract with Macedonian and Albanian data, Poppler) that are not installed by default; they are now listed in `tools/requirements.txt`. Quote checking compares texts after NFC normalisation and never treats Latin and Cyrillic look-alike letters as equal, so text recognition errors that swap scripts will show up as quote failures at stage 5.
- **2026-10-29, stage 0 (review page).** The hand-built skeleton was replaced by the generated page. The generator takes every status label from the gate log, the review packages present in `review/RUN-2026-01/` and the review status columns; it shows the run record's own wording for scope and status, so that wording should stay short and readable.
- **2026-10-29, stage 1 (freeze date and the 2026 report).** The method sets the freeze date just after the year's enlargement package. The freeze date chosen at G0 is 29 October 2026, but no 2026 North Macedonia report was listed on the Commission's country page that day, so the latest annual report in the window is the 2025 report. If the 2026 package appears after the freeze date it falls outside this run. Example: REQ-001.
- **2026-10-29, stage 1 (theme widens the EU source base).** Because technology and AI recommendations are in scope wherever they occur, every annual report has to be read in full and screening reports for all six clusters are candidates (REQ-001, REQ-004, REQ-005), not just the Chapter 2 sections. Chapter 2 alone would have needed a handful of short sections per report.
- **2026-10-29, stage 2 (blocked publishers).** Several publishers block automated downloads with HTTP 403 (OECD and SIGMA, UNDP, Council of Europe, Council press pages, the Health Insurance Fund, the State Audit Office site), and IOM blocked a second attempt after a first success. None was bypassed. Thirteen documents are registered as inaccessible and listed for manual drop-in, including the core SIGMA report for REQ-024 (MKD-IO-2024-003). The Secretariat for European Affairs site had an expired TLS certificate, so the NPAA could not be retrieved (MKD-GOV-2020-002).
- **2026-10-29, stage 2 (language).** Nearly all national government sources in scope are in Macedonian only, some bilingual with Albanian; only the data protection law, its amendments and the data protection strategy have English versions. Country-response evidence will therefore rely mostly on machine translation, capped at Medium confidence. Examples: MKD-GOV-2021-001, MKD-GOV-2025-007, MKD-GOV-2024-005.
- **2026-10-29, stage 2 (acquisition script).** Acquisition used an unversioned working script that calls the project tools. Three errors were caught and corrected the same day: a generic content type made one document look inaccessible (MKD-GOV-2024-001, status corrected), the text recognition note was parsed wrongly for 13 sources (notes rebuilt from the PDFs), and two webpages were not converted because of an environment setting (MKD-EU-2025-014, MKD-EU-2026-005, converted again). `convert_to_text.py` was extended to legacy Word files (version 1.1.0, 41 of 41 tests passed).
- **2026-10-29, stage 2 (search by family).** Three agents searched in parallel, one per group of families, and each URL was checked before download. This found 99 documents in under an hour, but the agents' metadata needed correcting in places (a shortened audit title, MKD-GOV-2024-008; an international organisation listed under research, MKD-IO-2026-001).
- **2026-10-29, stage 5 (report structure).** The 2025 Commission report gives each chapter twice: a short summary with the preparation and progress wording and the recommendations in the cluster overview (Chapter 2 on page 11, Chapter 10 on page 14), and a longer section later (Chapter 2 on page 68). It also calls Chapter 10 "Digital transformation and media". Extraction must cover both places and must not treat the summary and the long section as corroborating each other (same source). Example: MKD-EU-2025-001.
- **2026-10-29, stage 5 (sample extraction).** Seven agents extracted the 11 sample sources in parallel under extraction rules 0.1b: 1,054 items, every quote verified, no validation errors. About 6 to 7 hours of agent time in total, 20 to 25 minutes elapsed. Theme items far outnumber Chapter 2 items (742 theme only, 288 chapter only, 24 both), and 140 items carry theme_doubtful. The agents applied several rules differently (summary versus detailed section, sub-area progress wording, chapter basis for sources registered against Chapter 2); these are listed as R1 to R11 in review/RUN-2026-01/G3_rule_issues.csv. MKD-CSO-2024-001 had no in-scope content (the search agent's description was wrong), so REQ-034 is now not_found.
- **2026-10-29, stage 5 (hand-coding).** The analyst approved the sample but has not yet hand-coded passages P1 to P3. The 25 agent items inside those passages (MKD-EU-2025-001 pages 11 and 14, MKD-IO-2024-001 page 83) are withheld from the G3 review sheet until the passages are hand-coded, as procedure P2 requires.
- **2026-10-29, stage 6 (re-run under rules 0.2).** The same seven agents revised their files under extraction rules 0.2 (the 0.1b files are kept in review/RUN-2026-01/sample_0.1b/). The sample fell from 1,054 to 732 items: 521 theme only, 193 Chapter 2 only, 18 both; theme_doubtful fell from 140 to 29 and out_of_window from 21 to 7. Theme items still outnumber Chapter 2 items by about 2.7 to 1. The agents raised nine new boundary questions (R12 to R20 in G3_rule_issues.csv); the most important are digital public services inside sectors, which the 2020 and 2025 extractions treated differently (MKD-EU-2020-001-0093 kept, e-procurement removed from MKD-EU-2025-001), and whether nationals' access to EU labour markets is Chapter 2 (MKD-IO-2021-001).
- **2026-10-29, stage 6 (quote boundaries).** While revising, the 2023 agent found that three 0.1b quotes passed the quote check but started too early and included earlier sentences (MKD-EU-2023-001-0037, -0117, -0150). The automatic check only proves that a quote exists at the location, not that it is cut to the claim.
- **2026-10-29, stage 6 (page breaks).** verify_quotes.py was extended four times (1.1.0 to 1.2.3) to skip page numbers, running headers (including those carrying a page number or signature lines) and footnotes between pages. One audit claim still cannot be verified because a numbered subheading looks like a footnote (MKD-GOV-2025-011, pages 42 to 43, left out).
- **2026-10-29, stage 6 (second revision, rules 0.3).** The second and last refinement loop applied R12 to R20. The sample is now 725 items (from 732): online public services restored as digital government (for example e-procurement and eHealth in MKD-EU-2025-001), the ETF report emptied under R13 (MKD-IO-2021-001), summary-table and annex repeats removed (MKD-IO-2024-001, MKD-GOV-2025-011), 112 items removed. All quotes verify and validation is clean. One audit claim still cannot be verified (MKD-GOV-2025-011, pages 42 to 43). Agents still flag a few boundary cases (for example online court hearings MKD-EU-2020-001-0029, the farm aid application system in MKD-EU-2025-001); these are left for the analyst's sample check rather than another rule round.
- **2026-10-29, stage 6 (lighter manual check).** At the analyst's request, the G3 manual check is a simplified hand-coding of the three passages (type, sentence, scope only) and a 70-row review sample (50 kept items, stratified by source with doubtful and translated items over-represented, and 20 items removed by the rule revisions), focused on missed and misclassified items. review/RUN-2026-01/G3_review_sample.xlsx; the full item list stays in G3_sample_review.xlsx for reference.
- **2026-10-29, stage 6 (independent G3 review, decision E12).** One agent coded the three passages blind and five reviewer agents, none involved in extraction, reviewed all 725 items against rules 0.3 and the source text before fixing. Results (review/RUN-2026-01/g3_qa/g3_metrics.csv): 676 items fully correct (93%); type accuracy 99.6%; no wrong page references and no translation errors; 15 firm serious errors (2.1%), 12 of them quotes holding two claims; 121 missed in-scope claims (14% of the total, 7 of high importance), mostly neighbouring sentences in sections the theme screen did not reach (for example eHealth in Chapter 28, MKD-EU-2023-001-0208; ICT infrastructure, MKD-EU-2025-001-0205). Blind coding: 22 of 27 matched, all with the same type; differences were mostly granularity (one item where the coder made two). Routine fixes: 31 items corrected and 49 missed items added; the sample is now 775 items. Remaining judgement calls are cybercrime (R21) and sector digital systems (R22).
- **2026-10-29, stage 7 (EU extraction).** All 51 EU-family sources extracted under rules 0.4 by nine agents (about 15 to 20 minutes elapsed each), with the five sample files finalised to 0.4. The second pass (G3 condition 2), each source checked by an agent that did not extract it, added 51 missed claims and split 44 two-claim quotes. EU items: 1,885 (1,667 theme only, 197 Chapter 2 only, 19 both, 2 chain); every quote verified. Recurring grey areas raised by the agents: judicial digitalisation (court IT strategy and councils, for example MKD-EU-2022-001-0135, MKD-EU-2026-006-0002), anti-corruption data systems (asset declarations, MKD-EU-2023-003-0055), tax e-services versus tax IT (MKD-EU-2026-003-0002), use of EU systems inside sector reforms (MKD-EU-2025-006-0001), and horizontal cyber or hybrid-threat passages (MKD-EU-2022-004-0003). The validator now also requires a theme code on every theme-basis item (validate_data 1.2.0).
- **2026-10-29, stage 7 (taxonomy draft).** Two agents drafted reform issues from the EU requirement items (findings and recommendations): 13 Chapter 2 issues (RI-FMW-001 to 013) and 35 technology and AI issues (RI-TEC-001 to 035), all draft, merged into taxonomy/reform_issues.csv. Working mapping in review/RUN-2026-01/G4_issue_mapping.csv: 832 of 969 items fit an issue; the rest are chapter-level ratings or doubtful theme items that fit nothing. Points for G4: the two drafts overlap on electronic exchange of social security data (RI-FMW-010 and RI-TEC-031); artificial intelligence rests on only two EU items (RI-TEC-001), most AI evidence being national (the State Audit Office audit, MKD-GOV-2025-011); RI-TEC-003 (digital public services) is very large; several sector issues sit on the R12 and R22 boundary (tax, procurement, justice, customs, justice and home affairs systems). The filter that built the Chapter 2 input wrongly included two chain items (MKD-EU-2024-001-0032, -0100); the drafter left them unmapped.
- **2026-10-30, stage 7 (check of the G4 package on resuming).** Quotes (1,885 of 1,885), validation and tests all pass. The check found what the package had not stated plainly: RI-TEC-008 has no recommendation, which procedure P2 requires of a theme issue, and nothing tests for this automatically; all 13 items under RI-TEC-031 are also under Chapter 2 issues, so it adds no evidence of its own; RI-TEC-005 reads wider than decision R22 allows. The package had no list of decisions up front, so one was added (review/RUN-2026-01/G4_decisions.csv, export_review 1.6.0). A keyword check of EU text first seemed to show missed AI law conditions (MKD-EU-2024-007, page 60) and AI tools (MKD-EU-2026-004, page 34), but these concern Serbia and Türkiye, so the AI count stands. Most unquoted keyword hits in two annual reports (MKD-EU-2025-001, MKD-EU-2024-001) were headings, cybercrime (R21) or sector systems (R22). Keyword screens on regional documents give many false hits unless limited to the country's pages. The check was not a measure of the miss rate. The second-pass records name no agent, so the "different agent" part of G3 condition 2 rests on the 29 October note, not on the files. MEC-02 has a blank version field in taxonomy/mechanisms.csv, to be filled at G4.
