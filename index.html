Purpose of the alpha

RUN-2026-01 is an alpha product and a test of the methodology. It is meant to produce analysis and, just as much, to test and refine:

  • the end-to-end workflow;
  • source collection and processing;
  • extraction and classification;
  • taxonomy and matching;
  • the human review gates;
  • assessment and judgement;
  • outputs; and
  • the usefulness of this review page.

Lessons are kept in the alpha learning log and will inform scaling the tool to more chapters, clusters and accession countries.

North Macedonia alpha scope Draft

Set by Sofia on 29 October 2026; to be confirmed at gate G0.

Target country
North Macedonia (MKD)
Chapter
All of Chapter 2, Freedom of movement for workers (Cluster 2, Internal market)
Cross-cutting theme
Recommendations materially relating to technology and AI, wherever they occur in the accession material. Incidental references to technology are out of scope.
Analytical chain
The full chain is followed for every issue that originates from an in-scope Chapter 2 item or technology and AI recommendation.
Other material
Out of scope unless needed to follow the chain of an issue already in scope.
Time window
1 January 2020 to the evidence freeze date; earlier material only by logged exception
Language
English primary; Macedonian or Albanian only for core sources with no suitable English version; machine translation flagged and capped at Medium confidence
Comparators
Run 1b only, for two or three issues chosen at G6

Technology and AI theme areas

  • Artificial intelligence
  • Digital government and digital public services
  • Digital infrastructure and connectivity
  • Data and data governance
  • Cybersecurity
  • Digital identity
  • Interoperability and electronic systems
  • Digitalisation of government or economic activity
