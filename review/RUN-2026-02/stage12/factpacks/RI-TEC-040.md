# Fact pack RI-TEC-040: Semiconductor acquis not aligned

Legislation is not aligned with the EU acquis on semiconductors, in particular the Chips Act.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission found in 2025 that Moldova was not yet fully aligned with the Chips Act and in 2026 that it was yet to align with it. The 2026 sub-area wording on semiconductors, which matches this issue, is some progress, but it rests on the April 2026 law on topographies of semiconductor products, an intellectual property matter outside the issue, so alignment with the Chips Act has not moved. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0099;MDA-EU-2026-012-0134;MDA-EU-2026-012-0133
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2026 key recommendation asks for further alignment with the semiconductor acquis, and the government plans to transpose the Chips Act by the end of 2026 but states that there are no national rules on semiconductor production facilities and no responsible institution. What remains is transposition of the Chips Act and the designation and staffing of a competent authority. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0023;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0231;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0229;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0230
- priority: Medium (validated). Alignment with the semiconductor acquis is a key recommendation in the 2026 report, following a 2025 finding, but only in one report, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on it. The government describes the sector in Moldova as almost non-existent, which makes the issue less central to the theme. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0023;MDA-EU-2025-001-0099;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0228
- tractability: Low (validated). The government reports that no institution is responsible for the area, that no ministry employee covers it and that a new post is needed, and the sector is at an early stage. Transposition is planned for the end of 2026, but no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files records progress on the Chips Act itself. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0230;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0167;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0232;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0231

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0268 Draft law on investments: proposed. The law on investments appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0463 Law on the protection of topographies of semiconductor products: adopted. The Commission reports that Moldova adopted the law on the protection of topographies of semiconductor products in April 2026.
- INT-MDA-0566 New principal consultant posts (AI, semiconductors, trust services) in the MDED IT and digitalisation policy directorate: proposed. The new posts appear only as planned measures in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports that they were created or filled.
- INT-MDA-0683 Transposition of the EU Chips Act: proposed. Transposition of the EU Chips Act is planned by the end of 2026, and the Commission reported in 2026 that Moldova had yet to align with it.
