# Fact pack RI-TEC-037: Sector and local online public services limited

Online public services run by sector bodies or municipalities, outside the national portal, are limited in range, use or quality.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The chapter scale was used as context only, since this is part of the chapter. The Commission finds about 40% of mapped services available digitally in 2024, over 50% in 2025 with 64% for businesses and 54% for individuals in 2024, and 82% of business services and 61% of citizen services by June 2026, and its 2026 release decision confirms that the Reform Agenda objective of 75% of business services was met. The 2026 report also finds that user uptake increased significantly, including in rural areas. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0038;MDA-EU-2025-001-0040;MDA-EU-2025-001-0061;MDA-EU-2026-012-0117;MDA-EU-2026-004-0001;MDA-EU-2026-012-0039
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The business services reform is in place, with the State Register of Legal Units launched in December 2025 and the life events methodology approved, and the Commission judges the payment step satisfactorily fulfilled. Still, only 133 of the 414 business services counted are fully digital end to end, citizen services lag at 61%, the Commission noted low demand in 2025, and the systems of the entrepreneurship agency and the employment agency are still being procured or built. What remains is end-to-end online delivery and wider use of sector services, especially for citizens and in agencies such as the entrepreneurship and employment agencies. Items: MDA-EU-2026-004-0003;MDA-EU-2026-004-0004;MDA-EU-2026-004-0008;MDA-EU-2026-004-0002;MDA-EU-2026-012-0117;MDA-EU-2025-001-0045;MDA-EU-2026-012-0145;MDA-EU-2026-012-0142
- priority: High (validated). Digital services for businesses are the subject of key recommendations in four reports: digitalising processes to cut administrative burden in 2023 and 2024, and digitalising the entrepreneurship agency's processes in 2025 and 2026. The related Reform Agenda payment step (75% of business services digitalised, with the State Register of Legal Units and the life events methodology) is met, so the rating rests on the repeated recommendations; online public services are central to the digital government theme. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0015;MDA-EU-2023-001-0103;MDA-EU-2024-001-0031;MDA-EU-2025-001-0033;MDA-EU-2026-012-0028;MDA-EU-2026-004-0001
- tractability: High (validated). There is strong evidence of past progress, with the share of business services online rising each year from 2024 to 2026 and the payment step met. The work is led by the e-Governance Agency and the State Chancellery with shared platforms, although sector agencies and rural demand still need attention. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0038;MDA-EU-2026-012-0117;MDA-EU-2026-004-0001;MDA-EU-2026-012-0039

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0011 Public Services Portal (services.gov.md): in_practice. The Commission and SIGMA describe the portal as the working single point of access to public services, and the Government reports over 0.5 million unique visitors a year and user satisfaction in its annual national survey. Material in the Government's own implementation programme says only a minority of listed services can be completed fully online, the Commission notes the portal is not integrated with EU systems, and by 2026 services are listed on the integrated EVO portal.
- INT-MDA-0016 MDED interactive platform on public services for entrepreneurs: implemented. Civil society monitoring reports that the ministry developed an interactive platform showing how many public services for entrepreneurs are digitalised and how much they are used. No later source describes it.
- INT-MDA-0020 MPay: implemented. The Commission counts MPay among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes it as in place since 2020. SIGMA found in 2023 that online payment through MPay was possible but not yet integrated into digital service provision, and the Government's figures on savings and on payments collected through MPay are not corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0025 Modernization of Government Services project (MGSP): implemented. The World Bank-supported project was carried out by the e-Governance Agency and concluded in 2025, with the Government reporting 110 digitalised services and 94 re-engineered services. All figures on its delivery and results come from the Government and the Agency, without independent corroboration.
- INT-MDA-0026 Public services register: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the register was established in 2021 and covers about 718 public services, and the Commission states that it was used to map administrative services and support their re-engineering.
- INT-MDA-0031 Unified Public Service Centres (CUPS): implemented. The Commission confirms that 83 centres were set up in 2023, that the network reached 105 by 2025 and 118 by 2026, giving rural residents access to electronic public services. None of the items on the centres gives the number of people served through them, so use at scale is not shown.
- INT-MDA-0033 e-APL: implemented. The Government reports that the e-APL portal was developed and launched as a pilot, with 91 mayoralties taking part and nine State Fiscal Register services available through it. The target is use by at least half of first-level local authorities, and all items come from the Government's own machine-translated documents, so the status rests on Medium confidence evidence.
- INT-MDA-0037 "Migration" Information System (IOM, EU-funded project): proposed. The "Migration" information system is being developed with IOM support and EU funding, with work planned to end in June 2026. The latest source in 2026 still describes it as under development, and none reports that it was launched.
- INT-MDA-0050 AGEPI online platforms for electronic filing of intellectual property applications (e-AGEPI): unclear. The Government says the intellectual property agency uses online platforms for electronic filing, but the National Accession Programme sets developing, testing and launching the e-AGEPI system as a future target. ## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0007 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-004-0003: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Low. The claim of significant progress in 2023 rests on the Government's own report: the Commission gives about 40% of services available online in both its 2023 and 2024 reports, which does not show the rise, and its higher figures concern 2024 and later. The Strategy was also adopted only in September 2023.
- INT-MDA-0031 leads to MDA-EU-2026-012-0039: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Low. The Commission reports a significant rise in uptake, including in rural areas where the centres were set up, but gives no rural figure and does not say the centres caused it, and the centres' own service counts come from Government sources. None of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources measures use of the centres.
- INT-MDA-0099 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-001-0061: success mixed, attribution High. The Court of Accounts, an audit body, records improved accessibility of permit services, but the same audit finds only partial use of the system, reluctance by some authorities and major risks that its purpose will not be achieved.
- INT-MDA-0284 leads to MDA-EU-2026-004-0001: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission verified that 310 of 414 business services are digitalised, meeting the 75% objective, though only 133 are fully digital end to end.
- INT-MDA-0025 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-004-0012: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The claim rests on government reporting alone: none of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources refers to the services digitalised through the project in 2023, and the count is of services digitalised rather than their use.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MDA-GOV-2025-003-0297 vs MDA-GOV-2024-004-0013: Noted difference, possibly different measures: the 2024 implementation report gives 3.7 million business service requests in 2023 with 2.48 million online, while the 2025 programme gives 4,524,731 applications in 2023 with 3,058,794 online.
