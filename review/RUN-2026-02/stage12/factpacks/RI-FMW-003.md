# Fact pack RI-FMW-003: Public sector posts closed to EU citizens beyond the public authority exception

EU citizens are barred from public sector posts other than a short list of exemptions, rather than only from posts involving the exercise of public authority and safeguarding the general interests of the State.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Commission found that EU citizens had no access to posts in public administration bodies in the 2023 report and the 2023 analytical report, and repeated the finding unchanged in the 2024 and 2025 reports. The 2026 report records that new legislation opened certain technical, specialised and auxiliary posts to EU citizens while civil servant posts remain reserved to nationals, so the improvement is legal only and partial. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0052;MDA-EU-2023-002-0015;MDA-EU-2024-001-0054;MDA-EU-2025-001-0066;MDA-EU-2026-012-0079;MDA-EU-2026-012-0080
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Government Decision No. 566/2025 opens technical, specialist and auxiliary posts to persons without Moldovan citizenship, which the Commission confirms in 2026, and the Government plans an analysis, in the light of case law of the Court of Justice, of which civil service functions can be opened; its own account still excludes public functions that expressly require citizenship. What remains is opening civil servant posts to EU citizens, except those that involve the exercise of public authority and safeguarding the general interests of the State. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0014;MDA-EU-2026-012-0079;MDA-EU-2026-012-0080;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0015;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0122;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0013
- priority: Medium (validated). The finding is repeated in the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports and concerns a core rule of the chapter, but it sits in the body text: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on public sector posts. Partial legal progress is recorded in 2026, so the route to High for a core requirement with no progress does not apply. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0052;MDA-EU-2024-001-0054;MDA-EU-2025-001-0066;MDA-EU-2026-012-0080
- tractability: Medium (validated). The change is a national legal matter with no dependence on other states, and the 2025 Government decision shows that categories of posts can be opened. The remaining step needs a review of civil service functions against case law, which the Government has so far only planned. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0014;MDA-EU-2026-012-0079;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0015;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0122

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0356 Government Decision No. 566/2025 amending Annex 5 to Government Decision No. 201/2009 on the application of the Law on the public function: adopted. Government Decision No. 566/2025 allows technical, specialist and auxiliary posts in the public function to be filled by persons without Moldovan citizenship under employment contracts. The Commission confirms that these posts were opened to EU citizens while civil servant posts remain restricted.
- INT-MDA-0433 Law No. 200/2025 on the free movement and residence on the territory of the Republic of Moldova of citizens of the European Union and their family members: adopted. Law No. 200/2025 was adopted in July 2025 and, as the Commission confirms, entered into force in March 2026. The Commission notes that civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals, and no source yet describes how the law is applied in practice.
- INT-MDA-0582 Opening of certain public administration positions to EU citizens: adopted. The Commission reports that the new legislation opened certain technical, specialised and auxiliary public administration positions to EU citizens, while civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals. The linked items do not report EU citizens recruited to these posts.
