# Fact pack RI-FMW-007: Information and support for mobile workers on their rights

There is no designated body supporting EU mobile workers in their rights and the system for informing them of their rights is weak.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since this is part of the chapter and the only Chapter 2 sub-area wording among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files concerns legal alignment in general (2023) and the European Health Insurance Card (2025). The 2023 Analytical Report found no body responsible for enforcing the right to free movement of workers; Law No. 200/2025 gives the Equality Council that competence and the role of national contact point, as the Government reports in a machine-translated source, and the 2026 report confirms that the law entered into force in March 2026 to align with the acquis on workers' free movement rights. The improvement is legal only: the 2026 report does not assess the body, and the ten items of this run that name the Equality Council, all from the Government, concern its designation, staffing or planned measures, and none shows it at work for EU workers. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0013;MDA-EU-2023-002-0022;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0023;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0024;MDA-EU-2026-012-0078
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Law No. 200/2025 is adopted and designates the Equality Council, which answers the 2023 finding in law. The amendment of the Equality Council law, the Council's restructuring and extra staff, training for public authority and Council staff, and information measures by the General Inspectorate for Migration are all still planned, and the Council had filled 19 of 33 posts (Government figures, machine translated). What remains is a body that in practice supports EU workers and informs them of their rights, with the mandate, structure and staff to do so. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0013;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0023;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0025;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0035;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0039;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0085;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0104;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0105;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0037
- priority: Low (validated). The missing enforcement body is raised only in the 2023 Analytical Report: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no later EU item on the Equality Council or an enforcement body, and no benchmark, Reform Agenda payment condition or key recommendation naming it. It is a specific acquis obligation within the chapter but not central to it, and the 2026 key recommendations address alignment and equal treatment of EU workers in general terms. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0013;MDA-EU-2023-002-0022;MDA-EU-2026-012-0011;MDA-EU-2026-012-0012
- tractability: High (validated). The legal designation is done, and the remaining steps (amending the Equality Council law, restructuring and recruiting) are within the Government's control, with one lead body already in place. Staffing is the main constraint: the Council had 19 of 33 planned posts filled and its anti-discrimination directorate 4 of 7 (Government figures, machine translated). Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0023;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0085;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0039;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0025;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0026

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0254 Draft law amending Law No. 298/2012 on the Equality Council: proposed. The draft law amending the Equality Council law, so that the Council can carry out its tasks under Law No. 200/2025, is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the draft only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0313 Equality Council as national contact point under Law No. 200/2025 (Art. 5(8)): adopted. Law No. 200/2025 gives the Equality Council competence to promote and monitor equal treatment of EU citizens and to act as national contact point. Amendments to the Council's own law to carry out these tasks are still in draft, and the linked items do not report the Council acting in this role. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0325 General Inspectorate for Migration information and referral measures for Member State citizens: proposed. The National Accession Programme says the Ministry of Internal Affairs will determine how arriving Member State citizens are registered and that the Migration Inspectorate will take measures to inform and refer them. The items describe plans only. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0433 Law No. 200/2025 on the free movement and residence on the territory of the Republic of Moldova of citizens of the European Union and their family members: adopted. Law No. 200/2025 was adopted in July 2025 and, as the Commission confirms, entered into force in March 2026. The Commission notes that civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals, and no source yet describes how the law is applied in practice.
- INT-MDA-0630 Restructuring and staff increase of the Equality Council: proposed. Under Law No. 200/2025 the Equality Council is to revise its rules, structure and staffing to examine discrimination against EU citizens and act as contact point. No source shows the restructuring or staff increase made.
- INT-MDA-0678 Training on equal treatment of EU workers and their family members (Equality Council and public authority staff): proposed. Training of public authority and Equality Council staff on equal treatment of EU workers under Law No. 200/2025 is planned in the National Accession Programme. The items describe plans only. The linked items are machine-translated.
