# Fact pack RI-FMW-004: Legal and organisational framework for joining EURES

The country has not set up the legal and organisational arrangements needed to join the European network of employment services, such as a system for appointing EURES members and partners and access for private employment agencies.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 2 rating covers more than this issue and is context only. The 2023 report and Analytical Report found the preparatory work for EURES not completed, the 2024 report recorded its start with an action plan and the National Employment Agency designated as coordination body, and the 2025 report recorded a EURES department and the September 2025 Government Decision setting the national legal framework. The 2026 report adds the National Coordination Office, the procedure for admitting members and partners, 10 territorial EURES members, implementing acts on data and monitoring and technical standards for matching with the EURES portal. - unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Commission asked in 2023, 2024 and 2025 for the preparatory work for EURES to continue, and in 2026 it records the legal framework, coordination office and admission procedure as in place. The Government describes its 2024 and 2025 Decisions as only partial transposition of the EURES Regulation, says data exchange with the European platform and legislative alignment still need to be completed, and lists further agency orders, including on interoperability with EURES, as planned. What remains is completing legal alignment with the EURES Regulation and the technical connection and data exchange with the EURES platform, ahead of membership on accession. - priority: High (validated). Preparing to join EURES is a key recommendation in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, and the 2026 key recommendation asks for the measures, capacity and IT systems needed for full participation in EURES, while the Commission states that the previous year's recommendations remain valid. The requirement therefore runs through four reports and joining EURES is central to the chapter, although the Commission records the main legal and organisational steps as taken in 2026. - tractability: High (validated). The remaining technical connection depends on the employment service's IT capacity, which the 2025 recommendation asks Moldova to reinforce. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0076 angajat.md portal and mobile app: in_practice. The employment agency reports 637,000 visits and 69,000 active users on angajat.md, and an international organisation describes the portal and mobile app as tools young people use to explore job opportunities. New modules, including vacancy import from external sources, were added in 2025.
- INT-MDA-0078 ANOFM annual action plans (2025 and 2026): implemented. The Agency's 2025 activity report presents EURES preparation and an integrated labour market information system among the objectives it pursued in 2025 and repeats them for 2026. The Commission confirms the Agency's EURES preparation steps in 2025, while the integrated information system is not yet shown to be in use.
- INT-MDA-0081 ANOFM Director Order No. 53 of 28 November 2025 on the procedure for admission as EURES member or partner: adopted. The Government reports that ANOFM Director Order No. 53 of 28 November 2025 approved the procedure for admission as a EURES member or partner. No source reports any admission under it.
- INT-MDA-0084 ANOFM internal audit engagement on the implementation of the EURES network: implemented. The employment agency's internal audit service carried out an engagement on how the EURES network is being implemented in Moldova. Its findings are not reported.
- INT-MDA-0085 ANOFM Labour Market information system: unclear. The Commission reports that the National Employment Agency developed a new integrated information system in December 2025, aiming at fully digitalised services by 2027, while the Agency's own 2025 report says the lack of a modern information system affects its services. Neither source says whether the new system is in use, and data exchange with EURES is still being prepared.
- INT-MDA-0131 Bilateral social security agreements of the Republic of Moldova (as a group): implemented. The Commission reports that bilateral social security agreements were concluded in 2022 and that Moldova had agreements with 13 EU Member States and Türkiye by 2023, and the Government reported 17 agreements with EU Member States in 2025. The agreements are in force, but no source gives data on claims or benefits handled under them, and agreements with ten Member States remain to be concluded.
- INT-MDA-0166 Correlation tables between national skills, competences and occupations and ESCO: proposed. The correlation tables with ESCO are planned in the 2025 accession programme. Moldova has designated members of the ESCO Working Group, but no source reports the tables drafted.
- INT-MDA-0179 Designation of ANOFM as EURES National Coordination Office: implemented. The Commission confirms that a Government decision of September 2025 designated the National Employment Agency as the EURES National Coordination Office, that an order of November 2025 set its tasks and the admission procedure for members, and that 10 territorial EURES members have been designated. A dedicated Labour Migration and EURES department has existed since January 2025, but the work so far is preparatory to joining EURES.
- INT-MDA-0209 Draft ANOFM orders implementing EURES: proposed. The orders appear as drafts in the 2025 accession programme. The Commission reports in 2026 that the technical standards, procedures and formats for EURES were developed, but no source reports that the orders were issued.
- INT-MDA-0247 Draft Law amending Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance (single no. 180/MMPS/2026): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 105/2018 is a draft in consultation, with ministries still proposing changes to its definitions and entry-into-force clause. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0357 Government Decision No. 607/2025 approving the Regulation on organising national activities within EURES: adopted. The Commission confirms that in September 2025 the Government approved a decision establishing the national legal framework for EURES activities, Government Decision No. 607/2025, and setting out the duties of the National Coordination Office.
- INT-MDA-0358 Government Decision No. 617/2024 amending Government Decision No. 990/2018 on the organisation and functioning of the National Employment Agency: adopted. The Government reports that Government Decision No. 617/2024 amended the National Employment Agency's statute, partly aligning it with the EURES Regulation.
- INT-MDA-0385 Implementing acts on data collection, monitoring and evaluation of EURES activities: adopted. The Commission reports that further implementing acts on data collection, monitoring and evaluation of EURES activities were adopted in the reporting period.
- INT-MDA-0406 Interoperability of the ANOFM information system with the EURES platform: proposed. The Commission reports that technical standards, procedures and formats for matching vacancies and CVs with the EURES portal were developed. Interoperability of the ANOFM system with EURES is still described as a measure to be carried out, and no source reports it working.
- INT-MDA-0591 Preparation for Moldova's integration into the EURES network: implemented. The Commission reported in 2024 that an action plan for joining EURES had been drafted and the National Employment Agency designated. By 2026 it records a Government Decision of September 2025 setting the national framework, an Agency order of November 2025 on the National Coordination Office, ten territorial EURES members and technical standards for matching with the EURES portal; joining itself happens only on accession.
- INT-MDA-0631 Revision of job descriptions on the angajat.md portal to meet EURES criteria: proposed. Revising job descriptions on angajat.md to meet EURES criteria is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
