# Fact pack RI-TEC-006: Electronic public procurement tools underused

The electronic public procurement system works, but some of its modules, such as the e-marketplace for low-value contracts and e-catalogues, are incomplete or little used by contracting authorities, and its data are not yet used for red-flag analysis.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). Only two items fall under this issue: a 2021 Commission finding on use of the online tender platform for COVID-19 procurement and a 2023 report of a regulation requiring most low-value procurement to go through the State Registry of Public Procurement system. Across all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, the findings that MTender lacks planning and contract management modules and does not offer all procedures concern the replacement of the system and are held under that issue, so no sequence can be drawn on fuller use of the existing modules, and the chapter rating was not used. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0007;MDA-EU-2023-001-0038;MDA-EU-2025-002-0075;MDA-EU-2025-002-0076
- unresolved_gap: insufficient_evidence (validated). No item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files states an EU requirement on fuller use of the existing e-procurement modules or on use of procurement data for red-flag analysis, or reports use of the low-value procurement module required by the 2023 regulation; the Commission's requirements on MTender concern its replacement by a new system. What remains is not known from the evidence held. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0007;MDA-EU-2023-001-0038
- priority: Low (validated). A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no Commission recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on fuller use of the existing e-procurement modules, since those on MTender concern a new system, and the 2021 finding concerns the transparency of emergency procurement rather than the electronic tools themselves. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0007;MDA-EU-2023-001-0038
- tractability: insufficient_evidence (validated). The two items on this issue say nothing about the actors, costs or obstacles involved in fuller use of the e-procurement modules, so tractability cannot be judged. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0007;MDA-EU-2023-001-0038

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0512 MTender e-procurement system (State Register of Public Procurement, SIA RSAP): in_practice. The Commission states that MTender has enabled electronic procurement since its launch, with public access to documents, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet says all public tenders are published on it. The Commission also finds it outdated and not covering the full procurement cycle, and a replacement system is being procured.
- INT-MDA-0579 Online tender platform (use for COVID-19 related procurement): in_practice. The Commission reported in 2021 that the online tender platform was used for COVID-19 related procurement and that such tenders were monitored. No later source reports on it.
- INT-MDA-0619 Regulation on low-value public procurement: adopted. The Commission reports that the Regulation obliges contracting authorities to carry out all stages of most low-value procurement through the State Register of Public Procurement. The linked item does not report its application.
