# Fact pack RI-TEC-009: eHealth systems not interoperable or aligned with EU rules

Electronic health systems are not interoperable with health insurance data, referrals remain on paper, and the legislation is not aligned with the EU acquis on eHealth.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 report found limited progress on an e-health legal framework and no e-health strategy, and the 2024 report found no integrated health information system, although electronic prescriptions had operated since April 2024. The Reform Agenda in 2025 still notes no approved eHealth strategy or regulatory framework, and the 2026 report records the adoption of the National Programme for Digitalisation and Innovation in Health 2025-2030 and a modernised ePrescription system with fully digital certificates. The improvement is in strategy and in one service, as the integrated system and electronic health records are not yet in place. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0114;MDA-EU-2024-001-0071;MDA-EU-2025-002-0046;MDA-EU-2026-012-0102;MDA-EU-2026-012-0103
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). A health digitalisation programme is adopted and ePrescription works, but the legal framework, the electronic health record and the national e-health entity are still planned, with the record due for 40% of the insured population by December 2027. The Government itself names the lack of a functional electronic health record, limited interoperability and no dedicated institutional framework as major challenges. What remains is a legal framework on eHealth aligned with the EU acquis, an interoperable integrated health information system and electronic health records in use. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0102;MDA-EU-2024-001-0072;MDA-EU-2025-002-0048;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0423;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0432;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0029;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0060
- priority: High (validated). Electronic health records for 40% of the insured population by December 2027 are an open Reform Agenda payment condition, and the key recommendations on health in the 2024 and 2025 reports name an integrated e-health system, interoperability and the digitalisation of health services. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no 2026 recommendation on e-health, so the rating rests on the open payment condition together with the two key recommendations; eHealth is a major digital public service within the theme. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0048;MDA-EU-2024-001-0021;MDA-EU-2025-001-0024;MDA-EU-2025-002-0046
- tractability: Medium (validated). The Government has adopted a programme and runs an ePrescription system that the Commission reports as modernised, which shows delivery capacity. The health record and interoperability need new legislation, a new institution, several health bodies and substantial funding in the Government's own estimates. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0008 EVO: implemented. The Commission confirms that the EVO application was launched in June 2024 and EVO 2.0 in June 2026, and that the EVO portal lists 636 public services, 447 of them online. Figures on use (169,692 downloads and 66,733 active users by the end of 2025) come only from the Government's own machine-translated progress report, so use at scale is not independently corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0019 MPass: implemented. The Commission and the Government list MPass among the government platforms implemented, and an international partner describes it as the authentication service for public e-services. Government consultation material gives 208,500 identifiers authenticated from 2023 to September 2024, but no independent source reports on use.
- INT-MDA-0022 MSign: in_practice. SIGMA reported in 2023 that electronic signatures are available through MSign with about 200,000 active users, mostly businesses required to sign documents for the tax service. Government figures give more than 37 million documents signed through MSign in 2023 and more than 38 million in the first nine months of 2024.
- INT-MDA-0044 Action plan on alignment with Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (European Health Data Space): proposed. The action plan on alignment with the European Health Data Space Regulation is planned, and in 2026 the Government reports only that it is being drafted.
- INT-MDA-0198 Digital Transformation Unit in the Ministry of Health: proposed. The Government plans to create a Digital Transformation Unit in the Ministry of Health in 2026. No source shows it set up.
- INT-MDA-0278 Draft Order approving the methodology for processing and electronic exchange of health data: proposed. A draft order approving a methodology for processing and electronic exchange of health data appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0297 eHealth Network membership: proposed. The National Accession Programme plans normative measures on a national e-Health authority so that Moldova can meet the rules for joining the eHealth Network. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0298 eHealth Strategy and legal framework: insufficient_evidence. The Reform Agenda planned an eHealth strategy and legal framework by 2025, and the Government planned the regulatory framework for electronic health data exchange. Moldova adopted the National Programme for Digitalisation and Innovation in Health 2025-2030, recorded separately, but no source says whether it is this strategy, and no source reports the legal framework adopted.
- INT-MDA-0304 Electronic health record (EHR) system: proposed. The electronic health record is planned, with its concept and regulation due by the end of 2026 and the system for 40% of the insured population by 2027. In 2026 the Government reports only that the draft Government Decision on it is being prepared.
- INT-MDA-0306 Electronic prescription system (eRețeta, SIA ePrescription for reimbursable medicines): implemented. The Commission reports that the electronic prescription system has been operational since 1 April 2024 for reimbursed medicines and medical devices and was modernised by 2026. The Government reports over 270,000 reimbursed prescriptions a month at the end of 2024, but no independent source reports on its use.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0398 Integrated health information system: proposed. The Commission found in 2024 and 2025 that no integrated health information system was in place and recommended developing one, and the Reform Agenda commits Moldova to an eHealth strategy and legal framework. A stakeholder comment in the Government's draft implementation programme refers to such a system as already implemented, but gives no detail and is not corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0518 National architecture framework for health information systems and registers: proposed. Developing a national architecture framework for health information systems and registers is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0521 National Contact Point for eHealth: proposed. The Reform Agenda and the National Accession Programme planned to establish the National Contact Point for eHealth by December 2025. A search of all the country's items found no record that it has been established.
- INT-MDA-0533 National e-Health entity (Health Data Access Body): proposed. A draft Government Decision creating a national e-Health entity and its operation as the health data access body are planned in the National Accession Programme. The linked items do not record its creation. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0538 National Health Strategy 2030: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that implementation of the 2030 national health strategy progressed, with a focus on digital transformation and quality of care. It had earlier asked for the strategy to be implemented effectively, including an integrated e-health system, which is still not in place.
- INT-MDA-0544 National Programme for Digitalisation and Innovation in Health 2025-2030 (Government Decision No. 556/2025): adopted. The Commission confirms that Moldova adopted the National Programme for Digitalisation and Innovation in Health 2025-2030, approved by Government Decision No. 556/2025. No source yet reports on its delivery.
- INT-MDA-0555 National telemedicine and remote consultation platform: proposed. Telemedicine and remote consultation services are a planned action, and no source reports a platform launched.
- INT-MDA-0612 Reform 4.7.19-20: legal framework for digital health services and electronic health records: proposed. The Reform Agenda plans a legal framework for digital health services followed by electronic health records, and no item reports the framework approved.
- INT-MDA-0613 Reform Agenda of Moldova under the Reform and Growth Facility: adopted. The Commission's Reform Agenda document confirms that Moldova has established its Reform Agenda, which sets out the digital, cybersecurity and connectivity reforms with deadlines to 2027. These items give no evidence on how far the reforms have been delivered.
- INT-MDA-0622 Regulatory framework on access to personal data and consent management (digital mechanism): proposed. A digital mechanism for access to personal data and consent management appears only as a planned action.
- INT-MDA-0642 SIA AMP information system: implemented. The Government's implementation programme states that the SIA AMP system is used by about 400 institutions and processes over 11 million consultations a year. This is the Government's own machine-translated account, without independent corroboration of use.
- INT-MDA-0677 Training of health professionals in e-Health: proposed. Training of health professionals in e-health is planned for 2027, and no source reports it carried out.
