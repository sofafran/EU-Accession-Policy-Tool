# Fact pack RI-FMW-001: Nationality discrimination in access to employment

The law governing access to the labour market does not give EU workers equal treatment with nationals, for example by treating them as any other foreigner or reserving private sector posts for nationals.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Commission's 2023 analytical report found that the law on access to the labour market made no difference between EU workers and other third-country nationals, and the 2024 report recorded amendments letting EU citizens work without a work permit. The 2025 report recorded a law of July 2025 giving EU citizens a separate regime, and the 2026 report the entry into force in March 2026 of the law on free movement and residence of EU citizens, aimed at alignment; the improvement is legal only. The chapter rating (early stage in 2023 and 2024, some level in 2025, between some and moderate in 2026) covers the whole chapter and was used as context only. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0014;MDA-EU-2024-001-0052;MDA-EU-2025-001-0065;MDA-EU-2026-012-0078;MDA-EU-2023-001-0041;MDA-EU-2024-001-0014;MDA-EU-2025-001-0016;MDA-EU-2026-012-0008
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Law No. 200/2025, in force since March 2026, gives EU citizens a regime separate from other foreigners and, by the government's account, equal treatment with nationals, but the 2026 key recommendation still asks for continued alignment on access to the labour market and equal treatment of EU workers. The amendments to the employment law and its implementing decision, the adjustment of secondary legislation by several ministries and the removal of the citizenship condition for staff of private employment agencies are still at draft or planning stage. What remains is secondary and sectoral legislation giving EU workers equal access to employment services, active measures, service housing and private placement, and its application in practice. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0078;MDA-EU-2026-012-0012;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0034;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0031;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0032;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0034;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0022;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0023
- priority: Medium (validated). Access for EU citizens to the labour market and equal treatment are central to the chapter and are named in the key recommendations of the 2025 and 2026 reports, but not in those of 2023 or 2024, so the test of three or more reports is not met. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on the issue; the 2023 report also contains a general call to align legislation with the acquis, outside its key recommendations. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0020;MDA-EU-2026-012-0012;MDA-EU-2023-001-0044
- tractability: High (validated). The remaining work is national legislation and staff training led by the Ministry of Labour and other ministries, and the drafts are already prepared and under consultation. Past progress is clear: the Commission reports amendments in force from January 2024 and Law No. 200/2025 in force from March 2026. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0082;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0023;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0103;MDA-EU-2024-001-0052;MDA-EU-2026-012-0078

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0045 Adjustment of secondary legislation following Law No. 200/2025: proposed. The Government reports that several ministries and the Equality Council are adjusting secondary legislation after Law No. 200/2025, and that the Ministry of Internal Affairs still needs to revise its rules on registering EU citizens. No source reports any of these acts approved.
- INT-MDA-0064 Amendment of sectoral legislation on access to service housing for EU citizens: proposed. The Ministry of Infrastructure and Regional Development is amending the rules on service housing, and the change appears as a draft Government Decision. No source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0068 Amendments to Art. 2(1) of Law No. 274/2011 on the integration of foreigners: adopted. The Government reports that amendments to Article 2(1) of Law No. 274/2011, applicable from March 2026, entitle EU, EEA and Schengen citizens with a residence card to integration measures, including ANOFM services. No source yet reports their application.
- INT-MDA-0074 Amendments to the law on the regime of foreigners in force January 2024 (temporary residence for EU citizens): adopted. The Commission confirms that amendments to the law on the regime of foreigners entered into force in January 2024, allowing EU citizens and those of some other countries to work in Moldova without a work permit. The linked items do not report how many EU citizens have worked under these rules.
- INT-MDA-0083 ANOFM employment, counselling and career guidance services: implemented. The National Employment Agency provides employment, counselling and career guidance services, and Government figures show rising referrals for counselling. UNHCR finds that formal employment services such as the Agency remain underused, so the services operate but not at scale for the groups it examined.
- INT-MDA-0215 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 1276/2018 on procedures for access to employment measures: proposed. The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection was amending Government Decision No. 1276/2018 in 2025 to give EU citizens access to the National Employment Agency's services and active measures. No source shows the amendment approved.
- INT-MDA-0247 Draft Law amending Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance (single no. 180/MMPS/2026): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 105/2018 is a draft in consultation, with ministries still proposing changes to its definitions and entry-into-force clause. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0266 Draft law on free access to employment for qualified nuclear personnel: proposed. A draft law on free access to employment for qualified nuclear personnel appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0273 Draft Law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova (single no. 507/MAI/2025): proposed. The text is a draft law still under inter-institutional consultation, with comments being accepted and the quota provisions removed. It provides that it would enter into force on 1 June 2027 and repeal Law No. 200/2010, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0415 Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance: implemented. The Law is in force and sets the rules for access to employment services and measures, which the territorial employment offices apply. Government documents note its gaps, including the exclusion of foreigners with residence for work or study and no rules for EU jobseekers or frontier workers, which a draft amending law addresses; these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0419 Law No. 121/2012 on ensuring equality: adopted. Draft laws refer to Law No. 121/2012 as the law in force that sets the grounds of discrimination. The linked items do not report how the Law is applied. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0432 Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova: in_practice. The Law has been in force since 2010 and was amended in 2023 to let EU citizens work for 90 days in any 180 without a permit; otherwise EU and other foreign citizens need a temporary residence permit for work under it. The Commission records 10,290 residence permits granted in 2025, and a draft law on the admission and stay of foreigners would repeal it on entry into force. The Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0433 Law No. 200/2025 on the free movement and residence on the territory of the Republic of Moldova of citizens of the European Union and their family members: adopted. Law No. 200/2025 was adopted in July 2025 and, as the Commission confirms, entered into force in March 2026. The Commission notes that civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals, and no source yet describes how the law is applied in practice.
- INT-MDA-0464 Law providing a different regime for EU citizens wishing to work in Moldova (July 2025): adopted. The Commission reports that in July 2025 Moldova adopted a law giving EU citizens a different regime for working in Moldova from other third-country nationals, and that the law entered into force in March 2026. This appears to be Law No. 200/2025 (INT-MDA-0433).
- INT-MDA-0676 Training of ANOFM staff (EURES, Law No. 200/2025 and serving EU citizens): proposed. Training of employment agency staff on EURES and on serving EU citizens under Law No. 200/2025 is planned in the National Accession Programme. A search of all the country's items found no record that this training has taken place; the Commission notes in 2026 only that the Agency focused on staff training, without saying on what. The Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0678 Training on equal treatment of EU workers and their family members (Equality Council and public authority staff): proposed. Training of public authority and Equality Council staff on equal treatment of EU workers under Law No. 200/2025 is planned in the National Accession Programme. The items describe plans only. The linked items are machine-translated.
