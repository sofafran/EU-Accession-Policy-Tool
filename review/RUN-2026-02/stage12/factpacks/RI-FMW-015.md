# Fact pack RI-FMW-015: Domestic preconditions for the European Health Insurance Card not in place

The country has no national health insurance card or integrated health information system, so it cannot yet issue or accept the European Health Insurance Card at accession.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Sub-area wording on the European Health Insurance Card, which matches this issue, records no progress in 2025, and the finding that no national health insurance card exists because there is no integrated health information system is unchanged in the 2023 report, the 2023 Analytical Report and the 2024 and 2025 reports. In 2026 the Commission reports a finalised feasibility study and an approved EHIC Action Plan, which are planning steps and do not yet show a change in the card or the health information system. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0075;MDA-EU-2023-001-0057;MDA-EU-2023-002-0031;MDA-EU-2024-001-0065;MDA-EU-2025-001-0076;MDA-EU-2026-012-0097;MDA-EU-2026-012-0098
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2025 and 2026 key recommendations still require IT infrastructure, systems and capacity for the EHIC scheme. Only the feasibility study is implemented: the EHIC module in the health insurance information system, the amendments to the health care and health insurance laws, the technical specifications of the card and the integrated health information system are planned or under development, and the Government states that the EU acts on the card are not yet transposed (machine translated). What remains is a national health insurance card and the IT, legal and administrative basis to issue and accept the European Health Insurance Card at accession. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014;MDA-EU-2026-012-0097;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0077;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0072;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0098;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0063;MDA-EU-2024-001-0071
- priority: High (validated). The EHIC is named in the Chapter 2 key recommendations of 2025 and 2026, and the finding that its domestic preconditions are missing is repeated in each report from 2023 to 2025, with no progress recorded in 2025. Issuing and accepting the card is a core acquis requirement for accession, which is why the issue is rated High although the key recommendation itself appears in only two reports. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014;MDA-EU-2023-001-0057;MDA-EU-2024-001-0065;MDA-EU-2025-001-0076;MDA-EU-2025-001-0075
- tractability: Medium (validated). The national health insurance company coordinates the work, the feasibility study is finished, an action plan sets a roadmap and the law amendments are planned for 2026. Against that, the card depends on an integrated health information system that the Commission found missing in each report from 2023 to 2025, and the insurer's IT directorate had 3 of 8 planned specialists (machine translated). ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0062 Amendment of Law No. 411/1995 on health care and Law No. 1585/1998 on compulsory health insurance: proposed. The National Accession Programme plans amendments to the health care and compulsory health insurance laws for 2025 and 2026, including to introduce the European Health Insurance Card. The Commission reports only a feasibility study on the card, finalised in August 2026, so the amendments are not reported as adopted.
- INT-MDA-0140 CNAM website section "EU Integration": proposed. The "EU Integration" section on the CNAM website is planned, and no source reports it created.
- INT-MDA-0156 Connection of the Republic of Moldova to EESSI: proposed. The Commission reports that an EESSI action plan was approved, and the authorities are preparing the digital infrastructure and the ANOFM and CNAS systems for interoperability. The actual connection still depends on technical documentation from the Commission and a technical solution yet to be chosen, so Moldova is not connected to EESSI.
- INT-MDA-0211 Draft CNAM Order approving the technical specifications of the European Health Insurance Card and the provisional replacement certificate: proposed. The draft order of the National Health Insurance Company on the technical specifications of the European Health Insurance Card is listed in the National Accession Programme. The Commission reports in 2026 only a feasibility study on the card.
- INT-MDA-0299 EHIC module in the compulsory health insurance information system (SIAOAM): proposed. The Government reports that a module for issuing and managing the European Health Insurance Card is being developed in the compulsory health insurance information system. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MDA-0306 Electronic prescription system (eRețeta, SIA ePrescription for reimbursable medicines): implemented. The Commission reports that the electronic prescription system has been operational since 1 April 2024 for reimbursed medicines and medical devices and was modernised by 2026. The Government reports over 270,000 reimbursed prescriptions a month at the end of 2024, but no independent source reports on its use.
- INT-MDA-0319 Feasibility study on the introduction and financial impact of the European Health Insurance Card: implemented. The Commission reports that the feasibility study on the financial impact of introducing the European Health Insurance Card was finalised in August 2026.
- INT-MDA-0398 Integrated health information system: proposed. The Commission found in 2024 and 2025 that no integrated health information system was in place and recommended developing one, and the Reform Agenda commits Moldova to an eHealth strategy and legal framework. A stakeholder comment in the Government's draft implementation programme refers to such a system as already implemented, but gives no detail and is not corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0410 Introduction of the European Health Insurance Card in Moldova: proposed. The Commission found no progress on the European Health Insurance Card in 2025, and in 2026 reports only that an EHIC Action Plan was approved as a roadmap for operational implementation. The legal amendments needed to introduce the card were planned for 2026 and no source shows them adopted or the card issued.
- INT-MDA-0537 National health insurance card: insufficient_evidence. The Commission found in 2024 and 2025 that no national health insurance card exists because there is no integrated health information system. A search of all the country's items for the card found no plan, decision or later finding, so no status can be given.
- INT-MDA-0538 National Health Strategy 2030: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that implementation of the 2030 national health strategy progressed, with a focus on digital transformation and quality of care. It had earlier asked for the strategy to be implemented effectively, including an integrated e-health system, which is still not in place.
