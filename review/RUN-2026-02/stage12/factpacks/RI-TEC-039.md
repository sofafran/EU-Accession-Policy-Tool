# Fact pack RI-TEC-039: Public sector websites and apps not accessible to persons with disabilities

Public sector websites, mobile applications and other digital public services are not accessible to persons with physical, sensory or intellectual disabilities, and the EU accessibility requirements for them (the Web Accessibility Directive and the European Accessibility Act) are not applied or enforced.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Chapter 10 scale is context only, as this issue comes from a cross-cutting theme and was not decisive. SIGMA found in 2023 that government websites complied poorly with WCAG 2.0 despite guidelines, with an average of 45 errors per page across 26 tested sites, and the Commission found in 2026 that public sector websites and mobile applications remain insufficiently accessible and that accessibility is insufficiently enforced and funded. Items: MDA-IO-2023-001-0063;MDA-IO-2023-001-0065;MDA-IO-2023-001-0076;MDA-EU-2026-012-0135;MDA-EU-2026-012-0058
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2026 key recommendation asks Moldova to improve the accessibility of public sector websites and mobile applications. The law on accessibility requirements for products and services adopted in August 2026, which the Commission describes as in line with the acquis, is a legal step for products and services, while the order setting WCAG 2.2 rules for public websites had not started in 2026 because it awaits a Government Decision. What remains is binding accessibility rules for public sector websites and applications and their enforcement and funding. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0024;MDA-EU-2026-012-0041;MDA-EU-2026-012-0135;MDA-EU-2026-012-0058;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0046
- priority: Medium (validated). Accessibility of public sector websites and applications is a key recommendation in the 2026 report, the first Commission report to raise it in a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, and no Reform Agenda payment condition names it. It is a clear EU requirement but not yet repeated across reports. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0024;MDA-EU-2026-012-0135
- tractability: Medium (validated). Guidelines for government websites already exist, and the WCAG 2.2 rules are a single order of the e-Governance Agency once the Government Decision is approved. The poor compliance SIGMA found across many sites in 2023 and the Commission's finding that accessibility is insufficiently enforced and funded show that bringing existing sites into line needs resources across many institutions. Items: MDA-IO-2023-001-0063;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0046;MDA-EU-2026-012-0058

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0041 Accessibility of ICT products and services for persons with disabilities (e-Governance Agency order on WCAG 2.2 rules): proposed. The e-Governance Agency order approving accessibility rules under WCAG 2.2 had not started in 2025, because it depends on a prior Government decision on accessibility requirements. No source shows the order issued.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0456 Law on accessibility requirements for certain products and services (European Accessibility Act): adopted. The Commission reports that a law on accessibility requirements for certain products and services, including digital and e-commerce services, was adopted in August 2026 in line with the EU acquis. No source yet describes its application.
- INT-MDA-0528 National design system for web and mobile government solutions (Unitary Design Model): adopted. Government Decision No. 677 of 8 October 2025 approved the measures to implement the Unitary Design Model for all public service providers. The Government reports the work as in progress and says the model will be applied gradually, starting with MSign and MPass, so application across services is not yet shown.
