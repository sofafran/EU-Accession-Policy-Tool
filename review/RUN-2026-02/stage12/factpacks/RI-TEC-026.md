# Fact pack RI-TEC-026: Electronic identification and trust services not usable or recognised

Electronic identification and signature tools are costly, cumbersome and little used, qualified trust services are not widely available, and the country is not yet on the EU trusted list or in cross-border wallet arrangements.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The chapter scale was used as context only, since this is part of the chapter. The 2023 report asks Moldova to join the third-country trusted list and the 2024 report asks for interoperability with European Digital Identity Wallets; the 2026 report records amendments in December 2025 that fully align the rules for qualified trust service providers, joining the trusted list and cross-border testing of the national wallet. Use has not changed in the same way: the Commission gives 200 000 users, 9.8% of adults, in 2024 and over 266 000, 10% of adults, in 2025. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0096;MDA-EU-2024-001-0082;MDA-EU-2026-012-0120;MDA-EU-2026-012-0123;MDA-EU-2024-001-0042;MDA-EU-2025-001-0043
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Recognition has advanced through the December 2025 amendments, the trusted list and new identity cards carrying a qualified signature, but the Reform Agenda condition for December 2026 also requires an EU-aligned national wallet integrating at least 20 e-services, and the wallet was only started in September 2025, with remote identification moved to 2027. Use remains low, with about 10% of the population holding qualified certificates and fees of at least 11 euros a year reported by civil society in 2026. What remains is a working EU-aligned wallet, mutual recognition in practice and much wider, cheaper use of electronic identification and signatures. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0120;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0010;MDA-EU-2025-002-0035;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0087;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0085;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0033;MDA-CSO-2026-001-0076
- priority: High (validated). The national wallet aligned with EU standards, joining the third-country trusted list and alignment with eIDAS 2 are a Reform Agenda payment condition due in December 2026, and the Commission raised the trusted list and wallet interoperability in the 2023 and 2024 reports. Electronic identification is the centre of this theme. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0035;MDA-EU-2025-002-0041;MDA-EU-2023-001-0096;MDA-EU-2024-001-0082
- tractability: Medium (validated). Legal and recognition steps have moved quickly, with Law No. 117/2025, the December 2025 amendments and the trusted list, and the work lies mainly with the e-Governance Agency and the trust service provider. Against that, use has stayed near 10% of adults, the eKYC deadline has slipped to 2027, and within the 2025 consultation another government body described EVOSign as still at the pilot stage and not ready for use in systems. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0200;MDA-EU-2026-012-0120;MDA-EU-2025-001-0043;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0085;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0321

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0003 MCabinet (Citizen Portal): implemented. An international partner reports that MCabinet was launched in October 2020 and gives access to 17 data blocks. The Government reports that unique authenticated users rose from 92,619 in 2023 to 176,855 in the first three quarters of 2024, but this rests on its own reporting.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0008 EVO: implemented. The Commission confirms that the EVO application was launched in June 2024 and EVO 2.0 in June 2026, and that the EVO portal lists 636 public services, 447 of them online. Figures on use (169,692 downloads and 66,733 active users by the end of 2025) come only from the Government's own machine-translated progress report, so use at scale is not independently corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0009 EVOSign: implemented. The Commission reports that EVOSign was launched, and the e-Governance Agency says it has run in pilot mode in the production environment since 8 August 2024 and is available in the app stores. Other public bodies consulted on the 2025-2027 programme state that it is only at the pilot and testing stage, has not been launched and lacks the confirmations needed for use, so the sources conflict on whether it is in operation; it is also planned to be absorbed into the digital wallet.
- INT-MDA-0011 Public Services Portal (services.gov.md): in_practice. The Commission and SIGMA describe the portal as the working single point of access to public services, and the Government reports over 0.5 million unique visitors a year and user satisfaction in its annual national survey. Material in the Government's own implementation programme says only a minority of listed services can be completed fully online, the Commission notes the portal is not integrated with EU systems, and by 2026 services are listed on the integrated EVO portal.
- INT-MDA-0014 MCloud: implemented. The Commission counts MCloud among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and RUSI notes that e-government services have adopted cloud solutions such as MCloud. The Court of Accounts found information systems developed without alignment with MCloud and other government platforms, and none of the linked items measures independently how far the platform is used.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0017 MDelivery: implemented. The Commission reported in 2023 that a further 42 public services had been integrated with MDelivery, which delivers requested documents to the user's home, and the Government lists it among platforms delivered under the modernisation project. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0018 MDocs: implemented. The Government lists MDocs among the new platforms developed, and an international partner gives it the same operating status as the other government platforms. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0019 MPass: implemented. The Commission and the Government list MPass among the government platforms implemented, and an international partner describes it as the authentication service for public e-services. Government consultation material gives 208,500 identifiers authenticated from 2023 to September 2024, but no independent source reports on use.
- INT-MDA-0020 MPay: implemented. The Commission counts MPay among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes it as in place since 2020. SIGMA found in 2023 that online payment through MPay was possible but not yet integrated into digital service provision, and the Government's figures on savings and on payments collected through MPay are not corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0021 MPower: implemented. An international partner reports that MPower was launched in 2020 and lets people create electronic powers of attorney with the same value as notarised ones, and the Government lists it among its working platforms. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0022 MSign: in_practice. SIGMA reported in 2023 that electronic signatures are available through MSign with about 200,000 active users, mostly businesses required to sign documents for the tax service. Government figures give more than 37 million documents signed through MSign in 2023 and more than 38 million in the first nine months of 2024.
- INT-MDA-0023 MobiSign: insufficient_evidence. A search of all the country's items found no mention of MobiSign after 2023, so no source says whether it was launched, renamed or dropped.
- INT-MDA-0024 Mobile ID: in_practice. Civil society monitoring, citing the service's data, reports 18,400 Mobile ID certificates issued by Orange Moldova and 17,725 by Moldcell in September 2022. No later figures are given.
- INT-MDA-0025 Modernization of Government Services project (MGSP): implemented. The World Bank-supported project was carried out by the e-Governance Agency and concluded in 2025, with the Government reporting 110 digitalised services and 94 re-engineered services. All figures on its delivery and results come from the Government and the Agency, without independent corroboration.
- INT-MDA-0030 Semantic Catalog for MConnect: implemented. The concept and register regulation were approved by Government Decision No. 323/2021, and the Government lists the Semantic Catalog among its new platforms and reports employment data integrated through it. An international partner finds that the open data portal it supports is still lacking in use.
- INT-MDA-0032 Open Government Data Portal (date.gov.md): implemented. The portal has operated since 2011 and holds about 1,268 datasets, and a 2024 international partner report describes it as a one-stop shop for public sector data. The same body of evidence finds that the portal is still lacking in use and needs updating, so use at scale is not shown.
- INT-MDA-0033 e-APL: implemented. The Government reports that the e-APL portal was developed and launched as a pilot, with 91 mayoralties taking part and nine State Fiscal Register services available through it. The target is use by at least half of first-level local authorities, and all items come from the Government's own machine-translated documents, so the status rests on Medium confidence evidence.
- INT-MDA-0034 e-Consulate: implemented. The e-Governance Agency lists e-Consulate among its seven new digital platforms. This rests on one government item rated Low confidence, and no source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0035 eID cards: implemented. IPRE cites STISC data that in September 2022 only 66 of about 129,000 active qualified signature certificates were on eID cards issued by the Public Services Agency, so the cards existed but were barely used for signing. The linked item gives no later figures.
- INT-MDA-0049 Agency plans to centralise access to public services under EVO: implemented. The e-Governance Agency's 2025 plans to centralise access to public services under EVO were followed by Government Decision No. 677/2025 on the integrated EVO portal and its public launch in January 2026. The Commission confirms that the EVO portal brings together 636 public services, 447 of them online.
- INT-MDA-0056 Alignment of the national interoperability framework with the European Interoperability Framework: proposed. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet says the national framework follows the European Interoperability Framework, but the Government notes that the Interoperable Europe Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/903, has not been transposed. Further alignment is planned in the National Accession Programme; these Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0061 Amendment of Law No. 234/2021 and the Administrative Code (no electronic signature for authenticated users): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 234/2021 and the Administrative Code is still listed as a planned action in the 2025 progress report, and no source reports it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0069 Amendments to Law No. 124/2022 on electronic identification and trust services (December 2025): adopted. The Commission reports that amendments to the law on electronic identification and trust services in December 2025 fully aligned Moldova with EU requirements for qualified trust service providers. The Government's 2025 progress report still describes a draft law on electronic identification as being drafted (INT-MDA-0248).
- INT-MDA-0097 Association of Moldova to the Digital Europe Programme: adopted. The Commission reports that Parliament ratified the association agreement to the Digital Europe Programme in May 2024, and the Government dates Moldova's joining to 19 February 2024. No source shows Moldovan participation in calls under the programme.
- INT-MDA-0136 Central Electoral Commission online services (prior registration abroad, electoral complaints, electronic subscription lists): implemented. The Government lists the Central Electoral Commission's prior registration service for voters abroad, electronic subscription lists and electronic complaints module as implemented, following amendments to the Electoral Code. The status rests only on the Government's machine-translated account, with no data on use.
- INT-MDA-0152 Conformity audit of STISC by a Conformity Assessment Body: proposed. The Government's 2025 progress report says that, on the advice of DG CNECT, inclusion of STISC in the EU trusted list must be preceded by a conformity audit, and that STISC is taking the measures needed to organise it. The audit has not yet taken place. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0248 Draft law amending Law No. 124/2022 on electronic identification and trust services (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183, eIDAS 2): unclear. The sources conflict: the Commission reports that amendments to the law on electronic identification and trust services in December 2025 fully aligned Moldova with EU digital identity requirements for qualified trust service providers, while the Government's progress report for 2025 states that the draft law is still being drafted. It is not clear whether the December 2025 amendments are this draft or a narrower change.
- INT-MDA-0253 Draft law amending Law No. 273/1994 on identity documents (new electronic identity card): adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 68/2024 amended the law on identity documents to introduce the identity card, which appears to be the outcome of this draft. Neither source states the link in terms, and the Government item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0290 e-residency: proposed. The Digital Transformation Strategy refers to e-residency programmes that mutual recognition of electronic signatures would allow. No source shows an e-residency programme launched.
- INT-MDA-0300 eKYC remote identification service: proposed. The remote identification service has not been launched: the Government reports that the World Bank-supported contract for developing it was signed at the end of 2025 and that the project is in an advanced stage of technical development. All items come from the Government's own documents, mostly machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0307 Electronic submission of CSO registration documents (Public Services Agency): in_practice. A civil society report states that the Public Services Agency accepts registration documents signed with a qualified electronic signature and that over 723 organisations have used this since 2022, with 359 online applications in 2025. Registration cannot yet be completed fully online.
- INT-MDA-0316 EVOID mechanism: proposed. The EVOID mechanism is to be developed as the EVOSign enrolment flow is absorbed into the digital wallet. No source shows it in place.
- INT-MDA-0321 FOD platform: implemented. The Government lists the FOD platform among seven new platforms delivered under the government services modernisation project. The linked item gives no detail on its use.
- INT-MDA-0333 Government CERT (CERT-GOV-MD) within STISC: implemented. The Commission confirms that the Information Technology and Cyber Security Service acts as the government's computer security incident response team. It had a staff of four in November 2022, and no source gives evidence of the incidents it handles.
- INT-MDA-0349 Government Decision No. 491/2022: insufficient_evidence. The only item is a planned action to implement a technical solution for verifying and validating EU-issued signature certificates, which says nothing about the status of Government Decision No. 491/2022 itself.
- INT-MDA-0373 Government public key infrastructure: implemented. The 2023 Strategy states that STISC manages the Government's public key infrastructure, and IPRE cites STISC data of about 129,000 active qualified signature certificates in 2022. The figures come from STISC itself.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0388 Inclusion of Moldova and STISC in the EU/EEA third-country Trusted List: implemented. The Commission reports that, following amendments to the law on electronic identification and trust services in December 2025, Moldova joined the EU third country trusted list for advanced electronic signatures and seals. The Government's own progress report records the data for inclusion as sent to the Commission.
- INT-MDA-0398 Integrated health information system: proposed. The Commission found in 2024 and 2025 that no integrated health information system was in place and recommended developing one, and the Reform Agenda commits Moldova to an eHealth strategy and legal framework. A stakeholder comment in the Government's draft implementation programme refers to such a system as already implemented, but gives no detail and is not corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0401 Integration of the qualified electronic signature into company registration and filing with the business register: implemented. The Commission reported in 2024 that a qualified electronic signature was introduced for company registration and filing with the business register. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0409 Interoperability with the European Digital Identity Wallets (e-Governance Agency): proposed. The Government describes interoperability with the European Digital Identity Wallets as a task the e-Governance Agency is carrying out, and no source reports it achieved.
- INT-MDA-0418 Law No. 117/2025 amending Law No. 124/2022 on electronic identification and trust services: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 117/2025 amended Law No. 124/2022 to open the Moldovan market to EU qualified trust service providers and divide supervisory competences. No source describes its application, and it is not clear whether it is the same act as the December 2025 amendments the Commission reports (INT-MDA-0069).
- INT-MDA-0421 Law No. 124/2022 on electronic identification and trust services: adopted. Parliament approved the Law on electronic identification and trust services on 19 May 2022, and the Commission found it broadly but only partially aligned with the eIDAS Regulation. The sources give different dates for its entry into force, the Commission 10 December 2022 and the Government 1 January 2025, but agree it is in force; no source describes its application beyond its use as a reference for electronic submissions.
- INT-MDA-0422 Law No. 126/2023 on remote identification and interaction ("Contactless Business"): adopted. The Government lists Law No. 126/2023 on remote identification and interaction among its adopted measures, and an international partner describes the related Contactless Business initiative. No source reports how the law is applied.
- INT-MDA-0423 Law No. 130/2025 amending the Electoral Code: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 130/2025 amended the Electoral Code, including electronic subscription and voter lists and the use of electronic signatures on them. No source reports how these provisions are applied.
- INT-MDA-0427 Law No. 175/2021 amending certain normative acts: implemented. Law No. 175/2021 allowed documents for registering legal entities to be submitted electronically with an electronic signature, and the Public Services Agency applied it through a provisional e-mail registration service. This is the Government's own account, and the agency notes the e-mail route was due to delays in its systems.
- INT-MDA-0442 Law No. 321/2023 on stimulating the development and use of electronic services: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 321/2023 set the framework for stimulating electronic services, including the digital identity card, driving licence and registration certificate. The linked items do not report specifically on its application.
- INT-MDA-0445 Law No. 467/2003 on informatisation and state information resources: implemented. The law is in force and is the basis for state information resources and services such as EVOSign. An audit found that the e-Governance Agency had not drafted the methodological frameworks the law requires, so its application is incomplete.
- INT-MDA-0451 Law No. 68/2024 amending Law No. 273/1994 on identity documents: adopted. Law No. 68/2024 amending the law on identity documents introduced the identity card and was to enter into force once the technical conditions were met and no later than 31 March 2025. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report cards issued.
- INT-MDA-0472 M-services shared e-government infrastructure: in_practice. The e-Governance Agency operates the shared M-services infrastructure, and the Interoperable Europe factsheet gives these platforms the same status as MConnect, which independent sources find in wide use. For example, SIGMA reports about 200,000 active MSign users.
- INT-MDA-0482 Membership of the EU Digital Identity Cooperation Group: implemented. The Commission reports that Moldova has joined and is part of the EU Digital Identity Cooperation Group.
- INT-MDA-0496 MLearn platform: implemented. The Government lists MLearn among the reusable digital platforms developed and implemented. This is the Government's own account and no source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0497 MLog governmental logging service: implemented. The data protection authority states that people can consult the history of access to their personal data, drawn from MLog, through a government portal, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes MLog as a logbook of actions in government systems. The linked items do not measure how widely it is used.
- INT-MDA-0498 MNotify government notification service: implemented. The Government lists MNotify among the reusable platforms developed and implemented, and states that public bodies must use it exclusively for electronic notifications. The linked items give no figures on its use, and these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0508 Moldova's access to the EU Cybersecurity Reserve: implemented. The Commission reports that the Council renewed Moldova's access to the EU Cybersecurity Reserve for a further year in July 2026. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it activated the mechanism successfully, but this rests on its own account.
- INT-MDA-0512 MTender e-procurement system (State Register of Public Procurement, SIA RSAP): in_practice. The Commission states that MTender has enabled electronic procurement since its launch, with public access to documents, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet says all public tenders are published on it. The Commission also finds it outdated and not covering the full procurement cycle, and a replacement system is being procured.
- INT-MDA-0528 National design system for web and mobile government solutions (Unitary Design Model): adopted. Government Decision No. 677 of 8 October 2025 approved the measures to implement the Unitary Design Model for all public service providers. The Government reports the work as in progress and says the model will be applied gradually, starting with MSign and MPass, so application across services is not yet shown.
- INT-MDA-0531 National digital identity wallet: implemented. The Commission reports that a national digital identity wallet was launched within the EVO application in June 2024. The wallet aligned with the EU Digital Identity framework is a further version: the Government says development started in September 2025, the Commission reports cross-border interoperability tests in 2026, and the Reform Agenda sets its launch for December 2026.
- INT-MDA-0554 National strategy to align with eIDAS 2.0 (EU Digital Identity Wallet): unclear. The Commission reports that Moldova developed a national strategy to align with eIDAS 2.0, including the EU Digital Identity Wallet, and joined the EU Digital Identity Cooperation Group. It does not say whether the strategy was formally adopted.
- INT-MDA-0562 New electronic identity card with a qualified electronic signature certificate: in_practice. The Government reports that new identity cards with a chip and an integrated qualified signature certificate have been issued since 31 March 2025, and a civil society source reports that one million such cards were issued in 2025. This shows issue at scale, though no source reports how often the signature function is used.
- INT-MDA-0584 particip.gov.md online public consultation platform: implemented. The Government lists particip.gov.md among the platforms implemented, and CSO Meter notes continued government improvements to it as part of implementing the open data law. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0585 Pilot on mutual recognition of electronic signatures with Ukraine: implemented. The Commission reported in 2021 that Moldova benefited from an EU4Digital pilot on mutual recognition of electronic signatures with Ukraine. No later source reports on it.
- INT-MDA-0604 Qualified electronic signature certificates issued by STISC: in_practice. Civil society monitoring, citing the service's data, reports 129,384 active qualified electronic signature certificates in September 2022, of which 97,633 were USB tokens held by 45,870 unique users. The service is the only qualified trust service provider in Moldova and certificates are also issued through Public Services Agency offices.
- INT-MDA-0607 raportare.gov.md: implemented. The Government lists raportare.gov.md among the reusable platforms developed and implemented. The linked item is machine-translated and gives no figures on use.
- INT-MDA-0613 Reform Agenda of Moldova under the Reform and Growth Facility: adopted. The Commission's Reform Agenda document confirms that Moldova has established its Reform Agenda, which sets out the digital, cybersecurity and connectivity reforms with deadlines to 2027. These items give no evidence on how far the reforms have been delivered.
- INT-MDA-0674 Technical solution for verifying and validating EU-issued electronic signature certificates (STISC): proposed. Implementing a technical solution for verifying and validating EU-issued electronic signature certificates is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
