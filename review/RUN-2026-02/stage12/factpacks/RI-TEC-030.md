# Fact pack RI-TEC-030: Not ready for EU justice, home affairs and border information systems

The country lacks the systems, technical adaptations and funding to connect to EU justice and home affairs systems such as ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since the issue comes from a cross-cutting theme. In 2023 the Commission finds no electronic biometric database and asks for planning towards Eurodac, and in 2026 it finds security and data protection weaknesses in the border police system and asks for interoperability ahead of a future connection to EU large-scale systems; a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no item from the 2024 or 2025 reports on Eurodac, VIS, ECRIS or other EU large-scale systems. Wider use of SIENA, which the 2026 report records, is the one change, and it concerns Europol's exchange channel rather than the EU systems in this issue. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0024;MDA-EU-2023-001-0026;MDA-EU-2026-012-0061;MDA-EU-2026-012-0062;MDA-EU-2026-012-0059
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). Apart from SIENA, the responses to the latest requirements are plans: interoperability of the fingerprint register with Eurodac, a national visa system interoperable with VIS, an analysis, by the end of 2027, of how to make the criminal records register interoperable with ECRIS and ECRIS-TCN, and a joint study with eu-LISA. The government reports a partial transposition of the Eurodac regulation in the fingerprint law, which is legal only. What remains is building or upgrading the migration, border and justice systems so that they are secure and ready to connect to Eurodac, VIS, ECRIS and the other EU large-scale systems. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0060;MDA-EU-2026-012-0062;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0403;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0375;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0358;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0402;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0367
- priority: Medium (validated). The Commission repeats the call for data exchange through SIENA in the 2023 and 2026 key recommendations and raises Eurodac and EU large-scale systems in 2023 and 2026, but connection to most of these systems is expected only towards accession and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on these systems. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0119;MDA-EU-2026-012-0004;MDA-EU-2023-001-0026;MDA-EU-2026-012-0062
- tractability: Medium (validated). There is evidence of progress on SIENA, rolled out to several law enforcement bodies, and the government has a plan covering each system. Against that, the work needs the interior ministry, the migration inspectorate, the border police, justice bodies and eu-LISA, and the systems for Eurodac and VIS are still to be built or upgraded. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0059;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0401;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0402;MDA-EU-2026-012-0060;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0375

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0070 Amendments to Law No. 1549/2002 on state fingerprint registration: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 1549/2002 on state fingerprint registration was amended to partially transpose the Eurodac Regulation. The linked item is machine-translated and gives no date or detail on application.
- INT-MDA-0132 Biometric database: proposed. The Commission reported in 2023 that no electronic biometric database existed and that developing one had been set as an objective. The National Accession Programme still plans a system for capturing biometric data at border crossing points, so the database is not reported as built.
- INT-MDA-0154 Connection of Moldovan authorities to Europol SIENA: in_practice. The Commission reports that SIENA has been rolled out to several law enforcement authorities and that exchanges through it have increased. Government sources date the connections of the Criminal Assets Recovery Agency to February 2020 and of the National Anticorruption Centre to March 2023.
- INT-MDA-0227 Draft Government Decision on connecting the National Vehicle Register to EUCARIS: proposed. The decision on connecting the National Vehicle Register to EUCARIS appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0238 Draft Government Decision on the information system for cross-border electronic data exchange in judicial cooperation (e-CODEX): proposed. A draft Government decision to implement a system for cross-border electronic data exchange in judicial cooperation appears only in the Government's plans. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0245 Draft law amending certain normative acts on the digitalisation of judicial cooperation: adopted. The draft law on the digitalisation of judicial cooperation is listed in the National Accession Programme, and the Commission reports that normative amendments promoting the digitalisation of certain judicial procedures were made in August 2026. The Commission does not name the act, so the match with this draft is probable but not confirmed.
- INT-MDA-0355 Government Decision No. 565/2007 on the automated information system "Fingerprint Register" (proposed amendments in line with Eurodac): proposed. The Migration Inspectorate sent proposals in January 2025 to amend the Decision in line with Eurodac, and the National Accession Programme plans interoperability of the Fingerprint Register with Eurodac. No adoption of the amendments is recorded in these items. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0382 IGM information system: proposed. The Government planned in 2024 to create the preconditions for an IGM information system, and the Commission states in 2026 that the Inspectorate's systems still need upgrades for interoperability. No source reports the new system in place.
- INT-MDA-0405 Interoperability of national information systems with EU systems managed by eu-LISA: proposed. Interoperability with EU systems managed by eu-LISA is at the stage of a feasibility study and working meetings, and no source reports any national system connected.
- INT-MDA-0407 Interoperability of the Register of Forensic and Criminological Information with ECRIS and ECRIS-TCN: proposed. The Government plans to update the criminal records register and analyse its interoperability with ECRIS and ECRIS-TCN by the end of 2027. No source shows any connection made.
- INT-MDA-0501 Modernisation and interconnection of border management information systems: proposed. The Government plans to modernise border management information systems, interconnect them with EU systems and introduce biometric capture at crossing points. The Commission finds in 2026 that the border police information system still has security and data protection vulnerabilities, and no source shows the modernisation delivered.
- INT-MDA-0556 National Visa Information System interoperable with the EU Visa Information System: proposed. The National Accession Programme says the National Visa Information System needs to be made operational and interoperable with the EU Visa Information System, and lists a draft Government Decision for it. The items describe plans only. The linked items are machine-translated.

## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0154 leads to MDA-EU-2026-012-0059: success worked, attribution High. The Commission, an independent source, states that information exchanges through SIENA increased after its roll-out to several law enforcement bodies, which is the result the 2023 recommendation sought. No figures are given.
