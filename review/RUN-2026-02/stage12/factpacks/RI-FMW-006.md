# Fact pack RI-FMW-006: Alignment with the European Labour Authority regulation

National law is not aligned with the regulation establishing the European Labour Authority, including the arrangements for cooperating with it.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since this is only part of the chapter and the only Chapter 2 sub-area wording among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files concerns legal alignment in general (2023) and the European Health Insurance Card (2025). A search of all items for the European Labour Authority and Regulation (EU) 2019/1149 found the Commission's two records of the May 2025 cooperation agreement and Government items on the agreement and a draft law; none is a finding or recommendation on alignment, so there is no sequence from which to read a direction. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0018;MDA-EU-2025-001-0071;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0053
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Commission records that Moldova signed a cooperation agreement with the European Labour Authority in May 2025, which covers the arrangements for cooperating with it. The government states that the draft law to harmonise national legislation with Regulation (EU) 2019/1149 is still being prepared, and gives this only as its own account. What remains is adopting and applying the law that aligns national legislation with the regulation, including the Authority's role as mediator, and building the capacity of the ministry, the employment agency and the labour inspectorate to cooperate with it. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0071;MDA-EU-2025-001-0018;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0051;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0053;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0091;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0054
- priority: Low (validated). A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no Commission requirement, key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on the European Labour Authority; the Commission mentions it only to record the 2025 cooperation agreement. Cooperation with the Authority is a small part of the chapter. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0018;MDA-EU-2025-001-0071
- tractability: High (validated). The change is narrow, legal and within the control of the labour ministry, and the cooperation agreement was concluded in 2025, which shows recent progress with the Authority. A draft law has already been identified, although its adoption date is not given. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0071;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0053;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0091

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0135 Capacity building of MMPS, ANOFM and the State Labour Inspectorate for cooperation with the European Labour Authority: insufficient_evidence. The only linked item is a general Government statement that work is under way to strengthen the capacity of the Ministry, the employment agency and the Labour Inspectorate to cooperate with the European Labour Authority. It names no measure, so no status can be given. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0156 Connection of the Republic of Moldova to EESSI: proposed. The Commission reports that an EESSI action plan was approved, and the authorities are preparing the digital infrastructure and the ANOFM and CNAS systems for interoperability. The actual connection still depends on technical documentation from the Commission and a technical solution yet to be chosen, so Moldova is not connected to EESSI.
- INT-MDA-0162 Cooperation Agreement on the participation of the Republic of Moldova in the European Labour Authority (2 May 2025): implemented. The Commission confirms that Moldova signed a cooperation agreement with the European Labour Authority in May 2025. The Government reports that it then began a dialogue with the Authority on priority areas, including EESSI, but this rests on its own account.
- INT-MDA-0187 Dialogue with the European Labour Authority, including on EESSI: implemented. The Government reports that after signing the agreement Moldovan authorities started a dialogue with the European Labour Authority on priority areas including EESSI. This is the Government's own account.
- INT-MDA-0242 Draft law amending certain acts (role of the European Labour Authority as mediator under Regulation (EU) 2019/1149): proposed. The Government reports work under way on a draft law to align with Regulation (EU) 2019/1149 on the European Labour Authority. No source shows it adopted.
