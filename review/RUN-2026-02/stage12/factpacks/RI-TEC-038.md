# Fact pack RI-TEC-038: Electronic public procurement system outdated and in need of replacement

The electronic public procurement system is outdated or functionally inadequate: it does not support all procedures, tools or stages of the procurement cycle set out in law, hampers centralised procurement, and has to be re-engineered or replaced by a new system.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since the issue comes from a cross-cutting theme. The Commission finds the e-procurement system inadequate in 2021, calls for it to be overhauled or re-engineered in 2023, describes MTender as outdated in 2025, and in 2026 records limited progress on digitalising procurement and an outdated MTender that limits monitoring; a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no item from the 2024 report on e-procurement. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0012;MDA-EU-2023-001-0110;MDA-EU-2023-002-0005;MDA-EU-2025-001-0064;MDA-EU-2026-012-0070;MDA-EU-2026-012-0072
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The new system is still being procured: technical specifications were received in September 2025 and the call for bids was published in December 2025 with a deadline in February 2026, while the Reform Agenda sets a pilot of the new system by December 2027. MTender remains in use without planning, contract management and some procedures. What remains is designing, building and putting into operation a new e-procurement system that covers every procedure and stage of the procurement cycle. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-011-0013;MDA-GOV-2026-011-0016;MDA-GOV-2026-011-0019;MDA-EU-2025-002-0043;MDA-EU-2025-002-0075;MDA-EU-2025-002-0076;MDA-EU-2026-012-0006
- priority: High (validated). The new e-procurement system is a Reform Agenda payment condition, recalled in the 2025 release decision, and the Commission calls for it in the 2023 report and in the key recommendations of the 2025 and 2026 reports. It is central to digital public procurement. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0043;MDA-EU-2025-004-0001;MDA-EU-2023-001-0110;MDA-EU-2025-001-0015;MDA-EU-2026-012-0006
- tractability: Medium (validated). Funding from international partners is secured, a concept and programme exist, and procurement of the system has started with World Bank review. Against that, the system has been called for since 2021 with limited progress recorded in 2026, and building a full new system is a large technical project. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0063;MDA-GOV-2026-011-0014;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0378;MDA-EU-2021-001-0013;MDA-EU-2026-012-0070

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0137 Centre for Information Technologies in Finance (CTIF): implemented. The Centre is set up and working as owner and technical operator of the MTender procurement system, with 424 of 479 posts filled. This rests on government sources.
- INT-MDA-0222 Draft Government Decision approving the Concept of the information system State Register of Public Procurement (e-Procurement): unclear. The National Accession Programme lists a draft Government Decision approving the concept of the e-Procurement information system, while a 2026 Government monitoring report refers to adjusting the system in line with the approved concept without naming the act. The items do not make clear whether this Decision was adopted. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0265 Draft law on electronic invoicing in public procurement: proposed. A draft law on electronic invoicing in public procurement appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0512 MTender e-procurement system (State Register of Public Procurement, SIA RSAP): in_practice. The Commission states that MTender has enabled electronic procurement since its launch, with public access to documents, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet says all public tenders are published on it. The Commission also finds it outdated and not covering the full procurement cycle, and a replacement system is being procured.
- INT-MDA-0563 New integrated electronic public procurement system (re-engineering of the e-procurement system): proposed. The new system has not yet been built: the Commission asked in 2025 for its design and development to be sped up and in 2026 still described the existing MTender system as outdated. Government monitoring records that procurement of the developer was published in December 2025 with bids due in February 2026, and the Reform Agenda sets a pilot of the essential modules by December 2027.
- INT-MDA-0579 Online tender platform (use for COVID-19 related procurement): in_practice. The Commission reported in 2021 that the online tender platform was used for COVID-19 related procurement and that such tenders were monitored. No later source reports on it.
- INT-MDA-0598 Public procurement development programme: adopted. The Government describes the programme's action plan, including re-engineering of the electronic procurement system. No source reports how far it has been carried out.
- INT-MDA-0619 Regulation on low-value public procurement: adopted. The Commission reports that the Regulation obliges contracting authorities to carry out all stages of most low-value procurement through the State Register of Public Procurement. The linked item does not report its application.
