# Fact pack RI-FMW-014: Access and rights of third-country workers

Workers from outside the EU face barriers to accessing the labour market, or their rights after admission are not protected or monitored.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The issue is not raised as a Chapter 2 finding or requirement in EU material: across all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, the Commission items on third-country nationals record the temporary residence rule and counts of residence permits, and its 2023 call to register foreigners and give them access to services sits under Chapter 24, so the standard scale was not decisive. The issue's own items describe continuing barriers to third-country workers' access from 2024 to 2026: the migration inspectorate's 2024 proposals to remove problems found in the foreigners law, a 2025 study finding the procedure for employing foreigners bureaucratic, and UNHCR's 2026 finding that only 29 percent of working-age Ukrainian refugees are in paid work, with language, recognition of qualifications and childcare as barriers. The new law on admission of foreigners that would change the rules is a draft due to apply from June 2027. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0055;MDA-EU-2026-012-0082;MDA-GOV-2024-006-0022;MDA-RES-2025-001-0027;MDA-IO-2026-002-0002;MDA-IO-2026-002-0003;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0032;MDA-EU-2023-001-0051;MDA-EU-2023-001-0023
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). No EU item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files states a Chapter 2 requirement on third-country workers, so the gap is judged on the issue's own items. The draft law on admission of foreigners and the draft amendment extending employment services to more categories of foreigners are proposals, and consultation comments on the draft law point to work permits tied to one employer and no clear separation between labour inspection and immigration control, while refugees still face language, recognition and childcare barriers. What remains is a law in force that eases third-country workers' access to the labour market and protects their rights after admission, and support that reaches refugees in practice. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-006-0032;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0017;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0112;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0109;MDA-IO-2026-002-0003
- priority: Low (validated). The issue is not raised as a requirement in EU material, which counts residence permits and records the residence rules, and this keeps priority from being High: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no Commission recommendation on third-country workers' access to the labour market, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition, and the 2023 Chapter 24 recommendation on registering foreigners and giving them access to services does not concern employment. The evidence comes from national, international organisation and research sources, which do not raise priority. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0055;MDA-EU-2026-012-0082;MDA-IO-2026-002-0005;MDA-EU-2023-001-0023
- tractability: Medium (validated). A full draft law on admission of foreigners and a draft amendment to the employment law have been prepared and consulted, and the employment agency already supports refugees with partner organisations. Against that, the barriers named by UNHCR (language, recognition of diplomas, childcare) need several ministries and funding, and the draft law would apply only from June 2027. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-006-0032;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0017;MDA-GOV-2026-004-0007;MDA-GOV-2026-004-0019;MDA-IO-2026-002-0003

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0058 Alignment of the temporary protection regime with Directive 2001/55/EC and Council Implementing Decision (EU) 2025/1460: proposed. The Government states that its temporary protection framework is aligned with Directive 2001/55/EC and that it will align with Decision (EU) 2025/1460 extending protection until 1 March 2027. A search of all the country's items found no record of the extension being adopted, and both statements are the Government's own and machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0079 ANOFM ChatBot on the angajat.md platform: implemented. The employment agency reports that it launched a ChatBot on angajat.md answering frequent questions on registration, vacancies, training and conditions for persons with temporary protection. This is the agency's own account, and no source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0080 ANOFM cooperation agreements with partner organisations on refugee employment: adopted. The National Employment Agency signed cooperation agreements on the labour market inclusion of refugees and vulnerable groups with several organisations between 2023 and 2025. The items record only the signing, from the Agency's and the Migration Inspectorate's own machine-translated reports, and not what the agreements delivered.
- INT-MDA-0082 ANOFM employment support for displaced persons and refugees from Ukraine: implemented. The employment agency reports that it continued to support displaced persons from Ukraine in 2024 and 2025, including visits to placement centres, and that 450 displaced jobseekers registered and 164 found work from January to September 2025. These are the agency's own figures, not corroborated by an independent or Commission source.
- INT-MDA-0083 ANOFM employment, counselling and career guidance services: implemented. The National Employment Agency provides employment, counselling and career guidance services, and Government figures show rising referrals for counselling. UNHCR finds that formal employment services such as the Agency remain underused, so the services operate but not at scale for the groups it examined.
- INT-MDA-0160 Cooperation agreement between the General Inspectorate for Migration and the State Labour Inspectorate No. 5/284 of 20.08.2024: adopted. A Government document records the cooperation agreement No. 5/284 of 20 August 2024 between the Migration Inspectorate and the State Labour Inspectorate. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report on cooperation under it.
- INT-MDA-0205 Disposition No. 4 of 1 March 2022 of the Commission for Exceptional Situations (employment of Ukrainian citizens): adopted. Disposition No. 4 of 1 March 2022 of the Commission for Exceptional Situations allowed Ukrainian citizens to be employed on fixed-term contracts during the state of emergency, with notification of the employment agency within five days. No source states whether it remains in force.
- INT-MDA-0239 Draft Government Decision on the list of states whose citizens may work without prior right of residence for work: proposed. The General Inspectorate for Migration reports that the draft decision listing the states whose citizens may work without a prior right of residence was prepared and sent for promotion. No source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0247 Draft Law amending Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance (single no. 180/MMPS/2026): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 105/2018 is a draft in consultation, with ministries still proposing changes to its definitions and entry-into-force clause. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0251 Draft law amending Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners (unique number 94/ME/2023): proposed. The General Inspectorate for Migration reports the draft among the normative drafts it gave opinions on in 2023. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0273 Draft Law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova (single no. 507/MAI/2025): proposed. The text is a draft law still under inter-institutional consultation, with comments being accepted and the quota provisions removed. It provides that it would enter into force on 1 June 2027 and repeal Law No. 200/2010, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0303 Electronic employer notification platform: proposed. The draft law on foreigners of February 2026 provides for employers to notify the employment of foreigners through an electronic platform, and consultation comments ask for it to be integrated with the interoperability platform. No source shows the platform in place.
- INT-MDA-0318 Fast-track diploma recognition programmes: insufficient_evidence. The only item is an international partner's recommendation to introduce fast-track diploma recognition, and no source reports any such programme.
- INT-MDA-0338 Government Decision No. 115/2018 approving the list of priority occupations: adopted. Research reports that Government Decision No. 115/2018 on the list of priority occupations, together with Law No. 200/2010, governs the right of residence for work and gives facilities to foreign workers in listed occupations. No source describes its application.
- INT-MDA-0341 Government Decision No. 21/2023 on granting temporary protection to displaced persons from Ukraine: in_practice. The Decision grants temporary protection with the right to work, and the employment agency reports 1,598 notifications of employment of displaced Ukrainians under it. The ETF policy brief confirms that refugees with temporary protection work in practice, although often below their skills.
- INT-MDA-0387 Improvement of the regulatory framework for foreign IT specialists: proposed. The only item reports a working meeting with the ICT companies' association on improving the rules for foreign IT specialists, and no source reports any change adopted.
- INT-MDA-0415 Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance: implemented. The Law is in force and sets the rules for access to employment services and measures, which the territorial employment offices apply. Government documents note its gaps, including the exclusion of foreigners with residence for work or study and no rules for EU jobseekers or frontier workers, which a draft amending law addresses; these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0420 Law No. 1216/1992 on state fees: adopted. A 2025 doctoral thesis cites Law No. 1216/1992 on state fees, as amended by Law No. 452 of 28 December 2023, as setting the fee for the right to work. No source reports how it is applied.
- INT-MDA-0428 Law No. 180/2008 on labour migration: reversed. A 2025 doctoral thesis states that Law No. 180/2008 has been repealed and most of its provisions moved into Law No. 200/2010. The same thesis elsewhere describes it as the main law on the employment procedure, so the source is inconsistent on whether it is still in force.
- INT-MDA-0432 Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova: in_practice. The Law has been in force since 2010 and was amended in 2023 to let EU citizens work for 90 days in any 180 without a permit; otherwise EU and other foreign citizens need a temporary residence permit for work under it. The Commission records 10,290 residence permits granted in 2025, and a draft law on the admission and stay of foreigners would repeal it on entry into force. The Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0434 Law No. 203/2023 amending Art. 43^1 of Law No. 200/2010: adopted. The Migration Inspectorate reports that Law No. 203/2023 amended Article 43^1 of the law on foreigners and that a draft decision listing the states whose citizens may work without a prior permit followed. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report application.
- INT-MDA-0438 Law No. 274/2011 on the integration of foreigners: adopted. Law No. 274/2011 is in force and is described as one of the basic acts governing the employment of foreigners. No source in these items reports how it is applied.
- INT-MDA-0444 Law No. 452/2023 (state fee for the right to work paid by the employer): adopted. A doctoral thesis describes the state fee for the right to work, paid by the employer, as set by Law No. 452/2023. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report application.
- INT-MDA-0466 Legal work permits for refugees: implemented. UNHCR recommends that Moldova continue providing legal work permits to refugees, which indicates that they are being provided. No source gives the number issued.
- INT-MDA-0511 Monthly monitoring of employer notifications and vacancies for citizens of Ukraine: implemented. The employment agency reports that it monitored employers' notifications of employing Ukrainian citizens and vacancies for them each month. This is the agency's own account.
- INT-MDA-0571 One-Stop Shop for the documentation of foreigners (Government Decision No. 1187/2010): implemented. The One-Stop Shop for documenting foreigners was set up under Government Decision No. 1187/2010 and is retained in the new rules on admitting foreigners, which allow applications there or electronically. Research finds that employers recruiting foreign workers must still gather several documents, so the bureaucratic procedure is not avoided, and no source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0588 Platform for referring refugees and vulnerable persons to labour market opportunities (with Helvetas): proposed. The National Employment Agency reported the referral platform as under development and planned to be functional in the second half of 2025. No later source reports it launched.
- INT-MDA-0595 Programme on managing migration flows, asylum and integration of foreigners 2022-2025 (Government Decision No. 808/2022): adopted. The programme was approved by Government Decision No. 808 of 23 November 2022 for 2022 to 2025. No source reports on how far its digitalisation priorities were carried out before it ended.
- INT-MDA-0596 Proposals to amend Law No. 200/2010, Law No. 274/2011 and State Fee Law No. 1216/1992: proposed. A working group drafted proposals in 2024 to amend the laws on foreigners, on their integration and on state fees to stimulate the labour market. No source shows them adopted.
- INT-MDA-0652 State Labour Inspectorate inspections (2024): implemented. The Government reports that the State Labour Inspectorate carried out 2,599 inspections in 2024, 17 of them on labour migration. The figures are the Government's own and machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0695 Working group at the State Tax Service with IGM, CNAM, CNAS and ASP: implemented. The Government reports a meeting of the working group at the State Tax Service with migration, health insurance, social insurance and public services bodies to stop several personal identification numbers being assigned to one foreigner. No source reports whether the problem was resolved.
