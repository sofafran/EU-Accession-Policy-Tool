# Fact pack RI-TEC-002: Digital transformation strategy and governance weak

There is no adopted long-term strategy for digital transformation aligned with EU targets, or the ministry or agency responsible lacks the capacity, staff and inter-institutional coordination to lead and monitor it.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 key recommendation asked Moldova to adopt the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030 and ensure capacity to implement it; the strategy was adopted in September 2023 and a National Council for Digital Transformation set up in November 2023, as the 2024 report records. The 2025 report notes an implementation programme for 2025 to 2027 and tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the 2026 report again records progress on them. The Chapter 10 rating rose from some level of preparation in 2023 to 2025 to moderately prepared in 2026, but it rates the whole chapter and was not decisive. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0076;MDA-EU-2023-001-0088;MDA-EU-2024-001-0073;MDA-EU-2025-001-0081;MDA-EU-2025-001-0080;MDA-EU-2026-012-0104;MDA-EU-2023-001-0066;MDA-EU-2024-001-0022;MDA-EU-2025-001-0025;MDA-EU-2026-012-0016
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The strategy, the council and an implementation programme are in place, but the Commission repeats in 2024, 2025 and 2026 its key recommendation to ensure institutional capacity to implement the strategy. The Government reports 225 million lei of the programme's cost as uncovered, the ministry and the e-Governance Agency responsible for 53 and 37 of its 68 actions, and a lack of centralised coordination, and a 2024 follow-up audit by the Court of Accounts found its recommendation on documenting information systems only partly implemented, citing insufficient institutional capacity. What remains is the staff, funding and coordination capacity in the ministry and the e-Governance Agency to deliver and monitor the strategy. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0027;MDA-EU-2025-001-0030;MDA-EU-2026-012-0022;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0172;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0281;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0103;MDA-GOV-2024-001-0035;MDA-GOV-2024-001-0106;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0169
- priority: High (validated). A key recommendation on adopting the strategy and ensuring the institutional capacity to implement it appears in the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports, and strategy and governance are central to the digital transformation theme. The later recommendations name data protection and cybersecurity as the main areas for that capacity, which are covered by other issues. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0076;MDA-EU-2024-001-0027;MDA-EU-2025-001-0030;MDA-EU-2026-012-0022
- tractability: Medium (validated). The Government adopted the strategy, set up the council and approved the implementation programme within two years, and the Commission records progress on its indicators. Capacity is the harder part: the Government and the Court of Accounts report staff shortages, low pay for IT staff, staff turnover after reorganisations and reliance on external partners for funding. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0073;MDA-EU-2025-001-0081;MDA-EU-2026-012-0104;MDA-GOV-2023-001-0020;MDA-GOV-2024-001-0018;MDA-GOV-2024-004-0006;MDA-GOV-2024-004-0044

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0006 Digital Moldova page (MEDD interactive reporting): implemented. An international partner describes the Digital Moldova page as a tool for monitoring the progress of digitalisation. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0008 EVO: implemented. The Commission confirms that the EVO application was launched in June 2024 and EVO 2.0 in June 2026, and that the EVO portal lists 636 public services, 447 of them online. Figures on use (169,692 downloads and 66,733 active users by the end of 2025) come only from the Government's own machine-translated progress report, so use at scale is not independently corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0009 EVOSign: implemented. The Commission reports that EVOSign was launched, and the e-Governance Agency says it has run in pilot mode in the production environment since 8 August 2024 and is available in the app stores. Other public bodies consulted on the 2025-2027 programme state that it is only at the pilot and testing stage, has not been launched and lacks the confirmations needed for use, so the sources conflict on whether it is in operation; it is also planned to be absorbed into the digital wallet.
- INT-MDA-0016 MDED interactive platform on public services for entrepreneurs: implemented. Civil society monitoring reports that the ministry developed an interactive platform showing how many public services for entrepreneurs are digitalised and how much they are used. No later source describes it.
- INT-MDA-0046 Advisory Council to the Prime Minister for the Digitization of the Economy (iCouncil): implemented. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet reports that the Ministry leads digitalisation of the economy alongside the Advisory Council to the Prime Minister for the Digitization of the Economy. The linked item does not describe the Council's work.
- INT-MDA-0047 Agency for Cyber Security (establishment and operationalisation): implemented. The Agency was established by Government Decision No. 1028/2023 in December 2023 and launched in February 2024, and the Commission reports in 2026 that it occupies its offices with equipment installed and has been further strengthened. It was still recruiting in 2025, with 20 to 24 of about 45 posts filled, and the Commission finds that further efforts on operational capacity are needed, so there is no evidence yet of full operation at scale.
- INT-MDA-0164 Coordination by AGE of concepts of state information systems: adopted. According to the e-Governance Agency, the rules in force require authorities to submit their information system concepts to it for coordination, but the problems found arise from deficiencies in applying them. The status rests on one machine-translated audit item, capped at Medium confidence.
- INT-MDA-0165 Coordination of digital transformation projects with external partners through the NDTC: proposed. The ministry planned in 2024 to coordinate and prioritise digital projects with external partners through the National Digital Transformation Council and its working groups. No source describes this coordination taking place.
- INT-MDA-0172 Cyber Security Coordinating Council: adopted. The Government set the organisation and operation of the Cybersecurity Coordinating Council by decision, and civil society monitoring describes it as tasked with coordinating cybersecurity policy and made up only of state institutions. No source describes its meetings or work.
- INT-MDA-0194 Digital Society Development Programme: unclear. The Digital Society Development Programme was planned as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy, and an international partner reported in 2024 that the Ministry was developing them. The Government later reports that the three programmes were merged into a single programme, the Implementation Programme 2025-2027, so this programme was not adopted on its own.
- INT-MDA-0195 Digital transformation concept and common strategic vision for digitalisation and e-Government: unclear. The Commission reported in 2022 that a common strategic vision for digitalisation and e-Government had been developed, and in 2023 that the Government was preparing a digital transformation concept as the basis for a strategy for 2023 to 2030. No source states whether the concept itself was approved.
- INT-MDA-0196 Digital Transformation Coordinators in each ministry and central authority: proposed. The 2023 strategy provides for each ministry and central authority to appoint a Digital Transformation Coordinator, and no source reports the appointments.
- INT-MDA-0197 Digital Transformation Strategy annual implementation report: implemented. An annual implementation report on the Digital Transformation Strategy exists, and the Government notes that its baselines differ from those in the implementation programme.
- INT-MDA-0221 Draft Government Decision approving the 2028-2030 Programme for implementing the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: proposed. A draft Government decision approving the 2028-2030 implementation programme for the Digital Transformation Strategy appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0226 Draft Government Decision modernising the rules applicable to certain shared information systems (amending Government Decision No. 544/2019): proposed. The Court of Accounts reports that in 2024 the e-Governance Agency drafted, and was promoting through the Ministry, a draft decision amending Government Decision No. 544/2019 that was being re-endorsed. The linked item is machine-translated and records no adoption.
- INT-MDA-0296 Economic Council under the Prime Minister: proposed. The 2023 Digital Transformation Strategy proposes a governance and coordination structure under the Ministry. The linked item describes the proposal only.
- INT-MDA-0310 Energy Vulnerability Information System (SIVE): implemented. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes SIVE as an online platform for registering and processing requests for energy compensation, and the Court of Accounts examined how it was procured. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0331 Government Action Plan 2021-2022: adopted. The Commission reported in 2022 that the Government's Action Plan for 2021-2022 contains objectives and actions on digital public services. The plan has expired, and no source reports how far it was carried out.
- INT-MDA-0332 Government Activity Programme "EU, PEACE, DEVELOPMENT" (Parliament Decision No. 269/2025): adopted. The Government Activity Programme 'EU, PEACE, DEVELOPMENT' was approved by Parliament Decision No. 269/2025 and includes a priority on integrating science, technology and data into governance.
- INT-MDA-0352 Government Decision No. 544/2019 approving the Methodology for coordinating ICT procurement: in_practice. The Decision was adopted in November 2019 and applied from 2020, and SIGMA confirms that the e-Governance Agency coordinates ICT procurement plans under it. The Court of Accounts found that the Agency issued 689 opinions on ICT procurement procedures, but also found gaps in application, including bodies outside its scope and donated systems; these audit findings are machine-translated and so of Medium confidence.
- INT-MDA-0364 Government Decision No. 822/2020 (actions after the inventory of state information resources and systems): adopted. Government Decision No. 822/2020 approved the actions following the inventory of state information resources and systems and the regulation on their technical administration. The Court of Accounts found that registration of all systems by 2023 had not been achieved.
- INT-MDA-0380 ICT Sector and Digital Economy Development Programme: proposed. The ICT Sector and Digital Economy Development Programme is one of three programmes planned under the Digital Transformation Strategy, and the ministry was still developing these programmes in 2024. No source shows it approved.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0390 Indicator passport for MDTS result indicators: proposed. The 2024 implementation report plans to develop an indicator passport for the strategy's result indicators, and no source reports it done.
- INT-MDA-0404 Internet voting system e-Votare: proposed. An audit reports that the Central Electoral Commission planned and procured the development and piloting of an internet voting system in 2023 and 2024, and no source reports it launched. The status rests on one machine-translated item, capped at Medium confidence.
- INT-MDA-0445 Law No. 467/2003 on informatisation and state information resources: implemented. The law is in force and is the basis for state information resources and services such as EVOSign. An audit found that the e-Governance Agency had not drafted the methodological frameworks the law requires, so its application is incomplete.
- INT-MDA-0469 List of medium-term public policy priorities 2027-2029: insufficient_evidence. The only item refers to the list of medium-term policy priorities for 2027 to 2029 and its AI priority but does not say whether the list has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0471 Long-term plan for the digital economy 2025-2030: proposed. An international partner reported in 2024 that the ministry was working on a long-term plan for the digital economy for 2025 to 2030, and no source reports it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0477 MDED Directorate for policies on information technology and digitalisation: implemented. The Government reports that the Directorate for policies on information technology and digitalisation exists within the Ministry, with nine of its ten posts filled and only a few staff per policy area. The status rests on the Government's own machine-translated account.
- INT-MDA-0480 Mechanisms for identifying and reporting bottlenecks in MDTS implementation: proposed. The Government's 2023 strategy implementation report states that mechanisms for identifying and reporting bottlenecks will be detailed. No source shows them in place.
- INT-MDA-0489 Methodology for the digitalisation of public services: proposed. The Court of Accounts found in 2024 that two tenders to draft the methodology produced no winner and the activity was dropped from the World Bank project. The Government's implementation programme plans it again, and its 2025 progress report says completion is pushed to the fourth quarter of 2026; these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0523 National Council for Digitalisation (MEDD Order No. 165/2023): implemented. The National Council for Digitalisation, set up by ministry Order No. 165/2023, discussed and validated the digital transformation implementation programme. It appears to be the same body as the National Digital Transformation Council (INT-MDA-0532).
- INT-MDA-0530 National Development Strategy "European Moldova 2030": adopted. The National Development Strategy 'European Moldova 2030' was approved by Law No. 315/2022, and the Commission notes its objective of universal access to the internet and electronic services. No source assesses progress against that objective.
- INT-MDA-0532 National Digital Transformation Council: implemented. The Commission confirms that the National Council for Digital Transformation was established in November 2023 to coordinate the Digital Transformation Strategy, and it held its first meeting on 11 December 2023. The Commission also reports a sub-council on AI and data set up within it, but no source assesses how well the Council coordinates implementation.
- INT-MDA-0543 National Plan "Building a European Moldova" (2023): adopted. An international partner reports that the Government adopted the National Plan "Building a European Moldova" in 2023, with digitalisation as its fifth priority. No source reports on its delivery.
- INT-MDA-0553 National Strategy for Information Society Development "Digital Moldova 2020": implemented. The strategy was carried out and has expired. The Commission finds its implementation only partially successful, with 6 of 12 targets met and weak results on online public services and their take-up, and the Government's own final report also records failures from underfunding and limited capacity.
- INT-MDA-0566 New principal consultant posts (AI, semiconductors, trust services) in the MDED IT and digitalisation policy directorate: proposed. The new posts appear only as planned measures in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports that they were created or filled.
- INT-MDA-0571 One-Stop Shop for the documentation of foreigners (Government Decision No. 1187/2010): implemented. The One-Stop Shop for documenting foreigners was set up under Government Decision No. 1187/2010 and is retained in the new rules on admitting foreigners, which allow applications there or electronically. Research finds that employers recruiting foreign workers must still gather several documents, so the bureaucratic procedure is not avoided, and no source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0589 Post of Deputy Prime Minister for Digitalisation (August 2021): implemented. The Commission reports that Moldova created the post of Deputy Prime Minister for Digitalisation in August 2021. No later source in these items describes the post.
- INT-MDA-0590 Practical guide for implementing the Methodology for coordinating ICT procurement (AGE Director Order No. 3005-59 of 26 July 2022): adopted. The Court of Accounts reports that the e-Governance Agency approved the practical guide for the ICT procurement methodology by order of 26 July 2022. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report on its use.
- INT-MDA-0597 Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030: adopted. An international partner reports that the Government approved the strategy in 2023, and the Commission describes its priorities. These items give no evidence on how far its digitalisation measures have been carried out.
- INT-MDA-0600 Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030: proposed. The ministry was developing the Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030 in 2024 as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy. No source shows that it has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0615 Register of State Information Resources and Systems (SI RRSIS): implemented. The register was launched on 26 April 2023 and 67 authorities had registered 88 systems and 6 resources by the time of the Court of Accounts audit. The sources conflict on its coverage: SIGMA states that all state information resources and systems have been registered, while the Court of Accounts finds registration delayed, with 63% of registered systems not properly documented and some entries out of date.
- INT-MDA-0621 Regulation on technical administration and maintenance of state information resources and systems (Government Decision No. 414/2018): adopted. Government Decision No. 822 of 2020 added the regulation on technical administration and maintenance of state information systems to Government Decision No. 414/2018. No source reports how the regulation is applied.
- INT-MDA-0640 Shared digital units (EGA, ITCSS, PSA, ALRC): proposed. The 2023 strategy assigns the shared digital units a role in supporting its implementation, and no source reports how they have carried out that role.
- INT-MDA-0648 State Automated Information System Elections (SIASA): implemented. The Government reports that the elections information system has 16 modules, the newest launched in 2025. An audit found its concept had not been updated or aligned with the ICT rules, and no source gives data on its use; the audit item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0662 State Secretary for digitalisation (MDED): implemented. The Government reports that the State Secretary for digitalisation sets objectives and coordinates the Ministry's directorate for information technology and digitalisation. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0663 Strategic Delivery Unit within the e-Governance Agency: proposed. The Strategic Delivery Unit is set out as a planned structure in the 2023 strategy and again in the 2024 action plan, and no source reports that it was established.
- INT-MDA-0668 Sub-council for Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence: implemented. The Commission confirms that a sub-council on AI and data was set up within the National Digitalisation Council, and the Government reports that it was created in 2024 and contributed to the White Book on AI and Data Governance and to drafting the national AI programme. The body is set up and working, but there is no independent evidence of its influence.

## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0553 leads to MDA-EU-2023-001-0085: success mixed, attribution Medium. The Commission finds the strategy only partially successful, with 6 of 12 targets met, and attributes the shortfall to limited funding and weak project management and implementation capacity. The Government's final report likewise finds e-government objectives not fully achieved despite developments.
- INT-MDA-0352 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-001-0143: success mixed, attribution High. The Court of Accounts, an external auditor, finds that the agency's procurement opinions created conditions to secure the State's rights over systems, but rates the related recommendation only partly implemented. It also finds the methodology unamended from 2021 to August 2024, the agency not always involved in procurement and spending still not traceable.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MDA-CSO-2023-001-0041 vs MDA-GOV-2023-001-0118: IPRE says the Strategy sets 75% of public services digitised by the end of 2024, while the Strategy's indicator table gives targets of 80% and 100%.
- MDA-GOV-2024-001-0088 vs MDA-IO-2023-001-0050: SIGMA states that all state information resources and systems have been registered in the Register, while the Court of Accounts finds that registration of all systems in the Register is delayed.
