# Fact pack RI-TEC-017: Personal data protection law not aligned with EU rules

The personal data protection framework is not fully aligned with the General Data Protection Regulation, the Law Enforcement Directive and the ePrivacy Directive, rules on international transfers are missing, and sector laws are not harmonised.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). In 2021 the draft data protection law had been awaiting second reading since 2018, and in 2023 the Commission found the law only to some extent aligned with the GDPR and the Law Enforcement Directive. The 2024 report recorded adoption of the GDPR law, the 2025 report its entry into force due in August 2026, and the 2026 report its entry into force, the adoption in August 2026 of a law aligning with the Law Enforcement Directive and ratification of the second protocol to Convention 108; the improvement is legal only. The Chapter 10 rating was not decisive, as the issue spans several chapters. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0005;MDA-EU-2023-001-0016;MDA-EU-2024-001-0044;MDA-EU-2025-001-0052;MDA-EU-2026-012-0052;MDA-EU-2026-012-0054;MDA-EU-2026-012-0055
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The main laws the Commission has called for since 2023 are now adopted, but the 2026 report says Moldova should still implement EU standards on contractual clauses between operators and processors, and the 2026 key recommendation still asks for further alignment with EU data protection standards. The order on standard contractual clauses exists only as a draft, and beyond the 2024 and 2026 laws no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files reports the review of related national laws that the 2023 report described as pending since 2020 and the World Bank recommended in 2024. What remains is the rules on standard contractual clauses and the alignment of related national laws with the new data protection laws. - priority: High (validated). Alignment of data protection legislation is named in the Chapter 10 key recommendations of the 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports, after the 2023 report called for the data protection law to be amended, and it is central to the data theme. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no Reform Agenda payment condition on it. - tractability: High (validated). Moldova adopted the GDPR law in 2024 and, in 2026, the law enforcement data protection law and the ratification of the Convention 108 protocol, which is strong evidence of past progress. What is left is mainly an order on contractual clauses, already drafted, and the alignment of related national laws with the new data protection laws, both within the government's control. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0004 Code of Contraventions: adopted. The Code of Contraventions sets liability for processing personal data in breach of data protection law and for failing to comply with the data protection authority's requirements, and remains part of the framework until Law No. 195/2024 applies. No source describes sanctions imposed under these articles.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0014 MCloud: implemented. The Commission counts MCloud among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and RUSI notes that e-government services have adopted cloud solutions such as MCloud. The Court of Accounts found information systems developed without alignment with MCloud and other government platforms, and none of the linked items measures independently how far the platform is used.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0191 Digital medical leave certificate with QR code: proposed. A QR code on the digital medical leave certificate appears in a draft act, and the data protection authority asked for its access, data and security rules to be regulated explicitly. No source shows it introduced.
- INT-MDA-0206 Draft agreement between Moldova and India on migration and mobility: proposed. A draft agreement between Moldova and India on migration and mobility was under review, with the data protection authority asking for data protection provisions to be added. No source shows it signed.
- INT-MDA-0263 Draft law on data and data governance (Regulation (EU) 2022/868, Data Governance Act): proposed. The draft law transposing the Data Governance Act was prepared and sent for interinstitutional examination in October 2025, and the data protection authority commented on it in 2026. The Government's 2025 progress report records the action as suspended until 2027 under the National Accession Programme; the draft was not withdrawn, so it remains a proposal.
- INT-MDA-0270 Draft law on personal data protection: insufficient_evidence. Moldova later adopted Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection, which is recorded as a separate intervention. No source says whether the 2018 draft became that law or was withdrawn, so its status at the freeze date cannot be established.
- INT-MDA-0273 Draft Law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova (single no. 507/MAI/2025): proposed. The text is a draft law still under inter-institutional consultation, with comments being accepted and the quota provisions removed. It provides that it would enter into force on 1 June 2027 and repeal Law No. 200/2010, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0274 Draft law on the protection of personal data processed for the prevention and investigation of crime (Directive (EU) 2016/680): adopted. The Commission reports that Moldova adopted the law on the protection of personal data processed for crime prevention in August 2026, aligning with the Law Enforcement Directive. This rests on a single Commission statement, and no source yet describes its application.
- INT-MDA-0320 Fiscal Code: insufficient_evidence. The only item is the data protection authority's recommendation to amend fiscal legislation on the use of personal identification numbers on receipts, which gives no evidence on the status of any change.
- INT-MDA-0377 Guidelines for the correct application of Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection: unclear. The National Centre for Personal Data Protection reports that in 2025 it focused on drafting guidelines on applying Law No. 195/2024, but does not say whether they were issued.
- INT-MDA-0394 Information system for recording and managing human resources in public authorities (SI EMRU): proposed. The new SI EMRU is planned under the public administration reform programme to be functional in 2026, and in 2026 the data protection authority was still commenting on its design. The Commission found the previous system not functional, and no source reports the new system in operation.
- INT-MDA-0424 Law No. 133/2011 on personal data protection: reversed. The Law was repealed when Law No. 195/2024, which transposes the GDPR, entered into force on 23 August 2026, so it was replaced by newer law rather than abandoned as a policy. The data protection authority notes that its repeal leaves no special law transposing the Law Enforcement Directive.
- INT-MDA-0429 Law No. 182/2008 on the National Centre for Personal Data Protection: unclear. The data protection authority operates under this Law and had 36 staff at the end of 2025, nine short of its staffing limit. The Government lists the Law among the acts making up the framework until the new data protection law entered into force in August 2026, and the linked items do not say whether it remains in force since then.
- INT-MDA-0430 Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection: adopted. Parliament adopted Law No. 195/2024 on 25 July 2024 to align with the GDPR, and it entered into force on 23 August 2026, as the Commission confirms. The sources describe preparation for its application, such as guidelines from the data protection authority, but none yet shows it being applied.
- INT-MDA-0459 Law on personal data protection (number not stated): implemented. A civil society report states that personal data is protected under a dedicated law and that the National Centre for Personal Data Protection monitors compliance. The source does not give the law's number, so it may refer to Law No. 133/2011, which remains in force until Law No. 195/2024 takes effect.
- INT-MDA-0468 Legislation to align with the General Data Protection Regulation and Directive (EU) 2016/680: adopted. The Commission reports that the law aligning with the General Data Protection Regulation, adopted in 2024, entered into force in 2026, and that a law aligning with the Law Enforcement Directive was adopted in August 2026. No source yet describes either law being applied.
- INT-MDA-0497 MLog governmental logging service: implemented. The data protection authority states that people can consult the history of access to their personal data, drawn from MLog, through a government portal, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes MLog as a logbook of actions in government systems. The linked items do not measure how widely it is used.
- INT-MDA-0608 Ratification of the Protocol amending Convention 108 (CETS No. 223): unclear. Moldova signed the Protocol amending Convention 108 in February 2023, and in early 2026 the draft ratification law was in interministerial consultation, with ratification planned to coincide with the entry into force of Law No. 195/2024 in August 2026. The Commission reports ratification of the second Protocol of Convention 108 in May 2026, recorded under INT-MDA-0609, but no source says whether that is this Protocol, so the analyst should decide whether the two entities should be merged.
- INT-MDA-0609 Ratification of the Second Additional Protocol to Council of Europe Convention 108: adopted. The Commission reports that Moldova ratified the second Protocol of Council of Europe Convention 108 in May 2026. The sources do not make clear whether this is the same instrument as the amending Protocol CETS No. 223 recorded separately (INT-MDA-0608).
- INT-MDA-0622 Regulatory framework on access to personal data and consent management (digital mechanism): proposed. A digital mechanism for access to personal data and consent management appears only as a planned action.
- INT-MDA-0632 Revision of national laws to harmonise with the personal data protection law: proposed. The Commission reported in 2023 that harmonisation of national laws with the personal data protection law, pending since 2020, was still under way, with laws being revised before submission to Parliament. No later source reports completion.
- INT-MDA-0647 Standard contractual clauses between controllers and processors: proposed. A draft order on standard contractual clauses is listed in the National Accession Programme. The Commission asked in 2025 for the legal framework to be adopted, and the data protection authority repeated that call in its 2025 activity report, so it had not been adopted by then.
- INT-MDA-0665 Strengthening the administrative capacity of the National Centre for Personal Data Protection: proposed. Strengthening the Centre's capacity is set out as a planned measure. At the end of 2025 it had 36 staff against a limit of 45, and no source reports the strengthening carried out.
- INT-MDA-0693 White Book on Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence: unclear. The ministry presented and published the White Paper in February 2024, but the Government itself describes it as declarative and not a policy document as such, and no source shows formal approval. The Commission reports that an AI and data sub-council applies its principles, and its recommendations were to be taken forward in a later national programme.
