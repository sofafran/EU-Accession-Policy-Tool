# Fact pack RI-TEC-022: Cybersecurity legal and strategic framework incomplete

Network and information security law is not fully aligned with the NIS2 Directive and the Cybersecurity Act, the national cybersecurity strategy is late or fragmented in implementation, and responsibilities are split without effective coordination.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 10 scale is context only. The 2023 report recorded adoption of the Law on cybersecurity but said implementation had to start, the 2024 and 2025 reports recorded implementing Government Decisions aimed at NIS 2 alignment, and the 2026 report records further secondary legislation and the National Cybersecurity Programme 2026-2030 adopted in December 2025, replacing the strategy the Reform Agenda had described as lapsed. The improvement is mainly legal and strategic, as the Reform Agenda still found no holistic framework in 2025. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0071;MDA-EU-2024-001-0083;MDA-EU-2025-001-0091;MDA-EU-2025-002-0026;MDA-EU-2025-002-0068;MDA-EU-2026-012-0106;MDA-EU-2026-012-0125
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The key recommendation of 2024, 2025 and 2026 asks Moldova to align legislation with EU cybersecurity standards. The Government describes Law No. 48/2023 as only partially harmonising with NIS 2, and the amending law for NIS 2, the laws on the Cyber Resilience Act and cybersecurity certification and the related authority designations were still planned in 2025 and 2026. What remains is full transposition of NIS 2 and of the Cyber Resilience Act and certification framework, with the authorities designated to apply them. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0027;MDA-EU-2025-001-0030;MDA-EU-2026-012-0022;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0205;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0275;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0283;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0284;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0304;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0112
- priority: High (validated). Alignment with EU cybersecurity standards is in the key recommendation of the 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports, and NIS 2 alignment with clarified institutional roles is part of the Reform Agenda. The legal framework is central to the cybersecurity theme. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0027;MDA-EU-2025-001-0030;MDA-EU-2026-012-0022;MDA-EU-2025-002-0009;MDA-EU-2025-002-0073
- tractability: Medium (validated). Moldova has adopted the main law and a series of implementing Government Decisions since 2023, which shows legislative capacity. The Cybersecurity Agency reports that missing staff and an incomplete framework limited supervision in 2025, the ministry's cybersecurity unit has four posts with one vacant, and the Reform Agenda expects compliance costs to meet resistance from operators. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0047 Agency for Cyber Security (establishment and operationalisation): implemented. The Agency was established by Government Decision No. 1028/2023 in December 2023 and launched in February 2024, and the Commission reports in 2026 that it occupies its offices with equipment installed and has been further strengthened. It was still recruiting in 2025, with 20 to 24 of about 45 posts filled, and the Commission finds that further efforts on operational capacity are needed, so there is no evidence yet of full operation at scale.
- INT-MDA-0055 Alignment of cybersecurity legislation with the NIS Directive (before Law No. 48/2023): adopted. The Commission reported in 2023 that Moldova continued aligning its cybersecurity legislation with the NIS Directive, and RUSI reports legislation requiring critical infrastructure providers to adopt prescribed security measures. This alignment work is reflected in Cybersecurity Law No. 48/2023, which is now being amended to transpose NIS 2.
- INT-MDA-0063 Amendment of Law No. 467/2003 to align audit requirements for state information systems with Law No. 48/2023: proposed. The amendment to Law No. 467/2003 was planned, but the Government reports that the action will be reconsidered depending on changes to the cybersecurity law. No source reports it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0171 Cyber Security Agency 2025 action plan: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that most actions in its 2025 plan were fully implemented and some partly or carried over, mainly because of resource constraints and pending regulation. The Commission confirms that the Agency became operational in 2025, but the account of the plan's delivery is the Agency's own and is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0172 Cyber Security Coordinating Council: adopted. The Government set the organisation and operation of the Cybersecurity Coordinating Council by decision, and civil society monitoring describes it as tasked with coordinating cybersecurity policy and made up only of state institutions. No source describes its meetings or work.

The Cyber Security Agency reports courses for its own technical staff and thematic courses for CSIRT and public institution staff in 2025, so training has started on a small scale. A comment in the Government's draft implementation programme notes that it contains no specific actions for training civil servants, and all items are the Government's own machine-translated documents.
