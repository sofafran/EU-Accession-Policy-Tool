# Fact pack RI-FMW-012: Supplementary pension rights of mobile workers

National law is not aligned with the EU directives on supplementary pension rights, such as the acquisition and preservation of rights for workers who move between Member States.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since this is only part of the chapter and the only Chapter 2 sub-area wording among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files concerns legal alignment in general (2023) and the European Health Insurance Card (2025). The 2023 report, the 2023 Analytical Report and the 2024 report all find that EU rules on supplementary pension rights of mobile workers are not incorporated into Moldovan law and that there is no occupational pension scheme, and in 2025 the government still describes the occupational system as at an early stage and plans full transposition of Directive 98/49/EC. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0056;MDA-EU-2023-002-0019;MDA-EU-2024-001-0060;MDA-EU-2024-001-0059;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0079;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0080;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0081
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). Sources conflict: the Commission in 2024 says the EU rules are not incorporated into Moldovan law, while the government in 2025 says, on the basis of the financial market regulator's conformity tables, that the law is aligned with Directive 2014/50/EU and partly transposes Directive 98/49/EC. The responses named by the government, full transposition of Directive 98/49/EC and a draft amendment to Law No. 198/2020, are not yet adopted. What remains is incorporating the EU rules on acquiring and preserving supplementary pension rights into Moldovan law in a form the Commission accepts. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0060;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0078;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0081;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0099
- priority: Low (validated). The finding is repeated in the 2023 and 2024 reports, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on supplementary pension rights. Moldova has no occupational pension scheme, so the rules have limited practical reach, and the issue is a small part of the chapter. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0055;MDA-EU-2023-001-0056;MDA-EU-2024-001-0060
- tractability: Medium (validated). The change is legal and lies with the government and the financial market regulator, and a draft amendment to the voluntary pension funds law is identified. Against that, the government and the Commission disagree on how far the law is already aligned, and no adoption date is given for the draft. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0078;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0099;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0081;MDA-EU-2024-001-0060

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0153 Conformity tables of the National Commission for Financial Markets: implemented. The National Commission for Financial Markets has prepared conformity tables, which the Government cites as showing Moldovan law aligned with Directive 2014/50/EU and partly transposing Directive 98/49/EC. The Commission finds that EU rules on supplementary pension rights of mobile workers are not in Moldovan law, so what the tables show is disputed.
- INT-MDA-0250 Draft law amending Law No. 198/2020 on voluntary pension funds: proposed. The amendment to Law No. 198/2020 appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0322 Full transposition of Directive 98/49/EC on supplementary pension rights: proposed. The Government undertakes to fully transpose Directive 98/49/EC on supplementary pension rights. No source shows the transposition made.
- INT-MDA-0431 Law No. 198/2020 on voluntary pension funds: adopted. Law No. 198/2020 on voluntary pension funds was adopted, but the Government states that effective implementation of the occupational pension system remains at an early stage.
