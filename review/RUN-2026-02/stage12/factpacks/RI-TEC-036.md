# Fact pack RI-TEC-036: National e-services portal limited in range and use

The national e-services portal offers too few transactional services, is little used, or does not serve all users.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 10 scale is context only, though its progress wording cites the digitalisation of public services in 2024 and progress on all key performance indicators in 2025. Commission figures are about 40% in both 2023 (business services online) and 2024 (mapped services) and rise from then on, on differing measures: over 50% in 2025 (64% for businesses and 54% for individuals in 2024), and in 2026 70.3% of the 636 services on the EVO portal online, 82% of business and 61% of citizen services digitalised and user uptake up significantly. Government counts of 2023 business service requests conflict between its 2024 and 2025 documents, so they are not used for the trend. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0023;MDA-EU-2025-001-0026;MDA-EU-2023-001-0002;MDA-EU-2024-001-0037;MDA-EU-2025-001-0039;MDA-EU-2025-001-0061;MDA-EU-2026-012-0038;MDA-EU-2026-012-0117;MDA-EU-2026-012-0039;MDA-GOV-2024-004-0013;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0297
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Reform Agenda target of 75% of business services digitalised has been met, and EVO, the public services portal and 118 Unified Public Service Centres are in use; but in 2026 only 61% of citizen services are digitalised, only 133 of 414 business services run fully end to end, the Reform Agenda notes that the portal lacks integration with EU systems, and the link to the Single Digital Gateway is still planned. Demand remains low in rural areas, which is why the Commission says the centres were set up. What remains is a fuller range of citizen services delivered end to end online, integration with EU systems, and use by people with weaker digital access or skills. Items: MDA-EU-2026-004-0001;MDA-EU-2026-004-0002;MDA-EU-2026-012-0038;MDA-EU-2026-012-0040;MDA-EU-2026-012-0117;MDA-EU-2025-002-0064;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0190;MDA-EU-2025-001-0045;MKD-EU-2023-002-0054
- priority: High (validated). Digital public services are the subject of two Reform Agenda payment conditions: the EVO Sign wallet integrating at least 20 e-services by December 2026, which is still open and shared with the electronic identification issue, and 75% of business services digitalised, which the Commission found met in 2026. The Commission's 2024 Chapter 10 progress wording cites notable progress on digitalising public services, and the portal and its services are central to the digital government theme, so the rating rests on the open wallet condition rather than on the fulfilled step. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0035;MDA-EU-2025-002-0006;MDA-EU-2025-002-0015;MDA-EU-2026-004-0001;MDA-EU-2024-001-0023
- tractability: High (validated). Progress has been steady and measured by the Commission in every report from 2023 to 2026, the e-Governance Agency runs the shared platforms, and the work is tied to Reform Agenda funding. The main constraints are cost, which the Government says exceeds its own resources, and low demand among older and rural users. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0002;MDA-EU-2026-012-0117;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0078;MDA-GOV-2024-004-0044;MDA-EU-2025-001-0045;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0011

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0001 Annual National Survey on citizen satisfaction with public services: implemented. The e-Governance Agency reports that it measures citizens' perception and uptake of digital public services each year through the Annual National Survey, with six surveys carried out from 2019 to 2024. This is the agency's own account.
- INT-MDA-0002 Business Government Portal: implemented. The Interoperable Europe factsheet reports that the Business Government Portal was launched in December 2020 as an extension of MCabinet and offers 11 data blocks for businesses. No source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0003 MCabinet (Citizen Portal): implemented. An international partner reports that MCabinet was launched in October 2020 and gives access to 17 data blocks. The Government reports that unique authenticated users rose from 92,619 in 2023 to 176,855 in the first three quarters of 2024, but this rests on its own reporting.
- INT-MDA-0006 Digital Moldova page (MEDD interactive reporting): implemented. An international partner describes the Digital Moldova page as a tool for monitoring the progress of digitalisation. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0008 EVO: implemented. The Commission confirms that the EVO application was launched in June 2024 and EVO 2.0 in June 2026, and that the EVO portal lists 636 public services, 447 of them online. Figures on use (169,692 downloads and 66,733 active users by the end of 2025) come only from the Government's own machine-translated progress report, so use at scale is not independently corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0009 EVOSign: implemented. The Commission reports that EVOSign was launched, and the e-Governance Agency says it has run in pilot mode in the production environment since 8 August 2024 and is available in the app stores. Other public bodies consulted on the 2025-2027 programme state that it is only at the pilot and testing stage, has not been launched and lacks the confirmations needed for use, so the sources conflict on whether it is in operation; it is also planned to be absorbed into the digital wallet.
- INT-MDA-0010 Electronic services platform: implemented. The Commission reports that fifteen online services, including extracts from state registers, and later two car registration services were launched on the electronic services platform. No source gives figures on their use.
- INT-MDA-0011 Public Services Portal (services.gov.md): in_practice. The Commission and SIGMA describe the portal as the working single point of access to public services, and the Government reports over 0.5 million unique visitors a year and user satisfaction in its annual national survey. Material in the Government's own implementation programme says only a minority of listed services can be completed fully online, the Commission notes the portal is not integrated with EU systems, and by 2026 services are listed on the integrated EVO portal.
- INT-MDA-0014 MCloud: implemented. The Commission counts MCloud among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and RUSI notes that e-government services have adopted cloud solutions such as MCloud. The Court of Accounts found information systems developed without alignment with MCloud and other government platforms, and none of the linked items measures independently how far the platform is used.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0017 MDelivery: implemented. The Commission reported in 2023 that a further 42 public services had been integrated with MDelivery, which delivers requested documents to the user's home, and the Government lists it among platforms delivered under the modernisation project. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0019 MPass: implemented. The Commission and the Government list MPass among the government platforms implemented, and an international partner describes it as the authentication service for public e-services. Government consultation material gives 208,500 identifiers authenticated from 2023 to September 2024, but no independent source reports on use.
- INT-MDA-0020 MPay: implemented. The Commission counts MPay among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes it as in place since 2020. SIGMA found in 2023 that online payment through MPay was possible but not yet integrated into digital service provision, and the Government's figures on savings and on payments collected through MPay are not corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0021 MPower: implemented. An international partner reports that MPower was launched in 2020 and lets people create electronic powers of attorney with the same value as notarised ones, and the Government lists it among its working platforms. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0022 MSign: in_practice. SIGMA reported in 2023 that electronic signatures are available through MSign with about 200,000 active users, mostly businesses required to sign documents for the tax service. Government figures give more than 37 million documents signed through MSign in 2023 and more than 38 million in the first nine months of 2024.
- INT-MDA-0023 MobiSign: insufficient_evidence. A search of all the country's items found no mention of MobiSign after 2023, so no source says whether it was launched, renamed or dropped.
- INT-MDA-0025 Modernization of Government Services project (MGSP): implemented. The World Bank-supported project was carried out by the e-Governance Agency and concluded in 2025, with the Government reporting 110 digitalised services and 94 re-engineered services. All figures on its delivery and results come from the Government and the Agency, without independent corroboration.
- INT-MDA-0026 Public services register: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the register was established in 2021 and covers about 718 public services, and the Commission states that it was used to map administrative services and support their re-engineering.
- INT-MDA-0031 Unified Public Service Centres (CUPS): implemented. The Commission confirms that 83 centres were set up in 2023, that the network reached 105 by 2025 and 118 by 2026, giving rural residents access to electronic public services. None of the items on the centres gives the number of people served through them, so use at scale is not shown.
- INT-MDA-0033 e-APL: implemented. The Government reports that the e-APL portal was developed and launched as a pilot, with 91 mayoralties taking part and nine State Fiscal Register services available through it. The target is use by at least half of first-level local authorities, and all items come from the Government's own machine-translated documents, so the status rests on Medium confidence evidence.
- INT-MDA-0036 eTransformation Project: implemented. The e-Governance Agency reports that the projects carried out over 15 years cost USD 46 million and that the savings from MPay alone recovered the investment. This is the agency's own account.
- INT-MDA-0049 Agency plans to centralise access to public services under EVO: implemented. The e-Governance Agency's 2025 plans to centralise access to public services under EVO were followed by Government Decision No. 677/2025 on the integrated EVO portal and its public launch in January 2026. The Commission confirms that the EVO portal brings together 636 public services, 447 of them online.
- INT-MDA-0061 Amendment of Law No. 234/2021 and the Administrative Code (no electronic signature for authenticated users): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 234/2021 and the Administrative Code is still listed as a planned action in the 2025 progress report, and no source reports it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0188 Digital ambassadors: insufficient_evidence. The only item is a civil society recommendation to increase the number of digital ambassadors, which gives no evidence on the programme's status.
- INT-MDA-0204 Digitalisation of transport public services, including vehicle registration: implemented. The Commission reports that two online car registration services were launched on the electronic services platform, and the Government states that digitalisation of transport services, including vehicle registration, continues. No source gives figures on their use.
- INT-MDA-0252 Draft law amending Law No. 234/2021 on public services (Single Digital Gateway): proposed. The draft law amending Law No. 234/2021 to transpose the Single Digital Gateway Regulation was still being drafted in early 2026, with consultation with the Commission planned for mid-2026. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0284 E-government ecosystem for businesses: implemented. The Reform Agenda step for December 2025 required the State Register of Legal Units to be fully operational and the life events methodology approved. The Commission confirms that the register portal was launched on 29 December 2025 and the methodology approved on 18 December 2025, but no source yet shows the register in use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0291 e-services.md portal of the Public Services Agency: in_practice. SIGMA reported in 2023 that e-services.md is in use as the Public Services Agency's portal alongside the general government portal, though limited to that agency's services, and recommended integrating it into the single portal. No later source describes its status.
- INT-MDA-0304 Electronic health record (EHR) system: proposed. The electronic health record is planned, with its concept and regulation due by the end of 2026 and the system for 40% of the insured population by 2027. In 2026 the Government reports only that the draft Government Decision on it is being prepared.
- INT-MDA-0311 English-language access to information systems and services for foreign entrepreneurs and investors (integrated EVO portal): implemented. The Government reports that all information on Public Services Agency services published on the integrated EVO portal is now also available in English and that content is continuously updated. This is the Government's own account and no source gives evidence of use by foreign investors.
- INT-MDA-0329 Generative AI virtual assistant on the Public Services Portal and EVO: proposed. Piloting the generative AI assistant on the EVO portal is planned as a 2026 performance indicator for the e-Governance Agency, with a revised deadline, and no source reports the pilot launched.
- INT-MDA-0331 Government Action Plan 2021-2022: adopted. The Commission reported in 2022 that the Government's Action Plan for 2021-2022 contains objectives and actions on digital public services. The plan has expired, and no source reports how far it was carried out.
- INT-MDA-0334 Government Customer Support Service (SGSC): adopted. Government Decision No. 66/2026 established the Government Customer Support Service at the beginning of 2026, and the Government reports that the service was developed on time, with a pilot launch planned from January 2026. No source confirms that the pilot started or describes its use.
- INT-MDA-0350 Government Decision No. 5/2024 on the integrated governmental application EVO: implemented. Government Decision No. 5/2024 on the EVO application was approved, and the Commission confirms that EVO was launched in June 2024 and EVO 2.0 in June 2026.
- INT-MDA-0359 Government Decision No. 66/2026 on establishing the Government Customer Support Service: adopted. The Government reports that Government Decision No. 66/2026 establishing the Government Customer Support Service was approved at the beginning of 2026. No source reports the service working.
- INT-MDA-0360 Government Decision No. 677/2025 on access to electronic public services through the integrated government portal EVO and the Unitary Design Model: implemented. Government Decision No. 677/2025 on access to electronic public services through the integrated EVO portal was approved on 8 October 2025, and the portal was launched publicly in January 2026. The Commission confirms that the EVO portal brings together 636 public services.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0400 Integration of the Public Services Portal with the EU Single Digital Gateway: proposed. Integrating the Public Services Portal with the EU Single Digital Gateway is planned, and the Government reports only that the public launch of the integrated EVO portal was prepared for January 2026. No source reports the gateway integration.
- INT-MDA-0436 Law No. 234/2021 on public services: adopted. Law No. 234/2021 on public services sets digital by default delivery, once-only data provision and omnichannel delivery, as SIGMA and the Government describe. No source assesses its application in these items.
- INT-MDA-0442 Law No. 321/2023 on stimulating the development and use of electronic services: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 321/2023 set the framework for stimulating electronic services, including the digital identity card, driving licence and registration certificate. The linked items do not report specifically on its application.
- INT-MDA-0472 M-services shared e-government infrastructure: in_practice. The e-Governance Agency operates the shared M-services infrastructure, and the Interoperable Europe factsheet gives these platforms the same status as MConnect, which independent sources find in wide use. For example, SIGMA reports about 200,000 active MSign users.
- INT-MDA-0477 MDED Directorate for policies on information technology and digitalisation: implemented. The Government reports that the Directorate for policies on information technology and digitalisation exists within the Ministry, with nine of its ten posts filled and only a few staff per policy area. The status rests on the Government's own machine-translated account.
- INT-MDA-0489 Methodology for the digitalisation of public services: proposed. The Court of Accounts found in 2024 that two tenders to draft the methodology produced no winner and the activity was dropped from the World Bank project. The Government's implementation programme plans it again, and its 2025 progress report says completion is pushed to the fourth quarter of 2026; these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0490 Methodology on identifying, describing and institutionalising life and business events (State Chancellery Order No. 19 of 30.01.2025): adopted. The State Chancellery reports that it approved the methodology on life and business events by Order No. 19 of 30 January 2025. A later report gives Order No. 331 of 18 December 2025 as the act approving the methodology, so the relation between the two orders is not clear.
- INT-MDA-0491 Methodology on institutionalising life events for citizens and businesses (State Chancellery Order No. 331/2025): adopted. No source yet describes it being applied to services.
- INT-MDA-0497 MLog governmental logging service: implemented. The data protection authority states that people can consult the history of access to their personal data, drawn from MLog, through a government portal, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes MLog as a logbook of actions in government systems. The linked items do not measure how widely it is used.
- INT-MDA-0505 Moldova eGovernment Interoperability Framework (MeGIF) Programme (Government Decision No. 656/2012): implemented. The World Bank reports that Moldova launched the eGovernment Interoperability Framework Programme in 2012 and that a pilot of the MConnect data exchange platform was developed under it between 2014 and 2018. No recent source describes the programme as such, its work now being carried by the interoperability law and platform.
- INT-MDA-0513 Multifunctional Centres of the Public Services Agency: implemented. Civil society monitoring refers to specialists in the Public Services Agency's Multifunctional Centres in larger cities as able to support digital service users. No source describes the centres' work in more detail.
- INT-MDA-0528 National design system for web and mobile government solutions (Unitary Design Model): adopted. - INT-MDA-0529 National Development Plan 2025-2027 (Government Decision No. 361/2024): adopted. - INT-MDA-0530 National Development Strategy "European Moldova 2030": adopted. The wallet aligned with the EU Digital Identity framework is a further version: the Government says development started in September 2025, the Commission reports cross-border interoperability tests in 2026, and the Reform Agenda sets its launch for December 2026.
- INT-MDA-0546 National Programme for Promoting Entrepreneurship and Increasing Competitiveness: unclear. - INT-MDA-0548 National Programme for the Accession of the Republic of Moldova to the European Union 2025-2029 (Government Decision No. 306/2025): implemented. - INT-MDA-0562 New electronic identity card with a qualified electronic signature certificate: in_practice. ## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0553 leads to MDA-EU-2023-001-0086: success failed, attribution Medium. Against its own targets, the strategy failed on services online (12% against 100%) and acceptance (49% against 70%), and the Commission links the shortfall to limited money and capacity. The Commission finds the strategy as a whole only partly successful, with 6 of 12 targets met, and the Government's 2023 Strategy agrees that the e-government objectives were not fully achieved, so this is not government reporting alone.
- INT-MDA-0007 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-004-0003: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Low. The claim of significant progress in 2023 rests on the Government's own report: the Commission gives about 40% of services available online in both its 2023 and 2024 reports, which does not show the rise, and its higher figures concern 2024 and later. The Strategy was also adopted only in September 2023.
- INT-MDA-0031 leads to MDA-EU-2026-012-0039: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Low. The Commission reports a significant rise in uptake, including in rural areas where the centres were set up, but gives no rural figure and does not say the centres caused it, and the centres' own service counts come from Government sources. None of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources measures use of the centres.
- INT-MDA-0025 leads to MDA-GOV-2025-001-0027: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The only evidence is the e-Governance Agency's own anniversary publication: none of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources measures delivery times of the redesigned services, so success is not judged from government reporting alone.
- INT-MDA-0025 leads to MDA-GOV-2025-001-0037: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Low. The doubling of use comes from the e-Governance Agency's own publication, so success is not judged from government reporting alone: none of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources measures use over the project period, and the Commission's figures on rising use do not mention the project.
- INT-MDA-0025 leads to MDA-GOV-2025-001-0038: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. This figure comes from the e-Governance Agency's own publication, which the Agency reports as its own view, and none of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources measures access by vulnerable groups, so success is not judged from government reporting alone.
- INT-MDA-0020 leads to MDA-GOV-2025-001-0040: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The savings claim comes from the e-Governance Agency's own publication, with no method given, and none of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources estimates savings from MPay, so success is not judged from government reporting alone.
- INT-MDA-0025 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-004-0012: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The claim rests on government reporting alone: none of the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources refers to the services digitalised through the project in 2023, and the count is of services digitalised rather than their use.
