# Fact pack RI-TEC-020: Weak compliance with data protection rules

Data controllers, including public bodies, do not apply data protection law in practice, breaches are frequent and not always reported, awareness is low, and sanctions are rare.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since this is a cross-cutting data protection issue. Breaches are confirmed every year: 105 in 2022 in the 2023 report and 173 violations, given by CSO Meter for 2024 and by the authority's 2025 activity report for 2025, which may be one count reported for two different years; the 2025 report records a major health data breach, and the authority reports in 2026 that state bodies still access personal data through the COI technology. Rising complaints are read by CSO Meter as growing awareness rather than worse compliance, so they are not used as a trend. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0020;MDA-EU-2024-001-0046;MDA-CSO-2026-001-0022;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0006;MDA-EU-2025-001-0079;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0029;MDA-CSO-2026-001-0021
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The 2023 requirement to train public servants and raise awareness has been answered by the authority's 2025 training campaign of 84 courses for over 3,600 people, and Law No. 195/2024, adopted and in force from 23 August 2026, lets the authority impose administrative sanctions directly. Sources conflict on data access: the Government says, in a machine-translated source, that data exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect, while the authority finds state bodies still using the COI technology outside it, and only about 50 public authorities had designated a data protection officer. What remains is compliance in practice by public and private controllers, including in health, with the new sanctions applied and access to state registers made traceable. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0021;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0059;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0014;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0016;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0073;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0029;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0020;MDA-EU-2025-001-0024;MDA-CSO-2026-001-0031
- priority: Medium (validated). Data protection compliance is raised in the 2023 recommendation on training and awareness and in the 2025 key recommendation on health, which names the protection of personal data, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found it in no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition, and not across three or more reports. Applying data protection law is central to the data theme, but the volume of authority and civil society material does not raise priority. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0021;MDA-EU-2025-001-0024;MDA-EU-2024-001-0046
- tractability: Medium (validated). The new law gives the authority direct administrative sanctions, ending the duplicated procedures it blames for weak deterrence, and courts upheld its measures in 52 of 58 final cases in 2025. Against that, compliance depends on many public and private controllers, and the authority reports years of unanswered requests to move state bodies off the COI technology. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0003 MCabinet (Citizen Portal): implemented. An international partner reports that MCabinet was launched in October 2020 and gives access to 17 data blocks. The Government reports that unique authenticated users rose from 92,619 in 2023 to 176,855 in the first three quarters of 2024, but this rests on its own reporting.
- INT-MDA-0004 Code of Contraventions: adopted. The Code of Contraventions sets liability for processing personal data in breach of data protection law and for failing to comply with the data protection authority's requirements, and remains part of the framework until Law No. 195/2024 applies. No source describes sanctions imposed under these articles.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0012 Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the law obliges government agencies to exchange data only through the interoperability solution, and that the MConnect platform it governs is well functioning and used by many public and private organisations. The data protection authority finds that some state bodies still access data outside the platform in breach of the law, and the Government notes that replacing documents with data remains problematic in many cases.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0142 COI technology for access to state registers: in_practice. The data protection authority reports that state bodies continue to access personal data in state registers through COI technology, with 13 entities still using it and only 5 having aligned their access over two years. The e-Governance Agency has asked authorities to end these contracts because COI does not meet security and traceability requirements, but no progress was recorded.
- INT-MDA-0377 Guidelines for the correct application of Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection: unclear. The National Centre for Personal Data Protection reports that in 2025 it focused on drafting guidelines on applying Law No. 195/2024, but does not say whether they were issued.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0393 Information System for Monitoring and Evaluating HIV Infection (SIME HIV): insufficient_evidence. The only linked item is a data protection authority recommendation that rules on medical staff identifiers be applied across all medical information systems. It does not describe the HIV monitoring system's status.
- INT-MDA-0424 Law No. 133/2011 on personal data protection: reversed. The Law was repealed when Law No. 195/2024, which transposes the GDPR, entered into force on 23 August 2026, so it was replaced by newer law rather than abandoned as a policy. The data protection authority notes that its repeal leaves no special law transposing the Law Enforcement Directive.
- INT-MDA-0430 Law No. 195/2024 on personal data protection: adopted. Parliament adopted Law No. 195/2024 on 25 July 2024 to align with the GDPR, and it entered into force on 23 August 2026, as the Commission confirms. The sources describe preparation for its application, such as guidelines from the data protection authority, but none yet shows it being applied.
- INT-MDA-0497 MLog governmental logging service: implemented. The data protection authority states that people can consult the history of access to their personal data, drawn from MLog, through a government portal, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes MLog as a logbook of actions in government systems. The linked items do not measure how widely it is used.
- INT-MDA-0538 National Health Strategy 2030: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that implementation of the 2030 national health strategy progressed, with a focus on digital transformation and quality of care. It had earlier asked for the strategy to be implemented effectively, including an integrated e-health system, which is still not in place.
- INT-MDA-0558 NCPDP training and information campaigns on the new personal data protection framework: implemented. The National Centre for Personal Data Protection reports that it organised 84 courses and trained over 3,600 people in the reporting period, ahead of the new law taking effect on 23 August 2026. This rests on the Centre's own report.
- INT-MDA-0641 SIA Acces-Web: in_practice. The data protection authority reports continued access to personal data in state information resources through Acces-Web, with large volumes of access by bodies such as the Ministry of Internal Affairs, and limited traceability of users and purposes.
