# Fact pack RI-TEC-024: Security of 5G networks not ensured

The EU 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox, including supplier risk assessment and restrictions on high-risk suppliers, is not fully transposed or applied when 5G networks are deployed.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Sub-area wording on the 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox, which matches this issue, recorded no progress in 2024. The Commission asked in 2025 for implementation of the Toolbox measures to continue and in 2026 for Moldova to start implementing them, including restrictions on high-risk suppliers, so the core requirement is unchanged across the three reports; the secondary legislation on security of public 5G networks noted in 2026 and the supplier risk study under way are preparatory steps. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0086;MDA-EU-2025-001-0095;MDA-EU-2026-012-0130;MDA-EU-2026-012-0125;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0113
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2026 report asks Moldova to start implementing the Toolbox measures, including restrictions or exclusions for high-risk suppliers, which the Reform Agenda also sets as a step. The Government reports a supplier risk assessment by a consultancy still in progress and a draft Government Decision on 5G network security still being finalised in 2026, with a 5G security law planned by December 2027. What remains is the 5G security law, the completed supplier risk assessment and restrictions on high-risk suppliers applied to critical assets. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0130;MDA-EU-2025-002-0034;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0113;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0114;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0211
- priority: High (validated). Alignment with the 5G Toolbox is part of the key recommendation on the Digital Transformation Strategy priorities in the 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports and is a step of the Reform Agenda under the Reform and Growth Facility, after the sub-area recorded no progress in 2024. It is central to the cybersecurity theme. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0027;MDA-EU-2025-001-0030;MDA-EU-2026-012-0022;MDA-EU-2025-002-0034;MDA-EU-2024-001-0086
- tractability: Medium (validated). Funded preparatory work is under way, with a study funded by Swedfund and a consultancy contract covering supplier risk assessment, an operator compensation mechanism and a draft law. The Government notes that compliance requires major investment in 5G networks, so progress depends on operators bearing, or being compensated for, the cost of restricting high-risk suppliers. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0212;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0213;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0152;MDA-EU-2025-002-0059

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0060 Alignment with the EU 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox: proposed. Alignment with the EU 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox, including a supplier risk assessment and restrictions on high-risk suppliers, appears only as a Reform Agenda commitment with a December 2027 deadline. No source shows the legal framework adopted or the assessment carried out.
- INT-MDA-0159 Consultancy contract between MDED and KPMG AB on the EU 5G security toolbox (vendor risk assessment): implemented. The Ministry signed the consultancy contract with KPMG AB on 15 October 2025, and its 2025 progress report says the study is being carried out by KPMG AB. Both items are the Government's own and machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0232 Draft Government Decision on cybersecurity of 5G networks (Commission Recommendation (EU) 2019/534): proposed. The Government decision on the cybersecurity of 5G networks was still being finalised in 2026 after comments from the ministry, the Cybersecurity Agency and the regulator. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0255 Draft law amending Law No. 48/2023 on cybersecurity (Directive (EU) 2022/2555, NIS 2): proposed. The draft law amending the Cybersecurity Law to transpose NIS 2 is listed in the National Accession Programme, and the Reform Agenda treats its adoption as a future step. The Government's 2025 progress report still refers to the amendments as proposed changes, so the draft has not been adopted.
- INT-MDA-0259 Draft law approving the national cybersecurity certification scheme: proposed. A draft law approving a national cybersecurity certification scheme appears only in the Government's plans for aligning with the EU acquis. No source shows it submitted or adopted.
- INT-MDA-0262 Draft law on cybersecurity of 5G networks: proposed. The law on cybersecurity of 5G networks is planned in the National Accession Programme, with a deadline of December 2027, and no source reports a draft submitted or adopted.
- INT-MDA-0267 Draft law on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements (Cyber Resilience Act): proposed. A draft law on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements is listed in the National Accession Programme, and the Reform Agenda describes these requirements as future measures.
- INT-MDA-0383 Impact and risk analysis study for implementing the EU 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox (Swedfund grant): implemented. A grant agreement with Swedfund, approved by ministerial order in March 2025, funds the impact and risk study for the 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox, and the Government's 2025 progress report says KPMG AB is carrying it out. The status rests only on the Government's own machine-translated documents, and the study is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0508 Moldova's access to the EU Cybersecurity Reserve: implemented. The Commission reports that the Council renewed Moldova's access to the EU Cybersecurity Reserve for a further year in July 2026. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it activated the mechanism successfully, but this rests on its own account.
- INT-MDA-0568 Normative framework implementing the cybersecurity law (Government Decisions): adopted. The Commission reports that a normative framework implementing the cybersecurity law was approved through several Government Decisions to align with the NIS 2 Directive, that the framework was amended to adjust institutional roles, and that secondary legislation on cyber incidents and 5G security was adopted in 2026. The sources describe the acts approved but not how they are applied.
- INT-MDA-0613 Reform Agenda of Moldova under the Reform and Growth Facility: adopted. The Commission's Reform Agenda document confirms that Moldova has established its Reform Agenda, which sets out the digital, cybersecurity and connectivity reforms with deadlines to 2027. These items give no evidence on how far the reforms have been delivered.
