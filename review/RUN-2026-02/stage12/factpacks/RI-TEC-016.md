# Fact pack RI-TEC-016: Open data and re-use of public sector information incomplete

Institutions do not regularly publish and update datasets on the open data portal, and legislation is only partly aligned with the Open Data Directive and related EU data rules.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The chapter scale was used as context only, since this is part of the chapter; the issue's own items show change. The 2023 report and Analytical Report and the 2024 report find that no authority is responsible for open data policies, and the 2025 report finds Moldova not yet aligned with the Open Data Directive; the 2026 report records a law partially harmonising with that Directive, and the government reports a dedicated open data unit created in the economy ministry under Law No. 109/2025. The improvement is legal and institutional only: across all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files the only measure of publication is the Government's count of 1,268 datasets on the portal, a single figure with no trend, and the World Bank found the portal still lacking in use in 2024. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0081;MDA-EU-2023-002-0051;MDA-EU-2024-001-0078;MDA-EU-2025-001-0084;MDA-EU-2026-012-0118;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0191;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0193;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0152;MDA-IO-2024-003-0012
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The law on open data and re-use, its methodological rules and a two-person policy unit address the Commission's findings on alignment and on the absence of a responsible authority, but the law only partly transposes the Directive. The government itself says the data.gov.md portal needs updating and completing with data, the World Bank finds the portal still lacking in use, and the draft law on data governance has been suspended until 2027. What remains is full alignment with the Open Data Directive and related EU data rules, and regular publication and updating of datasets by institutions on the portal. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0118;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0191;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0192;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0193;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0092;MDA-IO-2024-003-0012;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0093
- priority: Medium (validated). The Commission repeats the finding on open data in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, and the 2024 Growth Plan Communication refers to implementing the Open Data Directive, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on open data. Open data is a defined part of the data theme rather than its centre. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0081;MDA-EU-2024-001-0078;MDA-EU-2025-001-0084;MDA-EU-2024-002-0016
- tractability: Medium (validated). Recent legal progress is recorded, with a law, methodological rules and a responsible unit in place, and the portal already exists. Against that, capacity is thin, with two posts in the ministry and one at the e-Governance Agency for the portal, and publication depends on many institutions, including local authorities, whose capacity the government lists as a challenge. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0118;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0170;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0194;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0158;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0196

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0030 Semantic Catalog for MConnect: implemented. The concept and register regulation were approved by Government Decision No. 323/2021, and the Government lists the Semantic Catalog among its new platforms and reports employment data integrated through it. An international partner finds that the open data portal it supports is still lacking in use.
- INT-MDA-0032 Open Government Data Portal (date.gov.md): implemented. The portal has operated since 2011 and holds about 1,268 datasets, and a 2024 international partner report describes it as a one-stop shop for public sector data. The same body of evidence finds that the portal is still lacking in use and needs updating, so use at scale is not shown.
- INT-MDA-0044 Action plan on alignment with Regulation (EU) 2025/327 (European Health Data Space): proposed. The action plan on alignment with the European Health Data Space Regulation is planned, and in 2026 the Government reports only that it is being drafted.
- INT-MDA-0056 Alignment of the national interoperability framework with the European Interoperability Framework: proposed. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet says the national framework follows the European Interoperability Framework, but the Government notes that the Interoperable Europe Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/903, has not been transposed. Further alignment is planned in the National Accession Programme; these Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0174 Data governance framework and national data governance strategy (recommended): proposed. The Government lists aligning the data governance framework among its e-government priorities. No source shows a data governance framework or strategy adopted.
- INT-MDA-0176 Dataset on late payment obligations published through MConnect: proposed. The Government's 2025 progress report says the dataset on late payment obligations is still to be defined before publication through MConnect. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0183 Designation of the national authority implementing the law on data and data governance: proposed. Designating the national authority responsible for the law on data and data governance appears only as a planned action.
- INT-MDA-0263 Draft law on data and data governance (Regulation (EU) 2022/868, Data Governance Act): proposed. The draft law transposing the Data Governance Act was prepared and sent for interinstitutional examination in October 2025, and the data protection authority commented on it in 2026. The Government's 2025 progress report records the action as suspended until 2027 under the National Accession Programme; the draft was not withdrawn, so it remains a proposal.
- INT-MDA-0269 Draft law on open data and the reuse of public sector information (partial transposition of Directive (EU) 2019/1024): adopted. The draft was adopted by Parliament in May 2025 as Law No. 109/2025, partially transposing Directive (EU) 2019/1024 (see INT-MDA-0417).
- INT-MDA-0283 e-Governance Agency open data support and reporting: proposed. The National Accession Programme plans a support unit for opening data in the e-Governance Agency and a reporting system for public authorities. The Government's 2025 progress report records only that one open data specialist post was created, so the unit and the reporting system are not yet in place. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0354 Government Decision No. 563/2025 approving methodological rules for Law No. 109/2025: adopted. Government Decision No. 563/2025 approving the methodological rules for the open data law was adopted in September 2025, as Promo-LEX confirms. The linked items do not report how the rules are applied.
- INT-MDA-0361 Government Decision No. 700/2014 on open government data: adopted. Government Decision No. 700/2014 approved the concept on the principles of open government data and remains part of the open data framework. No source describes its application.
- INT-MDA-0362 Government Decision No. 701/2014 approving the Methodology for publishing open government data: adopted. Government Decision No. 701/2014 approved the methodology for publishing open government data and is listed as part of the open data framework.
- INT-MDA-0374 Government work with trusted data intermediaries on a common data standard and technical safeguards (planned): proposed. The White Paper recommends identifying trusted data intermediaries and working with them on a common data standard and safeguards. No source shows this work started.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0417 Law No. 109/2025 on open data and the reuse of public sector information: adopted. Law No. 109/2025 on open data, partially transposing Directive (EU) 2019/1024, was adopted in May 2025 and followed by methodological norms in September 2025, according to the Government and civil society. The Commission dates the adoption of such a law to July 2026, which may refer to a later act; civil society reports training and platform improvements to apply it, but no source shows its effect on data publication.
- INT-MDA-0426 Law No. 148/2023 on access to information of public interest: adopted. CSO Meter describes the Law on Access to Information of Public Interest as aligned with EU standards and improving proactive publication and open data. This is an assessment of the Law's content; the linked item does not report its application.
- INT-MDA-0441 Law No. 305/2012 on the reuse of public sector information: unclear. Law No. 109/2025 on open data was adopted in May 2025 to align with the newer Directive (EU) 2019/1024, but no source states whether it repealed or replaced Law No. 305/2012.
- INT-MDA-0478 MDED open data policy service: implemented. The Government reports that an open data and reuse policy service with two staff was created within the ministry under Law No. 109/2025, with tasks including technical norms and the list of high-value datasets. No independent source confirms its work.
- INT-MDA-0536 National geoportal of the national spatial data infrastructure: implemented. The Government reports that the national geoportal has been integrated into the INSPIRE Geoportal since 2024. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0541 National mechanism for coordination with the European Data Innovation Board: proposed. Creating a national mechanism for coordination with the European Data Innovation Board is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0548 National Programme for the Accession of the Republic of Moldova to the European Union 2025-2029 (Government Decision No. 306/2025): implemented. The programme was approved by Government Decision No. 306/2025, and ministries cite its actions when drafting laws, such as the Chapter 2 action on unemployment benefit coordination and the data governance law. The evidence on its delivery comes only from government sources.
- INT-MDA-0549 National Programme on Artificial Intelligence and Data Governance 2026-2030: proposed. The concept note sets out a five-year programme to be approved by Government decision, with the ministry intending to draft and promote it in the second quarter of 2026. No source shows that the programme has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0580 Open Data Program: implemented. The World Bank's 2024 recommendations refer to the Open Data Program as ongoing. The linked item does not describe what the programme has delivered.
- INT-MDA-0584 particip.gov.md online public consultation platform: implemented. The Government lists particip.gov.md among the platforms implemented, and CSO Meter notes continued government improvements to it as part of implementing the open data law. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0601 Public-private data sharing framework (planned): proposed. The White Paper plans a public-private data sharing framework, with a normative framework still to be developed. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0602 Publication of asset declarations in open data format: adopted. The Commission reports that asset declarations will now be published in open data format and stored for 15 years. The linked item does not report publication in practice.
- INT-MDA-0603 Publication of high-value datasets: proposed. The National Accession Programme lists drafting technical norms for publishing data and approving the list of high-value datasets as steps still to take. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0679 Training programmes for public sector employees on managing and publishing data: proposed. Training for public sector employees on managing and publishing data is planned, to start once a responsible person is hired. No source shows the training under way.
- INT-MDA-0693 White Book on Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence: unclear. The ministry presented and published the White Paper in February 2024, but the Government itself describes it as declarative and not a policy document as such, and no source shows formal approval. The Commission reports that an AI and data sub-council applies its principles, and its recommendations were to be taken forward in a later national programme.
