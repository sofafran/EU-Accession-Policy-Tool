# Fact pack RI-FMW-009: National social security law not aligned with the coordination regulations

National pension, health and other social security laws are only partly aligned with the EU coordination regulations, for example through citizenship conditions, and the administrative measures for applying them at accession are not identified or in place.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 analytical report found that national law covers most branches of social security but not pre-retirement benefits, gives no unemployment benefit to non-nationals with temporary residence, and needs capacity building in coordination; a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no Commission item on this alignment in the 2024 or 2025 reports, so the wording is not repeated across three reports and the default of persistent does not apply. The 2026 report records the adoption of a law setting a legal basis for applying the coordination rules, which is improvement in law only: the Commission does not assess its alignment and asks for gap analyses of the coordination regulations, and the 2023 gap on unemployment benefit remains open. The Chapter 2 rating rose from an early stage in 2023 and 2024 to between some and a moderate level of preparation in 2026, but the progress it cites concerns bilateral agreements and EURES, so the scale was not decisive. - unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). A law establishing the legal basis for applying the EU coordination rules and EESSI interconnection has been adopted, which is a legal step only, while the draft law on keeping unemployment benefit when moving and on frontier workers remains a proposal, and the Government states that current law does not regulate these cases; only 3 of 8 planned staff of the legal directorate work on harmonisation with European rules. What remains is a gap analysis and full alignment of national pension, health, unemployment and other social security law with Regulations (EC) No 883/2004 and No 987/2009, together with the administrative measures to apply them at accession. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0012 Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the law obliges government agencies to exchange data only through the interoperability solution, and that the MConnect platform it governs is well functioning and used by many public and private organisations. The data protection authority finds that some state bodies still access data outside the platform in breach of the law, and the Government notes that replacing documents with data remains problematic in many cases.
- INT-MDA-0038 "Social Protection" information system: in_practice. The Commission reported in 2023 that the social protection information system carries out the registration, granting and payment of social benefits, which shows it in use. No later source reports on it.
- INT-MDA-0156 Connection of the Republic of Moldova to EESSI: proposed. The Commission reports that an EESSI action plan was approved, and the authorities are preparing the digital infrastructure and the ANOFM and CNAS systems for interoperability. The actual connection still depends on technical documentation from the Commission and a technical solution yet to be chosen, so Moldova is not connected to EESSI.
- INT-MDA-0215 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 1276/2018 on procedures for access to employment measures: proposed. The Ministry of Labour and Social Protection was amending Government Decision No. 1276/2018 in 2025 to give EU citizens access to the National Employment Agency's services and active measures. No source shows the amendment approved.
- INT-MDA-0218 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 230/2020 on the organisation and functioning of CNAS: proposed. The amendment to Government Decision No. 230/2020 on CNAS appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0243 Draft law amending certain acts on cross-border medical care in EU Member States, the EEA and Switzerland: proposed. The law on cross-border medical care appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0244 Draft law amending certain normative acts (coordination and exchange of data in the field of social security with EU Member States) (single no. 89/MMPS/2026): adopted. The Commission reports that a law amending several acts to set the legal basis for EU social security coordination rules and EESSI interconnection was adopted during its reporting period, after the draft was finalised in 2026. No source yet describes data exchange under it.
- INT-MDA-0247 Draft Law amending Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance (single no. 180/MMPS/2026): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 105/2018 is a draft in consultation, with ministries still proposing changes to its definitions and entry-into-force clause. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0415 Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance: implemented. The Law is in force and sets the rules for access to employment services and measures, which the territorial employment offices apply. Government documents note its gaps, including the exclusion of foreigners with residence for work or study and no rules for EU jobseekers or frontier workers, which a draft amending law addresses; these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0432 Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova: in_practice. The Law has been in force since 2010 and was amended in 2023 to let EU citizens work for 90 days in any 180 without a permit; otherwise EU and other foreign citizens need a temporary residence permit for work under it. The Commission records 10,290 residence permits granted in 2025, and a draft law on the admission and stay of foreigners would repeal it on entry into force. The Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0438 Law No. 274/2011 on the integration of foreigners: adopted. Law No. 274/2011 is in force and is described as one of the basic acts governing the employment of foreigners. No source in these items reports how it is applied.
- INT-MDA-0548 National Programme for the Accession of the Republic of Moldova to the European Union 2025-2029 (Government Decision No. 306/2025): implemented. The programme was approved by Government Decision No. 306/2025, and ministries cite its actions when drafting laws, such as the Chapter 2 action on unemployment benefit coordination and the data governance law. The evidence on its delivery comes only from government sources.
- INT-MDA-0552 National Social Security Information System: insufficient_evidence. The items mention the National Social Security Information System only in relation to its future interoperability with EESSI and say nothing about the system's own status.
