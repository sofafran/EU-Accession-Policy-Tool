# Fact pack RI-FMW-016: Host-language tuition for EU workers and their children

Not raised in EU material: arrangements to teach the official language to the children of EU workers, and to EU workers and their family members, in line with Directive 77/486/EEC on the education of the children of migrant workers, are not yet in place or resourced.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: new (validated). The issue is not raised in EU material: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files for host-language or Romanian-language tuition found eight items, none from an EU source, and the 2026 report says only that the free movement law aims to align with the acquis on the education of migrant workers' children, with no finding on tuition. The issue first appears in the Government's 2025 accession programme, which plans Romanian courses for EU citizens, their family members and their children, so there is no earlier item from which to judge a trend. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0040;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0041;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0489;MDA-EU-2026-012-0078
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). All the measures are proposals: Romanian courses in schools and through the national programme for learning Romanian, a new programme for 2026 to 2028 with study centres, and a mechanism to pay teachers, and the Government says these could need additional teaching posts. What remains is, on the Government's own account, putting in place and resourcing host-language tuition for EU workers, their family members and their children. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0040;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0041;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0489;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0490;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0491
- priority: Low (validated). The issue is not raised in EU material: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files for host-language or Romanian-language tuition found no EU item on it, which keeps priority from being High, and it rests on the Government's 2025 accession programme, which describes planned measures. Host-language tuition is a narrow strand of the chapter compared with access to employment and social security coordination. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0040;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0489
- tractability: Medium (validated). Delivery rests mainly with the Ministry of Education and Research and builds on an existing national programme for learning Romanian. However, the new programme was still to be drafted in 2025, and no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files reports funding for the planned teacher payment mechanism or for extra teaching posts. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0040;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0489;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0490;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0491

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0550 National programme on learning Romanian (Romanian courses for EU and EEA citizens and their children): proposed. Romanian courses for EU and EEA citizens and their children are planned under a new edition of the national programme for 2026 to 2028, to be drafted in 2025. No source reports the new programme adopted or courses provided.
