# Fact pack RI-FMW-002: Work permits, quotas and residence conditions applied to EU workers

EU workers and their family members are subject to work permits, annual quotas, residence permits or unspecified conditions (for example for seasonal work) that must be lifted or settled by accession.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). In 2023 the Commission found that EU citizens needed a temporary residence permit for work and that their family members were treated as other foreigners, while the 2023 report recorded amendments easing temporary residence for EU citizens; the 2024 report recorded amendments in force from January 2024 letting them work without a work permit. The 2025 report recorded a separate regime for EU citizens, and the 2026 report the entry into force of the free movement law in March 2026 and a standard operating procedure for their residence documents; the improvement is mainly legal. The chapter rating covers the whole chapter and was used as context only. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0016;MDA-EU-2023-002-0017;MDA-EU-2023-001-0048;MDA-EU-2024-001-0052;MDA-EU-2025-001-0065;MDA-EU-2026-012-0078;MDA-EU-2026-012-0083
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Under Law No. 200/2025 EU citizens need no work authorisation and, for stays over 90 days, a residence card not tied to a purpose, but the Commission reports that only 28 applications for the new residence documents had been submitted after the March 2026 procedure, while 422 EU citizens were still to be documented under the old stay-for-work regime in 2025. Full separation of the two regimes in the general law on foreigners depends on a draft law due to apply from 1 June 2027, and the Ministry of Internal Affairs' secondary legislation on registering EU citizens is still being revised. What remains is the practical move of EU citizens and their family members to the new residence cards and the completion of the implementing rules. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0010;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0011;MDA-EU-2026-012-0084;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0039;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0032;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0036
- priority: Medium (validated). Work permits and residence conditions for EU workers are central to the chapter, but they are not named separately in a key recommendation; the 2025 and 2026 key recommendations on access for EU citizens to the labour market can be read as covering them, which would still make only two reports. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on the issue. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0020;MDA-EU-2026-012-0012;MDA-EU-2023-002-0016
- tractability: Medium (validated). The legal change has been made and the General Inspectorate for Migration has an operating procedure, so what is left is mostly administrative and within national control. Few EU citizens had applied for the new documents by 2026, and full separation of the regimes depends on a new foreigners law that is still a draft, so progress depends on the Ministry of Internal Affairs and the Inspectorate following through. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0045 Adjustment of secondary legislation following Law No. 200/2025: proposed. The Government reports that several ministries and the Equality Council are adjusting secondary legislation after Law No. 200/2025, and that the Ministry of Internal Affairs still needs to revise its rules on registering EU citizens. No source reports any of these acts approved.
- INT-MDA-0051 Agreement by exchange of notes between Moldova and France on the International Volunteering in Enterprise Programme: adopted. The General Inspectorate for Migration reports that the agreement with France on the International Volunteering in Enterprise Programme was signed on 20 November 2025. No source reports its entry into force or use.
- INT-MDA-0068 Amendments to Art. 2(1) of Law No. 274/2011 on the integration of foreigners: adopted. The Government reports that amendments to Article 2(1) of Law No. 274/2011, applicable from March 2026, entitle EU, EEA and Schengen citizens with a residence card to integration measures, including ANOFM services. No source yet reports their application.
- INT-MDA-0071 Amendments to Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners (2016 to 2024): adopted. The Government reports that amendments to Law No. 200/2010 between 2016 and 2024 regulated the admission of EU citizens and simplified residence procedures for foreigners. No source in these items reports how they are applied.
- INT-MDA-0072 Amendments to Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners (2023): adopted. The Government reports that the 2023 amendments to Law No. 200/2010 let EU citizens work for 90 days in any 180-day period without a residence permit for work. No source in these items reports how they are applied.
- INT-MDA-0074 Amendments to the law on the regime of foreigners in force January 2024 (temporary residence for EU citizens): adopted. The Commission confirms that amendments to the law on the regime of foreigners entered into force in January 2024, allowing EU citizens and those of some other countries to work in Moldova without a work permit. The linked items do not report how many EU citizens have worked under these rules.
- INT-MDA-0131 Bilateral social security agreements of the Republic of Moldova (as a group): implemented. The Commission reports that bilateral social security agreements were concluded in 2022 and that Moldova had agreements with 13 EU Member States and Türkiye by 2023, and the Government reported 17 agreements with EU Member States in 2025. The agreements are in force, but no source gives data on claims or benefits handled under them, and agreements with ten Member States remain to be concluded.
- INT-MDA-0208 Draft amendment to Government Decision No. 125/2013 on the issuance of identity documents: proposed. The General Inspectorate for Migration reports the amendment to Government Decision No. 125/2013 as a draft, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0239 Draft Government Decision on the list of states whose citizens may work without prior right of residence for work: proposed. The General Inspectorate for Migration reports that the draft decision listing the states whose citizens may work without a prior right of residence was prepared and sent for promotion. No source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0247 Draft Law amending Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance (single no. 180/MMPS/2026): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 105/2018 is a draft in consultation, with ministries still proposing changes to its definitions and entry-into-force clause. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0273 Draft Law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova (single no. 507/MAI/2025): proposed. The text is a draft law still under inter-institutional consultation, with comments being accepted and the quota provisions removed. It provides that it would enter into force on 1 June 2027 and repeal Law No. 200/2010, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0324 Further amendment of the law on the regime of foreigners (work without a work permit, first reading July 2023): insufficient_evidence. The Government reports that 2023 amendments to Law No. 200/2010 let EU citizens work for 90 days without a residence permit for work, which are recorded as a separate intervention. No source says whether this amendment became that law, so its final status cannot be established.
- INT-MDA-0325 General Inspectorate for Migration information and referral measures for Member State citizens: proposed. The National Accession Programme says the Ministry of Internal Affairs will determine how arriving Member State citizens are registered and that the Migration Inspectorate will take measures to inform and refer them. The items describe plans only. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0326 General Inspectorate for Migration standard operating procedure for issuing residence cards to EU citizens and their family members: implemented. The Commission reports that the Migration Inspectorate approved the procedure in March 2026 and that 28 applications for residence documents had been submitted under it. Use is so far small, with 422 EU citizens still documented under the old regime in 2025.
- INT-MDA-0396 Integrated automated information system "Migration and asylum": in_practice. The Commission reported in 2023 that the integrated information system "Migration and asylum" keeps records of migrant workers, which shows it in use. No later source reports on it.
- INT-MDA-0432 Law No. 200/2010 on the regime of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova: in_practice. The Law has been in force since 2010 and was amended in 2023 to let EU citizens work for 90 days in any 180 without a permit; otherwise EU and other foreign citizens need a temporary residence permit for work under it. The Commission records 10,290 residence permits granted in 2025, and a draft law on the admission and stay of foreigners would repeal it on entry into force. The Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0433 Law No. 200/2025 on the free movement and residence on the territory of the Republic of Moldova of citizens of the European Union and their family members: adopted. Law No. 200/2025 was adopted in July 2025 and, as the Commission confirms, entered into force in March 2026. The Commission notes that civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals, and no source yet describes how the law is applied in practice.
- INT-MDA-0434 Law No. 203/2023 amending Art. 43^1 of Law No. 200/2010: adopted. The Migration Inspectorate reports that Law No. 203/2023 amended Article 43^1 of the law on foreigners and that a draft decision listing the states whose citizens may work without a prior permit followed. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report application.
- INT-MDA-0443 Law No. 38/2023 amending Law No. 200/2010: adopted. Law No. 38/2023 amending Law No. 200/2010 entered into force on 5 May 2023, and the Government reports follow-up changes to identity document rules for EU citizens being drafted.
- INT-MDA-0464 Law providing a different regime for EU citizens wishing to work in Moldova (July 2025): adopted. The Commission reports that in July 2025 Moldova adopted a law giving EU citizens a different regime for working in Moldova from other third-country nationals, and that the law entered into force in March 2026. This appears to be Law No. 200/2025 (INT-MDA-0433).
- INT-MDA-0565 New normative act on the regime of foreigners: proposed. The Government planned in 2024 to draft a new act on the regime of foreigners aligned with the acquis, and a draft law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners was published in February 2026. No evidence item in these sources records its adoption.
