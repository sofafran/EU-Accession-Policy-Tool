# Fact pack RI-TEC-032: Not ready to connect to other EU sectoral information systems

Systems in other sectors are not yet ready to interconnect with EU information systems, such as the business registers interconnection system, the Internal Market Information System, the civil protection system, EU taxation systems and the own resources reporting system.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scales were not decisive, since this issue spans several chapters and is part of each. The main thread is taxation: the Commission finds no IT strategy for interoperability with EU tax systems in 2023 and 2024, records a programme prepared in 2025, and in 2026 records a roadmap for 2026 to 2029 while finding that EMCS and SEED solutions remain to be developed and that VIES interoperability must advance. The plans are adopted but no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files records a new connection to an EU tax or excise system, and the 2026 report adds that Moldova is not yet in ECURIE or EURDEP. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0101;MDA-EU-2024-001-0088;MDA-EU-2025-001-0100;MDA-EU-2026-012-0140;MDA-EU-2026-012-0138;MDA-EU-2026-012-0137;MDA-EU-2026-012-0156
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). Some connections exist, such as access to TED since 2020, trademark data in EUIPO systems and use of TRACES, but they lie outside the latest requirements. The responses to those requirements are a tax IT programme and roadmap that are adopted, and connections to the CCN gateway, VIES and other tax systems, TESTA and IMI that are planned. What remains is building and connecting the tax, excise, radiological, civil protection and internal market systems to the EU systems the Commission names. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0014;MDA-EU-2023-002-0043;MDA-EU-2023-002-0070;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0441;MDA-EU-2026-012-0140;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0455;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0456;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0400;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0406;MDA-EU-2026-012-0138;MDA-EU-2026-012-0157
- priority: Medium (validated). Tax interoperability is a core acquis requirement raised in the 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 reports, but the Commission records good progress in the taxation chapter in 2025 and 2026, so the test for High on lack of progress is not met. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on it: the Reform Agenda's tax objective refers to EU taxation IT systems, but its step concerns taxpayer services. The issue groups many sectors, each with one or two items. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0099;MDA-EU-2024-001-0088;MDA-EU-2025-001-0107;MDA-EU-2026-012-0137;MDA-EU-2025-001-0032;MDA-EU-2026-012-0025;MDA-EU-2025-002-0081
- tractability: Medium (validated). The tax service now has an approved IT programme and a roadmap with a timetable, which is evidence of planning progress. Against that, the work spreads across many agencies and EU systems, and some connections depend on EU arrangements open only to Member States, as the government notes for the AGORA system. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0441;MDA-EU-2026-012-0140;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0318;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0523

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0040 Access to the EU Tenders Electronic Daily system: implemented. The Commission reports that Moldova was granted access to Tenders Electronic Daily in October 2020 and that its contracting authorities can publish contract notices in the Official Journal of the EU.
- INT-MDA-0042 Accession of Moldova to the Common Transit Convention: implemented. The Commission reports that Moldova formally acceded to the Common Transit Convention in November 2025, after its New Computerised Transit System was connected to the common transit network. No source yet describes transit operations under the Convention.
- INT-MDA-0057 Alignment of the Railway Agency operational systems and databases with those of the EU Agency for Railways: proposed. Developing and aligning the Railway Agency's systems and databases with those of the EU Agency for Railways is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0059 Alignment with EMSA digital systems and the European Maritime Single Window environment: adopted. The Commission reports that Moldova has partially aligned its legal framework with the European Maritime Single Window Regulation. Aligning procedures with EMSA's digital systems is still set out by the Government as a priority, and no source reports the systems connected.
- INT-MDA-0139 Climate monitoring, reporting and verification system with digital registers: proposed. The climate monitoring, reporting and verification system with digital registers is planned, and no source reports it developed.
- INT-MDA-0144 Common Emergency Communication and Information System (CECIS): proposed. The Commission asked in 2023 for Moldova to connect to CECIS through TESTA, and the National Accession Programme plans the TESTA connection. No connection is recorded in these items.
- INT-MDA-0157 Connection of the Republic of Moldova to the TESTA network: proposed. The Commission asked in 2023 for Moldova to connect to TESTA, and the National Accession Programme plans the connection, with the number of public entities connected as its indicator. The linked items do not record the connection.
- INT-MDA-0158 Connection to the CCN Gateway: proposed. Connection to the CCN Gateway is a planned action with the indicator of an operational connection, and no source reports it achieved.
- INT-MDA-0264 Draft law on digital services (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Digital Services Act): proposed. The Commission noted in 2025 that legislative work to align with the Digital Services Act had started, and the Government's 2025 progress report says the draft law is still being prepared with EU4Digital experts. The Government's own accession programme notes that the law has not yet been adopted.
- INT-MDA-0294 Early Warning and Response System (EWRS) operational officers: proposed. Designating operational officers for the EWRS platform appears only as a planned action.
- INT-MDA-0302 Electronic certification system for guarantees of origin: unclear. The Government says that by establishing an electronic certification system interoperable with the European register Moldova takes an important step towards the EU guarantees of origin market. The machine-translated wording does not make clear whether the system has been established or is planned.
- INT-MDA-0304 Electronic health record (EHR) system: proposed. The electronic health record is planned, with its concept and regulation due by the end of 2026 and the system for 40% of the insured population by 2027. In 2026 the Government reports only that the draft Government Decision on it is being prepared.
- INT-MDA-0308 Electronic system for the prior posting declaration: proposed. The National Accession Programme says an electronic system for submitting the prior posting declaration will need to be created. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0317 Excise Movement and Control System (EMCS): insufficient_evidence. The Commission found in 2023 that Moldova had yet to introduce the Excise Movement and Control System and in 2025 that it still has to align its legislation with the IT solutions needed. No source describes a Moldovan plan or step to introduce the system.
- INT-MDA-0397 Integrated eFTI information system for electronic freight transport information: proposed. The National Accession Programme says an integrated eFTI information system will be developed to be fully operational at accession. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0399 Integration into e-Phyto in preparation of full TRACES-NT integration: unclear. The Commission reports that Moldova further advanced its integration into the e-Phyto system in preparation for full TRACES-NT integration, but does not say whether the integration is already working.
- INT-MDA-0403 Interconnection of the national tachograph card system with TACHOnet: proposed. Connecting the national tachograph card system to TACHOnet appears only as a planned action.
- INT-MDA-0408 Interoperability of the State Tax Service with EU tax information systems (CESOP, OSS, VAT Refund, VAT REG, ViDA/VIES): proposed. Interoperability with the EU tax systems CESOP, OSS, VAT Refund, VAT REG and ViDA/VIES appears only as planned actions, and the Commission found in 2024 that Moldova had yet to familiarise itself with VIES. No source shows any of these connections in operation.
- INT-MDA-0411 IT Development Programme of the State Tax Service 2025-2027 (Government Decision No. 687/2025): adopted. The Government reports that the programme was approved by Government Decision No. 687 of 15 October 2025, aiming at interconnection with EU tax systems. No source describes its implementation.
- INT-MDA-0522 National contact point for EPREL (European Product Database for Energy Labelling): proposed. Moldova plans to set up a national contact point for EPREL by December 2026. No source shows it set up.
- INT-MDA-0536 National geoportal of the national spatial data infrastructure: implemented. The Government reports that the national geoportal has been integrated into the INSPIRE Geoportal since 2024. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0551 National Register of Qualifications (RNC) interoperability with the Europass database: proposed. Ensuring interoperability of the National Register of Qualifications with the Europass database is listed among the actions in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0633 Roadmap and timetable for interoperability of the State Tax Service with EU information systems 2026-2029: adopted. The Commission reports that the roadmap and timeline for interoperability of the tax administration with EU information systems for 2026 to 2029 was approved in November 2025. No source yet reports its delivery.
- INT-MDA-0645 Single liaison point and cooperation through the IMI system on posted workers: proposed. The single liaison point and cooperation through IMI on posted workers are planned, and no source reports them set up.
- INT-MDA-0659 State Register of Legal Units information system (SI RSUD): implemented. The Commission confirms that the State Register of Legal Units portal was launched on 29 December 2025 and that the system enables registration of all legal forms of entities. The Government describes a staged launch in pilot mode, with online applications for name reservation and register extracts available from February 2026, and no source yet gives evidence of use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0670 System for the Exchange of Excise Data (SEED): insufficient_evidence. The only item is the Commission's statement that Moldova will have to align with the IT solutions for EMCS and SEED, and no source reports any Moldovan action on SEED.
- INT-MDA-0690 Use of TRACES (TRACES NT) by Moldova: implemented. The Commission described Moldova in 2023 as an active participant in TRACES NT with agreed harmonised certificates, but in 2026 it reports Moldova still preparing for full TRACES-NT integration, and the Government notes that full integration with EU systems such as TRACES remains to be aligned.
