# Fact pack RI-TEC-035: Digital tools in company law not fully aligned

Company law is not fully aligned with EU rules on the use of digital tools and processes, such as online formation, electronic filing and director checks.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 6 scale is context only, since this is part of the chapter. The 2023 report and the 2023 Analytical Report found company law not aligned on digital tools and processes, the 2024 report repeated the recommendation while recording a qualified electronic signature for filing with the business register, and the 2026 report records the new State Register of Legal Entities, launched in December 2025, which enables registration of all legal forms. The Government's own assessment (machine translated) is that the directive on digital tools in company law is only partly transposed. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0065;MDA-EU-2023-002-0042;MDA-EU-2024-001-0020;MDA-EU-2024-001-0069;MDA-EU-2026-012-0099;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0415
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The latest EU requirement, from 2024, is further alignment on the use of digital tools and processes in company law. Electronic signature filing and the new register are implemented, but the draft law on digital tools in company law is still planned, online company registration ran by e-mail as a provisional solution, and in February 2026 the new platform offered online only name reservation and register extracts (machine translated). What remains is full transposition of the rules on digital tools and processes, with company formation and filings fully online through the new register. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0020;MDA-EU-2024-001-0069;MDA-EU-2026-012-0099;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0428;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0059;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0069
- priority: Medium (validated). Digital tools in company law appear in the 2023 recommendation and among many elements of the 2024 key recommendation on company law, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found them in no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition and in no 2025 or 2026 item repeating the recommendation. It is a defined acquis requirement but a narrow part of Chapter 6; the Reform Agenda step on the new register is counted under the base registers issue. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0065;MDA-EU-2024-001-0020;MDA-EU-2023-002-0042
- tractability: High (validated). The remaining work is mainly legal and within the Government's control: a draft law on digital tools in company law is planned and the new register is in place. Against that, the register slipped from a planned July 2025 launch to December 2025 and online company registration still ran by e-mail as a provisional solution, so delays are the main risk (Government figures, machine translated). Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0428;MDA-EU-2026-012-0099;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0273;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0059

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0003 MCabinet (Citizen Portal): implemented. An international partner reports that MCabinet was launched in October 2020 and gives access to 17 data blocks. The Government reports that unique authenticated users rose from 92,619 in 2023 to 176,855 in the first three quarters of 2024, but this rests on its own reporting.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0019 MPass: implemented. The Commission and the Government list MPass among the government platforms implemented, and an international partner describes it as the authentication service for public e-services. Government consultation material gives 208,500 identifiers authenticated from 2023 to September 2024, but no independent source reports on use.
- INT-MDA-0020 MPay: implemented. The Commission counts MPay among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes it as in place since 2020. SIGMA found in 2023 that online payment through MPay was possible but not yet integrated into digital service provision, and the Government's figures on savings and on payments collected through MPay are not corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0022 MSign: in_practice. SIGMA reported in 2023 that electronic signatures are available through MSign with about 200,000 active users, mostly businesses required to sign documents for the tax service. Government figures give more than 37 million documents signed through MSign in 2023 and more than 38 million in the first nine months of 2024.
- INT-MDA-0073 Amendments to Law No. 220/2007: adopted. The Government reports that the amendments to Law No. 220/2007, together with the State Register of Legal Units, partially transpose the EU rules on digital tools in company law.
- INT-MDA-0220 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 955/2022 approving the Concept of the State Register of Legal Units information system: proposed. The draft Government decision amending the Concept of the State Register of Legal Units has been sent for approval at a Government meeting. No source shows it approved.
- INT-MDA-0275 Draft law on the use of digital tools and processes in company law: proposed. The draft law on the use of digital tools and processes in company law is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the draft only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0336 Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 211/2019 on the interoperability platform (MConnect): proposed. The amendment to Government Decision No. 211/2019 on MConnect appears as a planned act in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0401 Integration of the qualified electronic signature into company registration and filing with the business register: implemented. The Commission reported in 2024 that a qualified electronic signature was introduced for company registration and filing with the business register. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0421 Law No. 124/2022 on electronic identification and trust services: adopted. Parliament approved the Law on electronic identification and trust services on 19 May 2022, and the Commission found it broadly but only partially aligned with the eIDAS Regulation. The sources give different dates for its entry into force, the Commission 10 December 2022 and the Government 1 January 2025, but agree it is in force; no source describes its application beyond its use as a reference for electronic submissions.
- INT-MDA-0425 Law No. 144/2025 amending certain normative acts (supporting the digital economy and electronic services): adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 144/2025 supporting the digital economy and electronic services was approved on 19 June 2025 and entered into force on 20 September 2025. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report application.
- INT-MDA-0427 Law No. 175/2021 amending certain normative acts: implemented. Law No. 175/2021 allowed documents for registering legal entities to be submitted electronically with an electronic signature, and the Public Services Agency applied it through a provisional e-mail registration service. This is the Government's own account, and the agency notes the e-mail route was due to delays in its systems.
- INT-MDA-0442 Law No. 321/2023 on stimulating the development and use of electronic services: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 321/2023 set the framework for stimulating electronic services, including the digital identity card, driving licence and registration certificate. The linked items do not report specifically on its application.
- INT-MDA-0573 Online business registration and E-Filing service for foreign investors: implemented. The Commission reported in 2024 that online business registration for foreign investors had been introduced, and the Government reports the E-Filing service optimised. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0577 Online state registration of companies and legal units (Public Services Agency): implemented. Since 2022 the Public Services Agency has offered remote registration of legal units, but only through a provisional e-mail service because its information systems were delayed. The service was meant to last until the new State Register of Legal Units took over online registration, and no source describes its volume of use.
- INT-MDA-0624 Remote identification of customers in the financial sector: implemented. The Commission reports that remote identification of customers in the financial sector has been introduced. No source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0659 State Register of Legal Units information system (SI RSUD): implemented. The Commission confirms that the State Register of Legal Units portal was launched on 29 December 2025 and that the system enables registration of all legal forms of entities. The Government describes a staged launch in pilot mode, with online applications for name reservation and register extracts available from February 2026, and no source yet gives evidence of use at scale.
