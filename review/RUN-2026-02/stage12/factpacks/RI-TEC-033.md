# Fact pack RI-TEC-033: Low digital adoption by businesses

Digitalisation of the economy and adoption of digital technologies by small businesses remain low compared with the EU and undermine competitiveness, and EU programmes and digital innovation hubs are little used.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since the issue comes from a cross-cutting theme and its items sit in the economic sections. The Commission finds digitalisation lagging behind in 2023, slowly improving in 2024, steadily advancing in 2025 and continuing to advance in 2026, but these findings concern the economy as a whole. Across all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, the only repeated measure of business adoption is the Government's share of enterprises making web sales, 0.9% both in its 2023 strategy and as the baseline of its 2025 programme, so no item shows a change in business adoption itself. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0032;MDA-EU-2024-001-0050;MDA-EU-2025-001-0059;MDA-EU-2026-012-0066;MDA-GOV-2023-001-0115;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0170
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Responses are in place: SME digitalisation programmes, association to the Digital Europe Programme and the first European Digital Innovation Hub, launched in April 2026. Against that, the Reform Agenda finds the share of businesses using digital services low, and the government's 2025 programme still gives a baseline of 0.9% of enterprises making web sales. What remains is wider adoption of digital technologies by small businesses and greater use of EU programmes and the new innovation hub. Items: MDA-IO-2024-002-0035;MDA-EU-2024-001-0077;MDA-EU-2026-012-0105;MDA-EU-2025-002-0052;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0137;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0170
- priority: Low (validated). The Commission's findings on this issue are general statements in the economic sections, positive since 2024, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on business adoption. Business adoption is not an acquis requirement. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0050;MDA-EU-2025-001-0059;MDA-EU-2026-012-0066
- tractability: Medium (validated). Support programmes, EU funding routes and the first innovation hub exist, and the Commission records steady progress in digitalisation. Against that, adoption depends on many small firms, and the government cites lack of knowledge, high costs and perceived long-term returns as barriers. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0105;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0446;MDA-EU-2025-001-0059;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0057

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0046 Advisory Council to the Prime Minister for the Digitization of the Economy (iCouncil): implemented. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet reports that the Ministry leads digitalisation of the economy alongside the Advisory Council to the Prime Minister for the Digitization of the Economy. The linked item does not describe the Council's work.
- INT-MDA-0052 AI factory antenna in Moldova: implemented. The Commission's 2026 enlargement Communication reports that an AI factory antenna was set up in Moldova, as in North Macedonia and Serbia. The linked item gives no detail on its activity.
- INT-MDA-0097 Association of Moldova to the Digital Europe Programme: adopted. The Commission reports that Parliament ratified the association agreement to the Digital Europe Programme in May 2024, and the Government dates Moldova's joining to 19 February 2024. No source shows Moldovan participation in calls under the programme.
- INT-MDA-0186 Development of electronic communications networks in identified white areas: proposed. Development of networks in white areas is planned in the digital strategy's implementation programme, and the Government's 2025 progress report says it can start only after the regulator's geographic survey under the new Electronic Communications Law and the broadband mapping system. The items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0190 Digital Europe Programme National Contact Point (e-Governance Agency): implemented. The e-Governance Agency reports that as National Contact Point for the Digital Europe Programme it supported about 200 institutions and companies and recorded 14 applications to calls in 2025. This rests on its own reporting.
- INT-MDA-0380 ICT Sector and Digital Economy Development Programme: proposed. The ICT Sector and Digital Economy Development Programme is one of three programmes planned under the Digital Transformation Strategy, and the ministry was still developing these programmes in 2024. No source shows it approved.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0422 Law No. 126/2023 on remote identification and interaction ("Contactless Business"): adopted. The Government lists Law No. 126/2023 on remote identification and interaction among its adopted measures, and an international partner describes the related Contactless Business initiative. No source reports how the law is applied.
- INT-MDA-0471 Long-term plan for the digital economy 2025-2030: proposed. An international partner reported in 2024 that the ministry was working on a long-term plan for the digital economy for 2025 to 2030, and no source reports it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0476 MDED and ODA programmes supporting SME digital transformation: implemented. The Commission confirms that the Government approved an SME digital transformation programme in 2022 alongside other SME support programmes, and the World Bank reports that the ministry and the SME agency created programmes to help businesses go digital. No source gives the number of enterprises supported or the results.
- INT-MDA-0507 Moldova Instant Payments system: implemented. The Commission reports that the Moldova Instant Payments system was launched and is helping to lower transaction costs. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0549 National Programme on Artificial Intelligence and Data Governance 2026-2030: proposed. The concept note sets out a five-year programme to be approved by Government decision, with the ministry intending to draft and promote it in the second quarter of 2026. No source shows that the programme has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0593 Programme for Digital Innovation and Tech Start-ups 2025-2030: implemented. The Commission reports that nine investment projects have already been approved under the Programme for Digital Innovation and Tech Start-ups 2025-2030. No source reports results from the projects.
- INT-MDA-0614 Regional digital innovation hubs (EDIH) in Moldova: implemented. The Commission reports that the first European Digital Innovation Hub in Moldova was launched in April 2026, after the e-Governance Agency prepared the consortium and application in 2025. No source yet reports on the hub's activity.
- INT-MDA-0635 Roadmap to boost digitalisation of the national economy and development of e-commerce: implemented. The Commission reported in 2021 that the Ministry had adopted the roadmap, and the Government states that substantial legislative amendments were promoted on its basis. The account of its delivery is the Government's own and machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0693 White Book on Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence: unclear. The ministry presented and published the White Paper in February 2024, but the Government itself describes it as declarative and not a policy document as such, and no source shows formal approval. The Commission reports that an AI and data sub-council applies its principles, and its recommendations were to be taken forward in a later national programme.
