# Fact pack RI-TEC-034: Digital single market rules not aligned

Legislation is not aligned with EU rules on online platforms and services, such as the Digital Services Act, the Digital Markets Act, the platform-to-business and geo-blocking regulations, and e-commerce is developing slowly.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 and 2024 reports found Moldova not aligned with the acquis on geo-blocking, platform-to-business relations, the Digital Services Act and the Digital Markets Act, and the 2025 report still found no alignment on the first two and the Digital Markets Act while noting that work on the Digital Services Act had started. The 2026 report finds alignment on geo-blocking and platform-to-business relations partial, with additional legislation in force in 2026, and initial steps on the institutional framework for both Acts. The improvement is legal and partial only. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0084;MDA-EU-2024-001-0080;MDA-EU-2025-001-0085;MDA-EU-2025-001-0086;MDA-EU-2026-012-0114;MDA-EU-2026-012-0112
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Two 2025 laws partly transpose the platform-to-business and geo-blocking regulations, although Government sources give conflicting law numbers for them, the Competition Council is designated for the first, and the e-Commerce Directive is broadly aligned. The digital services law is still being drafted, the Digital Markets Act mechanism needs an amendment to the competition law, no supervisory authority for digital services exists yet, and the Government notes missing enforcement rules under the platform-to-business regulation. What remains is a legal basis and enforcement capacity for the Digital Services Act and the Digital Markets Act, and full alignment on geo-blocking and platform-to-business relations. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0185;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0186;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0081;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0134;MDA-EU-2026-012-0115;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0078;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0251;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0137;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0315;MDA-EU-2026-012-0113
- priority: Medium (validated). The 2024 and 2025 key recommendations ask Moldova to adopt and implement digital services legislation, bundled with media legislation, and the 2026 report repeats the need in its body text on binding acquis. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found it in no key recommendation in three reports, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0028;MDA-EU-2025-001-0031;MDA-EU-2026-012-0113;MDA-EU-2026-012-0114
- tractability: Medium (validated). Two laws were adopted in 2025, and the Government plans to align with the Digital Services Act by the end of 2026, with EU4Digital experts helping on the draft. Enforcement needs new units in two regulators, while the ministry has one post for this area. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0185;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0188;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0078;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0297;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0299;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0187

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0178 Designation and capacity of the Competition Council for the Digital Markets Act and Law No. 53/2025: adopted. The Competition Council is designated as the authority for Law No. 53/2025 on fairness and transparency for business users of online intermediation services. Its designation for the Digital Markets Act and a dedicated digital markets subdivision are still planned, and no source describes its supervisory work.
- INT-MDA-0180 Designation of ARCOM (ANRC) as Digital Services Coordinator and of the Audiovisual Council for video-sharing platforms: proposed. The Government proposes, in transposing the Digital Services Act, to designate the communications regulator as digital services coordinator and the Audiovisual Council as competent authority for video-sharing platforms. The Commission reports in 2026 only initial steps to set up the institutional framework, and no source shows the designation made.
- INT-MDA-0210 Draft ANRC Board decisions implementing the European Electronic Communications Code: unclear. The Government listed these regulator board decisions as drafts in 2025. The regulator reports that it adopted the regulatory acts needed to implement the new electronic communications framework and continues drafting others in 2026, and the Commission reports approximated secondary legislation, but no source names these specific decisions as adopted.
- INT-MDA-0212 Draft Competition Council decision on the Regulation implementing Law No. 53/2025: proposed. The draft Competition Council decision on the regulation implementing Law No. 53/2025 is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the draft only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0246 Draft law amending Competition Law No. 183/2012 (Regulation (EU) 2022/1925, Digital Markets Act): proposed. The amendment to Competition Law No. 183/2012 appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0256 Draft law amending Law No. 53/2025 on fairness and transparency for business users of online intermediation services: proposed. The draft law amending Law No. 53/2025 on online intermediation services is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the draft only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0264 Draft law on digital services (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, Digital Services Act): proposed. The Commission noted in 2025 that legislative work to align with the Digital Services Act had started, and the Government's 2025 progress report says the draft law is still being prepared with EU4Digital experts. The Government's own accession programme notes that the law has not yet been adopted.
- INT-MDA-0276 Draft law transposing the e-Commerce Directive 2000/31/EC: proposed. Drafting a law to transpose the e-Commerce Directive is planned in the digital strategy's implementation programme, and the Government's 2025 progress report says full alignment will be ensured through the draft law on digital services, which is still being drafted. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0437 Law No. 247/2024 on Contracts for the Supply of Digital Content and Digital Services: adopted. A civil society source reports that the law on contracts for the supply of digital content and digital services was adopted in 2024. No source reports how it is applied.
- INT-MDA-0440 Law No. 284/2004 on electronic commerce: adopted. The Government describes Law No. 284/2004 as the law partially transposing the e-Commerce Directive. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report its application.
- INT-MDA-0447 Law No. 53/2025 on fairness and transparency for business users of online intermediation services: adopted. The Government reports that Law No. 53/2025 partially transposed Regulation (EU) 2019/1150, and the rules on penalties and enforcement are still missing. The 2025 progress report on the digital strategy gives the number 53/2025 to the geo-blocking law instead, so the law's number should be checked.
- INT-MDA-0448 Law No. 54/2025 on preventing unjustified geo-blocking: adopted. Government sources agree that the geo-blocking law was adopted in 2025 and that it only partially transposes Regulation (EU) 2018/302. They conflict on the law's number: the National Accession Programme names it Law No. 54/2025 and gives Law No. 53/2025 to the platform-to-business law, while the 2025 progress report on the digital strategy names it Law No. 53/2025.
- INT-MDA-0458 Law on Information Society Services (formerly the Law on Electronic Commerce): adopted. The Law on Information Society Services, called the Law on Electronic Commerce until 2023, sets the conditions for providing information society services and the responsibilities of providers. No source describes its application.
- INT-MDA-0475 Mapping and designation of digital platforms active in the national jurisdiction (ANRC): proposed. Mapping and designating digital platforms active in Moldova is a planned action, and no source reports it carried out.
- INT-MDA-0635 Roadmap to boost digitalisation of the national economy and development of e-commerce: implemented. The Commission reported in 2021 that the Ministry had adopted the roadmap, and the Government states that substantial legislative amendments were promoted on its basis. The account of its delivery is the Government's own and machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0638 Safer Internet Centre: insufficient_evidence. The only item reports a study visit by the regulator to the Irish Safer Internet Centre in May 2025 in the context of transposing the Digital Services Act. No source shows a Safer Internet Centre planned or set up in Moldova.
- INT-MDA-0644 Simplification of trade procedures for cross-border e-commerce: unclear. The Interoperable Europe factsheet states that Moldova is simplifying trade procedures for cross-border e-commerce with the EU but does not identify the measures or their status.
- INT-MDA-0686 Two posts in the consumer protection inspectorate legal directorate for advice under the geo-blocking law: proposed. Allocating two posts in the consumer protection inspectorate to advise consumers under the geo-blocking law appears only as a planned action.
