# Fact pack RI-FMW-005: Employment service capacity for EURES and labour mobility

The public employment service lacks trained staff or adequate IT and data systems to take part in EU labour mobility networks and vacancy exchange.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The issue is first raised in the Commission's 2025 key recommendation to reinforce the employment service's IT infrastructure for EURES, when the 2025 report also recorded a new EURES department with a designated officer from January 2025. The 2026 report records technical standards for matching vacancies and CVs with the EURES portal and a new integrated information system developed in December 2025, aiming at fully digitalised services by 2027, while widening the recommendation to systems for EESSI and the EHIC. The chapter rating was not decisive, as the issue is only part of the chapter. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0022;MDA-EU-2025-001-0068;MDA-EU-2026-012-0090;MDA-EU-2026-012-0142;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The employment agency has a Labour Migration and EURES directorate (five of six posts filled) and technical standards for exchange with the EURES portal, but the interoperability of its information system with EURES, staff training on EURES and the rules on personal data in that exchange are still planned. The agency's own reports for 2024 and 2025 say it lacks a modern information system, and the new system, funded through the EU Delegation, is still being developed. What remains is a working connection between the agency's information system and the EURES platform, operated by trained staff, before accession. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0020;MDA-EU-2026-012-0090;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0107;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0108;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0110;MDA-GOV-2025-004-0021;MDA-GOV-2026-004-0027;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0028
- priority: Medium (validated). The employment service's IT capacity for EURES is named in the key recommendations of the 2025 and 2026 reports, two reports, and supports a chapter requirement that applies from accession. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on it. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0022;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014
- tractability: Medium (validated). The work sits mainly with one agency, which has already set up a EURES directorate and technical standards, so there is evidence of past progress. The new information system depends on external funding and on technical connection work that is still planned, and the agency reports unfilled posts within its staff ceiling. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0068;MDA-EU-2026-012-0090;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0028;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0018;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0019

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0076 angajat.md portal and mobile app: in_practice. The employment agency reports 637,000 visits and 69,000 active users on angajat.md, and an international organisation describes the portal and mobile app as tools young people use to explore job opportunities. New modules, including vacancy import from external sources, were added in 2025.
- INT-MDA-0078 ANOFM annual action plans (2025 and 2026): implemented. The Agency's 2025 activity report presents EURES preparation and an integrated labour market information system among the objectives it pursued in 2025 and repeats them for 2026. The Commission confirms the Agency's EURES preparation steps in 2025, while the integrated information system is not yet shown to be in use.
- INT-MDA-0083 ANOFM employment, counselling and career guidance services: implemented. The National Employment Agency provides employment, counselling and career guidance services, and Government figures show rising referrals for counselling. UNHCR finds that formal employment services such as the Agency remain underused, so the services operate but not at scale for the groups it examined.
- INT-MDA-0085 ANOFM Labour Market information system: unclear. The Commission reports that the National Employment Agency developed a new integrated information system in December 2025, aiming at fully digitalised services by 2027, while the Agency's own 2025 report says the lack of a modern information system affects its services. Neither source says whether the new system is in use, and data exchange with EURES is still being prepared.
- INT-MDA-0156 Connection of the Republic of Moldova to EESSI: proposed. The Commission reports that an EESSI action plan was approved, and the authorities are preparing the digital infrastructure and the ANOFM and CNAS systems for interoperability. The actual connection still depends on technical documentation from the Commission and a technical solution yet to be chosen, so Moldova is not connected to EESSI.
- INT-MDA-0166 Correlation tables between national skills, competences and occupations and ESCO: proposed. The correlation tables with ESCO are planned in the 2025 accession programme. Moldova has designated members of the ESCO Working Group, but no source reports the tables drafted.
- INT-MDA-0179 Designation of ANOFM as EURES National Coordination Office: implemented. The Commission confirms that a Government decision of September 2025 designated the National Employment Agency as the EURES National Coordination Office, that an order of November 2025 set its tasks and the admission procedure for members, and that 10 territorial EURES members have been designated. A dedicated Labour Migration and EURES department has existed since January 2025, but the work so far is preparatory to joining EURES.
- INT-MDA-0209 Draft ANOFM orders implementing EURES: proposed. The orders appear as drafts in the 2025 accession programme. The Commission reports in 2026 that the technical standards, procedures and formats for EURES were developed, but no source reports that the orders were issued.
- INT-MDA-0406 Interoperability of the ANOFM information system with the EURES platform: proposed. The Commission reports that technical standards, procedures and formats for matching vacancies and CVs with the EURES portal were developed. Interoperability of the ANOFM system with EURES is still described as a measure to be carried out, and no source reports it working.
- INT-MDA-0492 Minimum requirements for processing personal data in data exchange with EURES: proposed. Drafting minimum requirements for processing personal data in EURES data exchange is a planned action, and no source reports them approved.
- INT-MDA-0534 National Employment Programme 2022-2026: adopted. The ETF reports that the National Employment Programme 2022-2026 aims to strengthen the employment agency and digitalise active labour market services. The linked item describes its aims, not its implementation.
- INT-MDA-0564 New integrated information system of the National Employment Agency (EU Delegation funding): proposed. The Commission reports that the National Employment Agency developed a new integrated information system in December 2025, aiming at fully digitalised services by 2027, and a 2026 Government document says the system, funded by the EU Delegation, is still being developed. Neither source reports it in use.
- INT-MDA-0591 Preparation for Moldova's integration into the EURES network: implemented. The Commission reported in 2024 that an action plan for joining EURES had been drafted and the National Employment Agency designated. By 2026 it records a Government Decision of September 2025 setting the national framework, an Agency order of November 2025 on the National Coordination Office, ten territorial EURES members and technical standards for matching with the EURES portal; joining itself happens only on accession.
- INT-MDA-0631 Revision of job descriptions on the angajat.md portal to meet EURES criteria: proposed. Revising job descriptions on angajat.md to meet EURES criteria is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0676 Training of ANOFM staff (EURES, Law No. 200/2025 and serving EU citizens): proposed. Training of employment agency staff on EURES and on serving EU citizens under Law No. 200/2025 is planned in the National Accession Programme. A search of all the country's items found no record that this training has taken place; the Commission notes in 2026 only that the Agency focused on staff training, without saying on what. The Government items are machine-translated.
