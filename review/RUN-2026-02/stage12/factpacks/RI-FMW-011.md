# Fact pack RI-FMW-011: Access to healthcare for EU citizens through the European Health Insurance Card

EU citizens can use the European Health Insurance Card or access medical services only where a bilateral arrangement exists, so access is not yet available to citizens of all Member States.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). Across all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, the EU items on the European Health Insurance Card concern its domestic preconditions: the 2025 sub-area wording records no progress on the Card and the 2026 report a feasibility study and an action plan, and these are used for the issue on those preconditions. None states whether EU citizens can obtain healthcare in Moldova with the Card, and the Government stated in 2025 that the EU acts on the Card are not transposed, so two points in time with no assessed change in access are not enough to set a direction. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0010;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0063;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0058;MDA-EU-2025-001-0075;MDA-EU-2026-012-0097;MDA-EU-2026-012-0098
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The Government stated in 2025 that the EU acts on the European Health Insurance Card are not transposed and listed applying the Card and a draft law on cross-border medical care as planned. In 2026 the Commission reported a feasibility study on the Card, finalised in August 2026, and an EHIC Action Plan approved the same month. What remains is a decision on introducing the Card and the legal and financial arrangements that would give EU citizens access to healthcare through it. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0063;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0058;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0092;MDA-EU-2026-012-0010;MDA-EU-2026-012-0097;MDA-EU-2026-012-0098
- priority: Medium (validated). The Card is part of the Chapter 2 acquis on social security coordination and is named in the key recommendations of the 2025 and 2026 reports, as IT infrastructure and capacity for the EHIC scheme to be developed alongside the Electronic Exchange of Social Security Information. Two reports fall short of the three-report test, which keeps it below High. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0010;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014
- tractability: Medium (validated). Preparation has started: a feasibility study on the Card's impact on public finances was completed and a draft law on cross-border medical care is planned. The study's focus on public finances points to cost as the main constraint, and the international cooperation section dealing with Member State institutions has three specialists. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0010;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0116;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0092;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0493

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0243 Draft law amending certain acts on cross-border medical care in EU Member States, the EEA and Switzerland: proposed. The law on cross-border medical care appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0319 Feasibility study on the introduction and financial impact of the European Health Insurance Card: implemented. The Commission reports that the feasibility study on the financial impact of introducing the European Health Insurance Card was finalised in August 2026.
- INT-MDA-0410 Introduction of the European Health Insurance Card in Moldova: proposed. The Commission found no progress on the European Health Insurance Card in 2025, and in 2026 reports only that an EHIC Action Plan was approved as a roadmap for operational implementation. The legal amendments needed to introduce the card were planned for 2026 and no source shows them adopted or the card issued.
