# Fact pack RI-TEC-029: Customs IT systems not aligned with EU electronic customs

National customs IT systems are not yet aligned with the EU electronic customs strategic plan, and upgrades such as the New Computerised Transit System phases, export and import control systems are delayed or understaffed.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 29 rating covers more than this issue and is context only; it recorded good progress in 2025 and 2026, naming the New Computerised Transit System both times. The issue's own items move from uncertainty about the transit system in 2021 and requests for an IT strategy in 2023 and 2024, to migration to NCTS Phase 5 in October 2024, Phase 6 in September 2025, full accession to the Common Transit Convention in November 2025 and a fully operational Customs Decisions System by June 2026. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0034;MDA-EU-2026-012-0029;MDA-EU-2021-001-0008;MDA-EU-2023-001-0106;MDA-EU-2024-001-0032;MDA-EU-2025-001-0103;MDA-EU-2026-012-0146;MDA-EU-2026-012-0147;MDA-EU-2026-006-0002
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The 2026 report asks Moldova to adopt the Customs Code amendments that give the legal basis for EU-interoperable IT systems and to advance the national customs IT programme approved in October 2025, of whose 25 systems three are operational. The Commission notes that implementation remains incomplete because of transitional provisions and a lack of IT tools, and the Government states that the programme has no budget coverage. What remains is the Customs Code amendments and most of the programme's systems, including the core interconnectivity systems and a Single Window Environment for Customs. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0030;MDA-EU-2026-012-0031;MDA-EU-2026-012-0151;MDA-EU-2026-012-0152;MDA-EU-2026-012-0148;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0535
- priority: High (validated). Customs IT is a key recommendation in every Commission report from 2023 to 2026, and accession to the Common Transit Convention, the transit system and the Customs Decisions System are steps of the Reform Agenda. Interoperability with the EU electronic customs environment is central to the customs chapter and to the interoperability theme. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0106;MDA-EU-2024-001-0032;MDA-EU-2025-001-0035;MDA-EU-2026-012-0031;MDA-EU-2025-002-0018;MDA-EU-2025-002-0019
- tractability: High (validated). The Customs Service delivered the transit system, Convention accession and the Customs Decisions System close to the dates set, the Commission judged the related Reform Agenda steps satisfactorily fulfilled, and EU expert support continues. The lack of budget coverage for the 2025 to 2030 programme, reported by the Government, is the main risk to keeping that pace. Items: MDA-EU-2026-004-0011;MDA-EU-2026-006-0004;MDA-EU-2026-012-0146;MDA-EU-2024-002-0009;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0535

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0042 Accession of Moldova to the Common Transit Convention: implemented. The Commission reports that Moldova formally acceded to the Common Transit Convention in November 2025, after its New Computerised Transit System was connected to the common transit network. No source yet describes transit operations under the Convention.
- INT-MDA-0168 Customs Decisions System (CDS): implemented. The Commission confirms that Government Decision No. 222/2026 of 1 May 2026 set the legal framework for the Customs Decisions System, that 8 of 17 modules were in place by January 2026, after operators could first submit applications digitally from December 2024, and that by June 2026 all 17 types of applications and authorisations could be submitted and processed electronically. No source yet gives evidence of the volume of use.
- INT-MDA-0169 Customs Service Orders No. 259-O/2026 and No. 272-O/2026 on the Customs Decisions System: adopted. The Commission reports that Customs Service orders No. 259-O/2026 and No. 272-O/2026 were adopted in June 2026, setting data requirements and regulating use of the Customs Decisions System.
- INT-MDA-0258 Draft Law amending the Customs Code: proposed. The Commission refers to a draft law amending the Customs Code that is to provide the legal basis for full automation of customs procedures. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0343 Government Decision No. 222/2026 on the Customs Decisions System: implemented. The Commission reports that Government Decision No. 222/2026 was adopted on 1 May 2026 and established the legal framework for the Customs Decisions System, and lists the national system among the customs systems currently operational.
- INT-MDA-0386 Import Control System 2 (ICS2): proposed. The Commission reports a monitoring mission's finding that technical arrangements for ICS2 are pending implementation.
- INT-MDA-0412 IT solution for the Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) status procedure: implemented. The Commission reported in 2024 that the procedure for obtaining Authorised Economic Operator status was digitalised through an IT solution. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0413 IT Strategy for the Customs Service 2024-2027: proposed. The Commission reported in 2024 that the draft Customs Service IT strategy was pending Government approval, and in 2025 it still recommended approving a multi-annual IT programme for customs. No source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0487 Methodological rules on the use of the Anti-Fraud Information System: adopted. The Commission reports that the methodological rules on the use of the Anti-Fraud Information System were approved in September 2026.
- INT-MDA-0545 National Programme for modernising and harmonising customs information systems with EU information systems 2025-2030: adopted. The Commission confirms that the Government approved the National Programme and Action Plan for harmonising customs information systems for 2025-2030 in October 2025. The Government notes that there is no budget coverage for implementing the programme.
- INT-MDA-0561 New Computerised Transit System (NCTS): in_practice. The Commission reports that Moldova migrated to NCTS Phase 5 nationwide on 1 October 2024, completed the Phase 6 rollout in September 2025 and connected it to the common transit network in November 2025, and lists it among the operational customs systems. Nationwide use for transit declarations shows application at scale.
- INT-MDA-0690 Use of TRACES (TRACES NT) by Moldova: implemented. The Commission described Moldova in 2023 as an active participant in TRACES NT with agreed harmonised certificates, but in 2026 it reports Moldova still preparing for full TRACES-NT integration, and the Government notes that full integration with EU systems such as TRACES remains to be aligned.

## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0561 leads to MDA-EU-2026-012-0147: success worked, attribution High. The Commission reports that full accession to the Common Transit Convention followed in November 2025 and that the interoperability of customs operations was significantly enhanced, and it records NCTS Phase 6 connected to the common transit network. This is an independent EU finding of an effect beyond the system's launch.
