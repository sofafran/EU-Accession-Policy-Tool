# Fact pack RI-FMW-008: Gaps in bilateral social security and healthcare agreements

Bilateral social security coordination and healthcare agreements do not yet cover all EU Member States or all branches, so workers moving before accession lack coordinated protection.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 2 rating covers more than this issue and is context only, but its progress statements of 2024, 2025 and 2026 turn on the bilateral social security agreements, moving from limited progress in 2023 and 2024 to good progress in 2025 and 2026. The issue's own items show agreements added in each year: Spain signed and Greece in force by 2023, Spain and Latvia in force in 2024, Italy and France signed in October 2024 and March 2025, Italy in force in September 2025 and Slovakia signed in March 2026. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0042;MDA-EU-2023-001-0061;MDA-EU-2023-001-0062;MDA-EU-2024-001-0015;MDA-EU-2024-001-0061;MDA-EU-2025-001-0017;MDA-EU-2025-001-0072;MDA-EU-2026-012-0009;MDA-EU-2026-012-0091
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Commission asked in each report from 2023 to 2026 for further social security coordination agreements with Member States, adding in 2026 a request for gap analyses on the coordination regulations, and it reports in 2026 that negotiations with other Member States should continue. The Government reported in 2025 agreements with 17 of the 27 Member States, its own count, and the Slovakia agreement signed in March 2026 is not yet reported in force. What remains is agreements with the Member States not yet covered, entry into force of those signed, and the gap analyses on the coordination regulations. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0045;MDA-EU-2024-001-0017;MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0013;MDA-EU-2026-012-0091;MDA-EU-2026-012-0093;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0495;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0059
- priority: High (validated). Concluding further agreements with Member States is a key recommendation in all four Commission reports from 2023 to 2026, and the Chapter 2 progress statements of 2024 to 2026 rest on it, so it is central to the chapter. Agreements have been added each year, but the recommendation is still repeated in 2026. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0045;MDA-EU-2024-001-0017;MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0013;MDA-EU-2024-001-0015;MDA-EU-2026-012-0009
- tractability: Medium (validated). The Commission records agreements signed or brought into force in every year from 2023 to 2026, negotiated by an established institution, the National Social Insurance House. The Government itself says it cannot commit to a number of agreements because the pace depends on the political will of the other states, so completion is not in Moldova's hands alone. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0062;MDA-EU-2024-001-0061;MDA-EU-2025-001-0072;MDA-EU-2026-012-0091;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0065;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0496
