# Fact pack RI-TEC-014: Broadband planning and investment framework incomplete

National broadband targets, plans and investment are not aligned with EU Digital Decade targets, and EU funding instruments, State aid rules and backhaul studies are not used or in place.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 and 2024 reports call for more investment in infrastructure, including to overcome the rural-urban digital divide, and the 2025 Reform Agenda notes the absence of a comprehensive broadband mapping system. In 2026 the Commission found that the regulator's coverage platform and a new map of very high capacity networks provide relevant monitoring for investment decisions, while the 2026 report says that infrastructure investment remains well below needs. The issue spans several chapters, so chapter ratings were not decisive. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0031;MDA-EU-2024-001-0013;MDA-EU-2025-002-0025;MDA-EU-2026-006-0018;MDA-EU-2026-006-0022;MDA-EU-2026-012-0161
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The mapping system is in operation, but the geographical survey of broadband coverage has a legal deadline of December 2030, the development of networks in white areas waits for it, and the state aid rules for broadband exist only as a draft. What remains is a national investment framework, with state aid rules and funding, that brings broadband to rural and white areas in line with EU connectivity targets. Items: MDA-EU-2026-006-0022;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0054;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0055;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0532;MDA-EU-2026-012-0161
- priority: Medium (validated). The broadband mapping system was a Reform Agenda step, now fulfilled, and the 2023 and 2024 key recommendations on competitiveness ask for infrastructure investment, but they name digital infrastructure alongside energy and transport. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation specific to broadband planning. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0039;MDA-EU-2026-006-0028;MDA-EU-2023-001-0031;MDA-EU-2024-001-0013
- tractability: Medium (validated). The mapping step was delivered by 2026 through data sharing among the regulator, the cadastre agency and operators, and a World Bank project is analysing financing models. Extending networks to small and isolated localities needs substantial investment, and the Government's own timetable for the coverage survey runs to 2030. Items: MDA-EU-2026-006-0019;MDA-EU-2026-006-0020;MDA-GOV-2026-014-0069;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0023;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0054

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0014 MCloud: implemented. The Commission counts MCloud among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and RUSI notes that e-government services have adopted cloud solutions such as MCloud. The Court of Accounts found information systems developed without alignment with MCloud and other government platforms, and none of the linked items measures independently how far the platform is used.
- INT-MDA-0133 Broadband coverage mapping system and Single Information Point: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that the regulator operates a public broadband coverage platform, expanded since 2025, and has put into operation a Very High Capacity Networks map populated with data from the three mobile operators and the six largest fixed operators. No source shows the Single Information Point in operation, and in January 2026 the Government was still agreeing a joint action plan for the wider mapping system.
- INT-MDA-0151 Concept of the Register of Utility Networks information system (draft Government Decision): proposed. The Government's 2025 progress report says drafting of the decision approving the concept of the Register of Utility Networks had been initiated by 31 December 2025. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0186 Development of electronic communications networks in identified white areas: proposed. Development of networks in white areas is planned in the digital strategy's implementation programme, and the Government's 2025 progress report says it can start only after the regulator's geographic survey under the new Electronic Communications Law and the broadband mapping system. The items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0194 Digital Society Development Programme: unclear. The Digital Society Development Programme was planned as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy, and an international partner reported in 2024 that the Ministry was developing them. The Government later reports that the three programmes were merged into a single programme, the Implementation Programme 2025-2027, so this programme was not adopted on its own.
- INT-MDA-0210 Draft ANRC Board decisions implementing the European Electronic Communications Code: unclear. The Government listed these regulator board decisions as drafts in 2025. The regulator reports that it adopted the regulatory acts needed to implement the new electronic communications framework and continues drafting others in 2026, and the Commission reports approximated secondary legislation, but no source names these specific decisions as adopted.
- INT-MDA-0213 Draft Competition Council Plenum decision on state aid for broadband networks: proposed. A Competition Council decision adopting a regulation on state aid for broadband networks was planned for August 2026. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0214 Draft Electronic Communications Law: adopted. The draft became Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025, in force from 1 January 2026, and the Commission reports primary legislation transposing the European Electronic Communications Code. The regulator was still drafting implementing acts in 2026.
- INT-MDA-0260 Draft law on access to property and use of infrastructure (Gigabit Infrastructure Act): adopted. The Commission reports that the law on access to property for public service providers, aligning with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act, was adopted in June 2026. No source yet reports its application.
- INT-MDA-0330 Geographical survey of broadband network coverage (ANRC): proposed. The regulator is to carry out a geographical survey of broadband coverage under Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025, in force since 1 January 2026, with a legal deadline of 30 December 2030. No source shows the survey under way.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0439 Law No. 28/2016 on access to property and shared use of infrastructure for electronic communications networks: implemented. The law has applied since 2016, and the regulator reports about 21 administrative procedures under it, 218 entities that have submitted access conditions and 1,932 registered access contracts at the end of 2025. These figures come from the regulator's own reporting, which also records uneven application and disputes over access fees.
- INT-MDA-0453 Law No. 72/2025 on electronic communications: adopted. Parliament adopted the law on 10 April 2025 and the Commission confirms it entered into force on 1 January 2026. The regulator was renamed under it, but in 2026 the secondary acts needed to implement it were still being drafted, and a civil society source notes only partial compatibility with Directive (EU) 2018/1972.
- INT-MDA-0500 Mobile services offers comparator: implemented. The regulator presented its mobile services offers comparator among its digital consumer tools. No source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0519 National broadband strategy: unclear. The Commission's 2023 enlargement Communication says national broadband strategies were developed in Moldova and Georgia. It does not say whether Moldova's strategy was adopted or how it is applied.
- INT-MDA-0530 National Development Strategy "European Moldova 2030": adopted. The National Development Strategy 'European Moldova 2030' was approved by Law No. 315/2022, and the Commission notes its objective of universal access to the internet and electronic services. No source assesses progress against that objective.
- INT-MDA-0548 National Programme for the Accession of the Republic of Moldova to the European Union 2025-2029 (Government Decision No. 306/2025): implemented. The programme was approved by Government Decision No. 306/2025, and ministries cite its actions when drafting laws, such as the Chapter 2 action on unemployment benefit coordination and the data governance law. The evidence on its delivery comes only from government sources.
- INT-MDA-0613 Reform Agenda of Moldova under the Reform and Growth Facility: adopted. The Commission's Reform Agenda document confirms that Moldova has established its Reform Agenda, which sets out the digital, cybersecurity and connectivity reforms with deadlines to 2027. These items give no evidence on how far the reforms have been delivered.
- INT-MDA-0617 Register of Utility Networks (RERE) information system: proposed. On 31 December 2025 the cadastre agency was still testing the Register of Utility Networks. No source shows it launched.
- INT-MDA-0681 Transposition of all EU electronic communications acts by end-2027: proposed. The Government plans to transpose all EU electronic communications acts by the end of 2027, starting with the Gigabit Infrastructure Regulation. No source reports completion.
- INT-MDA-0685 Trilateral agreement on spatial data for the VHCN map: adopted. The Commission records that the regulator, the Agency for Geodesy, Cartography and Cadastre and the national cadastre entered into a trilateral agreement on 18 March 2026 to share spatial datasets for the very high capacity network map. The linked items do not yet report the map itself.
- INT-MDA-0688 Universal Service and Access Fund: insufficient_evidence. The only item is the 2023 strategy's proposal to expand use of the Universal Service and Access Fund, which gives no evidence on the fund's status.
