# Fact pack RI-TEC-023: Operational cybersecurity capacity of public institutions weak

The national incident response centre and state institutions lack staff, expertise and secure infrastructure, so government services and digital infrastructure remain exposed to attacks.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Chapter 10 scale is context only. In 2021 the national CERT was pending approval of its mandate, in 2024 and 2025 the Commission asked for further effort on the operational capacity of the new Cybersecurity Agency, and the Reform Agenda found it short of staff, IT resources and legal authority. In 2026 the Commission reports the Agency operational as single point of contact and national CERT, with 32 staff by December 2025 and an incident platform in use, and judged the related Reform Agenda step satisfactorily fulfilled. Items: MDA-EU-2021-001-0003;MDA-EU-2024-001-0085;MDA-EU-2025-001-0093;MDA-EU-2025-002-0031;MDA-EU-2026-012-0124;MDA-EU-2026-004-0012;MDA-EU-2026-004-0015;MDA-EU-2026-004-0033
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Reform Agenda step on making the Agency fully operational with a national CERT is judged fulfilled, but the Agency's 2025 report says supervision was only partly carried out for lack of staff and a complete framework, its SIEM and incident playbooks were incomplete, and it lacks qualified IT specialists. The Government reports that cyber incidents rose by over 30 percent between 2021 and 2023, and the 2026 report notes security vulnerabilities in the border police information system. What remains is enough qualified staff and tools for the Agency and CERT to supervise providers and protect state systems. Items: MDA-EU-2026-004-0033;MDA-GOV-2026-010-0036;MDA-GOV-2026-010-0018;MDA-GOV-2026-010-0024;MDA-GOV-2026-010-0010;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0238;MDA-EU-2026-012-0061
- priority: Medium (validated). Operational capacity of the Agency was named in the 2024 and 2025 reports and in a Reform Agenda step, but the Commission judged that step satisfactorily fulfilled, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no 2026 Commission recommendation on the Agency's capacity. A fulfilled condition with no repeat of the requirement in 2026 does not on its own make the issue High, although it stays central to the cybersecurity theme. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0085;MDA-EU-2025-001-0094;MDA-EU-2025-002-0036;MDA-EU-2026-004-0033;MDA-EU-2026-012-0159
- tractability: Medium (validated). The Agency went from its launch in 2024 to an operational CERT in 2025, with substantial EU and bilateral support in experts, equipment, software and training. The Agency names recruitment of qualified specialists and limited own resources for equipment as its constraints. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0047 Agency for Cyber Security (establishment and operationalisation): implemented. The Agency was established by Government Decision No. 1028/2023 in December 2023 and launched in February 2024, and the Commission reports in 2026 that it occupies its offices with equipment installed and has been further strengthened. It was still recruiting in 2025, with 20 to 24 of about 45 posts filled, and the Commission finds that further efforts on operational capacity are needed, so there is no evidence yet of full operation at scale.
- INT-MDA-0098 Audit of the Real Estate Cadastre information system subsystems and their security: implemented. The Government reports that an audit of the Real Estate Cadastre information subsystems and their security was launched on 20 November 2025. No source reports its results.
- INT-MDA-0163 Cooperation with Lithuania (DNS firewall): implemented. The Cybersecurity Agency reports cooperation with Lithuania covering PESCO projects and technical partnership, including on a DNS firewall. This is the Agency's own account and no results are given.
- INT-MDA-0170 Cyber exercises (CTF and tabletop exercises) with authorities and service providers: implemented. The Cybersecurity Agency reports that it held capture-the-flag and tabletop exercises in 2025 with public and private participants, jointly with Romanian and Ukrainian counterparts and EU mission experts. This is the Agency's own account and is not corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0171 Cyber Security Agency 2025 action plan: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that most actions in its 2025 plan were fully implemented and some partly or carried over, mainly because of resource constraints and pending regulation. The Commission confirms that the Agency became operational in 2025, but the account of the plan's delivery is the Agency's own and is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0173 Cybersecurity training for civil servants: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports courses for its own technical staff and thematic courses for CSIRT and public institution staff in 2025, so training has started on a small scale. A comment in the Government's draft implementation programme notes that it contains no specific actions for training civil servants, and all items are the Government's own machine-translated documents.
- INT-MDA-0224 Draft Government Decision approving the framework for the national cyber incident and crisis response plan: proposed. The decision on the national cyber incident and crisis response plan appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0228 Draft Government Decision on coordinated response to large-scale cybersecurity incidents and crises: proposed. A draft Government decision on coordinated response to large-scale cybersecurity incidents and crises appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0240 Draft Government Decision on the Regulation on the State Register of Cyber Incidents: proposed. A draft Government decision approving the regulation on the State Register of Cyber Incidents appears only in the Government's plans. No source shows it adopted.
- INT-MDA-0333 Government CERT (CERT-GOV-MD) within STISC: implemented. The Commission confirms that the Information Technology and Cyber Security Service acts as the government's computer security incident response team. It had a staff of four in November 2022, and no source gives evidence of the incidents it handles.
- INT-MDA-0335 Government cyber event disclosure platform (cyberevent.gov.md): implemented. A civil society source reports that STISC launched the cyberevent.gov.md platform in June 2024. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0337 Government Decision No. 1028/2023 on the establishment, organisation and functioning of the Agency for Cyber Security: in_practice. The Decision of December 2023 established the Cyber Security Agency. The Commission states that the Agency became operational as the national single point of contact and CERT, and the Agency reports exercising its powers, including 265 designations of service providers in 2025.
- INT-MDA-0365 Government Decision No. 822/2025 on the Concept of the State Register of Cyber Incidents information system and its Regulation: adopted. The Cyber Security Agency reports that Government Decision No. 822/2025 approved the concept and regulation of the State Register of Cyber Incidents. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0373 Government public key infrastructure: implemented. The 2023 Strategy states that STISC manages the Government's public key infrastructure, and IPRE cites STISC data of about 129,000 active qualified signature certificates in 2022. The figures come from STISC itself.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0392 Information exchange with ANSSI (France) on the network sensor system: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it exchanges information with France's ANSSI on the network sensor system. The linked item is the Agency's own machine-translated report.
- INT-MDA-0446 Law No. 48/2023 on cybersecurity: implemented. The law has been in force since January 2025, and the Commission confirms that it empowers the Cybersecurity Agency to conduct audits. The Agency reports that it sent 307 notifications and issued 265 acts designating essential and important service providers in 2025, which is its own account and is not yet corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0474 Mandatory reporting of significant cyber incidents: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it records cyber incidents in a register, 40 by the end of 2025, and reports incidents with significant impact on critical infrastructure to the Intelligence and Security Service. The Commission confirms that 38 incidents were handled in 2025, but the reporting obligations on providers are described only by the Agency.
- INT-MDA-0481 Membership of FIRST: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that its technical team became a member of FIRST in 2025. The linked item is the Agency's own machine-translated report.
- INT-MDA-0483 Membership of the European Cyber Security Organisation (ECSO): implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it became a member of the European Cyber Security Organisation on 25 June 2025.
- INT-MDA-0485 Memorandum of Understanding with the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre (ECCC): implemented. The Cybersecurity Agency reports that it signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the European Cybersecurity Competence Centre in 2025, giving access to courses and technical support. This is the Agency's own account.
- INT-MDA-0501 Modernisation and interconnection of border management information systems: proposed. The Government plans to modernise border management information systems, interconnect them with EU systems and introduce biometric capture at crossing points. The Commission finds in 2026 that the border police information system still has security and data protection vulnerabilities, and no source shows the modernisation delivered.
- INT-MDA-0504 Moldova Cyber Security Forum: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it held the two-day Moldova Cyber Security Forum in April 2025, including a capture-the-flag exercise.
- INT-MDA-0508 Moldova's access to the EU Cybersecurity Reserve: implemented. The Commission reports that the Council renewed Moldova's access to the EU Cybersecurity Reserve for a further year in July 2026. The Cyber Security Agency reports that it activated the mechanism successfully, but this rests on its own account.
- INT-MDA-0510 Monitoring of domain names in Moldova's Internet address space and the .md domain: implemented. The Cyber Security Agency reports that domain names in Moldova's internet address space were scanned and monitored continuously in 2025. This rests on the Agency's own report.
- INT-MDA-0520 National CERT (Cyber Incident and Crisis Response Directorate) of the Agency for Cyber Security: in_practice. The CERT directorate was set up by Government Decision No. 1028/2023, and the Commission states that it became fully operational in 2025 and that its cyber incident management platform is installed and used by the team. The Agency's own report records 40 incidents and 52 early warnings in 2025, so the Commission's account is matched by evidence of incidents actually handled.
- INT-MDA-0525 National Cyber Incident and Crisis Response Plan: proposed. Government Decision No. 823/2025 approved the regulation on drafting, updating and implementing the National Response Plan for cyber incidents and crises, according to the Government and the Cybersecurity Agency. No source shows that the plan itself has been drawn up or approved.
- INT-MDA-0527 National Cybersecurity Programme 2026-2030: adopted. The Commission confirms that the National Cybersecurity Programme 2026-2030 was adopted in December 2025. The linked items do not yet report on its implementation, which began only in 2026.
- INT-MDA-0531 National digital identity wallet: implemented. The Commission reports that a national digital identity wallet was launched within the EVO application in June 2024. The wallet aligned with the EU Digital Identity framework is a further version: the Government says development started in September 2025, the Commission reports cross-border interoperability tests in 2026, and the Reform Agenda sets its launch for December 2026.
- INT-MDA-0539 National Information Security Strategy and Action Plan 2019-2024: adopted. The strategy ran from 2019 to 2024. The Commission reported in 2021 that the national CERT foreseen by it was still pending, and in 2025 that it had lapsed without addressing new threats, so no source shows its measures carried out.
- INT-MDA-0540 National Institute for Innovation in Cybersecurity (Cybercor): implemented. The Commission reports that Moldova launched the National Institute for Cyber Security in February 2024. No source describes its activity.
- INT-MDA-0583 Operating regulation of the CERT directorate: adopted. The Government reports that the operating regulation of the Cybersecurity Agency's CERT directorate was drafted and approved.
- INT-MDA-0600 Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030: proposed. The ministry was developing the Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030 in 2024 as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy. No source shows that it has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0621 Regulation on technical administration and maintenance of state information resources and systems (Government Decision No. 414/2018): adopted. Government Decision No. 822 of 2020 added the regulation on technical administration and maintenance of state information systems to Government Decision No. 414/2018. No source reports how the regulation is applied.
- INT-MDA-0657 State Register of Cyber Incidents (RSIC): adopted. Government Decision No. 822/2025 approved the concept of the register's information system and the regulation on keeping it. The Cyber Security Agency reports that the terms of reference for building the register are still being drafted, so the register is not yet in operation.
