# Fact pack RI-TEC-028: Interoperability platform and data exchange underused

Institutions do not use the national interoperability platform regularly, so administrative data are not shared ex officio between institutions, including for checks such as asset declarations and inspections.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission reported in 2023 that data exchange through MConnect let the electoral commission check party financing more effectively, but in 2025 it said interoperability between automated verification systems and state registries must still be strengthened, and in 2026 it noted constraints on the full practical functioning of interoperable verification tools. Other sources show connections continuing (336 institutions in 2023 according to SIGMA; 396 integrated state and private systems in 2025 according to the Government, a different measure) while the same underuse is reported: the World Bank in 2024 found interoperability yet to be streamlined, the government in 2025 found systems fragmented, and the data protection authority in 2026 found state bodies still using COI access outside the platform. The Chapter 10 rating was not decisive, as the issue is cross-cutting. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0007;MDA-EU-2025-001-0013;MDA-EU-2026-012-0049;MDA-IO-2023-001-0044;MDA-IO-2024-003-0008;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0094;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0029;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0093
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The platform and the legal duty to exchange data through it are in place, with sanctions since 2021, and SIGMA found the base registers connected to it in 2023. The data protection authority finds that state bodies still access personal data through COI technology outside the platform, which conflicts with the government's statement that data exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect, and the Commission in 2026 still finds the interoperable verification tools for asset declarations not fully functioning. What remains is regular use of the platform by all institutions, including an end to access outside it, and full data exchange for asset declaration checks. Items: MDA-IO-2023-001-0043;MDA-GOV-2021-002-0009;MDA-IO-2023-001-0007;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0029;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0073;MDA-EU-2026-012-0049
- priority: Medium (validated). Interoperability for asset declaration checks is part of a Chapter 23 Reform Agenda step on the new e-Integrity system, with interoperability ensured through MConnect, is named in the 2025 main findings on Chapter 23 and is repeated as a finding in 2026. These EU items concern only asset declaration checks, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no benchmark, key recommendation or Reform Agenda payment condition on use of the platform as a whole, although regular use of the platform is central to the interoperability theme. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0050;MDA-EU-2025-001-0013;MDA-EU-2026-012-0049
- tractability: Medium (validated). The platform, the law and sanctions already exist and connections have grown, which is evidence of past progress. Against that, the remaining problem lies with many institutions that the World Bank says work in silos, and the data protection authority reports that bodies notified repeatedly have still not moved to the platform. Items: MDA-IO-2023-001-0044;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0093;MDA-IO-2024-003-0011;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0028

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0003 MCabinet (Citizen Portal): implemented. An international partner reports that MCabinet was launched in October 2020 and gives access to 17 data blocks. The Government reports that unique authenticated users rose from 92,619 in 2023 to 176,855 in the first three quarters of 2024, but this rests on its own reporting.
- INT-MDA-0005 Amendments to the Code of Contraventions on data exchange and interoperability (Article 250 1): adopted. The Government reports that the Code of Contraventions was amended to set fines for breaches of the data exchange and interoperability law, effective from 18 January 2021. The linked items do not report any fines imposed.
- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0008 EVO: implemented. The Commission confirms that the EVO application was launched in June 2024 and EVO 2.0 in June 2026, and that the EVO portal lists 636 public services, 447 of them online. Figures on use (169,692 downloads and 66,733 active users by the end of 2025) come only from the Government's own machine-translated progress report, so use at scale is not independently corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0012 Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the law obliges government agencies to exchange data only through the interoperability solution, and that the MConnect platform it governs is well functioning and used by many public and private organisations. The data protection authority finds that some state bodies still access data outside the platform in breach of the law, and the Government notes that replacing documents with data remains problematic in many cases.
- INT-MDA-0013 Amendments to Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability (Article 6): adopted. The Government reports that Article 6 of Law No. 142/2018 was completed to set an express obligation to connect to the interoperability platform, effective from 18 January 2021. Its application is covered by the evidence on the law and MConnect.
- INT-MDA-0014 MCloud: implemented. The Commission counts MCloud among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and RUSI notes that e-government services have adopted cloud solutions such as MCloud. The Court of Accounts found information systems developed without alignment with MCloud and other government platforms, and none of the linked items measures independently how far the platform is used.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0017 MDelivery: implemented. The Commission reported in 2023 that a further 42 public services had been integrated with MDelivery, which delivers requested documents to the user's home, and the Government lists it among platforms delivered under the modernisation project. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0018 MDocs: implemented. The Government lists MDocs among the new platforms developed, and an international partner gives it the same operating status as the other government platforms. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0019 MPass: implemented. The Commission and the Government list MPass among the government platforms implemented, and an international partner describes it as the authentication service for public e-services. Government consultation material gives 208,500 identifiers authenticated from 2023 to September 2024, but no independent source reports on use.
- INT-MDA-0020 MPay: implemented. The Commission counts MPay among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes it as in place since 2020. SIGMA found in 2023 that online payment through MPay was possible but not yet integrated into digital service provision, and the Government's figures on savings and on payments collected through MPay are not corroborated by an independent source.
- INT-MDA-0021 MPower: implemented. An international partner reports that MPower was launched in 2020 and lets people create electronic powers of attorney with the same value as notarised ones, and the Government lists it among its working platforms. No source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0022 MSign: in_practice. SIGMA reported in 2023 that electronic signatures are available through MSign with about 200,000 active users, mostly businesses required to sign documents for the tax service. Government figures give more than 37 million documents signed through MSign in 2023 and more than 38 million in the first nine months of 2024.
- INT-MDA-0025 Modernization of Government Services project (MGSP): implemented. The World Bank-supported project was carried out by the e-Governance Agency and concluded in 2025, with the Government reporting 110 digitalised services and 94 re-engineered services. All figures on its delivery and results come from the Government and the Agency, without independent corroboration.
- INT-MDA-0027 SIA Colaborator: implemented. The Court of Accounts reports that the Ministry of Internal Affairs runs the SIA Colaborator system and was still analysing whether to connect it to MConnect. No source describes its use or any interconnection.
- INT-MDA-0028 SIA REA (electronic register of employees): implemented. The Court of Accounts treats the electronic register of employees as an information system developed and in use, owned by the Ministry of Finance since July 2022. It found that actions to interconnect it with other human resources systems were only partly implemented, without concrete results, and saw no evidence that data exchange with the health system was functional.
- INT-MDA-0029 SIERUSS: insufficient_evidence. The only item reports a decision to make SIERUSS interoperable with SIA REA and says nothing about the system's own status. The item is machine-translated, so it is capped at Medium confidence.
- INT-MDA-0030 Semantic Catalog for MConnect: implemented. The concept and register regulation were approved by Government Decision No. 323/2021, and the Government lists the Semantic Catalog among its new platforms and reports employment data integrated through it. An international partner finds that the open data portal it supports is still lacking in use.
- INT-MDA-0032 Open Government Data Portal (date.gov.md): implemented. The portal has operated since 2011 and holds about 1,268 datasets, and a 2024 international partner report describes it as a one-stop shop for public sector data. The same body of evidence finds that the portal is still lacking in use and needs updating, so use at scale is not shown.
- INT-MDA-0033 e-APL: implemented. The Government reports that the e-APL portal was developed and launched as a pilot, with 91 mayoralties taking part and nine State Fiscal Register services available through it. The target is use by at least half of first-level local authorities, and all items come from the Government's own machine-translated documents, so the status rests on Medium confidence evidence.
- INT-MDA-0034 e-Consulate: implemented. The e-Governance Agency lists e-Consulate among its seven new digital platforms. This rests on one government item rated Low confidence, and no source gives data on its use.
- INT-MDA-0056 Alignment of the national interoperability framework with the European Interoperability Framework: proposed. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet says the national framework follows the European Interoperability Framework, but the Government notes that the Interoperable Europe Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/903, has not been transposed. Further alignment is planned in the National Accession Programme; these Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0065 Amendment of the Concept and Regulation of SIA RFPFP (Government Activity Plan for 2024, Government Decision No. 887/2023): proposed. The Government Activity Plan for 2024 provides for amending the concept and regulation of SIA RFPFP, and no source reports the amendment adopted. The status rests on one machine-translated item, capped at Medium confidence.
- INT-MDA-0100 Automated information systems of the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection: in_practice. The Commission reports that the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection's social assistance IT system is in place and, since its integration with MConnect in 2022, automates the examination of social and winter aid applications.
- INT-MDA-0102 Automated verification systems: implemented. The Commission's 2025 finding that interoperability between automated verification systems and state registries must still be strengthened shows the systems are in place. It also finds them insufficiently connected and the services understaffed.
- INT-MDA-0104 Base registers: implemented. An international partner describes the base registers, with MConnect, as a centralised means of accessing public data, while noting that some challenges remain.
- INT-MDA-0142 COI technology for access to state registers: in_practice. The data protection authority reports that state bodies continue to access personal data in state registers through COI technology, with 13 entities still using it and only 5 having aligned their access over two years. The e-Governance Agency has asked authorities to end these contracts because COI does not meet security and traceability requirements, but no progress was recorded.
- INT-MDA-0145 Concept and Regulation of SI Colaborator (draft): proposed. The Court of Accounts reports that the Ministry of Internal Affairs drafted the concept and regulation of the Colaborator system, but that they were not supported by the e-Governance Agency and the State Chancellery and their approval was delayed. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0147 Concept of the automated information system Electronic Register of Employees (SIA REA): adopted. The Government approved the Concept of the electronic register of employees in December 2020, and the Court of Accounts found the system was being developed at the time of its audit.
- INT-MDA-0155 Connection of the budget sector 1C programme with SI REA: unclear. The Court of Accounts reports only that in 2022 the Centre for Information Technologies in Finance started connecting the budget sector 1C programme with SI REA on a test platform. The linked item does not say whether the connection was completed. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0176 Dataset on late payment obligations published through MConnect: proposed. The Government's 2025 progress report says the dataset on late payment obligations is still to be defined before publication through MConnect. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0273 Draft Law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova (single no. 507/MAI/2025): proposed. The text is a draft law still under inter-institutional consultation, with comments being accepted and the quota provisions removed. It provides that it would enter into force on 1 June 2027 and repeal Law No. 200/2010, and no source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0285 e-Integrity system: proposed. The Reform Agenda sets the target of all declarations submitted to a new e-Integrity system, and in August 2026 the National Integrity Agency put a new concept for the system out to consultation to replace the current one. The new system is not yet in place.
- INT-MDA-0288 e-Monitoring IT system for policy and planning documents: proposed. The e-Monitoring system is planned, and no source reports it developed or launched.
- INT-MDA-0303 Electronic employer notification platform: proposed. The draft law on foreigners of February 2026 provides for employers to notify the employment of foreigners through an electronic platform, and consultation comments ask for it to be integrated with the interoperability platform. No source shows the platform in place.
- INT-MDA-0321 FOD platform: implemented. The Government lists the FOD platform among seven new platforms delivered under the government services modernisation project. The linked item gives no detail on its use.
- INT-MDA-0336 Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 211/2019 on the interoperability platform (MConnect): proposed. The amendment to Government Decision No. 211/2019 on MConnect appears as a planned act in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0342 Government Decision No. 211/2019 on the interoperability platform (MConnect): in_practice. The Decision regulates the MConnect interoperability platform, and SIGMA reports 665.7 million transactions through it in 2022, so it is applied at scale. The Government states that data exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect, but the data protection authority finds that state bodies continue to access personal data through another technology, so how fully the Decision is applied is disputed.
- INT-MDA-0347 Government Decision No. 376/2020 (MNotify government notification service): adopted. Government Decision No. 376/2020 obliges public authorities to use only the MNotify service for electronic notifications and prohibits similar services. No source describes compliance with it.
- INT-MDA-0348 Government Decision No. 443/2022 (owner of SIA REA): adopted. Amendments to Government Decision No. 443/2022 made the Ministry of Finance the owner of the electronic register of employees from 22 July 2022.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0394 Information system for recording and managing human resources in public authorities (SI EMRU): proposed. The new SI EMRU is planned under the public administration reform programme to be functional in 2026, and in 2026 the data protection authority was still commenting on its design. The Commission found the previous system not functional, and no source reports the new system in operation.
- INT-MDA-0472 M-services shared e-government infrastructure: in_practice. The e-Governance Agency operates the shared M-services infrastructure, and the Interoperable Europe factsheet gives these platforms the same status as MConnect, which independent sources find in wide use. For example, SIGMA reports about 200,000 active MSign users.
- INT-MDA-0496 MLearn platform: implemented. The Government lists MLearn among the reusable digital platforms developed and implemented. This is the Government's own account and no source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0497 MLog governmental logging service: implemented. The data protection authority states that people can consult the history of access to their personal data, drawn from MLog, through a government portal, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes MLog as a logbook of actions in government systems. The linked items do not measure how widely it is used.
- INT-MDA-0498 MNotify government notification service: implemented. The Government lists MNotify among the reusable platforms developed and implemented, and states that public bodies must use it exclusively for electronic notifications. The linked items give no figures on its use, and these items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0503 Modernisation, automation and interoperability of state registers and information systems: proposed. Modernisation, automation and interoperability of state registers are planned in the 2023 Digital Transformation Strategy and in the National Accession Programme. The items describe plans only.
- INT-MDA-0505 Moldova eGovernment Interoperability Framework (MeGIF) Programme (Government Decision No. 656/2012): implemented. The World Bank reports that Moldova launched the eGovernment Interoperability Framework Programme in 2012 and that a pilot of the MConnect data exchange platform was developed under it between 2014 and 2018. No recent source describes the programme as such, its work now being carried by the interoperability law and platform.
- INT-MDA-0512 MTender e-procurement system (State Register of Public Procurement, SIA RSAP): in_practice. The Commission states that MTender has enabled electronic procurement since its launch, with public access to documents, and the Digital Public Administration Factsheet says all public tenders are published on it. The Commission also finds it outdated and not covering the full procurement cycle, and a replacement system is being procured.
- INT-MDA-0567 New series of measures to improve the business environment in the ICT sector: proposed. The Government's 2025 progress report lists a new series of measures for ICT businesses, including further digitalisation of authorisation procedures, as next steps. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0578 Online subsidy application system of the Agency for Intervention and Payments in Agriculture (AIPA): proposed. The online subsidy application system is planned, with a baseline of 1% for online application modules against a 30% target, and no source reports it launched.
- INT-MDA-0584 particip.gov.md online public consultation platform: implemented. The Government lists particip.gov.md among the platforms implemented, and CSO Meter notes continued government improvements to it as part of implementing the open data law. The linked items give no figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0594 Programme for implementing the Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2026 (Government Decision No. 352/2023): adopted. The Programme for implementing the Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2026 was approved by Government Decision No. 352/2023 and plans a new human resources information system to be functional in 2026. No source in these items reports on its implementation.
- INT-MDA-0600 Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030: proposed. The ministry was developing the Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030 in 2024 as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy. No source shows that it has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0607 raportare.gov.md: implemented. The Government lists raportare.gov.md among the reusable platforms developed and implemented. The linked item is machine-translated and gives no figures on use.
- INT-MDA-0610 Real Estate Cadastre information system and Real Estate Register: in_practice. The Commission describes an electronic cadastral system maintained by the Public Services Agency, through which ownership rights are registered via its offices and online systems, and the Government states that the Real Estate Register is digitalised. Modernisation and a new information system are planned, with re-engineering of cadastral services set for 2026.
- INT-MDA-0640 Shared digital units (EGA, ITCSS, PSA, ALRC): proposed. The 2023 strategy assigns the shared digital units a role in supporting its implementation, and no source reports how they have carried out that role.
- INT-MDA-0641 SIA Acces-Web: in_practice. The data protection authority reports continued access to personal data in state information resources through Acces-Web, with large volumes of access by bodies such as the Ministry of Internal Affairs, and limited traceability of users and purposes.
- INT-MDA-0643 SIERUSS Concept (Government Decision No. 24/2024): adopted. The Court of Accounts records that the SIERUSS Concept was approved by Government Decision No. 24/2024 and provides for data exchange with the employee register through MConnect. The audit saw no evidence that the exchange had been put in place or was functional.
- INT-MDA-0648 State Automated Information System Elections (SIASA): implemented. The Government reports that the elections information system has 16 modules, the newest launched in 2025. An audit found its concept had not been updated or aligned with the ICT rules, and no source gives data on its use; the audit item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0650 State Fiscal Register: implemented. The Government reports that nine services from the State Fiscal Register are available to local authorities through the external registers module. This rests on government reporting.
- INT-MDA-0656 State Register of Controls: implemented. The Interoperable Europe factsheet describes the State Register of Controls as a central database recording planned and completed inspections and their results. No source gives figures on its use.
- INT-MDA-0658 State Register of Legal Acts: insufficient_evidence. The only item names the State Register of Legal Acts as a system that the planned e-Monitoring system will connect to and says nothing about its own status.
- INT-MDA-0659 State Register of Legal Units information system (SI RSUD): implemented. The Commission confirms that the State Register of Legal Units portal was launched on 29 December 2025 and that the system enables registration of all legal forms of entities. The Government describes a staged launch in pilot mode, with online applications for name reservation and register extracts available from February 2026, and no source yet gives evidence of use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0661 State Register of Non-Commercial Organisations: in_practice. CSO Meter reports that the register is still the system through which non-commercial organisations are registered, but that it is outdated, not interoperable and slows down registration. A new State Register of Legal Units is being developed but is not yet operational.

## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0015 leads to MDA-EU-2023-001-0007: success worked, attribution High. The Commission, independent of the government, states that data exchange through MConnect enabled the Central Electoral Commission to check political party financing more effectively. The result is narrow and the report gives no measure of it.
- INT-MDA-0643 leads to MDA-GOV-2024-001-0052: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Medium. The impact the audit records is that legal conditions were created for data exchange between the two systems, which is a legal step and not a result, and the audit was given no evidence that the exchange had been carried out or works.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MDA-GOV-2024-003-0016 vs MDA-IO-2023-001-0008: Noted difference, possibly different measures: SIGMA gives 665.7 million MConnect transactions in 2022 alone, while the e-Governance Agency gives 600 million data exchanges in total.
- MDA-GOV-2026-009-0029 vs MDA-GOV-2025-002-0073: The Government says data exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect, while the data protection authority finds that state bodies continue to access personal data through COI technology outside the platform.
