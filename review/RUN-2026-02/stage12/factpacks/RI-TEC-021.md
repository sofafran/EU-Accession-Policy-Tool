# Fact pack RI-TEC-021: Personal data protection in electoral processes weak

The processing of personal data in elections, including biometric data and campaign use, lacks oversight and was introduced without assessing compliance or consulting the data protection authority.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The issue rests on a single item, found by a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files for personal data in elections and campaigns: a 2026 civil society report that illegal surveys in the parliamentary election campaign used personal data databases of unclear origin. No EU item raises it, and one item from one year cannot show a direction. Items: MDA-CSO-2026-001-0035
- unresolved_gap: insufficient_evidence (validated). The single item is a civil society finding, and no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files states an EU requirement on it or describes an intervention responding to it, so the gap cannot be measured against what the EU requires. What remains is, on that one report, oversight of how personal data are obtained, stored and used in election campaigns, which other sources would need to confirm. Items: MDA-CSO-2026-001-0035
- priority: Low (validated). The issue is raised only by one civil society source, the single item on it among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, and not in EU material, which keeps priority from being High. With no EU recommendation or benchmark, it is Low. Items: MDA-CSO-2026-001-0035
- tractability: insufficient_evidence (validated). The single item names no responsible institution and no past action on the issue, so how realistic progress would be cannot be judged. Items: MDA-CSO-2026-001-0035
