# Fact pack RI-FMW-013: Weak corruption prevention in the administration of worker mobility

The bodies administering free movement of workers, notably the employment service, lack a specific integrity policy, internal controls and audit capacity to prevent corruption.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The issue is not raised in EU material: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files for corruption prevention, integrity or internal audit in the bodies administering worker mobility found one item, the employment agency's own report of internal audit missions, one of them on how the EURES network is being implemented. A single item gives no sequence over time. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020
- unresolved_gap: insufficient_evidence (validated). No EU item states a requirement on corruption prevention in the administration of worker mobility: the only Moldova item on this issue, found by a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, is the employment agency's own report of internal audit missions, which says nothing on integrity policy or internal controls. What remains is unknown, since no source states a requirement or a problem for Moldova on this issue. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020
- priority: Low (validated). The only Moldova item on this issue, found by a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files, is the employment agency's own report, not an EU source, so priority cannot be High. The item reports audit activity and does not describe a problem. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020
- tractability: insufficient_evidence (validated). The one item shows that the employment agency has an internal audit service that carried out five assurance missions, but it gives no evidence on the actors, mechanisms or past progress needed to judge how tractable corruption prevention is. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0084 ANOFM internal audit engagement on the implementation of the EURES network: implemented. The employment agency's internal audit service carried out an engagement on how the EURES network is being implemented in Moldova. Its findings are not reported.
