# Fact pack RI-TEC-001: Artificial intelligence framework not aligned or supported by structures

The rules on artificial intelligence are not aligned with the EU Artificial Intelligence Act, or the enforcement structures and wider capacity needed to apply them and support trustworthy uptake are not in place.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Chapter 10 scale is context only, as this issue is part of the AI theme and the scale was not decisive. The Government recorded a lack of legal acts on AI in 2023, the Commission found in 2025 that Moldova is not yet aligned with the AI Act and in 2026 asked it to develop governance, regulatory and enforcement structures, and the Government's 2026 programme document still describes the field as at an early stage with no competent authority or AI-specific framework. The 2024 White Paper is declarative, and a 2026 civil society report says the AI sub-council produced no legislative initiative. Items: MDA-GOV-2023-001-0015;MDA-EU-2025-001-0096;MDA-EU-2026-012-0131;MDA-GOV-2026-003-0007;MDA-GOV-2026-003-0008;MDA-GOV-2026-003-0009;MDA-GOV-2026-003-0016;MDA-CSO-2026-001-0059
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The Commission requires governance, regulatory and enforcement structures in line with the AI Act. A draft AI law, a law on AI testing spaces, the 2026 to 2030 national programme and an AI governance institution are all planned or being drafted, and the Government reported in 2025 that no institution responsible for AI policy or supervision had been identified. What remains is a law aligned with the AI Act, a designated and staffed supervisory authority, and the governance structure to apply it. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0131;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0235;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0236;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0074;MDA-GOV-2026-003-0001
- priority: Medium (validated). AI Act alignment is raised by the Commission in 2025 as a finding and in 2026 as a recommendation, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found it in no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition. It is central to the AI theme but not yet repeated across three reports. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0096;MDA-EU-2026-012-0131
- tractability: Medium (validated). The National Accession Programme plans transposition of the AI Act and a governance institution by 2027, and a consultative sub-council exists. In 2025 no ministry employee covered AI policy, the competent authority was yet to be chosen, and funding for the national programme depends on the annual budget process. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0235;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0167;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0236;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0237;MDA-GOV-2026-003-0017

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0014 MCloud: implemented. The Commission counts MCloud among the government platforms Moldova has implemented, and RUSI notes that e-government services have adopted cloud solutions such as MCloud. The Court of Accounts found information systems developed without alignment with MCloud and other government platforms, and none of the linked items measures independently how far the platform is used.
- INT-MDA-0030 Semantic Catalog for MConnect: implemented. The concept and register regulation were approved by Government Decision No. 323/2021, and the Government lists the Semantic Catalog among its new platforms and reports employment data integrated through it. An international partner finds that the open data portal it supports is still lacking in use.
- INT-MDA-0052 AI factory antenna in Moldova: implemented. The Commission's 2026 enlargement Communication reports that an AI factory antenna was set up in Moldova, as in North Macedonia and Serbia. The linked item gives no detail on its activity.
- INT-MDA-0053 AI scenarios in service delivery and data management: proposed. The Digital Transformation Strategy plans AI scenarios in service delivery and data management. No source shows any such scenario implemented.
- INT-MDA-0097 Association of Moldova to the Digital Europe Programme: adopted. The Commission reports that Parliament ratified the association agreement to the Digital Europe Programme in May 2024, and the Government dates Moldova's joining to 19 February 2024. No source shows Moldovan participation in calls under the programme.
- INT-MDA-0103 Automatic decision-making in the public sector: proposed. The 2023 Digital Transformation Strategy lists automatic decision-making in the public sector as a line of action. The linked item describes the plan only.
- INT-MDA-0167 Council on Artificial Intelligence and Data Governance (SCIA): implemented. The Commission reports that a sub-council on AI and data was set up within the National Digitalisation Council, and civil society monitoring confirms it was established in 2024 with civil society members. However, it did not produce any initiative for a legal framework on AI in 2024 and 2025 as planned.
- INT-MDA-0185 Designation or establishment of an AI governance institution: proposed. The Government intends by 2027 to transpose the EU AI Act, develop a national AI law and designate or set up an institution for AI governance. No source shows the institution designated.
- INT-MDA-0261 Draft law on artificial intelligence: proposed. The law on artificial intelligence is planned, with transposition of the EU AI Act foreseen by the end of 2026 or 2027, and no source reports it drafted or adopted.
- INT-MDA-0272 Draft law on testing and innovation spaces in artificial intelligence and data governance: proposed. The Government reports that the law on testing and innovation spaces in AI and data governance is being drafted.
- INT-MDA-0328 Generative AI tool for transposing EU legislation into national legislation: proposed. The Government reports that the generative AI tool for transposing EU legislation is being piloted and tested, with feedback sent and further testing planned. It is not yet in use.
- INT-MDA-0329 Generative AI virtual assistant on the Public Services Portal and EVO: proposed. Piloting the generative AI assistant on the EVO portal is planned as a 2026 performance indicator for the e-Governance Agency, with a revised deadline, and no source reports the pilot launched.
- INT-MDA-0332 Government Activity Programme "EU, PEACE, DEVELOPMENT" (Parliament Decision No. 269/2025): adopted. The Government Activity Programme 'EU, PEACE, DEVELOPMENT' was approved by Parliament Decision No. 269/2025 and includes a priority on integrating science, technology and data into governance.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0469 List of medium-term public policy priorities 2027-2029: insufficient_evidence. The only item refers to the list of medium-term policy priorities for 2027 to 2029 and its AI priority but does not say whether the list has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0517 National AI Strategy (recommended): insufficient_evidence. The only item is an international partner's recommendation to adopt a National AI Strategy, and no source reports Moldovan action on it.
- INT-MDA-0549 National Programme on Artificial Intelligence and Data Governance 2026-2030: proposed. The concept note sets out a five-year programme to be approved by Government decision, with the ministry intending to draft and promote it in the second quarter of 2026. No source shows that the programme has been approved.
- INT-MDA-0566 New principal consultant posts (AI, semiconductors, trust services) in the MDED IT and digitalisation policy directorate: proposed. The new posts appear only as planned measures in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports that they were created or filled.
- INT-MDA-0570 Ombudsman strategic priority on human rights in digitalisation and the use of AI: adopted. CSO Meter reports that the Ombudsman made guaranteeing human rights in digitalisation and the use of AI a strategic priority. The linked item does not report any action taken under it.
- INT-MDA-0587 Planned national policy document, action plan and governance framework on data governance and AI: proposed. The White Book foresees a later national policy document, action plan and governance framework on data governance and AI, and no source reports them adopted.
- INT-MDA-0614 Regional digital innovation hubs (EDIH) in Moldova: implemented. The Commission reports that the first European Digital Innovation Hub in Moldova was launched in April 2026, after the e-Governance Agency prepared the consortium and application in 2025. No source yet reports on the hub's activity.
- INT-MDA-0667 Study on public sector readiness to adopt AI-based solutions: implemented. The Government reports that the sub-council on data governance and AI coordinated a study on the public sector's readiness to adopt AI-based solutions. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0668 Sub-council for Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence: implemented. The Commission confirms that a sub-council on AI and data was set up within the National Digitalisation Council, and the Government reports that it was created in 2024 and contributed to the White Book on AI and Data Governance and to drafting the national AI programme. The body is set up and working, but there is no independent evidence of its influence.
- INT-MDA-0693 White Book on Data Governance and Artificial Intelligence: unclear. The ministry presented and published the White Paper in February 2024, but the Government itself describes it as declarative and not a policy document as such, and no source shows formal approval. The Commission reports that an AI and data sub-council applies its principles, and its recommendations were to be taken forward in a later national programme.
