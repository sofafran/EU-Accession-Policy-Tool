# Fact pack RI-TEC-013: Broadband and high-capacity network coverage uneven

Small businesses and rural or non-urban areas face obstacles to fixed broadband, and high-capacity and 5G networks are still being rolled out.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). In 2023 the Commission reported that 67.5% of households had internet at home (58% in rural areas), in 2025 that high-speed broadband reached 57% of the population by mid-2024 while regional disparities persisted, and in 2026 that nearly 95% of households had internet access. The 2026 sub-area wording on 5G deployment, which matches this part of the issue, is some progress, with coverage of 25 to 35%; the Chapter 10 rating was used as context only, as the issue is part of the chapter. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0033;MDA-EU-2023-001-0034;MDA-EU-2025-001-0059;MDA-EU-2025-001-0060;MDA-EU-2026-012-0067;MDA-EU-2026-012-0128
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Coverage and take-up have risen, and the Commission's assessment for the third Reform and Growth Facility release records 6.9% more fixed-broadband connections and faster speeds between 2025 and 2026, but 5G coverage remains well below the EU average and the Reform Agenda describes a rural-urban digital divide. The government counts 431 localities in white areas without fixed broadband, and the plan to develop networks there is proposed and waits for the regulator's geographic survey. What remains is high-capacity fixed and 5G coverage in rural and small localities. Items: MDA-EU-2026-006-0023;MDA-EU-2026-006-0026;MDA-EU-2026-012-0069;MDA-EU-2025-002-0056;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0022;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0055
- priority: Medium (validated). The rural-urban digital divide is named in a 2024 economic key recommendation on infrastructure investment, the Reform Agenda describes it and the 2026 report rates 5G deployment, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no Chapter 10 key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on coverage. The related Reform Agenda step concerns alignment with the Electronic Communications Code, which belongs to another issue. Items: MDA-EU-2024-001-0013;MDA-EU-2025-002-0056;MDA-EU-2026-012-0128;MDA-EU-2026-006-0028
- tractability: Medium (validated). Spectrum was awarded through competitions in 2024 and 2025, operators are investing and the Commission records progress on 5G, which is evidence of past progress. Closing the remaining gaps depends on private operators and on public funding for small, isolated localities, which the government says require significant investment. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-014-0033;MDA-GOV-2026-014-0037;MDA-EU-2025-002-0058;MDA-EU-2026-012-0128;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0023

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0087 ANRCETI preparatory process to license 5G frequency bands: implemented. Freedom House reported in 2024 that the regulator had begun preparing to license the 5G frequency bands. The process led to the 2024 competition, whose results were announced in January 2025, with licences issued to three operators in February 2025.
- INT-MDA-0088 ANRCETI Standard Licence Conditions (700 MHz and 800 MHz): adopted. The regulator approved the standard licence conditions for the 700 MHz and 800 MHz bands on 23 August 2024, with coverage obligations due by October 2027 and 2035. No source reports compliance with them yet.
- INT-MDA-0133 Broadband coverage mapping system and Single Information Point: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that the regulator operates a public broadband coverage platform, expanded since 2025, and has put into operation a Very High Capacity Networks map populated with data from the three mobile operators and the six largest fixed operators. No source shows the Single Information Point in operation, and in January 2026 the Government was still agreeing a joint action plan for the wider mapping system.
- INT-MDA-0186 Development of electronic communications networks in identified white areas: proposed. Development of networks in white areas is planned in the digital strategy's implementation programme, and the Government's 2025 progress report says it can start only after the regulator's geographic survey under the new Electronic Communications Law and the broadband mapping system. The items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0194 Digital Society Development Programme: unclear. The Digital Society Development Programme was planned as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy, and an international partner reported in 2024 that the Ministry was developing them. The Government later reports that the three programmes were merged into a single programme, the Implementation Programme 2025-2027, so this programme was not adopted on its own.
- INT-MDA-0330 Geographical survey of broadband network coverage (ANRC): proposed. The regulator is to carry out a geographical survey of broadband coverage under Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025, in force since 1 January 2026, with a legal deadline of 30 December 2030. No source shows the survey under way.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0453 Law No. 72/2025 on electronic communications: adopted. Parliament adopted the law on 10 April 2025 and the Commission confirms it entered into force on 1 January 2026. The regulator was renamed under it, but in 2026 the secondary acts needed to implement it were still being drafted, and a civil society source notes only partial compatibility with Directive (EU) 2018/1972.
- INT-MDA-0488 Methodology for measuring and evaluating mobile service quality (ARCOM Board Decision No. 27 of 23.08.2024): implemented. The regulator reports that it repeatedly measured mobile coverage and quality under the methodology approved by Board Decision No. 27 of 23 August 2024. This rests on the regulator's own report.
- INT-MDA-0499 Mobile service quality measurements: implemented. The regulator reports that it has measured mobile service quality since 2019, covered 45 localities and 35 national roads, and added 5G networks to the 2025 measurements. The evidence comes only from the regulator's own report.
- INT-MDA-0605 Radio frequency licence competitions (2024 and 2025): implemented. The regulator held the 2024 competition, announced the results in January 2025 and issued the licences to three operators in February 2025, and held a further competition in 2025 that awarded spectrum to Moldtelecom. All items come from the regulator's and the Government's own machine-translated reports, so the status rests on Medium confidence evidence and reported coverage gains are not independently corroborated.
- INT-MDA-0606 Radio Spectrum Management Programme 2021-2025 (Government Decision No. 987/2020): implemented. The regulator reports that it held the 2025 radio frequency competition under the action plan of the Radio Spectrum Management Programme 2021-2025. The linked item is the regulator's own machine-translated report.
- INT-MDA-0685 Trilateral agreement on spatial data for the VHCN map: adopted. The Commission records that the regulator, the Agency for Geodesy, Cartography and Cadastre and the national cadastre entered into a trilateral agreement on 18 March 2026 to share spatial datasets for the very high capacity network map. The linked items do not yet report the map itself.

## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0453 leads to MDA-EU-2026-006-0010: success too_early, attribution insufficient_evidence. The law entered into force only on 1 January 2026, and the Commission describes the alignment as facilitating roll-out, which is an expected effect rather than an observed one.
- INT-MDA-0605 leads to MDA-GOV-2026-014-0089: success too_early, attribution Low. The spectrum was awarded in January 2025 and again during 2025, and the Commission records some progress on 5G deployment in 2026 with coverage of 25 to 35%, still well below the EU average, so roll-out is still under way.
- INT-MDA-0605 leads to MDA-GOV-2026-014-0047: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Medium. Only the regulator reports the expansion of Moldtelecom's 4G coverage: among the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources, the 4G figures are national (nearly the entire population covered in the Commission's 2023 report, 99.3% in 2024 according to CSO Meter) and none measures a change after the award.
