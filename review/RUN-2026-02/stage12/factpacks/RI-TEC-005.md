# Fact pack RI-TEC-005: Limited taxpayer e-services

The tax administration offers a limited range of electronic services to taxpayers.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 and 2024 reports find that the tax administration improved its electronic services, and the 2025 and 2026 reports record good progress in Chapter 16 that includes the digitalisation of tax administration services. The 2026 report still finds it essential to digitalise tax services further. The Chapter 16 statements cover the whole chapter, so the issue's own items were decisive. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0100;MDA-EU-2024-001-0087;MDA-EU-2025-001-0032;MDA-EU-2026-012-0025;MDA-EU-2026-012-0141
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Reform Agenda sets a target of 18 of 19 taxpayer services digitalised by December 2026 and the 2026 report asks for further digitalisation, while the Government's indicator gives 64% of companies using e-invoices against a target above 70%. SIGMA found that only 10% of citizens filed personal income tax returns electronically for 2022, and the Reform Agenda names limited staff and taxpayers' preference for in-person services as barriers. What remains is completing the digitalisation of taxpayer services and raising their use, especially by citizens. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0044;MDA-EU-2026-012-0141;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0138;MDA-IO-2023-001-0033;MDA-EU-2025-002-0079;MDA-EU-2025-002-0080
- priority: Medium (validated). The digitalisation of taxpayer services is a Reform Agenda payment condition due by December 2026 and is repeated in the 2026 report, but it is a narrow strand of digital government on which the Commission records good progress in 2025 and 2026. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation naming taxpayer e-services; the 2026 key recommendation on the tax administration concerns the State Tax Service's digital transformation programme and compliance risk management. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0044;MDA-EU-2026-012-0141;MDA-EU-2025-001-0032;MDA-EU-2026-012-0025;MDA-EU-2026-012-0026
- tractability: High (validated). The work lies with one agency, the Reform Agenda sets yearly targets with payments attached, and the Commission records improvement in every report from 2023 to 2026. Uptake depends partly on taxpayers' habits, which the Reform Agenda names as a barrier. Items: MDA-EU-2025-002-0082;MDA-EU-2023-001-0100;MDA-EU-2024-001-0087;MDA-EU-2025-001-0032;MDA-EU-2026-012-0025;MDA-EU-2025-002-0080

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0201 Digitalisation of State Tax Service services: proposed. The Reform Agenda sets a target of 18 of 19 taxpayer services digitalised by December 2026, with at least two in 2025 and two more in 2026. No source reports which services have been digitalised.
- INT-MDA-0305 Electronic invoice service (e-Factura) on the Electronic Fiscal Services platform: implemented. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet describes the e-Invoice service on the Electronic Fiscal Services platform, and the Government's implementation programme gives 64% of companies using electronic invoices. The usage figure is the Government's own, machine-translated and not independently corroborated.
