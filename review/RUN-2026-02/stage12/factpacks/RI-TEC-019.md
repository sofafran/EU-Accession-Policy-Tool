# Fact pack RI-TEC-019: Data protection authority not consulted and its advice not followed

The data protection authority is not systematically consulted on sector and implementing laws, its recommendations are often not implemented, and parliament does not follow up its reports.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). This issue is not raised in EU material: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no EU item on consulting the data protection authority on draft laws or on following its opinions, and the four items on the issue all date from 2026 (the data protection authority's 2025 activity report and its machine-translated opinion on a draft law). One year of evidence gives no sequence from which to read a direction. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-009-0033;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0034;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0105;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0085
- unresolved_gap: insufficient_evidence (validated). This issue is not raised in EU material, so there is no EU requirement to compare against. The authority's own report shows that it reviewed 208 draft acts in 2025 and asked for changes to most of them, but a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no item showing whether its advice on draft acts was then followed. What remains is to establish whether the authority's opinions are taken into account. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-009-0033;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0034;MDA-GOV-2026-006-0105
- priority: Low (validated). This issue is not raised in EU material, as a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no EU item on it, which keeps its priority from being High, and its four items come only from the data protection authority. Consultation of the authority is a procedural matter at the edge of the data protection theme. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-009-0034;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0085
- tractability: Medium (validated). Consulting the authority and acting on its opinions is a procedural step within the control of the Government and Parliament, and the authority already reviews a large number of drafts. Apart from the authority's account of years of unheeded requests to stop access to state registers outside the interoperability platform, a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no item showing past progress or resistance on the uptake of its opinions on draft acts, so the rating rests mainly on the nature of the mechanism. Items: MDA-GOV-2026-009-0033;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0034;MDA-GOV-2026-009-0024

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0263 Draft law on data and data governance (Regulation (EU) 2022/868, Data Governance Act): proposed. The draft law transposing the Data Governance Act was prepared and sent for interinstitutional examination in October 2025, and the data protection authority commented on it in 2026. The Government's 2025 progress report records the action as suspended until 2027 under the National Accession Programme; the draft was not withdrawn, so it remains a proposal.
- INT-MDA-0273 Draft Law on the admission, stay and supervision of foreigners in the Republic of Moldova (single no. 507/MAI/2025): proposed. The text is a draft law still under inter-institutional consultation, with comments being accepted and the quota provisions removed. It provides that it would enter into force on 1 June 2027 and repeal Law No. 200/2010, and no source reports its adoption.
