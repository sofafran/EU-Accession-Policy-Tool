# Fact pack RI-TEC-007: Online access for service providers and regulated professions incomplete

The point of single contact does not give service providers full online information and procedures as the Services Directive requires, and there is no transparent electronic register of regulated professions and qualifications.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found that the Commission's only items on these tools are two 2023 calls, in the analytical report and the 2023 report, for electronic platforms and procedures alongside legal alignment on professional qualifications, with no later EU assessment of them. The government's 2025 plans to reconfigure the public services portal as the point of single contact and to link the National Register of Qualifications with Europass are planned and under way respectively, so there is no sequence to judge. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0041;MDA-EU-2023-001-0064;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0407;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0449
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2023 Commission calls for electronic platforms and procedures are met so far only by plans: reconfiguring the public services portal as the point of single contact is planned, and making the National Register of Qualifications interoperable with Europass is under way. What remains is a point of single contact giving service providers full online information and procedures, and an electronic register of regulated professions and qualifications. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0041;MDA-EU-2023-001-0064;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0407;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0449
- priority: Low (validated). The Commission's calls for electronic platforms in this area appear only in 2023, as part of wider recommendations on professional qualifications, and their link to the technology theme is uncertain; a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on them. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0041;MDA-EU-2023-001-0064
- tractability: Medium (validated). The public services portal already works and could be reconfigured, and the work lies with national bodies. Against that, one measure is planned and one under way, the government expects possible problems of interoperability with EU platforms, and no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files reports past progress on either; the Government describes the EVO portal as a one-stop shop for public services in general, not as the point of single contact for service providers. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0407;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0449;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0405;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0127

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0011 Public Services Portal (services.gov.md): in_practice. The Commission and SIGMA describe the portal as the working single point of access to public services, and the Government reports over 0.5 million unique visitors a year and user satisfaction in its annual national survey. Material in the Government's own implementation programme says only a minority of listed services can be completed fully online, the Commission notes the portal is not integrated with EU systems, and by 2026 services are listed on the integrated EVO portal.
- INT-MDA-0551 National Register of Qualifications (RNC) interoperability with the Europass database: proposed. Ensuring interoperability of the National Register of Qualifications with the Europass database is listed among the actions in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0611 Reconfiguration of the government public services portal as Point of Single Contact: proposed. Reconfiguring the government public services portal to act as Point of Single Contact appears only as a planned action.
