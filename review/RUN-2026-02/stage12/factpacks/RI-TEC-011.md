# Fact pack RI-TEC-011: Electronic communications law not aligned with EU rules

The law on electronic communications is not fully aligned with the European Electronic Communications Code, the Gigabit Infrastructure Act and related rules on cost reduction, spectrum, roaming and open internet access.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2022 Opinion found limited alignment with the EU electronic communications framework, and the 2023 report asked for alignment with the Electronic Communications Code and the Roaming Regulation. The 2024 report found that alignment had started and the 2025 report noted legislative advancements; in 2026 the Commission found the roaming acquis correctly transposed in full, the Code transposed in primary law with secondary legislation approximated, and the Gigabit Infrastructure Act reflected in a law adopted in June 2026. The Chapter 10 rating rates the whole chapter and was not decisive. Items: MDA-EU-2022-001-0004;MDA-EU-2023-001-0073;MDA-EU-2023-001-0075;MDA-EU-2024-001-0075;MDA-EU-2025-001-0027;MDA-EU-2026-006-0005;MDA-EU-2026-006-0008;MDA-EU-2026-012-0108;MDA-EU-2026-012-0109
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Law No. 72/2025, in force since January 2026, the roaming law and its secondary acts, and the 2026 infrastructure law respond to the requirements, and the Commission judged the related Reform Agenda step satisfactorily fulfilled. The regulator is still drafting secondary acts under the new law, an end-user protection regulation is planned for 2026, the Government plans to transpose the remaining electronic communications acts by the end of 2027, and a civil society monitor describes the new law as only partly compatible with the Code. What remains is completing and applying the secondary legislation under the Code and the Gigabit Infrastructure Act and transposing the remaining related acts. Items: MDA-EU-2026-006-0006;MDA-EU-2026-006-0028;MDA-EU-2026-012-0109;MDA-GOV-2026-014-0077;MDA-GOV-2026-014-0080;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0181;MDA-CSO-2026-001-0056
- priority: Medium (validated). Alignment with the roaming acquis and the Code was a key recommendation in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports and part of a Reform Agenda step, which would usually mean High. The Commission now finds the roaming acquis fully transposed and the step fulfilled, and a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no recommendation on electronic communications or roaming in the 2026 report, so the remaining work is narrower. Items: MDA-EU-2023-001-0075;MDA-EU-2024-001-0026;MDA-EU-2025-001-0029;MDA-EU-2026-006-0008;MDA-EU-2026-006-0028;MDA-EU-2026-012-0107
- tractability: High (validated). The main laws were adopted in 2025 and 2026 with EU technical support, and the Commission confirms the results, including entry into Roam Like at Home in January 2026. The remaining work is secondary legislation by one regulator and one ministry, which the regulator is already drafting. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0173;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0174;MDA-EU-2026-012-0107;MDA-EU-2026-006-0008;MDA-GOV-2026-014-0077;MDA-GOV-2026-014-0066

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0087 ANRCETI preparatory process to license 5G frequency bands: implemented. Freedom House reported in 2024 that the regulator had begun preparing to license the 5G frequency bands. The process led to the 2024 competition, whose results were announced in January 2025, with licences issued to three operators in February 2025.
- INT-MDA-0088 ANRCETI Standard Licence Conditions (700 MHz and 800 MHz): adopted. The regulator approved the standard licence conditions for the 700 MHz and 800 MHz bands on 23 August 2024, with coverage obligations due by October 2027 and 2035. No source reports compliance with them yet.
- INT-MDA-0089 ARCOM Board Decision No. 16 of 02.06.2025 on detailed rules for applying Law No. 81/2025 (roaming): adopted. The regulator's Board Decision No. 16/2025 sets detailed rules for applying the roaming law, including providers' obligations under Roam Like At Home and compliance monitoring. This is the Government's and regulator's own account.
- INT-MDA-0090 ARCOM Board Decision No. 17 of 02.06.2025 on fair use policy and sustainability methodology for roaming: implemented. The regulator adopted detailed rules on fair use and on the sustainability of retail roaming at domestic prices in 2025. The Commission assessed the amended secondary legislation as correctly transposing the roaming acquis, and the rules have applied since Moldova joined Roam Like at Home on 1 January 2026.
- INT-MDA-0091 ARCOM Board Decision No. 22 of 20.06.2025 amending Board Decision No. 42/2017 on ex-ante measures for the wholesale local access market: adopted. The regulator reports that Board Decision No. 22 of 20 June 2025 amended its ex ante measures for the wholesale local access market.
- INT-MDA-0092 ARCOM Board Decisions No. 11 of 18.04.2025 and No. 47 of 17.11.2025 amending Board Decision No. 126/2009 on licence-exempt radio frequencies and equipment: adopted. The regulator reports that its Board decisions of April and November 2025 amended the rules on licence-exempt radio frequencies and equipment to harmonise with CEPT/ECC Recommendation ERC/REC 70-03.
- INT-MDA-0093 ARCOM Board decisions on access to property under Law No. 28/2016: implemented. The Commission reports that the updated infrastructure access tariffs entered into force on 1 March 2026 and that the reference offer was published on the incumbent operator's website. The regulator also reports issuing Board decisions where access disputes could not be settled by dialogue.
- INT-MDA-0094 ARCOM coordination mechanism with main providers for applying Roam Like At Home: implemented. The regulator reports that it set up a coordination mechanism with the main providers to apply Roam Like At Home and aligned their offers with the new rules. This is the regulator's own account.
- INT-MDA-0095 ARCOM inspections: implemented. The regulator reports 36 inspections of electronic communications and postal services in 2025 and the main violations found. The figures are the regulator's own and machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0096 ARCOM secondary regulatory acts implementing Law No. 72/2025 and Law No. 81/2025: adopted. The regulator reports that it drafted and adopted the regulatory acts needed to implement Law No. 72/2025 and the roaming Law No. 81/2025, with advice from Commission experts, and continues to draft further acts in 2026. This is the regulator's own account, and no source describes the acts in application.
- INT-MDA-0105 Bilateral agreement between Moldova and Romania liberalising roaming tariffs (February 2022): in_practice. A civil society source reports that Moldova and Romania signed the agreement in February 2022 and, citing the two regulators, that roaming internet traffic rose sharply in 2022 and the average roaming basket cost fell by almost 70% for Moldovan users. The evidence dates from 2022, and no source says how the agreement relates to Moldova joining Roam Like at Home in 2026.
- INT-MDA-0133 Broadband coverage mapping system and Single Information Point: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that the regulator operates a public broadband coverage platform, expanded since 2025, and has put into operation a Very High Capacity Networks map populated with data from the three mobile operators and the six largest fixed operators. No source shows the Single Information Point in operation, and in January 2026 the Government was still agreeing a joint action plan for the wider mapping system.
- INT-MDA-0151 Concept of the Register of Utility Networks information system (draft Government Decision): proposed. The Government's 2025 progress report says drafting of the decision approving the concept of the Register of Utility Networks had been initiated by 31 December 2025. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0210 Draft ANRC Board decisions implementing the European Electronic Communications Code: unclear. The Government listed these regulator board decisions as drafts in 2025. The regulator reports that it adopted the regulatory acts needed to implement the new electronic communications framework and continues drafting others in 2026, and the Commission reports approximated secondary legislation, but no source names these specific decisions as adopted.
- INT-MDA-0214 Draft Electronic Communications Law: adopted. The draft became Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025, in force from 1 January 2026, and the Commission reports primary legislation transposing the European Electronic Communications Code. The regulator was still drafting implementing acts in 2026.
- INT-MDA-0260 Draft law on access to property and use of infrastructure (Gigabit Infrastructure Act): adopted. The Commission reports that the law on access to property for public service providers, aligning with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act, was adopted in June 2026. No source yet reports its application.
- INT-MDA-0315 EU-Moldova Association Council Decision No. 1/2025 (roaming market opening, Annex XXVIII-B): implemented. The Government reports that the EU-Moldova Association Council adopted Decision No. 1/2025 on 4 August 2025, completing the conditions for Roam Like at Home. The Commission confirms that Moldova joined the Roam Like at Home area on 1 January 2026.
- INT-MDA-0327 General licence conditions for using numbering resources (ARCOM Board Decision No. 46 of 17.11.2025): adopted. The regulator reports that Board Decision No. 46 of 17 November 2025 approved the general licence conditions for using numbering resources.
- INT-MDA-0330 Geographical survey of broadband network coverage (ANRC): proposed. The regulator is to carry out a geographical survey of broadband coverage under Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025, in force since 1 January 2026, with a legal deadline of 30 December 2030. No source shows the survey under way.
- INT-MDA-0344 Government Decision No. 316/2025 on small-area wireless access points: adopted. The Government reports that Government Decision No. 316/2025, transposing Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/1070 on small-area wireless access points, was adopted on 28 May 2025. The linked item is machine-translated and does not report application.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. - INT-MDA-0389 Inclusion of roaming in the EU-Moldova Association Agreement: adopted. A civil society source reports that the European Commission accepted a proposal to include roaming in the Association Agreement, and the Government reports that Association Council Decision No. 1/2025 amended the relevant annex in August 2025.
- INT-MDA-0391 Information campaigns on consumer rights and Roam Like At Home rules: insufficient_evidence. The regulator states that it is stepping up information campaigns on consumer rights and Roam Like at Home rules, but gives no detail of campaigns carried out.
- INT-MDA-0416 Law No. 106/2026 on access of public electronic communications network providers to property and the use of infrastructure (Gigabit Infrastructure Act): adopted. The Commission reports that Moldova adopted Law No. 106 on 11 June 2026, transposing the Gigabit Infrastructure Act. No source yet reports its application.
- INT-MDA-0439 Law No. 28/2016 on access to property and shared use of infrastructure for electronic communications networks: implemented. The law has applied since 2016, and the regulator reports about 21 administrative procedures under it, 218 entities that have submitted access conditions and 1,932 registered access contracts at the end of 2025. These figures come from the regulator's own reporting, which also records uneven application and disputes over access fees.
- INT-MDA-0453 Law No. 72/2025 on electronic communications: adopted. Parliament adopted the law on 10 April 2025 and the Commission confirms it entered into force on 1 January 2026. The regulator was renamed under it, but in 2026 the secondary acts needed to implement it were still being drafted, and a civil society source notes only partial compatibility with Directive (EU) 2018/1972.
- INT-MDA-0455 Law No. 81/2025 on roaming services between Moldova and EU Member States: implemented. The Law was adopted on 17 April 2025, the Commission assessed that Moldova's legislation correctly transposed the whole EU roaming acquis, and Moldova joined the Roam Like at Home area on 1 January 2026. The linked items give no data yet on roaming use under the new regime.
- INT-MDA-0457 Law on Electronic Communications (in force since 2017): reversed. The new Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025 entered into force on 1 January 2026, as the Commission confirms, and the regulator was renamed under it. None of the linked items states the repeal of the earlier law in terms, but the new law has replaced it as the sector framework, which is a replacement rather than a policy reversal.
- INT-MDA-0467 Legislation and secondary legislation aligning with the EU roaming acquis: implemented. Operators agreed in May 2023 to lower roaming tariffs between Moldova and the EU from January 2024, and the Government reports the reduced tariffs applied from 1 January 2024. The Government describes this as a temporary mechanism for 2024 and 2025, followed by Moldova joining Roam Like at Home on 1 January 2026.

## Outcomes

- INT-MDA-0692 leads to MKD-EU-2023-002-0044: success worked, attribution High. The Commission reports tangible benefits from the voluntary arrangements, and an independent civil society source reports that all Moldovan mobile operators launched offers with significantly reduced tariffs from the start of 2024.
- INT-MDA-0692 leads to MDA-CSO-2024-001-0022: success worked, attribution High. The source reports reduced roaming tariffs offered by all mobile operators in 30 EU and EEA countries from the start of 2024, which the Commission also reports as tangible benefits.
- INT-MDA-0105 leads to MDA-CSO-2024-001-0027: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Medium. The fall in the roaming basket and the rise in traffic come from a report of the two countries' national regulators, relayed by a civil society source without separate verification. No other item among the 1,451 Moldova items of this run from EU, international, civil society or research sources confirms the effect.
- INT-MDA-0509 leads to MKD-EU-2026-009-0020: success worked, attribution High. The Commission states that roaming charges were removed and immediate benefits reached citizens and businesses, and the 2026 report confirms that Moldova joined Roam Like at Home on 1 January 2026.
- INT-MDA-0455 leads to MDA-GOV-2025-002-0149: success worked, attribution High. The outcome is legal: the Government's statement that the legislative preconditions were created is confirmed by the Commission, which found the roaming acquis correctly transposed in full and admitted Moldova to Roam Like at Home on that basis.
- INT-MDA-0453 leads to MDA-EU-2026-006-0010: success too_early, attribution insufficient_evidence. The law entered into force only on 1 January 2026, and the Commission describes the alignment as facilitating roll-out, which is an expected effect rather than an observed one.
- INT-MDA-0605 leads to MDA-GOV-2026-014-0089: success too_early, attribution Low. The spectrum was awarded in January 2025 and again during 2025, and the Commission records some progress on 5G deployment in 2026 with coverage of 25 to 35%, still well below the EU average, so roll-out is still under way.
