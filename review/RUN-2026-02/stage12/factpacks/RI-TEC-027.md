# Fact pack RI-TEC-027: Interoperability framework not aligned with EU

The national interoperability framework is not aligned with the latest European Interoperability Framework or the Interoperable Europe Act.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files for the Interoperable Europe Act and the European Interoperability Framework found no Commission finding or recommendation on the national framework's alignment; the EU items are a Growth Plan support measure and a 2026 note that the open data law is in line with the European Interoperability Framework. The government stated in 2025 that the national framework follows the Framework's principles but that the Interoperable Europe Act is not transposed, and drafting of the amending law was announced in February 2026, which is too short a record to show a direction. Items: MDA-EU-2024-002-0012;MDA-EU-2026-012-0118;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0332;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0101
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). By the government's own account the Interoperable Europe Act has not been transposed, and the law amending Law No. 142/2018 to do so was only initiated in February 2026; the Interoperable Europe factsheet notes that Moldova is not yet part of the European Interoperability Framework data collection. What remains is transposition of the Interoperable Europe Act into the data exchange and interoperability law and alignment of the national framework with the latest European Interoperability Framework. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0332;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0222;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0101;MDA-IO-2024-002-0016
- priority: Low (validated). The issue is not raised as a requirement in the Commission's reports or the Reform Agenda: a search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found no such item, which keeps priority from being High. It rests on the government's own gap analysis and an EU support measure under the Growth Plan. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-002-0332;MDA-EU-2024-002-0012
- tractability: Medium (validated). The change is one amendment to an existing law, prepared by the government, and Moldova already has a working legal and technical interoperability framework. Drafting began only in February 2026, and no item among the 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files shows progress beyond the February 2026 drafting notice. Items: MDA-GOV-2025-003-0222;MDA-GOV-2026-002-0101;MDA-GOV-2025-003-0099

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0007 National Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Government adopted the Strategy in September 2023, as the Commission confirms. The Commission reported in 2025 that the Government continued to implement it, with tangible progress on all key performance indicators, and the Government's 2025 progress report counts nine actions completed. Implementation of the Strategy as a whole says nothing yet about its results, which depend on its individual measures.
- INT-MDA-0012 Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the law obliges government agencies to exchange data only through the interoperability solution, and that the MConnect platform it governs is well functioning and used by many public and private organisations. The data protection authority finds that some state bodies still access data outside the platform in breach of the law, and the Government notes that replacing documents with data remains problematic in many cases.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0056 Alignment of the national interoperability framework with the European Interoperability Framework: proposed. The Digital Public Administration Factsheet says the national framework follows the European Interoperability Framework, but the Government notes that the Interoperable Europe Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/903, has not been transposed. Further alignment is planned in the National Accession Programme; these Government items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0174 Data governance framework and national data governance strategy (recommended): proposed. The Government lists aligning the data governance framework among its e-government priorities. No source shows a data governance framework or strategy adopted.
- INT-MDA-0217 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 211/2019 on the interoperability platform (MConnect): proposed. A draft Government decision amending Government Decision No. 211/2019 on MConnect appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0249 Draft law amending Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability (Regulation (EU) 2024/903, Interoperable Europe Act): proposed. The amending law is planned in the digital strategy's implementation programme, and the Government's 2025 progress report says the start of drafting was announced on particip.gov.md on 3 February 2026. The linked items are machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0342 Government Decision No. 211/2019 on the interoperability platform (MConnect): in_practice. The Decision regulates the MConnect interoperability platform, and SIGMA reports 665.7 million transactions through it in 2022, so it is applied at scale. The Government states that data exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect, but the data protection authority finds that state bodies continue to access personal data through another technology, so how fully the Decision is applied is disputed.
- INT-MDA-0384 Implementation Programme 2025-2027 of the Digital Transformation Strategy 2023-2030: implemented. The Commission confirms the programme was adopted on 28 May 2025, and its 2026 report finds tangible progress on the programme's key performance indicators and continued implementation. Government material records a funding gap of about MDL 225 million and capacity limits in the lead bodies, so delivery of the action plan is not yet complete.
- INT-MDA-0417 Law No. 109/2025 on open data and the reuse of public sector information: adopted. Law No. 109/2025 on open data, partially transposing Directive (EU) 2019/1024, was adopted in May 2025 and followed by methodological norms in September 2025, according to the Government and civil society. The Commission dates the adoption of such a law to July 2026, which may refer to a later act; civil society reports training and platform improvements to apply it, but no source shows its effect on data publication.
- INT-MDA-0505 Moldova eGovernment Interoperability Framework (MeGIF) Programme (Government Decision No. 656/2012): implemented. The World Bank reports that Moldova launched the eGovernment Interoperability Framework Programme in 2012 and that a pilot of the MConnect data exchange platform was developed under it between 2014 and 2018. No recent source describes the programme as such, its work now being carried by the interoperability law and platform.
- INT-MDA-0531 National digital identity wallet: implemented. The Commission reports that a national digital identity wallet was launched within the EVO application in June 2024. The wallet aligned with the EU Digital Identity framework is a further version: the Government says development started in September 2025, the Commission reports cross-border interoperability tests in 2026, and the Reform Agenda sets its launch for December 2026.
- INT-MDA-0541 National mechanism for coordination with the European Data Innovation Board: proposed. Creating a national mechanism for coordination with the European Data Innovation Board is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
- INT-MDA-0600 Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030: proposed. The ministry was developing the Public Sector e-Transformation Programme 2025-2030 in 2024 as one of three programmes under the Digital Transformation Strategy. No source shows that it has been approved.
