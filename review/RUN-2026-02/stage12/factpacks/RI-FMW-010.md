# Fact pack RI-FMW-010: Readiness for electronic exchange of social security information

The institutions lack the IT and human capacity to exchange social security information electronically with Member States (the EESSI system) and to use electronic health insurance data.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The 2023 analytical report found that authorities struggle with administrative capacity and data exchange in international cases, and the 2025 key recommendation asked Moldova to develop IT infrastructure for EESSI. In 2026 the Commission reports that the e-Governance Agency was designated as the EESSI National Access Point with two liaison bodies, a law setting the basis for EESSI interconnection was adopted and an EESSI Action Plan approved, while the 2026 key recommendation still asks for the IT infrastructure and capacity. The improvement is organisational and legal only, as no technical connection is reported. Items: MDA-EU-2023-002-0036;MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0094;MDA-EU-2026-012-0095;MDA-EU-2026-012-0096;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The access point, liaison bodies, legal basis and action plan are in place, but the Government reports that the employment agency and CNAS systems still need adjusting for EESSI, that the technical solution and its financing will be chosen later, and that only 3 of 8 planned IT directorate staff work on the health insurance card and interconnection with European systems. What remains is the IT infrastructure, interconnected information systems, staff and funding for full participation in EESSI and for electronic exchange of health insurance data. Items: MDA-EU-2026-012-0094;MDA-EU-2026-012-0095;MDA-EU-2026-012-0096;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0030;MDA-GOV-2026-007-0027;MDA-GOV-2025-002-0069;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014
- priority: Medium (validated). Electronic exchange is needed to apply social security coordination at accession, and it is named in the key recommendations of the 2025 and 2026 reports. A search of all 3,631 items held in the Moldova evidence files found EESSI in five Commission items, all from 2025 and 2026, and in no key recommendation repeated across three reports, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition. Items: MDA-EU-2025-001-0021;MDA-EU-2026-012-0014;MDA-EU-2023-002-0036
- tractability: Medium (validated). The 2026 designations and action plan show that organisational steps can be taken, and the Government reports the interoperability platform as technically ready for cross-border exchange. Connection, however, depends on technical documentation from the Commission, an investment not yet decided, several institutions and a new employment agency system funded externally. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MDA-0012 Law No. 142/2018 on data exchange and interoperability: in_practice. SIGMA reports that the law obliges government agencies to exchange data only through the interoperability solution, and that the MConnect platform it governs is well functioning and used by many public and private organisations. The data protection authority finds that some state bodies still access data outside the platform in breach of the law, and the Government notes that replacing documents with data remains problematic in many cases.
- INT-MDA-0015 MConnect interoperability platform: in_practice. SIGMA found in 2023 that MConnect is a well-functioning data exchange platform used by a large number of public and private organisations, and the Commission reports systems such as social assistance integrated into it. Government figures put use at about 1.5 million successful queries a day across 144 state and 252 private systems. The data protection authority found in 2025 that some state bodies still access personal data outside the platform through COI technology, which qualifies the Government's statement that exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect but not the platform's use at scale.
- INT-MDA-0038 "Social Protection" information system: in_practice. The Commission reported in 2023 that the social protection information system carries out the registration, granting and payment of social benefits, which shows it in use. No later source reports on it.
- INT-MDA-0077 ANOFM and CNAS information systems: proposed. The Government notes that the information systems of the employment agency and the social insurance house need to be adjusted and developed to be interoperable with EESSI. No source shows this work done.
- INT-MDA-0156 Connection of the Republic of Moldova to EESSI: proposed. The Commission reports that an EESSI action plan was approved, and the authorities are preparing the digital infrastructure and the ANOFM and CNAS systems for interoperability. The actual connection still depends on technical documentation from the Commission and a technical solution yet to be chosen, so Moldova is not connected to EESSI.
- INT-MDA-0181 Designation of the e-Governance Agency as EESSI National Access Point: adopted. The Commission reports that in February 2026 Moldova designated the e-Governance Agency as the National Access Point for EESSI, with liaison bodies for social insurance and health insurance. No source reports the access point working, and Moldova is not yet connected to EESSI.
- INT-MDA-0184 Designation of the National Office of Social Insurance and the National Health Insurance Company as liaison bodies: adopted. The Commission reports that in February 2026 Moldova designated the National Office of Social Insurance and the National Health Insurance Company as liaison bodies, and the e-Governance Agency as EESSI access point. The linked item does not report them acting in that role.
- INT-MDA-0187 Dialogue with the European Labour Authority, including on EESSI: implemented. The Government reports that after signing the agreement Moldovan authorities started a dialogue with the European Labour Authority on priority areas including EESSI. This is the Government's own account.
- INT-MDA-0218 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 230/2020 on the organisation and functioning of CNAS: proposed. The amendment to Government Decision No. 230/2020 on CNAS appears only as a draft in the 2025 accession programme, and no source reports its approval.
- INT-MDA-0219 Draft Government Decision amending Government Decision No. 788/2022 on the Concept of the "Social Protection" information system: proposed. A draft Government decision amending the Concept of the Social Protection information system appears only in the Government's plans.
- INT-MDA-0244 Draft law amending certain normative acts (coordination and exchange of data in the field of social security with EU Member States) (single no. 89/MMPS/2026): adopted. The Commission reports that a law amending several acts to set the legal basis for EU social security coordination rules and EESSI interconnection was adopted during its reporting period, after the draft was finalised in 2026. No source yet describes data exchange under it.
- INT-MDA-0247 Draft Law amending Law No. 105/2018 on employment promotion and unemployment insurance (single no. 180/MMPS/2026): proposed. The amendment to Law No. 105/2018 is a draft in consultation, with ministries still proposing changes to its definitions and entry-into-force clause. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MDA-0342 Government Decision No. 211/2019 on the interoperability platform (MConnect): in_practice. The Decision regulates the MConnect interoperability platform, and SIGMA reports 665.7 million transactions through it in 2022, so it is applied at scale. The Government states that data exchange takes place exclusively through MConnect, but the data protection authority finds that state bodies continue to access personal data through another technology, so how fully the Decision is applied is disputed.
- INT-MDA-0402 Interconnection of the "Social Protection" information system with EESSI: proposed. Interconnecting Moldova's social security system with those of EU Member States through EESSI appears only as a planned action. No source shows the connection made.
- INT-MDA-0410 Introduction of the European Health Insurance Card in Moldova: proposed. The Commission found no progress on the European Health Insurance Card in 2025, and in 2026 reports only that an EHIC Action Plan was approved as a roadmap for operational implementation. The legal amendments needed to introduce the card were planned for 2026 and no source shows them adopted or the card issued.
- INT-MDA-0552 National Social Security Information System: insufficient_evidence. The items mention the National Social Security Information System only in relation to its future interoperability with EESSI and say nothing about the system's own status.
- INT-MDA-0564 New integrated information system of the National Employment Agency (EU Delegation funding): proposed. The Commission reports that the National Employment Agency developed a new integrated information system in December 2025, aiming at fully digitalised services by 2027, and a 2026 Government document says the system, funded by the EU Delegation, is still being developed. Neither source reports it in use.
- INT-MDA-0672 Technical documentation for interoperability between the SAOAM information system and EESSI: proposed. Drafting technical documentation for interoperability between the SAOAM information system and EESSI is listed in the National Accession Programme. The linked item records the plan only. The linked item is machine-translated.
