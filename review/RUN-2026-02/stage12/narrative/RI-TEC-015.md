## In brief

The EU asks Moldova to complete the full digitalisation of the public property register, and the Reform Agenda requires a fully operational State Register of Legal Units by December 2025 and a digital cadastre covering 98 percent of the territory, with data regularly updated and online, by December 2027 (MDA-EU-2026-012-0005; MDA-EU-2025-002-0015; MDA-EU-2025-002-0017). Moldova digitalised the business register's paper archive between August 2023 and August 2024 and launched the State Register of Legal Units portal on 29 December 2025, with a World Bank loan funding its pilot phase (MDA-EU-2024-001-0070; MDA-EU-2026-004-0003; MDA-EU-2026-004-0005). The Commission judged the related Reform Agenda step satisfactorily fulfilled, and verified that 310 of 414 business services are digitalised, although only 133 are fully digital end to end (MDA-EU-2026-004-0008; MDA-EU-2026-004-0001). The civil status records system went into production on 3 November 2025, but no source reports its use since (MDA-GOV-2026-011-0007). Other registers have moved less: the public property register is still being prepared for digitalisation, the State Population Register concept was withdrawn in August 2025 and resumed in 2026, and the register of non-commercial organisations is described as outdated and not interoperable (MDA-EU-2026-012-0064; MDA-GOV-2026-002-0121; MDA-GOV-2026-002-0123; MDA-CSO-2026-001-0015). Sources disagree on whether all state information systems are registered in the register of state information systems. What remains is a fully digital public property register, the planned modernisation of the population register and the cadastre, and reliable data across base registers. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The evidence holds no Commission item on base registers from the 2022 Opinion. In 2023 the Commission described the electronic cadastral system maintained by the Public Services Agency without raising a requirement (MDA-EU-2023-001-0022). In 2024 it recorded that the Agency, which registers companies, continued to suffer from limited administrative capacity and digitalisation resources (MDA-EU-2024-001-0068). The 2025 Reform Agenda made two registers payment conditions, the State Register of Legal Units and the digital cadastre, and the 2025 report asked Moldova to finalise digitalising the public property registry and to advance the Public Services Agency's roadmap (MDA-EU-2025-002-0016; MDA-EU-2025-002-0017; MDA-EU-2025-001-0108; MDA-EU-2025-001-0078). In 2026 the Commission counted the new legal entities register as progress and repeated, in stronger terms, the call to complete the full digitalisation of the public property register (MDA-EU-2026-012-0015; MDA-EU-2026-012-0005).

## Open questions

- Whether the public property register is fully digitalised and operational is not yet shown; the Commission's next report would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0064; MDA-EU-2026-012-0005).
- Progress towards a digital cadastre covering 98 percent of the territory by December 2027 is not reported; coverage figures from the cadastre agency or the Commission's assessment of the Reform Agenda step would settle it (MDA-EU-2025-002-0017; MDA-GOV-2026-002-0118).
- Whether the State Register of Legal Units is used at scale since its launch is not shown; registration volumes would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-004-0003; MDA-GOV-2026-011-0008).
- SIGMA states that all state information systems are registered, while the Court of Accounts finds registration delayed and 63 percent of registered systems not properly documented; a follow-up audit would settle it (MDA-IO-2023-001-0050; MDA-GOV-2024-001-0088; MDA-GOV-2024-001-0081).
