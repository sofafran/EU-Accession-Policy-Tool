## In brief

The Commission asks Moldova to develop the IT infrastructure, systems, capacity and resources needed for full participation in the European Health Insurance Card scheme, and it has found in each report from 2023 to 2025 that no national health insurance card exists because there is no integrated health information system (MDA-EU-2025-001-0021; MDA-EU-2026-012-0014; MDA-EU-2023-001-0057; MDA-EU-2024-001-0065; MDA-EU-2025-001-0076). The National Health Insurance Company coordinates the work, and a feasibility study on the financial impact of the card was finalised in August 2026, when an EHIC Action Plan was also approved as a roadmap (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0068; MDA-EU-2026-012-0097; MDA-EU-2026-012-0098). The feasibility study is the only step assessed as implemented. The card module in the health insurance information system is being developed, the amendments to the health care and health insurance laws are planned for 2026, and the order on the card's technical specifications is a draft, none of them shown adopted or in operation (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0077; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0072; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0098). The Government states that the EU acts on the card are not yet transposed, and the Commission found in 2024 that an integrated health information system is not yet in place (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0063; MDA-EU-2024-001-0071). No card has been issued. What remains is a national health insurance card and the IT, legal and administrative basis to issue and accept the European Health Insurance Card at accession. The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's analytical report of February 2023 found that no national health insurance card exists because there is no integrated health information system, and that implementing the European Health Insurance Card might be challenging (MDA-EU-2023-002-0031; MDA-EU-2023-002-0033). The 2023 and 2024 reports repeat both findings in the same words (MDA-EU-2023-001-0057; MDA-EU-2023-001-0059; MDA-EU-2024-001-0065; MDA-EU-2024-001-0067). The 2024 report also recommended implementing the national health strategy, including an integrated e-health system (MDA-EU-2024-001-0021). The 2025 report records no progress on the card, repeats the finding on the national card and adds a key recommendation to develop IT infrastructure for the card (MDA-EU-2025-001-0075; MDA-EU-2025-001-0076; MDA-EU-2025-001-0021). The 2026 report widens this to the measures, capacity, resources and IT systems needed for full participation in the scheme, alongside EURES and EESSI, and reports the feasibility study and action plan (MDA-EU-2026-012-0014; MDA-EU-2026-012-0010; MDA-EU-2026-012-0098).

## Open questions

- Whether the amendments to the health care and health insurance laws have been adopted is not shown, and their publication in the Official Gazette or a Commission finding would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0064; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0072).
- Whether the card module in the health insurance information system is operating is not reported, and evidence of cards or provisional replacement certificates being issued would be needed (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0077; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0117).
- No source records a plan or decision on the national health insurance card itself, and a Government decision or Commission finding on it would establish whether this precondition is being addressed (MDA-EU-2025-001-0076).
- Whether the health insurance company has filled its planned IT posts is not known, and later staffing data would show whether it has the capacity to deliver the action plan (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0069; MDA-EU-2026-012-0098).
