## In brief

The EU's requirement under this issue centres on the civil service human resources information system: the Commission found national HR systems non-functional, and the Reform Agenda requires a human resource management information system for all central government institutions, with at least three modules operational, by December 2027 (MDA-EU-2024-001-0034; MDA-EU-2025-002-0045). Moldova has a long-standing set of shared e-government platforms, including MCloud, MPass, MPay and MConnect, which the Reform Agenda records as already implemented, and a World Bank-supported project added platforms such as MDelivery, MPower and MDocs (MDA-EU-2025-002-0062; MDA-GOV-2023-001-0013; MDA-GOV-2025-001-0024). Some of these are shown in use: the Commission reported 42 more services integrated with MDelivery in 2023, and the permits system had issued permits through 16 of the 31 issuing institutions when audited (MDA-EU-2023-001-0015; MDA-GOV-2024-001-0054). The HR system has not followed: the earlier system failed through problems with its private provider, and its replacement was in incipient design in 2025 and still in development in 2026 (MDA-EU-2023-001-0009; MDA-EU-2025-001-0036; MDA-EU-2026-012-0035). Institutions not subordinate to the Government declined to use the permits system, and the eArhiva electronic archive is still a planned action (MDA-GOV-2024-001-0129; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0233). Sources disagree on whether all state information systems are registered in the state register. What remains is a working HR management system and consistent use of shared systems by all institutions. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording concerns the civil service HR system rather than the shared platforms as a whole; the fact pack holds no Commission item on this issue from the 2022 Opinion. In 2023 the Commission reported that the national HR information system was not functional because of maintenance and updating problems with its private provider (MDA-EU-2023-001-0009). In 2024 it described civil service management as fragmented and understaffed, with non-functional national HR systems (MDA-EU-2024-001-0034). The 2025 Reform Agenda turned this into a payment condition, an HR management information system with at least three operational modules by December 2027, while recording MCloud, MPass, MPay and MConnect as platforms already in place (MDA-EU-2025-002-0045; MDA-EU-2025-002-0062). The Commission's 2025 and 2026 reports then recorded the new system as only in incipient design and later as still in development (MDA-EU-2025-001-0036; MDA-EU-2026-012-0035).

## Open questions

- Whether the new HR management system will have three modules operational by December 2027 is open; the Commission's assessment of the Reform Agenda step would settle it (MDA-EU-2025-002-0045; MDA-EU-2026-012-0035).
- SIGMA states that all state information systems are registered in the state register, while the Court of Accounts finds registration delayed; a follow-up audit of the register would settle the question (MDA-IO-2023-001-0050; MDA-GOV-2024-001-0088).
- How widely institutions use the permits system after the 2024 agreement on its new version is not reported; current usage data across all issuing institutions would show it (MDA-GOV-2024-001-0060; MDA-GOV-2024-001-0054).
- The savings attributed to MPay rest on the e-Governance Agency's own account with no method given; an independent evaluation would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-001-0040).
