## In brief

The EU asks Moldova to digitalise its justice system and connect the IT systems of justice institutions. The Reform Agenda requires, by December 2027, the E-File application to be operational in all first instance courts with at least 15 percent of cases processed through it, and the integrated case management system to cover all cases and connect with seven information systems; a 2026 key recommendation asks for substantial progress on an integrated electronic case management system for law enforcement and prosecution bodies (MDA-EU-2025-002-0051; MDA-EU-2026-012-0003). Moldova set up an Agency for Digitalisation in Justice and Judicial Administration on 1 January 2025, with a mandate for the development and interoperability of justice sector information systems, and in August 2026 made normative amendments promoting the digitalisation of certain judicial procedures (MDA-EU-2025-001-0048; MDA-EU-2026-012-0047). What has happened in practice is limited: the Commission finds that the Agency has an extremely limited capacity because of persistent staffing concerns and that the use of IT tools across the justice system remains uneven and partial (MDA-EU-2026-012-0045; MDA-EU-2026-012-0046). No source reports progress on the E-File application. What remains is digital communication with courts in practice and connected IT across justice institutions, run by an agency able to deliver it. The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is High and tractability is Low.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The evidence holds no Commission item on justice digitalisation from the 2022 Opinion or the 2023 report. The 2024 report stated in general terms that digitalisation of the justice system needs to be improved (MDA-EU-2024-001-0043). In 2025 the Commission recorded the new Agency and asked for its operationalisation to be sped up, and the Reform Agenda of the same year set concrete, dated targets for the E-File application and the integrated case management system (MDA-EU-2025-001-0048; MDA-EU-2025-001-0049; MDA-EU-2025-002-0051). By 2026 the Commission's wording had become more specific and more critical: it found the Agency's capacity extremely limited and IT use uneven and partial, called for further legal improvements and a decisive reinforcement of the Agency's mandate and capacity, and made an integrated case management system for law enforcement and prosecution bodies a key recommendation (MDA-EU-2026-012-0045; MDA-EU-2026-012-0046; MDA-EU-2026-012-0048; MDA-EU-2026-012-0003). The August 2026 amendments are recorded but not yet assessed (MDA-EU-2026-012-0047).

## Open questions

- Whether the E-File application will be operational in all first instance courts, with at least 15 percent of cases processed through it, by December 2027 is unknown; court usage data or the Commission's assessment of the Reform Agenda step would settle it (MDA-EU-2025-002-0051).
- The current state of the integrated case management system and its connections with other information systems is not reported; a technical status report from the Agency or the courts would settle it (MDA-EU-2025-002-0051).
- Which act the August 2026 amendments are, and what they change, is not stated by the Commission; the published text and a later assessment of its application would show it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0047).
- Whether the Agency's staffing problems are being resolved is unclear; staffing figures and the Commission's next report would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0045; MDA-EU-2026-012-0048).
