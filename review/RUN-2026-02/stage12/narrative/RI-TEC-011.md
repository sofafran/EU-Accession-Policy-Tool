## In brief

The Commission asked Moldova to align its law with the European Electronic Communications Code and the EU roaming acquis, including their secondary legislation, so that it could join the Roam Like at Home area (MDA-EU-2024-001-0026; MDA-EU-2025-001-0029). Parliament adopted Electronic Communications Law No. 72/2025 in April 2025 and roaming Law No. 81/2025 in the same month, the regulator adopted detailed roaming rules, and Law No. 106 of June 2026 transposes the Gigabit Infrastructure Act (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0173; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0174; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0175; MDA-EU-2026-006-0014). Law No. 72/2025 entered into force on 1 January 2026 (MDA-EU-2026-006-0006). The Commission assessed the legislation as correctly transposing the whole EU roaming acquis and judged the related Reform Agenda step satisfactorily fulfilled (MDA-EU-2026-006-0008; MDA-EU-2026-006-0028). On that basis Moldova joined Roam Like at Home on 1 January 2026, and the Commission states that this removed roaming charges and brought immediate benefits to citizens and businesses; the roaming outcome is assessed as having worked (MDA-EU-2026-012-0107; MKD-EU-2026-009-0020). Earlier, the voluntary operator agreement of 2023 led to reduced roaming tariffs from January 2024, which the Commission and an independent civil society source both report (MKD-EU-2023-002-0044; MDA-CSO-2024-001-0022). For the Code itself, effects on network roll-out are too early to judge, as the law has applied only since January 2026 (MDA-EU-2026-006-0010). The regulator is still drafting secondary acts under the new law and plans an end-user protection regulation for 2026, the Government plans to transpose the remaining electronic communications acts by the end of 2027, and a civil society monitor finds the law only partially compatible with the Code (MDA-GOV-2026-014-0077; MDA-GOV-2026-014-0080; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0181; MDA-CSO-2026-001-0056). What remains is completing and applying the secondary legislation under the Code and the Gigabit Infrastructure Act and transposing the remaining related acts. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is Medium and tractability is High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The 2022 Opinion found Moldova's alignment with the EU electronic communications framework limited (MDA-EU-2022-001-0004). The 2023 report asked for alignment with the Electronic Communications Code and for alignment with and implementation of the Roaming Regulation (MDA-EU-2023-001-0073; MDA-EU-2023-001-0075). In 2024 the key recommendation linked alignment with the roaming acquis and the Code to joining the Roam Like at Home area, and the report found that alignment had started (MDA-EU-2024-001-0026; MDA-EU-2024-001-0075). The 2025 report narrowed the request to implementing secondary legislation and noted legislative advances on roaming (MDA-EU-2025-001-0029; MDA-EU-2025-001-0027). The Reform Agenda step on electronic communications was then found satisfactorily fulfilled (MDA-EU-2026-006-0028). The 2026 report records the Code transposed in primary law, secondary legislation approximated and the Gigabit Infrastructure Act reflected in the June 2026 law, and it contains no recommendation on electronic communications or roaming (MDA-EU-2026-006-0005; MDA-EU-2026-012-0108; MDA-EU-2026-012-0109).

## Open questions

- Whether the regulator has completed the secondary acts under Law No. 72/2025, including the end-user protection regulation, is not reported, and their adoption and a Commission assessment of them would settle it (MDA-GOV-2026-014-0077; MDA-GOV-2026-014-0080).
- The civil society monitor's finding that the law is only partially compatible with the Code is not matched by a detailed Commission analysis of the gaps, and a screening or transposition assessment would show which provisions remain (MDA-CSO-2026-001-0056).
- No source reports how Law No. 106 on the Gigabit Infrastructure Act is applied, and evidence on access disputes, tariffs and network sharing under it would be needed, given the reported uneven application of the earlier access law (MDA-EU-2026-006-0014; MDA-GOV-2026-014-0055).
- No source gives data on roaming use under Roam Like at Home, and regulator or Commission statistics on traffic and prices would show its effect (MDA-EU-2026-012-0107).
