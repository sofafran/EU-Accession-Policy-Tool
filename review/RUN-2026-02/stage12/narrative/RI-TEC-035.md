## In brief

The Commission asks Moldova to align its company law with the EU acquis on the use of digital tools and processes, which covers matters such as online company formation and electronic filing (MDA-EU-2024-001-0020). Law No. 175/2021 allowed documents for registering legal entities to be submitted electronically with an electronic signature (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0408), and the Commission reported in 2024 that a qualified electronic signature had been introduced for filing documents with the business register (MDA-EU-2024-001-0069). In practice, however, online registration ran by e-mail as a provisional solution because the Public Services Agency's systems were delayed (MDA-GOV-2025-003-0059; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0060). The new State Register of Legal Entities, planned for July 2025, was launched in December 2025 and enables registration of all legal forms of entities (MDA-GOV-2025-003-0273; MDA-EU-2026-012-0099). In February 2026 the platform offered online applications such as name reservation and register extracts (MDA-GOV-2026-002-0069), and no source yet shows company formation and filings running fully online at scale. The Government itself considers the EU directive on digital tools in company law only partially transposed (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0415), and a law on digital tools and processes in company law is planned for December 2026 (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0428). What remains is full transposition of these rules, with company formation and filings fully online through the new register. The unresolved gap is partly addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is Medium and tractability is High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The 2023 analytical report listed gaps in alignment with the company law acquis, including the more recent EU laws on digital tools and processes (MDA-EU-2023-002-0042). The 2023 report stated that Moldova needed to align with the acquis on digital tools and processes and on cross-border operations (MDA-EU-2023-001-0065). In 2024 the key recommendation on company law asked Moldova to continue alignment on a long list of areas, among them the use of digital tools and processes, while the report recorded the qualified electronic signature for filing with the business register (MDA-EU-2024-001-0020; MDA-EU-2024-001-0069). The 2025 and 2026 reports in the evidence do not repeat the recommendation on digital tools; the 2026 report records the launch of the new register instead (MDA-EU-2026-012-0099). The requirement has not appeared in a benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition.

## Open questions

- Whether the planned law on digital tools and processes in company law will be adopted by December 2026, and how fully it transposes the directive, is not known; the adopted law and a table of concordance would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0428; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0415).
- It is not shown that companies can now be formed fully online through the new register; data from the Public Services Agency on online registrations, and whether the e-mail route has been closed, would answer it (MDA-GOV-2026-002-0069; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0060).
- The draft amendment to the concept of the State Register of Legal Units had been sent to the Government in 2026; the adopted decision would show what further functions the register is to provide (MDA-GOV-2026-002-0067).
- The Commission's view of alignment after 2024 is not stated; a future report or screening document assessing the digital tools rules would show whether the requirement is still considered open (MDA-EU-2024-001-0020).
