## In brief

The EU asks Moldova to prepare its justice, home affairs and border information systems for connection to EU large-scale systems. In 2026 the Commission found that the migration inspectorate's systems need upgrading for interoperability with national and EU IT systems, and that the border police system's security and data protection weaknesses must be addressed in anticipation of a future connection to EU large-scale IT systems; it also asks for better data exchange through Europol's SIENA channel (MDA-EU-2026-012-0060; MDA-EU-2026-012-0062; MDA-EU-2026-012-0004). Moldova has rolled out SIENA to several law enforcement bodies, and the Commission states that exchanges through it have increased, although it gives no figures (MDA-EU-2026-012-0059). For the EU systems themselves, the responses are plans: interoperability of the fingerprint register with Eurodac by June 2028, a national visa system interoperable with the EU Visa Information System, an analysis by the end of 2027 of how to connect the criminal records register to ECRIS and ECRIS-TCN, and a joint study with eu-LISA (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0403; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0375; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0358; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0402). The Government reports that amendments to the fingerprint law partially transpose the Eurodac Regulation, a step in law only (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0367). What remains is building or upgrading the migration, border and justice systems so that they are secure and ready to connect to Eurodac, the Visa Information System, ECRIS and the other EU large-scale systems. The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The evidence holds no Commission item on this issue from the 2022 Opinion. In 2023 the Commission found that there was no electronic biometric database with fingerprints and photos and that border crossing points were not connected, asked Moldova to plan the database's alignment with Eurodac, and made better data exchange through SIENA a key recommendation (MDA-EU-2023-001-0024; MDA-EU-2023-001-0026; MDA-EU-2023-001-0119). The 2024 and 2025 reports contain no item on Eurodac, the Visa Information System, ECRIS or other EU large-scale systems. In 2026 the Commission's wording shifted from a single database to the readiness of whole systems: it named security and data protection weaknesses in the border police system, the need for upgrades to the migration inspectorate's systems and interoperability ahead of a future connection to EU large-scale IT systems, while repeating the SIENA recommendation (MDA-EU-2026-012-0061; MDA-EU-2026-012-0060; MDA-EU-2026-012-0062; MDA-EU-2026-012-0004). There is no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on these systems.

## Open questions

- Whether the joint study with eu-LISA on the interoperability of the fingerprint register, border police system and visa system with SIS, VIS and Eurodac was completed by its December 2025 date is not reported; the study or a progress report would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0402).
- Whether the border police system's security and data protection weaknesses have been remedied is unknown; a security audit or the Commission's next assessment would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0061).
- Whether the proposed amendments to the fingerprint register decision in line with Eurodac were adopted is not recorded; the adopted Government decision would settle it (MDA-GOV-2026-005-0026; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0403).
- The scale of the increase in exchanges through SIENA is not given; Europol or national exchange statistics would show it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0059).
