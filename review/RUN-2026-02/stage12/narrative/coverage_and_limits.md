## Summary
The run met part of its core source requirements for Moldova, with gaps that matter for particular issues. Of 34 requirements, 9 were found, 15 partly found, 4 are not public, 2 were not found and 4 remain open. The main gaps are of three kinds. First, restricted EU documents: the screening outcomes for Clusters 2 and 3, the negotiating framework and the EU common positions that set the closing benchmarks are marked LIMITE and are not used, so what the benchmarks for Chapter 2 require is known only from public statements, such as the Commission's notice that benchmarks were set when the clusters opened on 13 October 2026 (LIM-0045). Second, Moldova's core laws, including those on foreigners, employment promotion, the Labour Code, personal data protection, electronic identification, cybersecurity and interoperability, are not held as texts, because the official legal database blocks automated access and, on the analyst's instruction, they were not retrieved by hand; findings on legal alignment rest on EU assessments, the National Accession Programme and government drafts, and say so (LIM-0047). Third, no monitoring of the rights of foreign or migrant workers from 2020 onwards was found, and no Rule of Law Report chapter exists for Moldova. Of 77 registered Moldova sources, 56 were available, 17 inaccessible and 4 excluded; the four annual Commission Communications on enlargement policy from 2023 to 2026, registered under North Macedonia, were also used for their passages on Moldova, which brings the sources counted below to 81, of which 60 are available. The evidence runs to the 2026 Moldova report, published on 4 November 2026 within the freeze set at G0 for 6 November 2026, and that report is included. Most national government sources are in Romanian only: 1,712 of the 3,534 Moldova items extracted are machine-translated Romanian, including 1,535 of the 3,173 in-scope items, all capped at Medium confidence, and no reviewer who reads Romanian was named (LIM-0042). Evidence on the Transnistrian region is recorded as sources state it, but Moldova's implementation is not judged where the Government does not exercise effective control, so findings on implementation cover the territory under constitutional control unless a source assesses the region directly (LIM-0044).

The comparator step covered three issues only, as approved at G6, with no full country model of any comparator country; the validated judgements of countries already in the project are context, never evidence about Moldova (LIM-0048). It used public sources only. Croatian documents from 2011 onwards were used for the European Health Insurance Card by logged exception (LIM-0049), and the Croatian Health Insurance Fund reports used are machine translated and capped at Medium (LIM-0050). The final World Bank assessment of Montenegro's electronic procurement system and the Montenegrin procurement administration's page on the system were inaccessible, so the Montenegrin case rests on interim findings.

## Methodological limitations
The run found these limitations, each recorded in its learning log. First-pass extraction read only parts of some long sources, such as the Reform Agenda and a doctoral thesis, so the second-pass check added 123 items, 17% of the sample, a higher share than in the alpha (ALN-0037). The automatic quote check confirms that a quote occurs in its source but not that it ends on a word boundary, and one quote cut mid-word passed (ALN-0038). Conflicts between sources were over-proposed: of 61 proposed, an independent check confirmed 8, and most of the others were figures that measure different things (ALN-0039). Parallel batches of issue judgements proposed the same outcome link twice where an intervention served issues in two batches, giving four links two conflicting pairs of success and attribution ratings, and checked claims of absence against different counts of the country's items (ALN-0042); the duplicates were left unvalidated. Records shared between runs let one run's material appear in another run's outputs until the tools were limited to their own run (ALN-0043), and a source extracted earlier as a comparator needed a change to the data model before this run could extract it again (ALN-0036). Several gates (G1, G2, G3, G2b and G5b) were passed under the analyst's standing instruction without row review and confirmed at a later gate. The analyst was the sole reviewer, no person read the source documents behind the extraction or the comparator cases, and the checks were made by independent agents, which decide nothing.
