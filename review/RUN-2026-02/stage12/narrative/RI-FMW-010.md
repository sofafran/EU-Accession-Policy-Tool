## In brief

The Commission asks Moldova to develop the IT infrastructure, systems, databases and administrative and financial capacity needed for full participation in the Electronic Exchange of Social Security Information (EESSI) system and the EHIC scheme (MDA-EU-2025-001-0021; MDA-EU-2026-012-0014). In February 2026 Moldova designated the e-Governance Agency as the EESSI National Access Point and the National Office of Social Insurance and the National Health Insurance Company as liaison bodies, adopted a law setting the legal basis for EU social security coordination rules and EESSI interconnection, and approved an EESSI Action Plan (MDA-EU-2026-012-0094; MDA-EU-2026-012-0095; MDA-EU-2026-012-0096). These steps are adopted, but no source reports the access point or liaison bodies working or any data exchanged, and Moldova is not connected to EESSI. The Government reports that the information systems of the employment agency and the social insurance house still need to be adjusted for EESSI, that the technical solution and its financing will be chosen only after technical documentation arrives from the Commission, and that 3 of 8 planned IT staff at the health insurance company work on the EHIC and interconnection with European systems (MDA-GOV-2026-007-0030; MDA-GOV-2026-007-0027; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0069). What remains is the IT infrastructure, interconnected systems, staff and funding for full participation in EESSI and for electronic exchange of health insurance data. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving, organisationally and legally only, as no technical connection is reported. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The 2022 Opinion contains no item on electronic exchange of social security information in the evidence. The Commission's 2023 analytical report found that national authorities struggle with administrative capacity and data exchange in international cases, leading to delays (MDA-EU-2023-002-0036). The 2025 report turned this into a key recommendation to develop IT infrastructure for the EHIC and EESSI and administrative capacity in view of joining the future EESSI Network, alongside concluding more social security agreements (MDA-EU-2025-001-0021). The 2026 report broadens the request to technical and organisational measures, administrative and financial capacity and resources, and IT infrastructure, systems and databases for full participation in EURES, EESSI and the EHIC scheme (MDA-EU-2026-012-0014). No Reform Agenda payment condition or benchmark addresses the issue.

## Open questions

- Whether Moldova has connected to EESSI depends on the technical solution and investment still to be decided, and a Commission or e-Governance Agency report of live exchange through the National Access Point would settle it (MDA-GOV-2026-007-0027; MDA-EU-2026-012-0094).
- Whether the employment agency and social insurance systems have been made interoperable with EESSI is not shown, and evidence that the new employment agency system and the adjusted social insurance system are in use would be needed (MDA-GOV-2026-007-0030; MDA-GOV-2026-007-0028).
- The Government states that data exchange takes place exclusively through the MConnect platform, while the data protection authority finds that state bodies still access personal data outside it, and an independent audit of data flows would show which account holds (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0073; MDA-GOV-2026-009-0029).
- Whether the health insurance company has filled its planned IT posts for the EHIC and European interconnection is not known, and later staffing data would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0069).
