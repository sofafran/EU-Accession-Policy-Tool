## In brief

The Commission asks Moldova to prepare its systems in several sectors to connect to EU information systems: above all to advance technical interoperability with the VAT Information Exchange System and the VAT in the Digital Age solutions and to develop digital solutions for the excise systems EMCS and SEED, but also to connect to the civil protection system CECIS through TESTA and to join the radiological exchange systems ECURIE and EURDEP (MDA-EU-2026-012-0137; MDA-EU-2026-012-0138; MDA-EU-2023-001-0109; MDA-EU-2026-012-0157). Some connections already exist, such as access to Tenders Electronic Daily since October 2020, trademark data in the EU Intellectual Property Office's systems and participation in TRACES, but they lie outside the latest requirements (MDA-EU-2021-001-0014; MDA-EU-2023-002-0043; MDA-EU-2023-002-0070). In response, Moldova approved a State Tax Service IT development programme for 2025 to 2027 in October 2025 and a roadmap for tax interoperability with EU systems for 2026 to 2029 in November 2025 (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0441; MDA-EU-2026-012-0140). These are adopted plans, and no source reports their delivery. Connection to the CCN Gateway, interoperability with VIES and other tax systems, connection to TESTA and a liaison point for the Internal Market Information System are all planned, with deadlines from December 2026 to January 2028 (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0455; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0456; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0400; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0406). No source records a new connection to an EU tax or excise system. What remains is building and connecting the tax, excise, radiological, civil protection and internal market systems to the EU systems the Commission names. The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's analytical report of February 2023 found that Moldova had yet to adopt the excise movement control system and that significant efforts were needed to equip the tax administration with IT systems, procedures and staff (MDA-EU-2023-002-0062; MDA-EU-2023-002-0065). The 2023 report found no technical capacity or IT strategy to prepare for interoperability with EMCS and VIES and asked Moldova to set up TESTA to connect to CECIS (MDA-EU-2023-001-0101; MDA-EU-2023-001-0109). In 2024 the Commission still found no IT strategy for interoperability and found Moldova not yet familiar with VIES (MDA-EU-2024-001-0088; MDA-EU-2024-001-0096). The 2025 report noted that the tax IT programme had been prepared but not approved and asked Moldova to align its legislation with the IT solutions for EMCS and SEED (MDA-EU-2025-001-0100; MDA-EU-2025-001-0107). The 2026 report records the roadmap but states that EMCS and SEED solutions remain to be developed and that VIES interoperability must advance, and it adds ECURIE and EURDEP (MDA-EU-2026-012-0138; MDA-EU-2026-012-0137; MDA-EU-2026-012-0156). The Reform Agenda refers to aligning with EU taxation IT systems, but its payment step concerns taxpayer services rather than interoperability (MDA-EU-2025-002-0081).

## Open questions

- Whether the State Tax Service has connected to the CCN Gateway, planned for December 2026, is not known, and a Commission or tax service report of a working connection would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0455).
- No source describes a Moldovan plan or step for EMCS or SEED, and a dated plan or a Commission finding on them would show whether excise interoperability has started (MDA-EU-2026-012-0138).
- Whether the TESTA connection and the IMI liaison point have been set up is not recorded, and evidence of the connection or designation would be needed (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0400; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0406).
- The Commission described Moldova as an active participant in TRACES in 2023 but reports it still preparing for full integration in 2026, and a statement of which TRACES functions are in use would clarify the position (MDA-EU-2023-002-0070; MDA-EU-2026-012-0158).
