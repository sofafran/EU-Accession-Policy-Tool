## In brief

The Commission asks Moldova to prepare to join the European Network of Employment Services (EURES) upon accession, and in 2025 added that this should include reinforcing the Public Employment Service's IT infrastructure (MDA-EU-2023-001-0046; MDA-EU-2025-001-0022). Moldova has created a Labour Migration and EURES Department in the National Employment Agency, adopted Government Decision No. 617/2024 and Government Decision No. 607/2025 setting the national legal framework, designated the Agency as National Coordination Office and approved a procedure for admitting EURES members and partners (MDA-EU-2025-001-0068; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0044; MDA-EU-2025-001-0070; MDA-EU-2026-012-0086; MDA-EU-2026-012-0087). The Commission also reports ten territorial EURES members, implementing acts on data collection and monitoring, and technical standards for matching vacancies and CVs with the EURES portal (MDA-EU-2026-012-0088; MDA-EU-2026-012-0089; MDA-EU-2026-012-0090). These are preparatory steps: no source reports any organisation admitted under the new procedure, and the Government itself describes its decisions as only partial alignment with the EURES Regulation (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0044; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0045). What remains, on the Government's own account, is better data exchange between the Agency and the European platform and the completion of legislative alignment, with an order on interoperability planned for December 2027 (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0046; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0090). There is no outcome evidence. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is High and tractability is High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The fact pack holds no Commission item on EURES from the 2022 Opinion. In February 2023 the Analytical Report found that Moldova had not completed the preparatory work needed for access to EURES, and the 2023 report repeated that finding and asked Moldova to undertake that work for joining upon accession (MDA-EU-2023-002-0023; MDA-EU-2023-001-0054; MDA-EU-2023-001-0046). In 2024 the Commission recorded limited progress, noted an action plan and the designation of the Agency as coordination body, and asked Moldova to continue the preparatory work (MDA-EU-2024-001-0015; MDA-EU-2024-001-0057; MDA-EU-2024-001-0018). The 2025 recommendation kept the same wording but added the reinforcement of the employment service's IT infrastructure (MDA-EU-2025-001-0022). By 2026 the Commission's language had moved from asking for preparation to recording it: Moldova "has taken a series of measures to prepare for joining the network", with the legal framework and the coordination office cited as the basis for good progress (MDA-EU-2026-012-0085; MDA-EU-2026-012-0009). The Commission's 2026 key recommendation asks for the measures, capacity and IT systems needed for full participation in EURES (MDA-EU-2026-012-0014).

## Open questions

- Whether any organisation has been admitted as a EURES member or partner under the November 2025 procedure is unknown; Agency records of admissions or the Commission's next report would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0123).
- Whether the Agency's information system can exchange vacancy and CV data with the EURES portal is not shown; evidence of a working technical connection, or the planned interoperability order, would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0090; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0090).
- Which provisions of the EURES Regulation remain to be transposed is not set out; a transposition table or the Government's next accession programme report would show the remaining gap in legal alignment (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0045; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0046).
- The findings of the Agency's internal audit of how the EURES network is being implemented are not reported; publication of the audit would show whether the arrangements work as intended (MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020).
