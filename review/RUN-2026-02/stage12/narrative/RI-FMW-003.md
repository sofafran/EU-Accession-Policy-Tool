## In brief

The issue is whether EU citizens are barred from public sector posts beyond those that involve the exercise of public authority and the safeguarding of the general interests of the State, which are the only posts that may be reserved for nationals. From 2023 to 2025 the Commission found that EU citizens had no access to posts in public administration bodies (MDA-EU-2023-001-0052; MDA-EU-2024-001-0054; MDA-EU-2025-001-0066). Government Decision No. 566/2025 allows technical, specialist and auxiliary posts to be filled by persons without Moldovan citizenship under employment contracts (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0014). The Commission's 2026 report confirms that the new legislation opened these posts to EU citizens, while civil servant posts remain restricted to Moldovan nationals (MDA-EU-2026-012-0079). No source reports EU citizens actually recruited to the opened posts, so the change is shown in law, not in practice. The Government's own account still excludes public functions that expressly require Moldovan citizenship (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0013), and it plans an analysis, including the case law of the Court of Justice, before deciding which further functions or posts can be opened (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0015; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0122). What remains is opening civil servant posts to EU citizens, except those involving the exercise of public authority. The unresolved gap is partly addressed and the direction of travel is improving, legal only. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The 2023 analytical report stated that employment in the public administration is reserved for Moldovan citizens (MDA-EU-2023-002-0015). The 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports repeated, in identical words, that EU citizens "still" have no access to posts in public administration bodies (MDA-EU-2023-001-0052; MDA-EU-2024-001-0054; MDA-EU-2025-001-0066). The 2026 report changes the finding: it records that certain technical, specialised and auxiliary positions have been opened to EU citizens and narrows the remaining restriction to civil servant posts (MDA-EU-2026-012-0080). The finding sits in the body of the reports throughout; the evidence contains no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition on public sector posts.

## Open questions

- It is not known whether any EU citizen has been recruited to the technical, specialist and auxiliary posts opened in 2025; recruitment data from the public employers or the State Chancellery would show whether the legal change works in practice (MDA-EU-2026-012-0079).
- The Government's planned analytical note on EU citizens' access to public functions, due in August 2026, is not in the evidence; the note itself would show which civil servant functions the Government considers open and on what reasoning (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0122).
- The scope of the "public functions that expressly require Moldovan citizenship" is not set out in the evidence; a list of those functions in the Law on the public function or its implementing acts would show how far the restriction goes beyond the public authority exception (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0013).
- Whether the Commission would accept a partial opening limited to non-civil-servant posts is not stated; a future report or screening document setting out the Commission's test for civil servant posts would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0080).
