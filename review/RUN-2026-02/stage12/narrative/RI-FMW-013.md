## In brief

This issue concerns whether the bodies that administer free movement of workers, notably the employment agency, have an integrity policy, internal controls and audit capacity that prevent corruption. The issue is not raised in EU material, and no Commission document in the evidence states a requirement on it. The only Moldova item is the employment agency's own report that its internal audit service carried out five assurance missions and one unplanned consultancy activity, one of them on how the EURES network is being implemented in Moldova (MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020). The findings of that audit are not reported, and the item says nothing about an integrity policy or internal controls. Nothing is shown to have happened as a result, and what remains cannot be stated, since no source sets out a requirement or a problem for Moldova on this issue. The unresolved gap is insufficient evidence and the direction of travel is insufficient evidence. Priority is Low and tractability is insufficient evidence.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The issue is not raised in EU material for Moldova. Neither the 2022 Opinion, the 2023 analytical report nor the annual reports from 2023 to 2026 contain an item in the evidence on corruption prevention, integrity or internal audit in the bodies administering worker mobility, and no Reform Agenda condition addresses it. There is therefore no EU wording whose change can be traced. The one item available is a national report from 2025, which describes audit activity rather than a requirement (MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020).

## Open questions

- The findings of the employment agency's internal audit of the EURES network are not in the evidence; the audit report itself would show whether any control or integrity weaknesses were found (MDA-GOV-2025-004-0020).
- It is not known whether the employment agency has an integrity plan or corruption risk assessment; the agency's integrity documents, or an assessment by the national anti-corruption body, would settle it.
- No external view of corruption risks in work permit, registration or placement procedures is available; an audit by the Court of Accounts or an independent study of these procedures would show whether there is a problem to address.
- Whether the Commission sees this as a matter for the chapter at all is not stated; a future report or screening document mentioning integrity in the administration of worker mobility would establish an EU requirement against which progress could be judged.
