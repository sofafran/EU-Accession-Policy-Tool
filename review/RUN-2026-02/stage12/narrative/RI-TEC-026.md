## In brief

The Commission asked Moldova to join the third-country trusted list as a first step towards mutual recognition of qualified trust services and to make its wallet interoperable with the European Digital Identity Wallets, and a Reform Agenda payment condition due in December 2026 requires an EU-aligned EVO Sign, membership of the trusted list, eIDAS 2 alignment and 20 e-services in EVO (MDA-EU-2023-001-0096; MDA-EU-2024-001-0082; MDA-EU-2025-002-0035). The Government reports that Law No. 117/2025 opened the market to EU qualified trust service providers, and the Commission reports that amendments of December 2025 fully aligned the rules on qualified trust service providers and that Moldova joined the EU third-country trusted list (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0200; MDA-EU-2026-012-0120). The sources conflict on the related draft law: the Commission reports full alignment in December 2025, while the Government's 2025 progress report still describes the draft as being prepared. They also conflict on EVOSign: the Commission reports it launched and the ministry says it is officially available, while other public bodies say it is still at the pilot stage and lacks the confirmations needed for use (MDA-EU-2025-001-0044; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0299; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0252; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0321). New identity cards with a qualified signature have been issued since March 2025, one million of them in 2025, though no source shows how often the signature is used (MDA-GOV-2026-002-0010; MDA-CSO-2026-001-0077). The EU-aligned wallet was started only in September 2025, its cross-border interoperability has been tested, and remote identification has moved to 2027 (MDA-GOV-2026-002-0087; MDA-EU-2026-012-0123; MDA-GOV-2026-002-0085). Use has barely moved: the Commission gives 200 000 electronic signature users, 9.8% of adults, in 2024 and over 266 000, 10%, in 2025, and civil society reports fees of at least 11 euros a year (MDA-EU-2024-001-0042; MDA-EU-2025-001-0043; MDA-CSO-2026-001-0076). What remains is a working EU-aligned wallet, mutual recognition in practice and much wider, cheaper use of electronic identification and signatures. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The 2022 Opinion contains no item on electronic identification in the evidence. In 2023 the Commission noted progress in making trust services compatible with eIDAS and recommended joining the third-country trusted list (MDA-EU-2023-001-0093; MDA-EU-2023-001-0096). In 2024, after the EVO wallet was launched, it called for further work on interoperability with the European Digital Identity Wallets (MDA-EU-2024-001-0081; MDA-EU-2024-001-0082). The Reform Agenda turned these into a single payment condition for December 2026, adding an eIDAS 2 compliant law on mutual recognition and at least 20 public services through EVO Sign (MDA-EU-2025-002-0035; MDA-EU-2025-002-0041). The 2026 report records the December 2025 amendments, the trusted list, a national strategy to align with eIDAS 2.0 and wallet interoperability tests, and sets no new recommendation on the issue in the evidence (MDA-EU-2026-012-0120; MDA-EU-2026-012-0121; MDA-EU-2026-012-0123).

## Open questions

- Whether the December 2025 amendments are the eIDAS 2 draft law or a narrower change is unclear, and the text of the adopted act or a Commission transposition assessment would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0120; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0200).
- Whether EVOSign is in full operation is disputed, and evidence of signatures made through it in public services would settle it (MDA-GOV-2025-003-0299; MDA-GOV-2025-003-0321).
- Whether the EU-aligned wallet was launched with at least 20 e-services by December 2026 is not yet known, and the Commission's assessment of the Reform Agenda payment request would show it (MDA-EU-2025-002-0035; MDA-GOV-2026-002-0087).
- Whether electronic signature use rises above about 10% of adults, now that identity cards carry a signature, is not known, and later usage data, ideally from the Commission, would settle it (MDA-EU-2025-001-0043; MDA-CSO-2026-001-0077).
