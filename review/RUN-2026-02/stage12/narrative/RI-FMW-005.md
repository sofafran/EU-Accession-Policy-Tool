## In brief

The Commission asks Moldova to ensure the capacity, resources and IT infrastructure, systems and databases needed for full participation in EURES, the Electronic Exchange of Social Security Information system and the EHIC scheme (MDA-EU-2026-012-0014). Moldova has set up a Labour Migration and EURES department in the National Employment Agency from January 2025, with five of its six posts filled, assigned the Agency the role of EURES National Coordination Office, and developed technical standards, procedures and formats for matching vacancies and CVs with the EURES portal (MDA-EU-2025-001-0068; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0020; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0049; MDA-EU-2026-012-0090). The Commission reports that the Agency developed a new integrated information system in December 2025, aiming at fully digitalised services by 2027, and a 2026 Government document says the system, funded by the EU Delegation, is still being developed (MDA-EU-2026-012-0142; MDA-GOV-2026-007-0028). No source shows the new system in use, and the Agency's own reports for 2025 and 2026 state that the lack of a modern information system affects the efficiency and quality of its services (MDA-GOV-2025-004-0021; MDA-GOV-2026-004-0027). Interoperability with the EURES platform, staff training on EURES and minimum requirements for personal data in that exchange are planned for 2028 and are not reported as delivered (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0107; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0108; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0110). What remains is a working connection between the Agency's information system and the EURES platform, operated by trained staff, before accession. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The issue does not appear in the evidence from the 2022 Opinion or the 2023 and 2024 reports. It is first raised in the 2025 report, whose key recommendation asks Moldova to continue preparatory work for joining EURES upon accession, including by reinforcing the Public Employment Service's IT infrastructure (MDA-EU-2025-001-0022). The 2026 report widens the recommendation from EURES alone to the technical and organisational measures, administrative and financial capacity and IT systems and databases needed for full participation in EURES, EESSI and the EHIC scheme (MDA-EU-2026-012-0014). No Reform Agenda payment condition or benchmark addresses the issue.

## Open questions

- Whether the new integrated information system is in use is not reported, and an Agency activity report or Commission assessment showing it in operation would settle it (MDA-EU-2026-012-0142; MDA-GOV-2026-007-0028).
- Interoperability between the Agency's Labour Market information system and the EURES platform is planned for November 2028, and evidence of a tested data exchange on vacancies and CVs would show whether it has been achieved (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0049; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0107).
- No source records that staff training on EURES or on serving EU citizens under Law No. 200/2025 has taken place, and training records or reports naming its content would be needed (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0103; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0108; MDA-EU-2026-012-0142).
- The Agency reports unfilled posts within its staff ceiling, and later staffing data would show whether its capacity to run the EURES functions has grown (MDA-GOV-2025-002-0018; MDA-GOV-2025-002-0019).
