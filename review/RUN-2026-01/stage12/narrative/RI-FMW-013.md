## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to strengthen the framework for preventing corruption in free movement of workers, and in particular asks the Employment Agency to sign an integrity policy, nominate a person to implement it, reinforce its internal audit staff and ensure controls, documentation and monitoring (MKD-EU-2025-003-0114; MKD-EU-2025-003-0116; MKD-EU-2025-003-0195; MKD-EU-2025-003-0118). The Commission states that the agency applies the code for civil servants but has not adopted an integrity policy, and that it had planned to sign one but had not done so (MKD-EU-2024-001-0070; MKD-EU-2025-003-0113; MKD-EU-2025-001-0110). No country intervention responding to this issue is linked in the evidence, so there is no implementation status or outcome to report. The Commission describes the framework as weak and the country as still at an early stage in this domain (MKD-EU-2024-001-0069; MKD-EU-2025-003-0111; MKD-EU-2025-003-0117). The validated assessment is that the gap is open, the direction of travel is persistent, priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The requirement first appears in the evidence in 2023, when the Commission states that the Employment Service Agency needs to adopt an integrity policy document (MKD-EU-2023-001-0101). In 2024 the Commission adds that the specific framework for preventing corruption in free movement of workers remains weak (MKD-EU-2024-001-0069; MKD-EU-2024-001-0070). The 2025 screening report widens and sharpens the wording: it asks for the policy to be signed, for a person to be nominated to implement it, for internal audit staff to be reinforced, for controls within the institutions involved and for the Ministry of Labour and Social Policy's capacities to be reinforced, and it rates the country as still at an early stage (MKD-EU-2025-003-0116; MKD-EU-2025-003-0195; MKD-EU-2025-003-0118; MKD-EU-2025-003-0112; MKD-EU-2025-003-0117). The November 2025 report repeats that no integrity policy has yet been adopted (MKD-EU-2025-001-0110).

## Open questions

- The evidence contains only Commission statements, so it does not show whether the agency has since signed the integrity policy or named a person to implement it; a government or agency document recording the signature and appointment would settle this (MKD-EU-2025-003-0113; MKD-EU-2025-001-0110).
- No source reports the current staffing of the agency's internal audit department or the controls in place, so the scale of the remaining capacity gap is unknown; audit reports or staffing data for the agency would settle it (MKD-EU-2025-003-0116).
- Two screening report items rest on Medium confidence evidence, so a second source confirming the planned signature and the weakness of the framework would strengthen the record (MKD-EU-2025-003-0113; MKD-EU-2025-003-0111).
