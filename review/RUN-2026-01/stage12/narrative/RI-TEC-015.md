## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to complete the digitalisation of institutions' registers and data exchange, to improve the quality of population register data, and to digitalise intellectual property registration further (MKD-EU-2025-001-0004; MKD-EU-2023-001-0155; MKD-EU-2025-003-0165). The Central Population Register is assessed as in practice: the Commission reported it operational in 2020 and used to cross-check the 2021 census (MKD-EU-2020-001-0016; MKD-EU-2022-001-0017). The Commission nonetheless found its use still limited in 2024, and the government's machine-translated 2025 strategy also describes it as underused and in need of a larger data set (MKD-EU-2024-001-0026; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0067; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0141). The platform for digitalising registers, the Register of Registers and the digital register of charges are assessed only as proposed, and the 2023 strategy's baseline was no digital registers in 2022 (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0025; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0233; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0159; MKD-GOV-2023-002-0069). The Commission still finds municipal property registers analogue (MKD-EU-2024-001-0047). No outcome has been assessed. The gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, the priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's focus has moved from the population register to registers in general. In 2021 it asked for population register data to be fully uploaded and checked and used to cross-check census data, and in 2022 and 2023 it asked for better data quality, continuously verified against other registers (MKD-EU-2021-001-0106; MKD-EU-2022-001-0112; MKD-EU-2023-001-0155). From 2023 a key recommendation asks the country to complete the digitalisation of institutions' registers and data exchange as part of fully implementing the law on general administrative procedures, repeated almost word for word in 2024 and 2025 (MKD-EU-2023-001-0002; MKD-EU-2024-001-0002; MKD-EU-2025-001-0004). The 2025 report notes efforts but keeps the recommendation (MKD-EU-2025-001-0003). The 2025 screening report adds intellectual property registration (MKD-EU-2025-003-0164; MKD-EU-2025-003-0165).

## Open questions

- No later source confirms that the register digitalisation platform came into use in 2023 as planned, and a count of digitalised registers against the strategy's baseline and targets would settle progress (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0025; MKD-GOV-2023-002-0069).
- The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed while the 2023 strategy states that the 2019 digital laws are aligned with European legislation, a confirmed conflict that a Commission alignment assessment of the law would settle (MKD-EU-2023-001-0138; MKD-GOV-2023-002-0016).
- The fully digital National Real Estate Register rests only on the country's own account, while the Commission finds municipal property registers analogue, so independent evidence on use of each register is missing (MKD-EU-2023-003-0085; MKD-EU-2023-003-0086).
- Much of the evidence on register plans and on the underuse of the population register comes from machine-translated government strategies, capped at Medium confidence, which an official English version or independent usage data would strengthen (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0067; MKD-GOV-2023-002-0077).
