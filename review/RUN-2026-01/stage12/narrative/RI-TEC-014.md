## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align its national broadband targets with the Digital Decade, to align further with EU rules on aid for broadband deployment, and to strengthen the capacity of the public broadcasting enterprise mandated to build optical infrastructure (MKD-EU-2025-004-0011; MKD-EU-2025-003-0192; MKD-EU-2025-001-0132). The National Operational Broadband Plan remains in force, but the Commission reported its implementation slowed in 2021 and further delayed in 2022 (MKD-EU-2025-001-0130; MKD-EU-2021-001-0087; MKD-EU-2022-001-0090). The country states that its targets will be aligned with the Digital Compass and that a mapping study is in preparation (MKD-EU-2025-004-0018; MKD-EU-2025-004-0026). A feasibility study for a national transport optical network has started, but no network is built (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0039; MKD-GOV-2024-003-0003). The Broadband Competence Office, in machine translation, reports that none of the three measures towards the 100 Mbps target was implemented (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0038). The validated judgements are priority Medium, direction of travel persistent, unresolved gap partly_addressed and tractability Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

In 2020 the Commission recorded some progress, citing ICT strategy preparations and the new Broadband Competence Office (MKD-EU-2020-001-0103). In 2022 and 2023 its assessments found a strategic approach to broadband access lacking, the 2023 one adding national strategic investment and the targets of the National Broadband Plan (MKD-EU-2022-006-0040; MKD-EU-2023-006-0038). The 2025 screening made the requirement more specific: align the targets with the Digital Decade and align further on State aid for broadband, while noting that the Western Balkans Investment Framework and the Connecting Europe Facility had not been used for infrastructure (MKD-EU-2025-004-0011; MKD-EU-2025-003-0192; MKD-EU-2025-004-0025). In November 2025 the Commission added the need to strengthen the public broadcasting enterprise (MKD-EU-2025-001-0132). No 2026 Commission item addresses the framework.

## Open questions

- Sources conflict on the plan's period, with ITU and the 2025 report giving 2019 to 2029 and the 2021, 2022 and 2024 reports giving 2019 to 2023, and the adopted plan text would settle how delays should be measured against its targets (MKD-IO-2022-002-0019; MKD-EU-2021-001-0087; MKD-EU-2022-001-0090; MKD-EU-2024-001-0083; MKD-EU-2025-001-0130).
- The claim that the 2030 ICT strategy goals are aligned with the Digital Decade, and the Office's finding on the three unimplemented measures, rest on machine-translated government text capped at Medium confidence, so official English versions or the Office's report itself would be needed (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0017; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0004; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0038).
- The reported non-use of the Western Balkans Investment Framework sits beside its technical assistance for the network feasibility study, which appears to separate study from construction but should be checked against funding records (MKD-EU-2025-004-0025; MKD-GOV-2024-003-0002).
- The financing figures for the network come from a table flagged as possibly misread, so a confirmed financing plan is needed before the funding gap can be assessed (MKD-GOV-2024-003-0003).
