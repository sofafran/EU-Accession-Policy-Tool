## In brief

The Commission has repeatedly called for wider broadband access, and the Reform Agenda ties a payment to 5G roll-out and a 5G security by-law (MKD-EU-2021-001-0044; MKD-EU-2024-007-0008). The Commission reports continued 5G expansion, and a machine-translated national source gives 96.99% population coverage (MKD-EU-2025-001-0143; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0023); 5G spectrum assignment and roll-out are validated as in_practice. The national broadband plan is validated as implemented, but its success is insufficient_evidence, and the national optical network is only proposed. The Commission reports household internet access rising to 90.8% in 2024 (MKD-EU-2025-001-0083), yet in 2025 it found that smaller companies and rural areas still face significant obstacles (MKD-EU-2025-001-0086), and take-up of the fastest connections remains very low (MKD-EU-2024-008-0025). Sources conflict on fixed coverage against the EU average. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording has been largely stable. From 2021 to 2023 it repeated that there is a particular need to increase access to broadband and that smaller companies still face obstacles to fixed broadband (MKD-EU-2021-001-0044; MKD-EU-2022-001-0055; MKD-EU-2023-001-0072). From 2024 it added fast internet for small businesses and non-urban areas and named rural areas alongside smaller companies (MKD-EU-2024-009-0008; MKD-EU-2024-001-0053). The 2024 Reform Agenda added a payment condition on 5G roll-out (MKD-EU-2024-007-0008). In 2025 the wording hardened to significant obstacles for smaller companies and rural areas (MKD-EU-2025-001-0086), while the 2026 assessment repeated the earlier sentence (MKD-EU-2026-003-0005).

## Open questions

- The size of the gap with the EU is unsettled because the ICT strategy and the Commission give conflicting fixed coverage figures and EU averages (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0035; MKD-EU-2025-001-0084); checking the definitions behind each measure would settle it.
- No series tracks the rural and small-business gap itself, as the 2023 rural figure is a single point (MKD-EU-2024-009-0010), so repeated coverage and take-up data by area and firm size are needed.
- The outcome of the 2025 spectrum tender rests only on machine-translated regulator reporting capped at Medium confidence, and its success and attribution are insufficient_evidence (MKD-GOV-2026-003-0012); a published award decision would settle it.
- Whether the national broadband plan caused the 5G expansion is insufficient_evidence (MKD-EU-2023-001-0125), so evidence on use and on the plan's own contribution, as distinct from operator investment, is needed.
