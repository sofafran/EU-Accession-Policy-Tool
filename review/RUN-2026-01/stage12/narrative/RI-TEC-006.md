## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to complete its e-procurement platform, build the confidence of contracting authorities to use the e-market for low-value contracts, and develop the red flag system and data analysis to report on possible corruption or bid-rigging (MKD-EU-2020-001-0093; MKD-EU-2023-003-0137; MKD-EU-2023-003-0123). The Commission states that the electronic procurement system works efficiently and that its use is obligatory, and the system is assessed as in_practice (MKD-EU-2024-001-0055; MKD-EU-2025-001-0092; MKD-EU-2023-003-0112). The e-market was launched in 2022, and the Commission reports 70 awards through it in 2022 and 258 in 2023 (MKD-EU-2022-001-0056; MKD-EU-2023-001-0075; MKD-EU-2024-001-0056). The Commission credits e-appeals and the electronic system with a high level of transparency, assessed as worked, though not with a fall in corruption (MKD-EU-2023-003-0121). What remains is raising e-market use to the 800 awards set as a Reform Agenda payment condition for 2026, wider use of e-catalogues and stronger data analysis (MKD-EU-2024-007-0001; MKD-EU-2023-003-0114). The gap is assessed as partly_addressed. Priority is Medium, direction of travel is improving and tractability is High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording has moved from building the platform to getting it used. In 2020 it asks for the e-procurement platform to be completed and the portal upgraded with new modules, and in 2021 for adoption of the bylaw on the low-value e-marketplace (MKD-EU-2020-001-0093; MKD-EU-2020-001-0094; MKD-EU-2021-001-0045). In 2023 the focus turns to use: the Commission finds poor use of the e-market, little interest from procuring entities and e-catalogues only just starting, and asks for further development of the red flag system and data analysis (MKD-EU-2023-003-0137; MKD-EU-2023-001-0074; MKD-EU-2023-003-0114; MKD-EU-2023-003-0123). From 2024 the requirement becomes a quantified payment condition of at least 800 e-market awards in 2026, while the Commission repeats that the core system functions efficiently (MKD-EU-2024-007-0001; MKD-EU-2025-001-0092).

## Open questions

- The Commission's 2020 report says the e-appeals section was still being developed, while North Macedonia reports it fully operational from 1 April 2019, and this confirmed conflict remains unresolved pending a dated record from the Bureau or the State Appeals Commission (MKD-EU-2020-001-0096; MKD-EU-2023-003-0115).
- The 2024 figure of 350 small-value transactions does not name the e-market, so e-market award data for 2024 to 2026 would be needed to judge progress towards the 800 target (MKD-EU-2025-001-0091).
- Whether the bylaw on the low-value e-marketplace was ever adopted is insufficient_evidence, and the official gazette or a Bureau statement would settle it (MKD-EU-2021-001-0046).
- No evidence after 2023 shows how e-catalogues or the red flag system are used, and usage figures or Commission findings on data analysis would be needed (MKD-EU-2023-003-0122; MKD-EU-2023-003-0114).
