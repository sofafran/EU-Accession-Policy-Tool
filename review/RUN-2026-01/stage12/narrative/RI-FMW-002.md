## In brief

The Commission requires that, by accession, EU citizens can access North Macedonia's labour market without restrictions such as work permits, can reside for work without a visa or residence permit, and are specifically excluded from the annual quotas for foreign workers (MKD-EU-2025-003-0025; MKD-EU-2025-003-0028). The Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners has been in force since 2015 and was amended in 2021 and 2025, but the 2025 amendments only rename ministries and bodies (MKD-GOV-2015-001-0001; MKD-GOV-2021-001-0001; MKD-GOV-2025-001-0001; MKD-GOV-2025-001-0002). The Commission reports that the Employment Agency issues work permits under annual quotas, set at 10 000 for 2025 (MKD-EU-2023-001-0093; MKD-EU-2025-001-0099). A law on seasonal work allows EU citizens access, but the Commission states that the conditions remain to be set in implementing legislation (MKD-EU-2025-001-0102; MKD-EU-2025-001-0103). Further amendments are listed only as proposals (MKD-GOV-2025-003-0011; MKD-GOV-2026-001-0007). The validated judgements are priority Medium, direction of travel persistent, unresolved gap open and tractability Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's description of the regime has hardly changed: from 2020 its reports state that the country sets annual work permit quotas, and the 2024 and November 2025 reports repeat this (MKD-EU-2020-001-0081; MKD-EU-2024-001-0059; MKD-EU-2025-001-0099). What changed is how explicit the requirement became. In 2024 the Commission asked only for further alignment of the Law on work and employment of foreigners (MKD-EU-2024-001-0048). The February 2025 screening report then set out the requirement in full: no work permit, no visa or residence permit for work, and explicit exclusion from the quotas (MKD-EU-2025-003-0025; MKD-EU-2025-003-0028). In November 2025 the Commission added that the conditions for EU citizens in seasonal work remain to be specified (MKD-EU-2025-001-0103).

## Open questions

- The Commission states that the law does not distinguish EU citizens from other foreigners, while North Macedonia states that the quotas do not apply to EU citizens under a reciprocity agreement, and this confirmed conflict stands; evidence that such an agreement was concluded and applied would settle it (MKD-EU-2024-001-0058; MKD-EU-2025-003-0010; MKD-GOV-2015-001-0026).
- The legal provision that partly explains the country's position, Article 5(2) of the 2015 law, is available only in machine translation and is capped at Medium confidence, so an official English text or a checked translation is needed (MKD-GOV-2015-001-0026).
- Whether the secondary legislation due within 30 days under the 2021 amending law was adopted is not shown (insufficient evidence), and its publication in the Official Gazette would settle this (MKD-GOV-2021-001-0032).
- The permit counts of 755 for 2021 and about 8 900 for 2024 appear to rest on different bases, so a consistent annual series of permits by type, with any figures for EU citizens, would be needed to read a trend (MKD-EU-2022-001-0068; MKD-EU-2025-001-0104; MKD-GOV-2022-002-0005).
