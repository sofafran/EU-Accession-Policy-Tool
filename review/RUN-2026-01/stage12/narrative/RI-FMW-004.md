## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to establish a system for appointing EURES members and partners and to give private employment agencies access to it, and its key recommendation asks the country to strengthen the Employment Service Agency and align legislation and good practice with the EURES and European Labour Authority regulations (MKD-EU-2025-003-0066; MKD-EU-2025-003-0067; MKD-EU-2025-001-0012). At screening, North Macedonia stated that the Agency will be the main EURES implementing body and National Coordination Office, and that it foresees an EU-funded analysis of harmonisation needs and stronger Agency capacity (MKD-EU-2025-003-0049; MKD-EU-2025-003-0060). The amendments needed to comply fully have a deadline of 2030, with implementation upon accession (MKD-EU-2025-003-0068). All four responding measures remain proposed, and the Commission reported no developments on EURES in November 2025 (MKD-EU-2025-001-0106). There is no outcome evidence. The validated judgements are priority High, direction of travel persistent, unresolved gap partly_addressed and tractability Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

From 2020 to 2022 the Commission recorded only that nothing had been done to prepare for joining EURES on accession (MKD-EU-2020-001-0083; MKD-EU-2021-001-0067; MKD-EU-2022-001-0069). From 2023 the key recommendation asked the country to strengthen the Employment Service Agency and align legislation and good practice with EURES and European Labour Authority rules, in near-identical words in 2023, 2024 and 2025 (MKD-EU-2023-001-0089; MKD-EU-2024-001-0007; MKD-EU-2025-001-0012). The 2025 screening report made the requirement specific: an appointment system for EURES members and partners, and access and adequate connection for private employment agencies (MKD-EU-2025-003-0066; MKD-EU-2025-003-0067; MKD-EU-2025-003-0070). It also rated preparation as good and alignment as good at IT and structural levels (MKD-EU-2025-003-0062; MKD-EU-2025-003-0069).

## Open questions

- The screening report's rating of a good level of preparation sits in tension with its own finding that no appointment system exists and with the annual reports of no developments, and the analysis reports this tension rather than resolving it; the Commission's next assessment or a statement of the basis for the rating would settle it (MKD-EU-2025-003-0062; MKD-EU-2025-003-0058; MKD-EU-2025-001-0106).
- No source shows whether the planned analysis of harmonisation needs, the designation of the Agency as National Coordination Office or the capacity strengthening has started, and Agency or government progress reports would settle this (MKD-EU-2025-003-0049; MKD-EU-2025-003-0060).
- The content and timing of the legislative amendments within the 2030 deadline are unknown, and a draft law or a dated legislative plan would show whether the appointment system and private agency access are covered (MKD-EU-2025-003-0068).
