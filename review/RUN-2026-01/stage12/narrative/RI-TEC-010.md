## In brief

The Commission has asked North Macedonia to digitalise its justice system, including digital communication with courts, videoconferencing for hearings and connections between justice IT systems (MKD-EU-2020-001-0030; MKD-EU-2024-001-0035; MKD-EU-2025-001-0058). The Commission's 2026 Rule of Law report describes the digital interconnection of the Administrative Court, the Higher Administrative Court and other public bodies as a key advancement that enables electronic exchange of data and documents (MKD-EU-2026-006-0002). Its implementation status is validated as implemented, but this rests on one statement with no usage data, and no outcome is recorded, so no result can be claimed. The Luris software, which North Macedonia said in 2023 needed connecting between the Ministry of Justice and the Public Prosecutor's Office, is at insufficient_evidence (MKD-EU-2023-003-0130). The validated unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is Medium and tractability is Medium, a tentative rating.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording has narrowed over time. In 2020 it said that IT connections needed to be prioritised, including full digitalisation of cases and summoning, with training for judges and judicial staff (MKD-EU-2020-001-0030). In 2023 it said that digitalisation, including interoperability between IT systems, and coordination between the relevant authorities needed to improve (MKD-EU-2023-003-0089). In 2024 and 2025 it used identical wording, calling for more efforts to digitalise the justice system, in particular communication with courts and videoconferencing for court hearings (MKD-EU-2024-001-0035; MKD-EU-2025-001-0058). No Commission item from 2021 or 2022 is in the evidence.

## Open questions

- Whether the administrative courts' interconnection is used and has improved coordination is not settled, because the only evidence is one statement without usage data (MKD-EU-2026-006-0002); system usage figures or case processing data would settle it.
- Whether the Luris software works and was connected between the Ministry of Justice and the Public Prosecutor's Office is at insufficient_evidence (MKD-EU-2023-003-0130); a later Commission or government statement on its status would settle it.
- Progress on digital communication with courts generally and on videoconferencing for hearings is not shown by any intervention item, so government or Commission reporting on these specific measures is needed.
- The evidence base is thin, with eight items, none for 2021 or 2022 and most flagged theme_doubtful, so further Commission or justice sector sources would strengthen the direction of travel judgement.
