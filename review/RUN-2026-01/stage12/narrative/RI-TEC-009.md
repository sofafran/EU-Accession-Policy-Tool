## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to make eHealth data interoperable with Health Insurance Fund data, to improve the eHealth appointment system and to align its legislation with the EU acquis on eHealth (MKD-EU-2025-001-0018; MKD-EU-2025-001-0213; MKD-EU-2025-003-0183). The country runs the Moj Termin eHealth system, which the Commission says was introduced in 2013 and helped to significantly reduce waiting times, and it launched the MyHealth mobile application in 2023 and later added new modules (MKD-EU-2022-006-0041; MKD-EU-2023-001-0208; MKD-EU-2025-001-0123). The Commission's statement on waiting times is judged as worked with Medium attribution, but it gives no figures. The Commission also finds that referrals were still on paper in 2024 and that the system is not yet interoperable with Health Insurance Fund data (MKD-EU-2024-001-0077; MKD-EU-2025-001-0124). Planned connections to the population register and the digital wallet remain proposed (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0235; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0236). The validated assessment is that the gap is open, the direction of travel is persistent, priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

In 2022 and 2023 the Commission's wording is identical: Moj Termin should be extended to primary care doctors (MKD-EU-2022-006-0042; MKD-EU-2023-006-0040). In 2024 the focus shifts to the system's shortcomings, as the Commission finds that referrals are still done on paper and that the application is not interoperable with Health Insurance Fund data (MKD-EU-2024-001-0077). The 2025 screening report adds a new element, stating that national legislation is not aligned with the EU acquis on eHealth (MKD-EU-2025-003-0174; MKD-EU-2025-003-0176; MKD-EU-2025-003-0183). In November 2025 interoperability with Health Insurance Fund data is raised to a key recommendation, and the Commission adds that the appointment system needs to be improved (MKD-EU-2025-001-0018; MKD-EU-2025-001-0124; MKD-EU-2025-001-0213).

## Open questions

- The Commission's 2024 finding that referrals are on paper sits beside the screening report's account of a central tool that stores referrals; this is a noted difference rather than a confirmed conflict, and evidence on how referrals are issued in practice would settle it (MKD-EU-2024-001-0077; MKD-EU-2025-003-0176).
- The platform for appointments and traceability of health data is rated insufficient_evidence because no item after 2023 says what became of it, so a later government or Commission account would settle its status (MKD-EU-2022-006-0043; MKD-EU-2023-006-0041).
- The reported reduction in waiting times rests on Commission statements with no figures, and ITU's statement on improved patient management is flagged as a possible misread, so waiting-time data from the health system would settle the size of the effect (MKD-EU-2022-006-0041; MKD-IO-2022-002-0074).
- The planned connections of Moj Termin to other systems come from a machine-translated national strategy capped at Medium confidence, and none names Health Insurance Fund data, so a plan or report covering that link would settle whether the core requirement is being addressed (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0235; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0236; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0156).
