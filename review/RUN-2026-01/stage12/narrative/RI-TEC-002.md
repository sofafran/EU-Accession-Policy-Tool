## In brief

The Commission asked North Macedonia from 2020 to 2024 to finalise and adopt a long-term ICT strategy, and since 2022 it has asked for stronger ministry capacity to coordinate digital transformation (MKD-EU-2020-001-0105; MKD-EU-2024-001-0015; MKD-EU-2022-001-0099; MKD-EU-2025-001-0128). The Commission reports that a Ministry of Digital Transformation was set up and that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan were adopted in 2025 (MKD-EU-2025-001-0045; MKD-EU-2025-001-0127). Adoption is not improvement in governance: national audit and strategy texts, machine translated and capped at Medium confidence, report under half of ministry IT posts filled, no confirmed National Council activity in 2023 and no central register of ICT systems (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0037; MKD-GOV-2024-007-0070; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0082). The Commission still asks for more IT staff and better coordination, and encouraged a digital agency whose need the country said it would reassess (MKD-EU-2025-001-0052; MKD-EU-2025-014-0004; MKD-EU-2025-004-0004). The validated assessment is partly_addressed, with priority High, direction of travel persistent and tractability Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The key recommendation to finalise the long-term ICT strategy appeared in 2020 and, as finalise and adopt, in every report from 2021 to 2024, with the 2022 and 2024 versions adding alignment with electronic communications and network security rules and, in 2024, adoption of the cybersecurity strategy (MKD-EU-2020-001-0105; MKD-EU-2021-001-0083; MKD-EU-2022-001-0087; MKD-EU-2023-001-0119; MKD-EU-2024-001-0015). It left the list in 2025, when the Commission reported the strategy adopted (MKD-EU-2025-001-0127). The capacity request has been stable since 2022, moving from the Ministry of Information Society and Administration to the Ministry of Digital Transformation (MKD-EU-2022-001-0099; MKD-EU-2023-001-0149; MKD-EU-2024-001-0080; MKD-EU-2025-001-0128). In 2025 the Commission added a call to recruit and retain more IT staff, while its screening assessment described the institutional set-up as well structured (MKD-EU-2025-001-0052; MKD-EU-2025-004-0005).

## Open questions

- The Commission's 2025 statement that a long-term ICT strategy was adopted is linked to SMART/MK: 2030 only by timing, and whether the earlier unadopted drafts became that strategy is not stated, so a text naming the adopted strategy would settle it (MKD-EU-2025-001-0127; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0005).
- Sources differ on the National Council for Digital Transformation, which the 2025 strategy describes as formed to plan and coordinate while the State Audit Office could not confirm any activity in 2023, and records of its meetings and decisions since then would show whether it functions (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0167; MKD-GOV-2024-007-0070).
- Whether a national digital agency will be set up is unresolved, since the Commission encouraged it and the country said it would reassess the need, and a government decision either way would settle it (MKD-EU-2025-014-0004; MKD-EU-2025-004-0004).
- The staffing and coordination evidence rests on machine-translated national texts capped at Medium confidence, and current IT staffing figures and evidence of the adopted strategy being carried out would be needed to judge whether governance is improving (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0037; MKD-GOV-2024-007-0078; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0169).
