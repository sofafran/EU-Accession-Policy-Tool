## In brief

The Commission finds that digitalisation of the economy remains low and restrains the competitiveness of domestic companies, and it asks North Macedonia to support companies through the European Digital Innovation Hub and to take part actively in Digital Europe Programme calls (MKD-EU-2026-003-0003; MKD-EU-2025-001-0029; MKD-EU-2025-014-0005). The country signed an association agreement to the Digital Europe Programme in June 2023, and the Commission reports that the first European Digital Innovation Hub was set up (MKD-EU-2023-001-0143; MKD-EU-2025-001-0157). No item shows actual participation in calls. SME vouchers, a support portal and a grant mechanism appear only as plans in a machine-translated strategy (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0240; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0239; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0159). The Commission notes that SME digital adoption has accelerated but remains uneven and largely basic (MKD-EU-2025-001-0085). No outcome is linked to any intervention. The validated assessment is that the gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, priority is Medium and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording is close to unchanged. From 2020 to 2023 it states that digitalisation of the economy is advancing but remains low compared with the EU (MKD-EU-2020-001-0070; MKD-EU-2021-001-0039; MKD-EU-2022-001-0050; MKD-EU-2023-001-0068). In 2024 and 2025 the wording becomes advancing slowly, and economic governance documents link low digitalisation and weak innovation to weak business competitiveness (MKD-EU-2024-001-0050; MKD-EU-2025-001-0082; MKD-EU-2024-009-0003; MKD-EU-2025-012-0003). In 2025 the Commission adds more specific asks, on active participation in Digital Europe calls and support to companies through the European Digital Innovation Hub, and notes accelerated but uneven SME adoption (MKD-EU-2025-014-0005; MKD-EU-2025-001-0029; MKD-EU-2025-001-0085). In 2026 it still describes digitalisation as low (MKD-EU-2026-003-0003).

## Open questions

- No item shows North Macedonia taking part in Digital Europe Programme calls or how many companies the innovation hub has supported, so participation and hub activity data would settle whether these channels are used (MKD-EU-2025-014-0005; MKD-EU-2025-001-0157).
- The online sales figures of 3% of SMEs and 19.7% of enterprises use different populations and the 54.5% website figure is repeated across reports, so a consistent time series on enterprise digital adoption would be needed to show any trend (MKD-IO-2022-002-0079; MKD-GOV-2024-010-0003; MKD-EU-2021-001-0043; MKD-EU-2023-001-0071).
- The C-JET grant to assess SME digital needs is rated insufficient_evidence, so a World Bank report on its status and findings would settle it (MKD-IO-2024-001-0109).
- The evidence on planned support schemes and on the absence of a support mechanism comes from a machine-translated strategy capped at Medium confidence, so programme documents or budget data showing schemes in operation would strengthen the record (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0084; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0240).
