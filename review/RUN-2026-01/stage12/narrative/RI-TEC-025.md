## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align its law on electronic identification and trust services with the revised EU Digital Identity Regulation, including a national Digital Identity Wallet, and the Reform Agenda ties a payment to an updated law (MKD-EU-2024-001-0030; MKD-EU-2024-007-0009). Parliament adopted amendments transposing eIDAS 2.0 on 27 June 2025, and the Commission considered the Reform Agenda step achieved while finding the law not fully aligned (MKD-EU-2025-011-0027; MKD-EU-2025-011-0031; MKD-EU-2025-011-0033). The amendments are validated as adopted; a machine-translated law text says they apply only from 1 June 2027 (MKD-GOV-2025-009-0041). The 14 by-laws and the wallet remain proposed, with some by-laws tied to December 2027 (MKD-EU-2025-011-0029). The 2019 framework is validated as in_practice, with narrow use. No outcome is recorded. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is High and tractability is High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording has moved from basic alignment to the revised regulation. In 2022 it said North Macedonia had yet to align with the eIDAS Regulation, and in 2023 that alignment was further delayed (MKD-EU-2022-001-0103; MKD-EU-2023-001-0138). In 2024 it treated the law as harmonised with the 2014 regulation and asked for alignment with the revised 2024 regulation, including a Digital Identity Wallet, which the Reform Agenda made a payment condition (MKD-EU-2024-001-0029; MKD-EU-2024-001-0030; MKD-EU-2024-007-0009). In 2025 it recorded some progress through the eIDAS 2.0 amendments but said further efforts are required for full alignment (MKD-EU-2025-001-0001; MKD-EU-2025-001-0148).

## Open questions

- Sources conflict on whether the law was already aligned with the 2014 regulation before 2024, with the screening report and a government strategy against the Commission's 2022 and 2023 reports (MKD-EU-2023-003-0041; MKD-GOV-2023-002-0016; MKD-EU-2022-001-0103; MKD-EU-2023-001-0138); a Commission statement on the earlier alignment status would settle it.
- No source up to the freeze date reports any of the 14 by-laws as adopted (MKD-EU-2025-011-0029), so official gazette records or a later Commission assessment are needed.
- The 1 June 2027 application date rests on a machine-translated law text capped at Medium confidence (MKD-GOV-2025-009-0041), so the official text or an English Commission source is needed to confirm it.
- No outcome evidence shows use of electronic identification or trust services by citizens, so usage data are needed to judge any result.
