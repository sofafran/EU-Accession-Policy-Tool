## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to prepare several sectoral systems to connect with EU information systems, notably the civil protection system CECIS, the Business Registers Interconnection System (BRIS), EU taxation IT systems and the own resources reporting system (MKD-EU-2025-005-0019; MKD-EU-2025-003-0150; MKD-EU-2025-001-0026; MKD-EU-2025-001-0190). The Commission reported steps towards the CECIS connection from 2020 to 2022 but no progress in 2023, and says in 2025 that the link is still not made, while the country plans it by 2027 (MKD-EU-2020-001-0157; MKD-EU-2022-001-0127; MKD-EU-2023-001-0181; MKD-EU-2025-001-0177; MKD-EU-2025-005-0018). Some areas show more: the Commission states that alignment with the EU Digital COVID Certificate was completed in 2021 and that the country has achieved high-level integration of EU food safety IT tools (MKD-EU-2021-002-0002; MKD-EU-2025-006-0003). Most other responses are proposed, and BRIS and the Internal Market Information System also depend on EU decisions or accession (MKD-EU-2025-003-0190; MKD-EU-2025-005-0003). The validated assessment is open, with priority Medium, direction of travel persistent and tractability Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The requirement has grown by sector rather than changed in wording. The CECIS link has been raised since 2020, and the Commission moved from reporting steps towards it to no progress in 2023, an issue for immediate attention in 2024 and still needing to be done in 2025 (MKD-EU-2020-001-0157; MKD-EU-2023-001-0181; MKD-EU-2024-001-0110; MKD-EU-2025-005-0019). Business register readiness for BRIS appears from 2023 in the same words, and the 2025 screening report adds that alignment on structured, machine-readable data is needed (MKD-EU-2023-001-0109; MKD-EU-2024-001-0075; MKD-EU-2025-003-0150). Preparing for interoperability with EU taxation IT systems, and the own resources IT system and reporting access, entered in 2024 and are repeated in 2025, when the own resources items are described as still pending (MKD-EU-2024-001-0017; MKD-EU-2024-001-0111; MKD-EU-2025-001-0026; MKD-EU-2025-001-0190).

## Open questions

- North Macedonia states it is technically ready to interconnect with BRIS, while the Commission says alignment on machine-readable data and with EU company law is still needed, and a Commission assessment of the register against those provisions would settle readiness (MKD-EU-2025-003-0138; MKD-EU-2025-003-0150; MKD-EU-2025-003-0145).
- The country's use of SDMX for transmitting data to Eurostat and the synchronisation of its phytosanitary information system rest on its own presentation, and independent confirmation from the Commission would be needed to show use (MKD-EU-2023-003-0124; MKD-EU-2025-006-0007).
- The priority and tractability ratings are averages over a residual group of sectors that differ, so sector-by-sector evidence, for example on the CECIS connection planned by 2027, would be needed to judge each link separately (MKD-EU-2025-005-0018; MKD-EU-2025-006-0004).
- No item shows how the EU Digital COVID Certificate alignment was used after August 2021, so later evidence of use would be needed to say more than that alignment was completed (MKD-EU-2021-002-0002).
