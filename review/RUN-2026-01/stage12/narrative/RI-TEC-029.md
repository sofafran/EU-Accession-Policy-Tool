## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to keep consolidating and upgrading its customs IT systems in line with the Union Customs Code work programme and the electronic customs strategic plan, to upgrade its transit system to Phase 6 and to recruit IT staff, and states that alignment on accession is critical to EU financial interests and security (MKD-EU-2025-001-0033; MKD-EU-2025-001-0034; MKD-EU-2025-001-0167; MKD-EU-2025-002-0010). After delays reported in 2023 and 2024, the Commission states that the New Computerised Transit System Phase 5 was completed and launched in January 2025, and notes good progress (MKD-EU-2024-001-0108; MKD-EU-2025-001-0031; MKD-EU-2025-001-0191). Phase 6, the export system and the import control system remain Reform Agenda steps due between 2026 and 2027 (MKD-GOV-2024-001-0117; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0123; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0125). The screening report rates alignment between low and partial, with remaining systems costed at about EUR 34.5 million (MKD-EU-2025-004-0155; MKD-EU-2025-004-0161). No usage data exist for Phase 5. The validated judgements are priority High, direction of travel improving, unresolved gap partly_addressed and tractability High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The core recommendation has barely changed: from 2021 the Commission asks the country to consolidate and apply its customs IT systems in line with the strategic plan modules and ensure their upgrade, and from 2024 it adds the Union Customs Code work programme and continued legal alignment (MKD-EU-2021-001-0116; MKD-EU-2022-001-0124; MKD-EU-2023-001-0173; MKD-EU-2024-001-0019; MKD-EU-2025-001-0033). The specific ask moved with events: implement Phase 5 in 2023 and 2024, then upgrade to Phase 6 in November 2025 (MKD-EU-2023-001-0172; MKD-EU-2024-001-0018; MKD-EU-2025-001-0034). In 2024 the Reform Agenda set payment conditions for Phase 6, the export system and the import control system (MKD-EU-2024-007-0019; MKD-EU-2024-007-0020; MKD-EU-2024-007-0021), and staff recruitment became a recurring ask (MKD-EU-2024-001-0109; MKD-EU-2025-001-0167).

## Open questions

- No source gives usage or performance data for Phase 5 since its launch, so transit declaration and processing statistics would be needed to show it working in practice (MKD-EU-2025-001-0031).
- The Reform Agenda's own text gives a 2 to 4 per cent time reduction for Phase 6 while the payment condition gives 2 to 5 per cent, and the signed Reform Agenda annex would settle which applies (MKD-GOV-2024-001-0117; MKD-EU-2024-007-0019; MKD-GOV-2024-002-0023).
- The plan to hire 15 more IT experts and the 2024 upgrade of the economic operators module are not shown as done, and customs staffing figures or a later Commission report would settle this (MKD-EU-2025-004-0162; MKD-EU-2025-004-0151).
- The draft Law on Single Window for Trade, needed for the new national single window, is not reported as adopted, and its publication would settle whether that system can proceed (MKD-IO-2024-001-0108).
