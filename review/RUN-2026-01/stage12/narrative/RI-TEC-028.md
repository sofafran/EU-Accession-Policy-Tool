## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to fully implement the law on general administrative procedures by completing data exchange, and a Reform Agenda payment condition asks for more data exchange between institutions and documents obtained ex officio (MKD-EU-2025-001-0004; MKD-EU-2024-007-0016; MKD-GOV-2024-002-0018). The interoperability platform is in practice, and the Commission reports that customs exchanges data regularly through it (MKD-EU-2025-001-0166). Government figures, machine translated, show connections rising from 39 institutions in 2021 to 58 or 63 in 2025 and transactions reaching 8 million in 2024, with the Commission noting more connections and transactions (MKD-GOV-2022-004-0030; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0064; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0184; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0185; MKD-EU-2024-001-0028). The Commission still finds only a few institutions using it, and the government's 2021 conclusions on connecting about 1,300 institutions are assessed as failed, with Low attribution, after the audit found only six new requests (MKD-GOV-2022-004-0049; MKD-GOV-2022-004-0051). The planned upgrade remains proposed (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0183). The gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, the priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The diagnosis has hardly changed, while the requirement has become firmer. In 2020 the Commission found that many institutions did not use the system despite having the equipment, and asked for political will and funding (MKD-EU-2020-001-0121; MKD-EU-2020-001-0020). In 2021 and 2022 it asked for administrative data sharing to be reinforced and simplified, citing missing communications software and lack of will (MKD-EU-2021-001-0007; MKD-EU-2022-001-0008; MKD-EU-2022-001-0110). From 2023 a key recommendation asks the country to complete data exchange under the law on general administrative procedures, repeated in 2024 and 2025, and in 2024 the Reform Agenda tied ex officio data exchange to a payment condition (MKD-EU-2023-001-0002; MKD-EU-2024-001-0002; MKD-EU-2025-001-0004; MKD-EU-2024-007-0016). Specific demands also appeared for asset declaration checks and inspections (MKD-EU-2024-001-0037; MKD-EU-2025-003-0009).

## Open questions

- North Macedonia told the 2023 screening that the once-only principle is applied, while its 2024 Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio, a confirmed conflict that data on ex officio requests made through the platform would settle (MKD-EU-2023-003-0029; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0097).
- Connection and transaction figures come from machine-translated government documents capped at Medium confidence and differ by measure and period, including a fall from about 3.6 million transactions in 2021 to 1.5 million in 2022, so a consistent independent series would settle the trend (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0060; MKD-GOV-2022-004-0083; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0185).
- The State Audit Office evidence behind the failed outcome is filed under the government family and machine translated, so the reviewers' confirmation of its independence and an English or verified text would firm up that judgement (MKD-GOV-2022-004-0049).
- ITU describes a ministers' council while the audit found no body coordinating systems used by several institutions, a noted difference over different bodies that leaves open how far coordination worked in practice, which evidence of that council's decisions on the platform would settle (MKD-IO-2022-002-0066; MKD-GOV-2022-004-0017; MKD-GOV-2022-004-0065).
