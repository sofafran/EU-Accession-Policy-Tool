## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align fully with the General Data Protection Regulation, to adopt a law transposing the Law Enforcement Directive, to ensure safeguards for international transfers and to harmonise sector laws (MKD-EU-2023-001-0027; MKD-EU-2023-001-0030; MKD-EU-2023-001-0031; MKD-EU-2025-001-0066). The 2020 Law on Personal Data Protection has applied in full since August 2021, and the Agency reports supervisions and new rulebooks (MKD-EU-2022-001-0021; MKD-GOV-2025-010-0085). A draft law transposing the Law Enforcement Directive was withdrawn in September 2022, and the Agency reports that neither that transposition nor its own draft amendments had started moving in 2025, in machine-translated reporting (MKD-GOV-2026-002-0035; MKD-GOV-2026-002-0037). In 2025 Parliament amended the law to allow transfers to NATO countries without prior consultation of the Agency, and the Commission found no progress and an insufficient framework (MKD-EU-2025-001-0065; MKD-EU-2025-001-0063; MKD-EU-2025-001-0062). The validated assessment is that the gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, priority is High (an analyst override) and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

In 2020 the Commission's wording centres on the Law Enforcement Directive, recording no concrete progress on alignment (MKD-EU-2020-001-0036). From 2023 the requirement broadens: the screening report calls the framework partially aligned and asks for the recast of the law to be completed and a new law on the Law Enforcement Directive, and the 2023 report adds transfers, the Agency's independence and the harmonisation of sector laws (MKD-EU-2023-003-0073; MKD-EU-2023-003-0074; MKD-EU-2023-003-0075; MKD-EU-2023-001-0028; MKD-EU-2023-001-0031). In 2024 and 2025 the Commission records no progress and states that full alignment remains pending, and it adds ePrivacy alignment (MKD-EU-2024-001-0039; MKD-EU-2024-001-0102; MKD-EU-2025-001-0064; MKD-EU-2025-001-0148). In 2025 it also calls the framework insufficient and notes the absence of safeguards for transferred data (MKD-EU-2025-001-0062; MKD-EU-2025-001-0066).

## Open questions

- Sources conflict on alignment: the State Audit Office and the Agency's strategy describe the legislation as aligned with EU law, while the Commission calls the framework insufficient and full alignment pending, and only a Commission assessment of a new or amended law would settle this (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0154; MKD-GOV-2024-006-0005; MKD-EU-2025-001-0064; MKD-EU-2025-001-0062).
- North Macedonia states that its electronic communications law is fully aligned with the ePrivacy Directive, while the Commission seeks further efforts and could not yet fully evaluate it, so a completed Commission evaluation would settle this conflict (MKD-EU-2025-004-0023; MKD-EU-2024-001-0102; MKD-EU-2025-004-0071).
- Article 122 of the unofficial translation of the 2020 law reads as suspending core chapters until accession, which contradicts the other evidence of application and may be a translation fault, so a check against the Macedonian text would settle it (MKD-GOV-2020-001-0043).
- Much of the evidence on what the Agency has done comes from its own machine-translated reports, capped at Medium confidence or lower, and the effect of the Law on financial companies is rated insufficient_evidence for success, so independent evidence on supervision results and complaint trends would strengthen the record (MKD-GOV-2026-002-0037; MKD-EU-2023-001-0206).
