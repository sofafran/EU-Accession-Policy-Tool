## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to strengthen the Employment Service Agency through continued training and to align legislation and the exchange of good practice with EURES and European Labour Authority rules (MKD-EU-2023-001-0089; MKD-EU-2024-001-0007; MKD-EU-2025-001-0012). The Commission reports that capacity building for civil servants on EURES and the European Labour Authority took place in 2023 (MKD-EU-2023-001-0087; MKD-EU-2023-001-0096), and the 2025 screening report states that all main IT elements for EURES are in place and that administrative capacity is satisfactory (MKD-EU-2025-003-0063; MKD-EU-2025-003-0064). The capacity building and the Agency's web portal are assessed as implemented, but no source shows how the training or the portal is used, so no result is established (MKD-EU-2025-003-0053). What remains is updated EURES training after staff turnover, connection of private employment agencies and, later, ESCO mapping (MKD-EU-2025-003-0065; MKD-EU-2025-003-0070; MKD-EU-2025-003-0055). The validated assessment is partly_addressed, with priority Medium, direction of travel persistent and tractability High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The key recommendation is nearly unchanged from 2023 to 2025: in each report the Commission asks the country to strengthen the capacity of the Employment Service Agency by continuing training, and to align legislation and the exchange of good practices with EURES and ELA regulations (MKD-EU-2023-001-0089; MKD-EU-2024-001-0007; MKD-EU-2025-001-0012). The 2025 wording differs only in spelling out the two acronyms, and the 2025 report states that the previous recommendations were not implemented (MKD-EU-2025-001-0011). The February 2025 screening report adds more specific points: it finds the IT elements in place and capacity satisfactory, but asks for the 2014 to 2015 twinning training to be updated, for private employment agencies to be given access and adequately connected, and for ESCO mapping at a later stage (MKD-EU-2025-003-0063; MKD-EU-2025-003-0064; MKD-EU-2025-003-0065; MKD-EU-2025-003-0067; MKD-EU-2025-003-0070; MKD-EU-2025-003-0055).

## Open questions

- No source shows the EURES training or the Agency's web portal being used, so independently checked data on vacancy exchange or online service use would be needed to judge results (MKD-EU-2023-001-0096; MKD-EU-2025-003-0053).
- The planned upgrade that would let the Agency take data from private employment agencies and act as the single point for EURES exchange is not shown as delivered, and an Agency or Commission report on that specific function would settle it (MKD-EU-2025-003-0061).
- Whether private employment agencies have been connected to the system is not reported, and a later Commission assessment of EURES readiness would show it (MKD-EU-2025-003-0067; MKD-EU-2025-003-0070).
- The planned strengthening of the Agency's capacities and the EU-funded analysis of harmonisation needs are only proposed, and evidence that they were carried out would be needed to update the gap (MKD-EU-2025-003-0060).
