## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align its law on access to the labour market with the EU acquis, in particular on non-discrimination on grounds of nationality against EU workers in all sectors, so that by accession EU citizens can work there without restrictions such as work permits (MKD-EU-2025-001-0013; MKD-EU-2025-003-0025; MKD-EU-2025-003-0033). The Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners is in practice, and it was amended in 2021 and in April 2025, but the 2025 amendment only renames ministries and bodies, and further amendments on the employment of foreigners are listed only as proposed in machine-translated government reports (MKD-GOV-2021-001-0001; MKD-GOV-2025-001-0001; MKD-GOV-2025-003-0011; MKD-GOV-2026-001-0007). The Commission states that the law does not distinguish EU citizens from other foreigners and that some private sector posts are reserved for nationals (MKD-EU-2025-001-0097; MKD-EU-2025-001-0101). North Macedonia points to the ban on nationality discrimination in the Law on Labour Relations, which is adopted but not shown in application (MKD-EU-2025-003-0018; MKD-EU-2025-003-0019). No outcome has been assessed. The gap is open, the direction of travel is persistent, the priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The core recommendation has barely changed. From 2020 to 2022 the Commission asked the country to continue adapting the legal framework on access to the labour market, in particular on non-discrimination on grounds of nationality against EU workers (MKD-EU-2020-001-0076; MKD-EU-2021-001-0060; MKD-EU-2022-001-0063). From 2023 it added the words "in all sectors", and in 2025 "adapt" became "align" (MKD-EU-2023-001-0090; MKD-EU-2024-001-0008; MKD-EU-2025-001-0013). Each report from 2020 to 2024 found no progress, and the 2025 report found no legislative developments (MKD-EU-2020-001-0079; MKD-EU-2024-001-0057; MKD-EU-2025-001-0096). The February 2025 screening report made the requirement more concrete: partial alignment, an end-point at accession with no work permit or residence permit for EU workers, and exclusion of EU citizens from the annual foreign worker quotas (MKD-EU-2025-003-0031; MKD-EU-2025-003-0025; MKD-EU-2025-003-0028).

## Open questions

- The Commission states that the foreigners law makes no distinction for EU citizens, while North Macedonia states that the quotas do not apply to EU citizens under a reciprocity agreement, and this confirmed conflict could be settled by the text of the law or the agreement and data on how permits are issued to EU citizens (MKD-EU-2024-001-0058; MKD-EU-2025-003-0010).
- The content of the proposed amendments on the employment of foreigners, and whether they are the law adopted in April 2025, is not known from the machine-translated Employment Agency reports, so the draft or adopted text would settle it (MKD-GOV-2025-003-0011; MKD-GOV-2026-001-0007).
- No source shows how the non-discrimination rule in the Law on Labour Relations is applied to foreign or EU workers, which inspection, court or complaints data could show (MKD-EU-2025-003-0018).
