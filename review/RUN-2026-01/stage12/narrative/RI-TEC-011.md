## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align its electronic communications law with the European Electronic Communications Code and the Gigabit Infrastructure Act, and ties adoption of an aligned law to a Reform Agenda payment (MKD-EU-2024-007-0006; MKD-EU-2025-001-0022). A new Law on Electronic Communications was adopted in June 2025 and amended in December 2025, with application from 1 June 2026 and by-laws due within 15 months (MKD-EU-2025-011-0015; MKD-EU-2026-002-0002; MKD-GOV-2025-008-0032). Both measures are validated as adopted, not as applied. The Commission found the Reform Agenda step not achieved in 2025 and 2026 while the country considers it achieved, a confirmed conflict (MKD-EU-2025-011-0021; MKD-EU-2026-002-0001; MKD-EU-2026-002-0014). Separately, the Regional Roaming Agreement is judged to have worked, on Commission statements without price data (MKD-EU-2022-001-0134). What remains is closing the gaps with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act in two laws and adopting the by-laws. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is improving. Priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

From 2020 to 2022 the Commission repeated that the Law on Electronic Communications needed further alignment with the EU acquis (MKD-EU-2020-001-0115; MKD-EU-2021-001-0097; MKD-EU-2022-001-0101). From 2022 its recommendations named the Electronic Communications Code and the Broadband Cost Reduction Directive, and in 2023 it found no progress (MKD-EU-2022-001-0087; MKD-EU-2023-001-0136). In 2024 the Reform Agenda made adoption of a new law aligned with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act a payment condition (MKD-EU-2024-007-0006). After the 2025 law, the Commission found significant harmonisation gaps (MKD-EU-2025-011-0018), and in May 2026, after the December amendments, it found alignment only partially achieved and asked for amendments to both the electronic communications and construction laws (MKD-EU-2026-002-0006; MKD-EU-2026-002-0015).

## Open questions

- The outcome of the Reform Agenda grace period ending 30 June 2026 is not in the evidence (MKD-EU-2026-002-0017), so a later Commission assessment of the step is needed to settle whether alignment was reached.
- Whether the 2025 law actually began to apply on 1 June 2026 and whether its by-laws were adopted is not shown, since the dates rest on the legal text in machine translation capped at Medium confidence (MKD-GOV-2025-008-0029; MKD-GOV-2025-008-0032); regulator or Commission reports after June 2026 would settle it.
- Two confirmed conflicts remain unresolved: on the Broadband Cost Reduction Directive between the Commission and the country's screening view (MKD-EU-2024-001-0082; MKD-EU-2025-004-0015), and on the start date of roam like at home (MKD-GOV-2026-003-0033; MKD-EU-2021-001-0086); a Commission statement on the current alignment and an authoritative date source would settle them.
- The roaming results rest on Commission statements, and later reductions towards 2028 are judged too_early (MKD-EU-2023-002-0026), so actual roaming price data for North Macedonia are needed.
