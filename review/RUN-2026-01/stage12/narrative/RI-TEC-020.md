## In brief

The Commission finds that, in general, data controllers do not implement the Law on Personal Data Protection, calls for awareness raising in all sectors, and reports in 2025 that breaches are becoming more frequent, some go unreported and state bodies show low awareness (MKD-EU-2023-001-0033; MKD-EU-2023-001-0044; MKD-EU-2025-001-0071; MKD-EU-2025-001-0072). The law is assessed as in_practice, and the Agency reports 196 supervisions in 2025, with 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed (MKD-GOV-2026-002-0040; MKD-GOV-2026-002-0053). The Agency adopted a new rulebook on security of processing and took over an e-classroom platform, but the data protection officer network and the sanction amendments remain proposed (MKD-GOV-2025-010-0085; MKD-GOV-2025-010-0077; MKD-GOV-2024-006-0056; MKD-GOV-2024-006-0024). Success on awareness and on the e-classroom is insufficient_evidence, and no independent evidence shows changed behaviour. The Agency reports its Misdemeanour Commission disrupted by staff departures (MKD-GOV-2026-002-0060). Much Agency evidence is machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. The gap is assessed as open. Priority is Medium, direction of travel is persistent and tractability is Low.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's concern has stayed the same while its findings have become broader. In 2020 it notes that misdemeanour fines for data protection breaches remain low (MKD-EU-2020-001-0173; MKD-EU-2020-001-0047). In 2023 it states more generally that controllers do not implement the law and that there is a strong need for awareness raising and a culture of data protection in all sectors (MKD-EU-2023-001-0033; MKD-EU-2023-001-0044). By November 2025 it adds that breaches are increasingly frequent, some are not reported as the law requires, and state administrative bodies still show low awareness and insufficient IT protection of personal data (MKD-EU-2025-001-0071; MKD-EU-2025-001-0072). Compliance has not been a named key recommendation in these reports.

## Open questions

- For 2021 and 2022 the Commission's report gives 17 and 10 reprimands while the country presentation gives 4 for each year, and this confirmed conflict needs the Agency's annual reports for those years to settle it (MKD-EU-2023-001-0038; MKD-EU-2023-003-0068; MKD-EU-2023-003-0072).
- Success of the law on awareness and of the e-classroom is insufficient_evidence because the only evidence is the Agency's own reporting, so independent awareness surveys or user figures would be needed (MKD-GOV-2024-006-0003; MKD-GOV-2026-002-0104).
- No evidence measures compliance among controllers over time, and repeated supervision findings on comparable controllers, or independent audits, would show whether behaviour is changing (MKD-GOV-2026-002-0045; MKD-GOV-2026-002-0043).
- Whether the sectoral amendments on misdemeanour sanctions and the data protection officer network have been delivered is not shown, and later Agency or Commission reports would settle it (MKD-GOV-2024-006-0024; MKD-GOV-2024-006-0056).
