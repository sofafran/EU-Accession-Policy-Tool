## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to implement the EU 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox, including supplier risk assessment and restrictions on high-risk suppliers, and the Reform Agenda requires a fully implemented 5G security by-law by December 2027 (MKD-EU-2025-001-0145; MKD-EU-2024-007-0008; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0065). April 2021 amendments gave the regulator prior security approval of 5G equipment, and the Commission reported the regulator adopted by-laws in 2022 (MKD-EU-2021-001-0093; MKD-EU-2022-001-0097), but the screening report says not all the amendments were fully implemented (MKD-EU-2025-004-0047). The 2025 Law on Electronic Communications, validated as adopted, empowers the regulator to assess suppliers and restrict high-risk ones, according to a machine-translated text (MKD-GOV-2025-008-0022). The 5G security by-law and the supplier risk assessment remain proposed, and no item shows an assessment carried out. No outcome is recorded. The unresolved gap is partly_addressed and the direction of travel is persistent. Priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission has named the 5G toolbox in successive recommendations from 2022 to 2025, and the core requirement has not changed (MKD-EU-2022-001-0087; MKD-EU-2023-001-0121; MKD-EU-2024-001-0015; MKD-EU-2025-001-0022). In 2024 the Reform Agenda turned it into a payment condition linked to a 5G security by-law and 5G roll-out (MKD-EU-2024-007-0008). In 2025 the wording became more specific, asking the country to continue implementing the toolbox, including assessing suppliers' risk profiles and restricting or excluding high-risk suppliers (MKD-EU-2025-004-0073; MKD-EU-2025-001-0145). The Commission's 2025 enlargement communication also presents secure 5G infrastructure under the toolbox as a regional priority (MKD-EU-2025-002-0011).

## Open questions

- Whether the regulator has carried out any supplier risk assessment or restriction is not shown, although one was planned from 2023 (MKD-EU-2025-004-0048); regulator supervision reports or a Commission assessment would settle it.
- The supplier assessment power rests on a machine-translated legal text capped at Medium confidence (MKD-GOV-2025-008-0022), so the official text or an English Commission summary is needed.
- The Reform Agenda says the step is largely achieved while its own baseline records partial compliance (MKD-GOV-2024-001-0067; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0066); a Commission assessment of the 5G toolbox step itself would settle how far it has been met.
- No EU item assesses the 2025 law's application since June 2026, so later Commission reporting is needed to judge whether the direction of travel is changing.
