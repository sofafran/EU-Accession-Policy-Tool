## In brief

The Commission asks that all institutions regularly provide and update data, that proactive disclosure of datasets be encouraged, and that North Macedonia step up alignment with the Open Data Directive (MKD-EU-2020-001-0013; MKD-EU-2022-001-0011; MKD-EU-2025-004-0109). The Law on the Use of Public Sector Data is adopted, and the government reports 580 datasets from 74 institutions on the open data portal by December 2022 (MKD-EU-2025-004-0086; MKD-GOV-2023-002-0011). Amendments to align the law with the Directive have been with a working group since 2023 and are assessed as proposed (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0012; MKD-EU-2025-004-0084; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0198). The Commission stated in 2020 that access to public information improved thanks to the transparency strategy that set up the portal, assessed as a mixed success with Medium attribution, because the State Audit Office reports that the portal has not functioned since early 2025 owing to procurement problems, in machine-translated text (MKD-EU-2020-001-0012; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0030). The portal's status is therefore unclear. The gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, the priority is Medium and tractability is High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The requirement has moved from practice to legal alignment. In 2020 the Commission recorded some progress on transparency, including an operational open government data portal, and asked all institutions to provide and update data regularly so the system works smoothly (MKD-EU-2020-001-0001; MKD-EU-2020-001-0013). In 2022 it asked for proactive disclosure of information and datasets to be further encouraged (MKD-EU-2022-001-0011). In 2025 the screening reports state that the Law on the Use of Public Sector Data is partially aligned with the Open Data Directive, ask the country to invest further efforts in aligning with it, and ask it to monitor other EU data legislation (MKD-EU-2025-004-0082; MKD-EU-2025-004-0109; MKD-EU-2025-005-0013). The matter does not appear among the Commission's main findings.

## Open questions

- The country's February 2025 presentation describes the portal as in line with best practice while the State Audit Office says it has not functioned since early 2025, in machine-translated text capped at Medium confidence, and a later source on whether the portal was restored would settle both its status and whether the 2020 result has lasted (MKD-EU-2025-004-0083; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0030).
- The October 2025 strategy states that the country is not aligned with the Open Data Directive while the February 2025 screening report states that the law is partially aligned, a confirmed conflict that a Commission table of concordance or alignment assessment would settle (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0087; MKD-EU-2025-004-0086).
- The ITU profile of 2022 lists the network and information security law as an existing enabler while the Commission's 2022 report says the draft had not been adopted, a confirmed conflict that dated legislative records would settle (MKD-IO-2022-002-0058; MKD-EU-2022-001-0098).
- There are no data on use of the portal or on how regularly institutions update their datasets after 2022, which portal statistics or an Open Data Maturity assessment would provide (MKD-GOV-2023-002-0011; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0040).
