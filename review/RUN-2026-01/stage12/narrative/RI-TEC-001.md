## In brief

The Commission invites North Macedonia to align fully with the EU Artificial Intelligence Act and to put in place the necessary enforcement structures (MKD-EU-2025-001-0149). The State Audit Office reports that a working group formed in 2021 met only once and that no national AI strategy or legislation for public sector AI projects has been adopted (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0008; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0009; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0089). The 2025 digital strategy lists drafting an AI strategy and legislation aligned with the AI Act as planned actions, assessed as proposed (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0206; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0207). The audit reports that the ADA digital assistant is not functioning after about EUR 150 thousand was spent, and the strategy route and ADA are each assessed as failed (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0049). The Commission welcomed the signed AI Factory Antenna grant, but no activity under it is reported (MKD-EU-2026-005-0001). Much of the national evidence is machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. The gap is assessed as open. Priority is Medium, direction of travel is new (as an EU requirement) and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The requirement is recent, so there is no repeated Commission wording to compare. The first Commission item appears in the November 2025 report, which invites North Macedonia to "fully align" with the AI Act and to ensure the necessary enforcement structures (MKD-EU-2025-001-0149). The only later Commission item, from April 2026, does not mention the AI Act: it welcomes the AI Factory Antenna grant and encourages outreach to neighbouring countries, a regional ecosystem of experts and users, and use of the European Digital Innovation Hubs (MKD-EU-2026-005-0002). The requirement is therefore new as an EU ask, although national sources show that the gap itself is older (MKD-GOV-2024-001-0024; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0009).

## Open questions

- The State Audit Office says data protection in AI projects is governed by law aligned with the GDPR, while the Commission says full GDPR alignment is still pending, and this confirmed conflict is left unresolved pending a comparison of the law against the GDPR (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0156; MKD-EU-2025-001-0064).
- The success of the AI pilot in the 112 emergency service is insufficient_evidence, and measured data on response times or call handling would be needed to judge it (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0165; MKD-EU-2024-001-0093).
- Key evidence comes from the machine-translated State Audit Office report and digital strategy, capped at Medium confidence, so checking the Macedonian originals or finding independent English sources would strengthen the assessment (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0089; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0207).
- The audit finds that no public sector AI project has been implemented while also reporting the Buki platform at a state university, and a clarification of scope from the audit office would settle whether these differ only in scope (MKD-GOV-2025-011-0093; MKD-GOV-2025-011-0067).
