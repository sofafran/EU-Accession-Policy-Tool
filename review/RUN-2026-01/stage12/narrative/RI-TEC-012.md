## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to strengthen the independence and capacity of the electronic communications regulator and to improve competition in the telecom sector (MKD-EU-2025-001-0023; MKD-EU-2025-001-0133; MKD-EU-2020-001-0117). Parliament adopted a new Law on Electronic Communications in June 2025, which states that the Agency is independent, but that is a legal provision and no item confirms its application (MKD-GOV-2025-008-0005; MKD-EU-2025-014-0006). The Commission reports that parliament dismissed all five members of the regulator's commission in September 2024 (MKD-EU-2024-001-0087). On competition, machine-translated regulator reporting states that a spectrum tender received a single compliant bid, with the award decision postponed to 12 January 2026, and the Commission says a new operator took part in a tender (MKD-GOV-2026-003-0012; MKD-GOV-2026-003-0014; MKD-EU-2025-001-0144). Success and attribution for that tender are insufficient_evidence, and the mobile market remains concentrated (MKD-GOV-2026-003-0006). The validated assessment is that the gap is open, the direction of travel is persistent, priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's concern about the regulator's independence recurs in nearly unchanged terms from 2020 to 2025: concerns over appointments in 2020 and 2021, concerns over independence in 2022, calls to ensure full independence in 2023 and 2024, and a call to bolster independence in 2025 (MKD-EU-2020-001-0114; MKD-EU-2021-001-0096; MKD-EU-2022-001-0100; MKD-EU-2023-001-0150; MKD-EU-2024-001-0086; MKD-EU-2025-001-0133). The key recommendation on the regulator's independence and capacity appears in every report from 2021 to 2025 (MKD-EU-2021-001-0084; MKD-EU-2025-001-0023). On competition, the wording moves from no relevant developments in 2020 and 2021, to a market mainly shared by two operators in 2023, to a new operator taking part in a tender in 2025 (MKD-EU-2020-001-0116; MKD-EU-2023-001-0140; MKD-EU-2025-001-0144). In 2025 and 2026 the Commission adds points on the new law, including concerns about its provisions on financing the Operational and Technical Agency (MKD-EU-2025-014-0006; MKD-EU-2026-002-0009; MKD-EU-2026-002-0012).

## Open questions

- No item shows whether the regulator's independence has improved in appointments or in practice, so evidence on appointments to the new commission and on how the 2025 law is applied would settle this (MKD-EU-2024-001-0087; MKD-GOV-2025-008-0005).
- The outcome of the spectrum tender is unknown, with success and attribution rated insufficient_evidence on machine-translated regulator reporting capped at Medium confidence, so an award decision and later market share data would settle whether competition improved (MKD-GOV-2026-003-0012; MKD-GOV-2026-003-0014).
- The Commission and North Macedonia disagree on whether the law's provisions on financing the Operational and Technical Agency are consistent with the EU telecoms code, and the evidence does not resolve this; a later Commission assessment or an amendment to the law would settle it (MKD-EU-2026-002-0009; MKD-EU-2026-002-0010).
- The planned third mobile network operator rests on a machine-translated strategy and no item shows it introduced, so a regulatory decision or licence would settle its status (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0113).
