## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to put in place administrative measures to prepare for accession and for electronic exchange of social security data, and to align its pension and disability and health insurance laws with the coordination regulations by accession, including on nationality conditions (MKD-EU-2025-001-0014; MKD-EU-2025-003-0107). The Commission finds the national framework partly aligned and notes that both laws in some cases require citizenship for access to insurance (MKD-EU-2025-003-0093; MKD-EU-2025-003-0094; MKD-EU-2025-003-0104). The pension, health insurance and unemployment insurance laws and the 2023 law on the register of mandatory social insurance are assessed as adopted, with no evidence on how they are applied or that the register exists (MKD-EU-2024-001-0067; MKD-EU-2025-003-0083; MKD-EU-2025-003-0084). The country plans within five years to analyse its law, capacity and IT infrastructure, amend the law and define EESSI access points, assessed as proposed (MKD-EU-2025-003-0097). The Commission rates preparation on coordination as good because of experience with bilateral agreements, assessed as worked with Medium attribution but narrow in scope (MKD-EU-2025-003-0090; MKD-EU-2025-003-0105). The gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, the priority is High and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The key recommendation on administrative measures has run through every report since 2020. In 2020 to 2022 the Commission asked the country to identify which administrative measures would be needed before accession, with 2021 and 2022 adding further bilateral agreements on coordination and health insurance (MKD-EU-2020-001-0077; MKD-EU-2021-001-0061; MKD-EU-2022-001-0064). From 2023 the wording shifts from identifying measures to putting them in place, which was assessed as a change of wording rather than a reported step (MKD-EU-2023-001-0091; MKD-EU-2024-001-0009). The 2025 report adds electronic exchange of social security data (MKD-EU-2025-001-0014). The February 2025 screening report adds a specific legal requirement: removing citizenship conditions from the pension and health insurance laws by accession (MKD-EU-2025-003-0095; MKD-EU-2025-003-0107).

## Open questions

- No item shows the register of mandatory social insurance established after the 2023 law, so evidence of the register in operation would settle whether the law has gone beyond adoption (MKD-EU-2024-001-0067).
- The description of how the health insurance and unemployment insurance laws work comes only from the country's own presentation at screening, so independent evidence on their application, for example to EU or other foreign workers, is missing (MKD-EU-2025-003-0083; MKD-EU-2025-003-0084).
- The five-year plan to amend the legislation and define EESSI access points has no reported steps, and a draft amendment, the planned analysis or an EESSI readiness report would show whether it is moving (MKD-EU-2025-003-0097).
