## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to align its network and information security law with the NIS2 Directive and the Cybersecurity Act, to adopt and implement a cybersecurity strategy, and to improve coordination, and the Reform Agenda ties payments to the law and strategy, an operational competent authority and the NIS2 disclosure and crisis frameworks (MKD-EU-2024-001-0095; MKD-EU-2025-004-0106; MKD-EU-2023-001-0146; MKD-EU-2024-007-0002; MKD-EU-2024-007-0004; MKD-EU-2024-007-0005). After a 2019 draft stalled, the Government adopted the Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028 in January 2025 and Parliament adopted the law in June 2025, both assessed as adopted (MKD-EU-2021-001-0095; MKD-EU-2025-011-0008; MKD-EU-2025-011-0002). The Commission found the law sufficient for the Reform Agenda step but not fully aligned with the acquis (MKD-EU-2025-011-0007; MKD-EU-2025-011-0012). The 15 by-laws, entity lists, disclosure and crisis frameworks and working coordination are not shown in place, and the State Audit Office found the National Cyber Security Council met only once (MKD-EU-2025-011-0004; MKD-GOV-2024-008-0038). No outcome has been assessed. The gap is assessed as partly_addressed. Priority is High, direction of travel is improving and tractability is Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The Commission's wording has moved from adoption to completeness of alignment. In 2021 it records no progress on the 2019 draft law and calls for more effort on a fragmented strategy (MKD-EU-2021-001-0095; MKD-EU-2021-001-0089). From 2022 to 2024 it repeatedly asks North Macedonia to adopt the law, aligned with NIS and then NIS2, and a new cybersecurity strategy, adding in 2023 a call for better coordination and a stronger National Cybersecurity Council (MKD-EU-2022-001-0087; MKD-EU-2023-001-0148; MKD-EU-2023-001-0128; MKD-EU-2023-001-0146; MKD-EU-2024-001-0095). The 2024 Reform Agenda turns these into payment conditions (MKD-EU-2024-007-0002). In 2025, after adoption, the Commission records some progress but asks the country to continue aligning with NIS2 and the Cybersecurity Act (MKD-EU-2025-001-0020; MKD-EU-2025-001-0022; MKD-EU-2025-004-0106).

## Open questions

- Whether the 15 by-laws, the entity lists and the response team and network required by the 2025 law were adopted or set up by their deadlines is not shown, and official gazette entries or ministry reports would settle it (MKD-GOV-2025-007-0054; MKD-GOV-2025-007-0055; MKD-GOV-2025-007-0058).
- The regulator states that the strategy is applied, but this is government reporting without specifics, and the half-yearly implementation reports the strategy promises would show what has been delivered (MKD-GOV-2026-003-0021; MKD-GOV-2025-005-0096).
- Confirmed conflicts remain on whether the law existed in February 2022, on whether the Council was formed in 2019 or July 2020, and on whether the action plan runs to 2027 or 2028, and the official gazette and Government decisions would settle them (MKD-IO-2022-002-0058; MKD-EU-2022-001-0098; MKD-EU-2021-001-0036; MKD-GOV-2024-008-0093; MKD-GOV-2025-006-0001; MKD-EU-2025-011-0010).
- The law's text and the audit findings are machine translated and capped at Medium confidence, and no evidence shows the competent authority staffed or performing supervisory checks, which later Commission Reform Agenda assessments would show (MKD-GOV-2025-007-0016; MKD-EU-2024-007-0004).
