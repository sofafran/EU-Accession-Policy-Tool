## In brief

The Commission has asked every year from 2020 to 2026 for the Public Revenue Office to extend or keep expanding e-services for taxpayers, alongside better business processes and compliance risk management (MKD-EU-2020-001-0140; MKD-EU-2025-001-0151; MKD-EU-2026-003-0012). The Commission reports that the Office issues pre-filled annual returns through its electronic personal tax platform and names the e-Tax system and the e-VAT application as working e-services; all three are assessed as in practice (MKD-EU-2020-001-0143; MKD-EU-2025-004-0122; MKD-EU-2022-006-0026). The Commission states that e-Tax simplified tax reporting, assessed as worked with Medium attribution though no measure is given; for e-VAT, success and attribution are insufficient_evidence (MKD-EU-2022-006-0026; MKD-EU-2023-006-0028). The electronic tax administration and e-invoicing remain at the planning and draft legislation stage, in machine-translated plan items capped at Medium confidence (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0201; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0237; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0202). The validated assessment is partly_addressed, with priority Medium, direction of travel persistent and tractability High.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The request has changed very little. From 2020 to 2022 the Commission asked the country to develop a new integrated IT tax model or system, improve risk management and further extend e-services for taxpayers (MKD-EU-2020-001-0140; MKD-EU-2021-001-0112; MKD-EU-2022-001-0115). In 2023 it said the Public Revenue Office still had to replace its fragmented IT system with an integrated one and continue to expand e-services (MKD-EU-2023-001-0163). From 2024 the annual reports speak of continuing the process of expansion of e-services and no longer name the integrated IT system in that sentence, although the February 2025 screening report still asks for it (MKD-EU-2024-001-0104; MKD-EU-2025-001-0151; MKD-EU-2026-003-0012; MKD-EU-2025-004-0121). The 2021 report recorded some progress through upgraded e-tax services (MKD-EU-2021-001-0110).

## Open questions

- No usage figures are given for the e-Tax system, the e-VAT application or pre-filled returns, so data on the number and share of filings made online would be needed to judge results beyond the Commission's statements (MKD-EU-2022-006-0026; MKD-EU-2025-004-0122).
- The Commission's statement that e-Tax simplified tax reporting gives no measure and the 2023 wording repeats 2022, so evidence such as filing time or compliance cost data would be needed to confirm the result independently (MKD-EU-2022-006-0026; MKD-EU-2023-006-0028).
- Sources differ on whether an integrated tax information system exists to be expanded or has still to be built, with the Commission asking in 2025 for a new system and an international report referring to expanding the existing one, and a clear statement from the Public Revenue Office or the Commission would settle it (MKD-EU-2025-004-0121; MKD-IO-2024-001-0095).
- Progress on the electronic tax administration and e-invoicing is known only from machine-translated plan rows, and evidence of the project documentation, the law and the systems being delivered would be needed (MKD-GOV-2025-004-0201; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0237; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0202).
