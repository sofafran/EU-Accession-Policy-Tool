## In brief

The Commission asks state institutions to strengthen their cyber infrastructure and expertise and the national Computer Incident Response Centre to increase its staffing and operational capacity substantially, and the Reform Agenda ties payments to operational response teams, a staffed competent authority and a secure government network (MKD-EU-2026-007-0115; MKD-EU-2026-007-0116; MKD-EU-2024-007-0003; MKD-EU-2024-007-0004; MKD-EU-2024-007-0007). North Macedonia adopted a cyber security strategy and action plan in January 2025 and a law on network and information security in June 2025, which establishes the national and government response teams (MKD-GOV-2025-007-0025). In 2026 the Commission reports that the Ministry of Digital Transformation established MKD-GOV-CSIRT for the executive authorities, investing in an incident response platform and staff capacity, and that a National Cybersecurity Council and a Cybersecurity Department were set up under the strategy (MKD-EU-2026-007-0114; MKD-EU-2026-007-0140). No item yet shows the response teams working to the Reform Agenda standard. The State Audit Office reports, in machine-translated findings, that only 3 of 11 MKD-CIRT posts were filled and that 67% of surveyed institutions had no response team, and the disaster recovery centre is used by 3 of about 140 bodies (MKD-GOV-2024-008-0054; MKD-GOV-2024-008-0068; MKD-GOV-2025-004-0042). In April 2026 the Commission still reports major deficiencies in the security of digital infrastructure and government services (MKD-EU-2026-003-0009). The assessment is that the gap is partly_addressed, the direction of travel is persistent, priority is High and tractability is Low.

## How the EU requirement has changed

From 2020 to 2023 the Commission frames cybersecurity mainly at regional level, as a priority for capacity building and resilience against hybrid threats (MKD-EU-2020-002-0019; MKD-EU-2021-002-0006; MKD-EU-2022-002-0021). From 2023 its country wording is essentially unchanged: state institutions need stronger cyber capacity, infrastructure and expertise, and the national response centre needs more staff (MKD-EU-2023-001-0147; MKD-EU-2024-001-0096; MKD-EU-2024-001-0097; MKD-EU-2025-001-0141). The 2025 wording sharpens this to significant strengthening of the centre, and the 2026 wording to a substantial increase in its staffing and operational capacity (MKD-EU-2025-001-0142; MKD-EU-2026-007-0116). In October 2024 the Reform Agenda adds concrete payment conditions on response teams, the competent authority and a secure network (MKD-EU-2024-007-0003; MKD-EU-2024-007-0004; MKD-EU-2024-007-0007). The Commission's reform programme assessments move from large deficiencies in 2025 to major deficiencies in 2026 (MKD-EU-2025-012-0009; MKD-EU-2026-003-0008).

## Open questions

- The 2026 report records MKD-GOV-CSIRT established, but no item shows the national or government response teams operating to the NIS2 and SIM3 standard the Reform Agenda requires, and the Reform Agenda's 2024 baseline of not operational sits against statements that MKD-CIRT is in place (MKD-EU-2026-007-0114; MKD-GOV-2024-002-0004; MKD-EU-2025-004-0093). A Commission payment assessment of these steps would settle their status.
- Incident figures do not show a trend in capacity, since sources give conflicting counts for 2020 (1,443 and 1,441) and later figures use different definitions, so consistent mandatory incident reporting under the new law would be needed (MKD-GOV-2022-003-0006; MKD-GOV-2024-008-0055; MKD-GOV-2024-001-0048).
- The audit evidence on staffing and protection is machine translated, capped at Medium confidence, and based on a survey of 18 institutions, and the 2026 report gives no staffing figures, so an updated audit or staffing data would test whether capacity has changed (MKD-GOV-2024-008-0043; MKD-GOV-2024-008-0068; MKD-EU-2026-007-0116).
- The new 40% salary supplement for cyber security staff is adopted but no item shows its effect on hiring or retention, so later vacancy data for MKD-CIRT and institutions would settle this (MKD-GOV-2025-007-0048; MKD-GOV-2022-004-0009).
