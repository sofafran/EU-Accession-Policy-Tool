## In brief

In every report from 2020 to 2025 the Commission finds that the Personal Data Protection Agency is not systematically consulted on sectoral and implementing laws and that its recommendations are not fully followed, and in 2023 and 2024 that Parliament does not follow up its reports (MKD-EU-2020-001-0049; MKD-EU-2022-001-0028; MKD-EU-2023-001-0041; MKD-EU-2024-001-0045). In 2025 the Commission reported, as a concerning development, that the government removed the Agency from the list of bodies that must be consulted, and that Parliament amended the data protection law to allow transfers to NATO countries without consulting it (MKD-EU-2025-001-0068; MKD-EU-2025-001-0065). The Agency's own report, machine translated and capped at Medium confidence, confirms the deletion in the Government's Rules of Procedure of 31 December 2024 (MKD-GOV-2025-010-0035). The Commission found no progress on responsiveness to the Agency's recommendations in 2025 (MKD-EU-2025-001-0070). No intervention is recorded as responding to the issue. The validated assessment is open, with priority Medium, direction of travel worsening and tractability Medium.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The finding was stable in substance from 2020 to 2024, with small shifts in wording. In 2020 and 2021 the Commission said recommendations were not fully implemented and not all laws were submitted before adoption, noting some progress in 2021 (MKD-EU-2020-001-0049; MKD-EU-2021-001-0031). From 2022 it said the Agency was not systematically consulted on sectoral or implementing legislation and its advice not always taken on board, and the 2023 screening report called for more systematic consultation (MKD-EU-2022-001-0028; MKD-EU-2023-003-0077; MKD-EU-2023-001-0032; MKD-EU-2024-001-0043). In 2023 and 2024 it added the lack of parliamentary follow-up and weak responsiveness (MKD-EU-2023-001-0041; MKD-EU-2024-001-0044; MKD-EU-2024-001-0045). In 2025 the wording changed: the Commission reported the Agency's removal from the mandatory consultation list and no progress on responsiveness (MKD-EU-2025-001-0068; MKD-EU-2025-001-0070).

## Open questions

- The Agency's counts of opinions given on draft laws, 5 in 2024 and 15 in 2025, come from machine-translated reports and do not show whether consultation improved, so data on the share of relevant draft laws sent to the Agency would settle this (MKD-GOV-2025-010-0036; MKD-GOV-2026-002-0064).
- No source shows whether ministries now act on the Agency's recommendations or whether Parliament has debated its reports, and records of that follow-up would be needed to judge these parts of the requirement (MKD-EU-2024-001-0044; MKD-EU-2024-001-0045).
- Two confirmed conflicts on the number of reprimands stand unresolved, with the Commission's 2023 report giving 10 cases for 2022 and 17 for 2021 against 4 in each year in the country's screening presentation, and the Agency's own annual figures would settle them (MKD-EU-2023-001-0038; MKD-EU-2023-003-0072; MKD-EU-2023-003-0068).
- Whether the Government will restore the consultation duty in Article 24 of its Rules of Procedure, as the Agency proposes in a machine-translated report, is not known, and an amendment to the Rules would show it (MKD-GOV-2026-002-0063).
