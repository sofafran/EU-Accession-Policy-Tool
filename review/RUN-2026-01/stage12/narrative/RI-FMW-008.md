## In brief

The Commission asks North Macedonia to negotiate and conclude bilateral social security coordination and healthcare insurance agreements with EU Member States and to put in place administrative measures to prepare for accession (MKD-EU-2021-001-0061; MKD-EU-2024-001-0009; MKD-EU-2025-001-0014). The ministry lists agreements it describes as in force and applied, including with 13 Member States, but this list is machine translated, capped at Medium confidence and gives no case data (MKD-GOV-2023-001-0001; MKD-EU-2025-003-0085). Negotiations with Italy, the USA and Greece began in 2023, yet the Commission reports no new agreements signed in any year from 2021 to 2025 (MKD-EU-2024-001-0065; MKD-EU-2021-001-0072; MKD-EU-2022-001-0070; MKD-EU-2023-001-0097; MKD-EU-2024-001-0066; MKD-EU-2025-001-0107). The agreements are judged to have worked, with Medium attribution, in a narrow sense: the Commission rates preparation on coordination as good because of that experience (MKD-EU-2025-003-0090). What remains is agreements with the other Member States and administrative measures for accession. The validated assessment is partly_addressed, with priority High, direction of travel persistent and tractability Low.

## How the EU requirement has changed

The core request has been stable since 2021, with small shifts in verb and scope. In 2021 the Commission asked the country to take steps to conclude further bilateral agreements and to identify administrative measures for accession (MKD-EU-2021-001-0061). In 2022 it asked it to step up signature of agreements with EU Member States, now naming healthcare insurance (MKD-EU-2022-001-0064). From 2023 the wording became to continue negotiating and concluding agreements and to put in place administrative measures (MKD-EU-2023-001-0091; MKD-EU-2024-001-0009). The 2025 report adds preparation for electronic exchange of social security data (MKD-EU-2025-001-0014). The 2025 screening report states that further agreements need to be signed, specifically with EU Member States (MKD-EU-2025-003-0106; MKD-EU-2025-003-0091).

## Open questions

- Two confirmed conflicts on the number of agreements stand unresolved, 14 against 13 with EU Member States between the 2020 and 2021 reports and 23 against 22 in total between the 2023 report and the screening report, and a current official list checked against the Commission's count would settle them (MKD-EU-2020-001-0084; MKD-EU-2021-001-0069; MKD-EU-2023-001-0098; MKD-EU-2025-003-0085).
- The ministry's list of agreements in force and applied is machine translated and gives no case data, so figures on claims handled, periods aggregated or benefits exported under each agreement would be needed to show the agreements working in practice (MKD-GOV-2023-001-0001).
- No outcome of the negotiations with Italy and Greece begun in 2023 is recorded, and a report of signature or ratification would settle their status (MKD-EU-2024-001-0065; MKD-EU-2025-001-0107).
- The Commission's good preparation rating is a single 2025 finding with no earlier rating to compare, and later Commission assessments would show whether readiness is changing (MKD-EU-2025-003-0090).
