## In brief

The Commission's 2023 screening report sets out what North Macedonia needs to connect to EU justice and home affairs systems: an analysis of the means to implement ECRIS, significant technical adaptations and funding for the Visa Information System, and an IT structure compatible with Eurodac by accession (MKD-EU-2023-003-0091; MKD-EU-2023-003-0095; MKD-EU-2023-003-0096). It found no functional system yet to share criminal records (MKD-EU-2023-003-0090). Frontex prepared a Masterplan for interoperable IT systems, but it is validated only as proposed, as no source shows the systems being built (MKD-EU-2022-001-0044; MKD-EU-2024-001-0049). The Commission reports that the roll-out of CT SIENA increased opportunities for counter-terrorism exchange, judged to have worked, but this narrow outcome says nothing about readiness for EU large-scale systems (MKD-EU-2020-001-0060). The country's request for Eurodac access before accession has no basis in EU law (MKD-EU-2023-003-0103). The unresolved gap is open and the direction of travel is insufficient_evidence. Priority is Medium and tractability is Low.

## How the EU requirement has changed

No change can be traced. The requirements on ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac come essentially from one 2023 screening report, so there is no earlier or later Commission wording on the same points to compare (MKD-EU-2023-003-0091; MKD-EU-2023-003-0095; MKD-EU-2023-003-0096). The same report also asks for adequate funding of the border sector, including IT systems in line with EU standards (MKD-EU-2023-003-0099). Commission items from 2021, 2022 and 2024 describe the Frontex biometric plan rather than restating a requirement, so they do not show whether the requirement has moved (MKD-EU-2021-001-0122; MKD-EU-2022-001-0044; MKD-EU-2024-001-0049).

## Open questions

- Whether readiness has improved since 2023 is insufficient_evidence, because no later Commission item repeats the screening requirements; a later Commission assessment of ECRIS, Visa Information System and Eurodac readiness would settle it.
- The status of the planned biometric registration system following the Eurodac model is insufficient_evidence, as the only item dates from 2021 (MKD-EU-2021-001-0122); a later Commission or Frontex report would settle it.
- No source shows whether the Frontex Masterplan is being carried out or funded (MKD-EU-2024-001-0049), so implementation and budget reporting on the planned systems is needed.
- Whether a functional criminal records system and the ECRIS analysis now exist is not shown after 2023 (MKD-EU-2023-003-0090), so Ministry of Justice or Commission reporting is needed.
