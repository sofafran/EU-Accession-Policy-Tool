# Fact pack RI-TEC-032: Not ready to connect to other EU sectoral information systems

Systems in other sectors are not yet ready to interconnect with EU information systems, such as the business registers interconnection system, the Internal Market Information System, the civil protection system, EU taxation systems and the own resources reporting system.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The sectors do not share one trend, so this rests on those with repeated wording: CECIS from 2020 to 2025 (with 'no progress' in 2023), the business registers link from 2023 to 2025, and EU tax systems and own resources in 2024 and 2025. Some areas are better, including the 2021 COVID certificate alignment and food safety tools in 2025, which the residual label hides. The chapter scales were context only and not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0157;MKD-EU-2021-001-0119;MKD-EU-2022-001-0127;MKD-EU-2023-001-0181;MKD-EU-2024-001-0110;MKD-EU-2025-001-0177;MKD-EU-2023-001-0109;MKD-EU-2024-001-0075;MKD-EU-2025-003-0150;MKD-EU-2024-001-0017;MKD-EU-2025-001-0026;MKD-EU-2024-001-0111;MKD-EU-2025-001-0190;MKD-EU-2021-002-0002;MKD-EU-2025-006-0003
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The latest Commission items still ask for the CECIS link, business register readiness, tax interoperability and the own resources system, and the responding interventions are nearly all proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals. North Macedonia states it is technically ready for BRIS, but the Commission says machine-readable data alignment is still needed. What remains is connection to those systems, noting that BRIS and the Internal Market Information System also depend on EU decisions or accession. Items: MKD-EU-2025-005-0019;MKD-EU-2025-001-0177;MKD-EU-2025-003-0138;MKD-EU-2025-003-0150;MKD-EU-2025-001-0026;MKD-EU-2025-001-0190;MKD-EU-2025-003-0127;MKD-EU-2025-003-0190
- priority: Medium (validated). Each requirement is repeated, and tax interoperability reaches the main findings in 2024 and 2025 and CECIS is an issue for immediate attention in 2024, but none is central to the theme as a whole and the only Reform Agenda references are railway and freight outputs in the government's text. This is a residual group spanning several chapters, so the rating is an average that would differ by sector. Items: MKD-EU-2024-001-0017;MKD-EU-2025-001-0026;MKD-EU-2024-001-0110;MKD-EU-2025-005-0019;MKD-EU-2025-003-0150;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0129;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0134
- tractability: Medium (validated). The sectors differ: food safety shows active use of EU tools, the CECIS link looks technically limited yet has not been finished in over five years, and BRIS requires full company law alignment and access to groups open only to Member States and EEA countries. The rating is a mid-point, not a uniform finding. Items: MKD-EU-2025-006-0004;MKD-EU-2023-001-0181;MKD-EU-2025-005-0019;MKD-EU-2025-003-0145;MKD-EU-2025-003-0185

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0155 Electronic freight transport information system (eFTI): proposed. The Reform Agenda sets this out as a planned phase, and no source reports it carried out.
- INT-MKD-0182 Connection to the Common Emergency Communication and Information System (CECIS): proposed. The Commission reports steps towards the technical connection from 2020, and in July 2025 says the link still needs to be done, with the country planning it by 2027.
- INT-MKD-0183 Alignment with the EU Digital COVID Certificate system: implemented. The Commission states that North Macedonia completed alignment in August 2021. This is the last evidence, and no item says how the certificate was used after that.
- INT-MKD-0188 Internal Market Information System access: proposed. North Macedonia requested access and the Commission took note, saying it is being considered under the Growth Plan. No item shows access granted.
- INT-MKD-0285 IT system for managing A and B accounts: proposed. The Commission says that establishing the system remains pending.
- INT-MKD-0312 Phytosanitary information system: implemented. The screening report, from the country's account, says the system was synchronised with the EU database and shared with stakeholders. That is reported only, so it does not reach in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0316 SDMX-based architecture for data transmission to Eurostat: implemented. The country says it uses this architecture for transmitting data to Eurostat, but this is its own presentation in the screening report. No independent source confirms use, so in_practice is not reached.
- INT-MKD-0321 TRACES module for phytosanitary certification: proposed. The country has only expressed an interest in using this module, and no item shows it taken up.
- INT-MKD-0327 Upgrade of the EMCS including the System for the Exchange of Excise Data (SEED): proposed. The upgrade is planned for before accession, according to the country's account. No item shows it started.
- INT-MKD-0450 Decision 2020/263 on computerising the movement and surveillance of excise goods: proposed. North Macedonia says the decision will be implemented at accession. It is a future commitment only.
- INT-MKD-0481 Interconnection with the Business Registers Interconnection System (BRIS) prior to accession: proposed. North Macedonia requested this interconnection, and no item shows the request granted.
- INT-MKD-0532 Transfer of vehicle registration data to the European Vehicle Register: proposed. This is a listed Reform Agenda output. No item shows the data transferred.
