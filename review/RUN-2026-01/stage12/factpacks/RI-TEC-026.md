# Fact pack RI-TEC-026: Electronic identification and trust services not usable or recognised

Electronic identification and signature tools are costly, cumbersome and little used, qualified trust services are not widely available, and the country is not yet on the EU trusted list or in cross-border wallet arrangements.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission said in 2024 that the tools were cumbersome and neither free nor user friendly, and in 2025 that they were still not, while its 2021 report recorded only a modest rise in electronic signature use. In 2026 it found the trusted list step not achieved despite the request of 30 October 2025, so steps were taken without a changed result. The chapter scale is context only and was not decisive, and the 2024 audit evidence that high-level eID did not raise use is machine translated and capped at Medium. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0013;MKD-EU-2023-003-0042;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0102;MKD-EU-2024-001-0031;MKD-EU-2025-001-0050;MKD-EU-2025-001-0147;MKD-EU-2026-002-0026;MKD-EU-2026-002-0029
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Two providers offer advanced and qualified signature services, as the Commission reports and as the Reform Agenda step asks, but the same step is not achieved, the tools remain not free or user friendly, and the wallet and mutual recognition are still proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals. The two sides conflict: North Macedonia reports the trusted list preparation complete and considers the step achieved, while the Commission says the self-assessment and published list are outstanding. What remains is inclusion in the trusted list (grace period to 31 December 2026), usable and free tools in common use, the wallet and cross-border recognition. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0010;MKD-EU-2024-007-0014;MKD-EU-2026-002-0018;MKD-EU-2026-002-0022;MKD-EU-2026-002-0024;MKD-EU-2026-002-0028;MKD-EU-2026-002-0029;MKD-EU-2026-002-0030;MKD-EU-2025-001-0050
- priority: High (validated). Joining the trusted list and implementing the wallet are Reform Agenda steps, the first with a stated payment provision, and the Commission repeated its concern about the tools in 2024 and 2025. Recognition and use are central to the electronic identification theme. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0010;MKD-EU-2024-007-0014;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0012;MKD-EU-2024-001-0031;MKD-EU-2025-001-0147
- tractability: Medium (validated). The outstanding trusted list tasks are specific and technical, and two providers have passed ministry checks, which favours progress. Use and usability need many institutions and users, the wallet depends on regional partners and the mutual recognition agreement, and the list depends on Commission assessment. The audit found failed procurement of institutional seal certificates (machine translated, capped at Medium), which is a warning on delivery. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0038 2019 digitalisation law package: implemented. The package was adopted in 2019 and measures followed, including the portal and the coordination groups. The State Audit Office found about 140 laws still not aligned in March 2024, long after the deadline of 25 August 2020, so implementation is partial and the 2025 strategy repeats that finding.
- INT-MKD-0052 Law on General Administrative Procedures: adopted. The law has been in force for about nine years, but the Commission finds it is not systematically implemented and the Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio. These findings are preferred to a claim of application, so nothing higher than adopted is supported, although some partial application is likely.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0108 National Digital Identity Wallet: proposed. The wallet is a Reform Agenda step due by December 2027, and the October 2025 digital strategy still describes it in the future tense. The 2025 law provides the legal basis, but no source shows a wallet in operation.
- INT-MKD-0109 Digital card for citizens: proposed. The 2023 strategy plans the card, and no later source mentions it being set up. Later plans refer to a digital wallet, but no source says the wallet replaces it.
- INT-MKD-0110 Digital identity (eID): in_practice. The State Audit Office, reporting after its audit, describes eID as issued on registration and used to log in to the national portal and other portals. It also found that some portals require separate profiles and that high-level eID conditions did not raise use, so use is real but limited.
- INT-MKD-0111 Joining the EU Third Countries trusted list: proposed. The request to join was submitted on 30 October 2025 but joining has not happened: the Commission found the process incomplete and the step not achieved, while the country considers it achieved. The Commission's assessment is preferred and the conflict is for the reviewer. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0112 Trust services and qualified certificates: implemented. The Commission reports two registered providers offering advanced and qualified signature services, certified and audited by EU-accredited bodies, which overtakes the audit's 2024 note of no procurement contract. Use is not shown, as the audit could not confirm full use of digital certificates.
- INT-MKD-0113 Authority responsible for issuing qualified certificates: proposed. The Reform Agenda says the reform will dedicate such an authority. No source reports it as designated.
- INT-MKD-0124 National e-Services Portal (uslugi.gov.mk): in_practice. The State Audit Office, an independent source, confirms the portal has been in use since 2019, but finds low use (0.556 services per registered user), and the 2025 strategy reports 314 services and about 13 of 100 adults registered. Use is real but limited. The Commission and the audit differ on access for people with disabilities, which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0127 Central Population Register: in_practice. The Commission reported the register operational in 2020 and used to crosscheck the 2021 census data, and the State Audit Office describes it as an integrated working database. Later government text that services will draw on it obligatorily shows full use is still to come.
- INT-MKD-0141 Unified document management system: implemented. The Commission reports a central document management system launched and in use by 44 institutions by the end of June 2026, against the 2025 pilot in 22 of about 140 central institutions; the two counts may not measure the same system. Use is still partial against the target of all central institutions by December 2026, so in practice is not rated.
- INT-MKD-0142 Human Resources Management Information System: in_practice. The Commission reports in 2020 and 2023 that use of the system by institutions has increased. The Reform Agenda plans a further update, which is separate from the system already in use.
- INT-MKD-0143 Single unified social insurance registry: proposed. Both sources describe the registry as a plan, with the screening report placing it in the coming two years through a World Bank loan. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0164 E-Authorisation register: proposed. The latest source lists the register among applications developed or to be developed and describes it in the future tense. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0282 Electronic identification and authentication system in the administration (A 4.1.3.7): proposed. This is an action plan activity quoted by the audit, and the item does not report delivery.
- INT-MKD-0283 Electronic signing by officials: proposed. The 2023 strategy says officials will have to sign electronically once legislation and the document system are in place. No source reports this as done.
- INT-MKD-0294 Mastercard digital identity integration in the e-services portal: unclear. A government statement says 150,000 citizens would be able to open a digital identity through this arrangement, but the State Audit Office did not confirm full use of the certificates. The audit is preferred to the statement, and the delivery position is left open. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0297 MojTermin connection with the digital wallet (A 3.2.8.5): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0317 Single Sign-On System: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that Single Sign-On was integrated across service providers. This follows the State Audit Office's 2024 finding that access to e-services did not go through the system; the two describe different dates, so they are a change over time and not a conflict. No item measures use of the system.
- INT-MKD-0340 electronic signatures in the e-portal for services: implemented. The Commission said in 2020 that signatures can be used on the portal, which shows availability, not use. The State Audit Office did not confirm full use of certificates in 2024, so in_practice is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0355 DigInfo centres: proposed. The Ministry says it will carry out an analysis for establishing these centres. No source shows that this happened.
- INT-MKD-0447 Cross-border recognition of electronic signatures (A 3.1.6.8): proposed. This is an action plan row, and its in_progress label means work under way that is not complete. No item shows cross-border recognition in force.
- INT-MKD-0492 Measure 3.1.6 Digital wallet and eID interoperability: proposed. This is a measure in the 2025 strategy. The June 2025 law introduces the wallet, but the Commission says the wallet by-laws are foreseen by December 2027, and no item shows a wallet in use.
- INT-MKD-0516 Registration of the third trust service provider: proposed. The Commission says the registration procedure is ongoing, and two providers are registered so far.
- INT-MKD-0521 Review and supervision of qualified trust service providers by the Ministry of Digital Transformation: implemented. The Commission records that the ministry reviewed the certificates and decisions and carried out supervisory inspections of both providers. The process for the trusted list is not complete, but that is a separate step.
- INT-MKD-0536 Wallet upgrade to eIDAS 2.0 (A 3.1.6.6): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row, with no evidence of delivery.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0030 leads to MKD-EU-2026-002-0021: success mixed, attribution Medium. The Commission records two providers accredited in 2021 and still offering advanced and qualified signatures, so the immediate outcome occurred. The wider result is weaker: the tools are still not user friendly or free, and the trusted list step is not achieved.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2026-002-0024 vs MKD-EU-2026-002-0028: North Macedonia reports having completed all preparatory activities for the trusted list, while the Commission says it still needs to finalise its self-assessment and publish its national trusted list in the required format.
- MKD-EU-2026-002-0018 vs MKD-EU-2026-002-0029: On the same trusted list step in the same assessment, the beneficiary considers it achieved and the Commission considers it not achieved.
