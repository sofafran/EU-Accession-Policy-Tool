# Fact pack RI-TEC-005: Limited taxpayer e-services

The tax administration offers a limited range of electronic services to taxpayers.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission's recommendation to keep extending taxpayer e-services recurs every year from 2020 to 2026 with almost no change, moving only from 'further extend' to 'continue the process of expansion', so the default applies; this is one repeated view, not independent evidence. The Chapter 16 'Some progress' wording of 2021 is context only and was not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0140;MKD-EU-2021-001-0110;MKD-EU-2021-001-0112;MKD-EU-2022-001-0115;MKD-EU-2023-001-0163;MKD-EU-2024-001-0104;MKD-EU-2025-001-0151;MKD-EU-2026-003-0012
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Commission still asks in 2026 for expansion, while e-Tax, e-VAT and pre-filled returns are proposed as in practice and the electronic tax administration only as proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals, and the Macedonian-language plan items are machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. What remains is a wider range of taxpayer e-services, with the electronic tax administration still at project documentation and draft legislation stage. Items: MKD-EU-2026-003-0012;MKD-EU-2025-001-0151;MKD-EU-2025-004-0122;MKD-EU-2022-006-0026;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0201;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0237
- priority: Medium (validated). The recommendation is repeated across more than three reports, but taxpayer e-services are one clause beside business processes, the integrated IT system and compliance risk, so the issue is not central to Chapter 16 or the theme. The Reform Agenda's e-services step names the fiscal sector only as an example, and this was not read as a payment condition on this issue. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0140;MKD-EU-2021-001-0112;MKD-EU-2022-001-0115;MKD-EU-2025-004-0124;MKD-EU-2024-007-0015
- tractability: High (validated). One agency leads, working platforms exist, and upgrades are reported by the Commission, so progress depends little on other actors or the EU. A caution is that the source does not say how far further e-services depend on the integrated IT system, which has been outstanding since 2020. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0111;MKD-EU-2025-004-0122;MKD-EU-2022-006-0026;MKD-EU-2025-004-0121

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0144 Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS): proposed. North Macedonia reported that documentation had been prepared in 2023, and the Reform Agenda calls the IFMIS reform ongoing in 2024. No item shows the system in operation.
- INT-MKD-0145 Integrated tax information system (ITIS): proposed. The 2022-2024 Economic Reform Programme foresaw the system and the Reform Agenda calls the work ongoing, while the Commission in 2025 still asks for a new integrated system. The World Bank wording suggests an ITIS already exists to be expanded; I follow the Commission and flag the difference. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0146 e-Tax system: in_practice. The Commission twice names the e-Tax system among e-services that simplified tax reporting, and this is independent of the government's description. The evidence shows use, not success, and the latest item is a 2025 reported statement.
- INT-MKD-0147 Electronic personal tax platform (pre-filled annual tax returns): in_practice. The Commission states in reports from 2020 to 2025 that the Public Revenue Office issues pre-filled returns through the platform. This is a specific and repeated description of current use by an independent source, although no usage figures are given.
- INT-MKD-0156 Open Finance Portal: implemented. The Commission reports that the portal went live and gives the public transaction data, and the ITU profile lists it. No item measures use, and the last evidence is from 2022.
- INT-MKD-0157 National open data portal (data.gov.mk): unclear. The country's presentation to the Commission in February 2025 describes the portal as in line with best practice, but the State Audit Office says it has not functioned since the beginning of 2025 because of procurement problems. The audit is given more weight because it is specific and independent. No later source says it was restored. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0166 E-Invoice system: implemented. The Commission reports that the Public Revenue Office launched an e-invoice pilot phase, enabling non-cash transactions to be registered in real time. A pilot in operation is partial roll-out, and no item reports its use.
- INT-MKD-0240 IT strategy 2019-2022 of the Public Revenue Office: adopted. The screening report, from the country's presentation, describes the strategy's priorities. Its period ended in 2022 and no item shows what was delivered under it.
- INT-MKD-0326 Upgrade of e-tax services: implemented. The Commission reports the upgrading of e-tax services in 2021. It gives no separate evidence of use of the upgrade.
- INT-MKD-0334 e-VAT mobile application: in_practice. The Commission cites the application as a working service that individual taxpayers can use to file VAT returns. This is its own assessment, in two successive years, though the latest is 2023.
- INT-MKD-0337 ePension Portal: implemented. The World Bank report lists the portal among initiatives launched by 2022. It gives no usage data and there is no later evidence.
- INT-MKD-0338 ePersonal Income Tax Portal: implemented. The source lists the portal among initiatives launched by 2022. It gives no use figures.
- INT-MKD-0356 Digitalisation of the Public Revenue Office: proposed. The Commission reports it as a government aim among ongoing efforts. No item shows the digitalisation completed.
- INT-MKD-0461 Electronic Tax Administration (e-UJP) (A 3.2.11.1): proposed. This is an action plan row for preparing project documentation, which is work under way before any system exists. No item shows delivery.
- INT-MKD-0485 Legislation establishing e-UJP (A 3.2.11.5): proposed. This is an action plan row in the SMART/MK action plan, so it is a plan however worded. No item shows delivery.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0146 leads to MKD-EU-2022-006-0026: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission states that e-Tax is a successful e-service that has simplified tax reporting, and its 2021 report on reduced burden points the same way, but no measure of the simplification is given.
- INT-MKD-0334 leads to MKD-EU-2022-006-0026: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The Commission lists e-VAT among successful e-services, but the outcome stated is only that it enables VAT returns, which is availability and not a result. No figures on use are given.
- INT-MKD-0146 leads to MKD-EU-2023-006-0028: success worked, attribution Medium. The 2023 sentence repeats the 2022 wording on e-Tax, so it is not independent support for LNK-000432 and the same limits apply: a Commission statement with no measure.
- INT-MKD-0334 leads to MKD-EU-2023-006-0028: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The 2023 wording repeats 2022 and states only that e-VAT makes online returns possible, which is availability and not a result. No figures on use are given.
