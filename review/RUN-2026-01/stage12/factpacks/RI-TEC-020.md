# Fact pack RI-TEC-020: Weak compliance with data protection rules

Data controllers, including public bodies, do not apply data protection law in practice, breaches are frequent and not always reported, awareness is low, and sanctions are rare.

## Validated issue judgements

- priority: Medium (validated). Compliance is central to whether the law has effect, but the Commission's direct findings on it are thin, mainly 2023 and 2025, and it is not a named key recommendation. The large volume of items comes from the Agency's own reports and was not treated as importance. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0033;MKD-EU-2023-001-0044;MKD-EU-2025-001-0071;MKD-EU-2025-001-0072
- tractability: Low (validated). Progress needs behaviour to change across very many public and private controllers, and it depends on an Agency whose supervision and misdemeanour capacity is shrinking. The evidence shows awareness work and enforcement activity, but no sustained improvement in compliance. The Agency's items are machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0033;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0040;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0060;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0014;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0050;MKD-EU-2025-001-0072
- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission called fines low in 2020, found that controllers do not implement the law in 2023, found low awareness and more frequent breaches in 2025, and in 2026 still finds digital safeguards in state bodies insufficient, so the default of persistent applies; the chapter scale was not decisive. On breaches the reports conflict, and both are cited: the 2025 report finds breaches increasingly frequent and the 2026 report finds reported incidents declining, while the Agency's estimate of severe under-reporting, given in 2026, means neither figure shows whether compliance changed, so the conflict is reported and not resolved. Supervision counts measure enforcement rather than compliance, and the machine-translated Agency findings are capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0047;MKD-EU-2023-001-0033;MKD-EU-2023-001-0044;MKD-EU-2025-001-0071;MKD-EU-2025-001-0072;MKD-EU-2026-007-0044;MKD-EU-2026-007-0045;MKD-EU-2026-007-0046;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0091;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0043
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The security rulebook has been adopted and an e-classroom is in place, but the data protection officer network and the sanction amendments are still only planned, no independent evidence shows changed behaviour, and the 2026 report again finds digital safeguards in state bodies insufficient and severe under-reporting of breaches. What remains is for controllers, including public bodies, to apply the law, report breaches and face effective sanctions. Two conflicts are reported and not resolved: reprimand counts differ between the Commission's report (10 for 2022, 17 for 2021) and the country presentation (4 for each), and the 2025 finding of increasingly frequent breaches conflicts with the 2026 finding of declining reported incidents, which low notification numbers (19 for 2022, 11 for 2025) and the Agency's under-reporting estimate do not settle. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0071;MKD-EU-2025-001-0072;MKD-EU-2026-007-0044;MKD-EU-2026-007-0045;MKD-EU-2026-007-0046;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0006;MKD-GOV-2024-006-0056;MKD-GOV-2024-006-0050;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0060;MKD-EU-2023-001-0038;MKD-EU-2023-003-0068;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0058;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0059

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0018 Strategy for the Implementation of the Right to Personal Data Protection 2025-2030: implemented. The Agency reports the strategy was adopted in 2024 and implementation began in 2025. The Commission's finding that budget and consultation problems negatively affected implementation is preferred to the Agency's account on how far it has gone, but it also treats implementation as under way.
- INT-MKD-0071 Law on financial companies: adopted. The Commission says recent changes to the law led to more complaints, which shows the changes exist. It gives no detail of measures put in place, so I do not go beyond adopted.
- INT-MKD-0127 Central Population Register: in_practice. The Commission reported the register operational in 2020 and used to crosscheck the 2021 census data, and the State Audit Office describes it as an integrated working database. Later government text that services will draw on it obligatorily shows full use is still to come.
- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0203 Rulebook on Security of Personal Data Processing: adopted. The Agency adopted the rulebook on 26 December 2024 and says it began to apply on 1 July 2025. Entry into application is not evidence that controllers or the Agency carried it out, and the only source is the Agency's own report, so I have not used the stated implemented.
- INT-MKD-0217 Rulebook on carrying out supervision (amendment): adopted. The Agency reports adopting and publishing the amendment. No item shows it being applied, and the only source is the Agency's own report.
- INT-MKD-0219 Rulebook on notifying personal data breaches (supplement): adopted. The Agency reports it adopted and published the rulebook. No evidence of application is given.
- INT-MKD-0234 Communication Strategy 2026-2030: proposed. The Agency planned in 2025 to start preparing it. Its 2026 report instead lists preparing a draft of a Communication Strategy 2027-2032, so this strategy appears not to have been adopted under its stated period.
- INT-MKD-0235 Communication Strategy 2027-2032: proposed. The Agency lists preparing a draft strategy as a future task. No draft is shown to exist.
- INT-MKD-0279 E-classroom web platform: implemented. Metamorphosis created the tool with the Agency, and the Agency took it over in 2024 and hosts it on its website. The rise in users is reported only by the Agency, which is not enough for in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0291 Interoperability platform for data protection officers: proposed. The Agency's strategy puts this forward as a possible measure, and no item shows it established.
- INT-MKD-0381 Network of data protection officers: proposed. The Agency's strategy sets establishment for 2026 with a yes or no indicator. No item shows the network established.
- INT-MKD-0417 Agency memoranda of cooperation: implemented. The Agency reports signing memoranda in 2024 and 2025 with named bodies. This shows signature only and says nothing of the work done under them.
- INT-MKD-0433 Amendment of sectoral legislation on misdemeanour sanctions: proposed. The Agency's 2024 plan calls for amending sectoral law, with a target of late 2025 to early 2026. Its April 2026 report describes misdemeanour work but no such amendment in the items reviewed.
- INT-MKD-0455 Documents on certification and codes of conduct: proposed. The item is a task in the Agency's 2024 plan. No item shows the documents adopted.
- INT-MKD-0502 National Programme for the Adoption of the Acquis: implemented. The Agency reports activities it carries out under the programme, such as supervisions and by-laws. This is the Agency's own reporting and it covers one chapter only, so it does not support in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0508 Personal Data Protection Agency oversight of security services under the Law on Interception of Communications: implemented. North Macedonia reports that the Agency inspected the two security bodies in 2021-2022. This is specific, but it is reported by the country at screening with no independent confirmation, so I stop short of in practice.
- INT-MKD-0527 Supervisory activities on the position of data protection officers: proposed. The Personal Data Protection Strategy says the Agency should carry out these activities. No source reports that they took place.
- INT-MKD-0528 TAIEX support for the Strategy: implemented. The Agency says TAIEX supported the process. It is the Agency's own report and the item has an OCR flag.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0071 leads to MKD-EU-2023-001-0206: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Medium. The Commission states that the changes led to more complaints, but not what the changes were meant to do. More complaints could reflect more misuse or more willingness to complain, so the outcome cannot be scored as a result.
- INT-MKD-0010 leads to MKD-GOV-2024-006-0003: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The only evidence for increased awareness is the Agency's own statement about its own law, so success cannot be judged from it. The same strategy lists low public awareness as a weakness, and the Commission found a need for awareness raising in 2023 and low awareness in 2025.
- INT-MKD-0279 leads to MKD-GOV-2026-002-0104: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The only evidence is the Agency's report, machine translated and capped at Medium confidence, so success cannot be judged from it. It reports a rise in quiz users without a current figure, and the only count found is over 50 users in 2024.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2023-001-0038 vs MKD-EU-2023-003-0072: For 2022 the Commission's 2023 Report gives reprimands in 10 cases, while the country presentation in the 2023 screening report gives reprimands in 4 cases for the same period.
- MKD-EU-2023-001-0038 vs MKD-EU-2023-003-0068: For 2021 the Commission's 2023 Report gives reprimands in 17 cases, while the country presentation in the 2023 screening report gives reprimands in 4 cases.
- MKD-EU-2026-007-0044 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0071: The 2025 report found data breaches increasingly frequent; this report finds reported breach incidents declining.
