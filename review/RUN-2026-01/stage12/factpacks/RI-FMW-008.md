# Fact pack RI-FMW-008: Gaps in bilateral social security and healthcare agreements

Bilateral social security coordination and healthcare agreements do not yet cover all EU Member States or all branches, so workers moving before accession lack coordinated protection.

## Validated issue judgements

- priority: High (validated). Concluding agreements is in the key recommendation in the 2021 to 2025 reports, and social security coordination is a main strand of the chapter. The Commission's good preparation rating lowers the urgency but not the repeated requirement, so reviewers may reasonably weigh centrality differently. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0061;MKD-EU-2022-001-0064;MKD-EU-2023-001-0091;MKD-EU-2024-001-0009;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014;MKD-EU-2025-003-0090
- tractability: Low (validated). Progress depends on other states agreeing, not only on the government. No new agreement is recorded from 2021 to 2025, including after the negotiations started in 2023. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0072;MKD-EU-2025-001-0107;MKD-EU-2024-001-0065;MKD-EU-2024-001-0066
- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The key recommendation to negotiate and conclude bilateral agreements is unchanged from 2021 to 2026, and the chapter scale is context only. The 2026 report records two steps, the entry into force in December 2025 of the social insurance agreement with Italy signed in 2014 and an information exchange agreement signed in June 2026 between the pension fund and the Austrian pension authority, but the first took eleven years from signature and the 2025 report found no new agreement signed in its period, so they do not yet show a change of direction. The two confirmed count contradictions (14 against 13 EU agreements; 23 against 22 in total) still stand, and the 2026 report does not say whether the Italy agreement adds a Member State or replaces the 1961 convention the government lists. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0061;MKD-EU-2022-001-0064;MKD-EU-2023-001-0091;MKD-EU-2024-001-0009;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014;MKD-EU-2026-007-0010;MKD-EU-2026-007-0081;MKD-EU-2026-007-0082;MKD-EU-2025-001-0107;MKD-EU-2024-001-0066;MKD-EU-2025-003-0085;MKD-GOV-2023-001-0027
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). What remains is agreements with the other Member States and the administrative measures to prepare for accession, which the 2026 key recommendation repeats. Agreements with 13 Member States were in force by the government's list and the Commission's count, the 2014 agreement with Italy came into force in December 2025 and an information exchange agreement with the Austrian pension authority was signed in June 2026, but the report does not say whether the Italy agreement adds to the 13 or updates the existing convention, and the negotiations with Greece have no recorded outcome. Further agreements would not by themselves close the gap. Items: MKD-EU-2026-007-0010;MKD-EU-2026-007-0081;MKD-EU-2026-007-0082;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014;MKD-EU-2025-003-0106;MKD-EU-2025-003-0085;MKD-GOV-2023-001-0027;MKD-EU-2024-001-0065

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0198 leads to MKD-EU-2025-003-0090: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission itself rates preparation on coordination as good and gives the experience of the agreements as the reason, so this is not government reporting alone. The result is narrow: it concerns readiness, and the same report still finds the law only partly aligned.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2020-001-0084 vs MKD-EU-2021-001-0069: The 2020 report gives 14 social security agreements with EU Member States and the 2021 report gives 13 out of the same total of 23, while the 2021 report also states there were no changes in this area (MKD-EU-2021-001-0068).
- MKD-EU-2023-001-0098 vs MKD-EU-2025-003-0085: The 2023 report gives 23 bilateral agreements, while North Macedonia states 22 in the screening report, whose bilateral meeting took place in March 2023, and no item under this issue records an agreement ending.
