# Fact pack RI-TEC-027: Interoperability framework not aligned with EU

The national interoperability framework is not aligned with the latest European Interoperability Framework or the Interoperable Europe Act.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission repeated the recommendation to align with the European Interoperability Framework and the Interoperable Europe Act in near-identical terms in 2023, 2024 and 2025, which sets the persistent default under the rules. The 2025 wording ('efforts need to continue') reports no result. The chapter scale is context only and was not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0014;MKD-EU-2023-001-0015;MKD-EU-2024-001-0027;MKD-EU-2024-001-0091;MKD-EU-2025-001-0049;MKD-EU-2025-001-0137
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The only responding intervention, alignment with the framework, has proposed status at the freeze, and this status is itself a proposal, so no aligned text is shown. Items are in tension and this is not recorded in the contradictions file: the 2023 screening says the framework is aligned with framework 2.0, while the 2023 report asks for alignment with the 2017 revision and the audit (machine translated, capped at Medium) found no study confirming alignment. What remains is a national framework shown to be aligned with the current European framework and with the Interoperable Europe Act. Items: MKD-EU-2023-003-0012;MKD-EU-2023-003-0013;MKD-EU-2023-001-0015;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0028;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0069;MKD-EU-2023-003-0009;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0140;MKD-EU-2025-001-0137
- priority: Medium (validated). This is a repeated EU requirement across three reports, but no item places it in a benchmark, a Reform Agenda payment condition or the main findings list of a report. It supports the interoperability theme, yet the Commission itself treats use of the platform as the wider obstacle. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0014;MKD-EU-2023-001-0015;MKD-EU-2024-001-0091;MKD-EU-2025-001-0137
- tractability: High (validated). The mechanism is legal and framework alignment, which one ministry can lead without depending on others. The caution is the record: the recommendation has not moved in three years and the plan is still only stated in a government strategy. Items: MKD-EU-2023-003-0009;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0140;MKD-EU-2025-001-0137

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0130 Alignment with the European Interoperability Framework: proposed. Alignment remains a stated plan: the 2023 screening and 2025 strategy describe future or ongoing work, and the Commission still asks for it in 2024 and 2025. The State Audit Office found in 2022 no study of alignment behind the ministry's claim to follow EIF 2.0.
