# Fact pack RI-TEC-025: Electronic identification and trust services law not aligned

The law on electronic identification and trust services is not fully aligned with eIDAS and the European Digital Identity Regulation, including the Digital Identity Wallet, and implementing rules are pending.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The Commission moved from 'yet to align' (2022) and 'further delayed' (2023) to treating the law as harmonised with the 2014 regulation and asking for the 2024 revision (2024), then to amendments transposing eIDAS 2.0, with 'some progress' for e-government and further efforts required (2025). Sources conflict on whether alignment with the 2014 regulation already existed in 2020 (screening report and 2023 strategy against the 2022 and 2023 reports), which affects the start point but not the trend. The chapter scale is context only and was not decisive; the judgement rests on the issue's own items. Items: MKD-EU-2022-001-0103;MKD-EU-2023-001-0138;MKD-EU-2023-003-0041;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0016;MKD-EU-2024-001-0029;MKD-EU-2024-001-0030;MKD-EU-2025-001-0001;MKD-EU-2025-001-0148;MKD-EU-2025-011-0030
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The law was amended in June 2025 and the Commission counted the step as achieved for Reform Agenda purposes, while saying the law does not fully align and asking for further efforts in its 2025 report; the intervention statuses used here are themselves proposals. What remains is full alignment of the partially harmonised provisions, adoption of the 14 by-laws, which no item shows as adopted, and actual application. The machine-translated law text, capped at Medium confidence, says the amendments apply only from 1 June 2027. Items: MKD-EU-2025-011-0027;MKD-EU-2025-011-0029;MKD-EU-2025-011-0030;MKD-EU-2025-011-0031;MKD-EU-2025-011-0033;MKD-EU-2025-011-0050;MKD-EU-2025-001-0148;MKD-GOV-2025-009-0041
- priority: High (validated). The updated law is a Reform Agenda payment condition, and eIDAS alignment was raised by the Commission in each report from 2022 to 2025. It is the legal base of the electronic identification theme. The rating rests on these requirements and not on the number of items, most of which are provisions of one law. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0009;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0011;MKD-EU-2022-001-0103;MKD-EU-2023-001-0138;MKD-EU-2024-001-0099;MKD-EU-2025-001-0148
- tractability: High (validated). The remaining work is mainly legal drafting led by one ministry, with a gap analysis by SIGMA and a by-law roadmap already available, and the ministry met the June 2025 law deadline. Earlier delays in 2022 and 2023 and the staged by-laws, some tied to the wallet in December 2027, are the main limits. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025 and amended on 29 December 2025; it entered into force on the eighth day after publication on 4 July 2025 and was due to apply from 1 June 2026, but no later item confirms application or reports measures under it, and the security supervision reported for 2025 rests on the earlier law. By-laws were due within 15 months (October 2026) and operators have 18 months to align (January 2027), so that deadline has not yet passed. The Commission's May 2026 finding that the step is not achieved, with a grace period to 30 June 2026 whose outcome is not in the evidence, concerns alignment, and 'draft' or 'proposal' wording in GOV-2025-004-0172 and GOV-2026-002-0100 is historic rather than a conflict.
- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0031 Law amending the Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services 2025: adopted. Parliament adopted the law on 27 June 2025 and the Commission confirms this, while noting it does not fully align with the EU regulation. Article 27 of the law says it will start to apply only from 1 June 2027, so it cannot be implemented at the freeze date. That provision is mapped in the data to the parent law INT-MKD-0030.
- INT-MKD-0032 By-laws under the Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services (2020): adopted. The screening reports state that all by-laws were adopted in the first half of 2020. The Commission disputes whether this achieved eIDAS harmonisation, but that is a question of alignment, not of whether the by-laws were adopted.
- INT-MKD-0033 Bylaws to the 2025 amendments of the Law on Electronic Document, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: proposed. The Commission says the 14 bylaws fall under later steps, some in December 2025 and some by December 2027, and the law sets six months for adopting them. No source up to the freeze date reports any as adopted.
- INT-MKD-0034 Roadmap on the implementation of the Law on Electronic Document, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: adopted. The Commission records that the beneficiary submitted the roadmap listing by-laws and deadlines. No item shows those by-laws adopted, and submission is the most that the evidence supports.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0036 Law on the Central Population Register: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019, and the audit and government sources describe an operating register that is used as a basis for electronic identification. The evidence of use is descriptive and mostly from government, and the 2025 strategy says the data set must be expanded, so I stop short of in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0042 Law on Security of Network and Information Systems 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025, published on 4 July 2025, entered into force on the eighth day and applies from 1 January 2026, except that entities in Art. 4(2) points 8 to 16 are covered only from EU accession. No item shows the measures it requires, such as the by-laws (due within six months), the entity lists, or the government team and network (due within twelve months, so by July 2026), and MKD-CIRT predates the law; the regulator's 'applies' statement gives the legal status only. 'Proposal' in GOV-2026-002-0097 comes from an annex of 2025 opinions, so it is historic rather than a conflict, and the Commission's finding that alignment is incomplete concerns content, not status.
- INT-MKD-0047 Law on Archival Material and Archival Activity 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms adoption on 27 June 2025 and publication on 4 July 2025, and treats the central document management system as still to be completed. A digital strategy row on preparing a new law is a plan row and does not contradict adoption. The link between the earlier Reform Agenda items and this law is inferred, as the entity note says.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: adopted. The Commission reports in November 2025 that the country adopted a long-term ICT strategy and action plan, and the strategy text says some activities began before formal adoption. The goals in the text are targets, so they do not show delivery. The link between the Commission's sentence and SMART/MK is inferred from timing and is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0108 National Digital Identity Wallet: proposed. The wallet is a Reform Agenda step due by December 2027, and the October 2025 digital strategy still describes it in the future tense. The 2025 law provides the legal basis, but no source shows a wallet in operation.
- INT-MKD-0207 Methodology for accreditation of the compliance assessment bodies 2020: adopted. The Commission records this by-law as adopted in 2020. No item shows it applied separately.
- INT-MKD-0208 Rulebook setting the standards for electronic signatures and electronic seals 2020: adopted. The Commission records the rulebook adopted in 2020 as part of the electronic signature framework. No item shows steps taken specifically under it.
- INT-MKD-0324 Unique Electronic Number as primary identifier (A 3.1.5.4): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row on changing the legal framework, with no evidence of delivery.
- INT-MKD-0427 Alignment with eIDAS 2.0, the Digital Services Act and the Digital Markets Act: proposed. The 2025 strategy states this as a plan. The eIDAS 2.0 part has since been legislated, but the Commission says alignment with it is not full and the Digital Services Act and Digital Markets Act are still to be aligned.
- INT-MKD-0454 Digital wallet legal framework revision (A 3.1.6.4): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row. The law was amended in June 2025, but no source links that to this row.
- INT-MKD-0478 Identification of laws to be aligned (A 3.1.1.1): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0542 eIDAS Regulation alignment: adopted. The June 2025 amendments transpose eIDAS 2.0, but the Commission finds sufficient and not full alignment and says further efforts are needed, with by-laws due later. This is legislative alignment only, and no item shows it carried out.

## Outcomes


## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2022-001-0103 vs MKD-EU-2023-003-0041: The 2022 report says national legislation has yet to align with eIDAS, while the screening report says the by-laws of the first half of 2020 established a legal environment harmonised with eIDAS.
- MKD-EU-2023-001-0138 vs MKD-EU-2023-003-0041: The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2023 screening report says the legal environment has been harmonised with eIDAS since the first half of 2020.
- MKD-EU-2023-001-0138 vs MKD-GOV-2023-002-0016: The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2023 public administration reform strategy says the 2019 law on electronic documents, identification and trust services is aligned with European legislation on digitalisation.
- MKD-EU-2023-001-0138 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0029: The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2024 report says national legislation is harmonised with the 2014 eIDAS Regulation, and the list of amendments to the law in MKD-GOV-2025-009-0003 shows none between the two reports.
