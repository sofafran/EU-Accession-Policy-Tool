# Fact pack RI-TEC-013: Broadband and high-capacity network coverage uneven

Small businesses and rural or non-urban areas face obstacles to fixed broadband, and high-capacity and 5G networks are still being rolled out.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission's sentence that smaller companies still face obstacles repeats from 2021 to 2026 with the same figures, so the default applies, though repeats are not independent evidence, and in 2025 the wording hardened to significant obstacles for smaller companies and rural areas. National averages did rise, with home internet access at 90.8% in 2024, 5G coverage at 76% in 2023 and 97% in a machine-translated 2025 national source, and some progress on ICT infrastructure in matching sub-area wording in 2025, while differences between earlier household figures reflect different measures, not conflict. No series tracks the rural and small-business gap itself, so the averages do not show that the unevenness has narrowed, and the chapter scale was not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0041;MKD-EU-2022-001-0052;MKD-EU-2023-001-0070;MKD-EU-2024-001-0052;MKD-EU-2024-009-0009;MKD-EU-2025-001-0086;MKD-EU-2025-001-0083;MKD-EU-2025-001-0205;MKD-EU-2026-003-0004;MKD-EU-2024-008-0024;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0023;MKD-IO-2022-002-0004;MKD-EU-2021-001-0090
- priority: High (validated). Roll-out of 5G network and services is named in a Reform Agenda step, and the Commission repeats the particular need to increase access to broadband in its assessments from 2021 to 2026, which makes it central to the digital infrastructure theme. The payment condition covers the 5G part, not the rural and small-business part, which appears in repeated assessments and not in the chapter's key recommendations. The large volume of items on this issue, much of it repeated wording and national statistics, did not raise the rating. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0008;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0010;MKD-EU-2021-001-0044;MKD-EU-2022-001-0055;MKD-EU-2023-001-0072;MKD-EU-2024-009-0004;MKD-EU-2025-012-0004;MKD-EU-2025-001-0086
- tractability: Medium (validated). Past progress is clear on 5G and on general access, where operators have invested and the Commission records continued expansion. The harder part depends on resources and on others: rural and low-demand areas lack commercial interest, the national network is at feasibility stage on a timetable to 2030 with mixed financing, and the government's own strategy, in machine translation, names lack of finance and high prices as challenges. Delivery therefore needs the ministry, the public broadcasting enterprise, operators and outside funders. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0135;MKD-EU-2025-001-0143;MKD-EU-2024-001-0103;MKD-GOV-2024-003-0003;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0168;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0036;MKD-EU-2025-001-0132
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The latest EU items still find bottlenecks for rural areas and small businesses and ask the public broadcasting enterprise to strengthen its capacity to bring broadband to areas with limited commercial investment, while 5G continued to expand and the national optical network remains a plan. Sources conflict on the size of the gap, and both sides are cited: the ICT strategy gives 77% fixed coverage against an EU average of 78%, the Commission 79.6% against 93.1%, the strategy elsewhere 77% as very high capacity coverage after stating 69%, and the Broadband Competence Office's fast fixed coverage is 87.29% for 2024 in the 2025 report but 75% for 2025 in the 2026 report, a fall the report does not explain; household access figures (91.2% or 92% in 2025) measure take-up, not coverage, and do not settle this. What remains is fixed broadband for small firms and rural areas, very low take-up of the fastest connections and completing the 5G roll-out named in the Reform Agenda; the regional WiFi4WB hotspots do not name the country and cannot alone support a finding here. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The Commission reports that the new law became fully applicable in June 2026, which settles the date of application. No item reports by-laws or measures taken under it, and the Commission's statement that it strengthens consumer protection and competition is a stated outcome, not evidence of application in practice.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0092 National Operational Broadband Plan 2019-2029: implemented. The plan remains valid, and measures have been carried out in part: the Commission records 5G expanding to Skopje and biannual Broadband Competence Office reports. Commission reports for 2021 and 2022 record delay, and the Office's own report finds most 5G goals met but none of the three measures on 100 Mbps subscriptions implemented. These sources describe different parts of the plan, so they do not conflict.
- INT-MKD-0100 Reform 2.2.2.1 Secure Digital infrastructures roll-out: proposed. The reform as a whole is a Reform Agenda plan for infrastructure, 5G and related law. A new Law on Electronic Communications was adopted in June 2025 as one part, but no item shows the secure infrastructure rolled out.
- INT-MKD-0174 National broadband infrastructure deployment: proposed. The items describe a planned investment and project. A feasibility study had only started by October 2025, and no item shows the network built.
- INT-MKD-0189 5G spectrum assignment: in_practice. The Commission reports that approvals for 5G frequencies were granted and that 5G coverage keeps expanding, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. One frequency remained to be tendered in 2024, and that tender is rated separately as INT-MKD-0512.
- INT-MKD-0190 5G roll-out: in_practice. The input items are Reform Agenda plans, but the Commission records 5G reaching Skopje and coverage continuing to expand, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. The Reform Agenda step also covers the security toolbox and one remaining frequency, which are separate and not shown complete.
- INT-MKD-0191 5G security bylaw: proposed. The by-law is a planned measure, with full implementation in the Reform Agenda due by December 2027. No item shows it adopted.
- INT-MKD-0195 Plan for the allocation of radio frequency bands 2025: adopted. The Government gave consent on 24 July 2025 and the plan was published on 25 August 2025, according to the regulator's report. The February 2025 screening item predates this plan and so must refer to an earlier one; the entity link should be checked.
- INT-MKD-0314 Platform giving an overview of free state WiFi needs: implemented. The country presentation says the platform exists. No independent source shows its use, so in_practice is not reached.
- INT-MKD-0416 6G spectrum allocation preparations: proposed. The strategy says the country will begin preparing spectrum for 6G, which is a future commitment.
- INT-MKD-0446 Cooperation with the Agency for Promotion and Support of Tourism on free WiFi: insufficient_evidence. The item says only that there is ongoing cooperation, with no detail of what is being done. It is a reported statement.
- INT-MKD-0466 Free Wi-Fi zones: proposed. The strategy says the state will continue to increase the number of zones, which is a forward commitment. No delivery figures are mapped here.
- INT-MKD-0467 Freeing up of the 700MHz, 3.6GHz and 26 GHz spectrum bands: proposed. A 2022 report describes ongoing efforts, and the regulator reports a 2025 tender for 700 MHz and 3.x GHz with a decision due by January 2026. No source confirms an award, and the 26 GHz band is not covered.
- INT-MKD-0490 Mapping of locations for free WiFi: implemented. The country's presentation says the government has mapped the locations. This completed step is its own account, and it does not show WiFi being provided.
- INT-MKD-0512 Public tender for radio frequency bands 700 MHz, 1800 MHz and 3.x GHz: proposed. The regulator published the tender in 2025 and received one compliant bid, but the decision was postponed and the deadline moved to 12 January 2026. No source gives the outcome, so the assignment is treated as work under way and not concluded.
- INT-MKD-0514 Radio frequency approvals (A 1.1.4.2): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row. The regulator reports a tender in progress for some bands, with no award confirmed.
- INT-MKD-0535 Unified tables of free WiFi needs prepared by the Broadband Competence Office: implemented. The country's presentation says the tables were prepared and sent to all municipalities. This is its own account and shows no further use.
- INT-MKD-0540 Digital connection as a basic right: proposed. The digital strategy says it is important to introduce this notion, which is a statement of intent. No legal right is reported.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0092 leads to MKD-EU-2023-001-0125: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Low. The Commission reports the 5G signal expanded to Skopje in line with the plan's targets, which shows network roll-out and not use or effect, and the coverage figures held do not fill that gap. It says in line with and states no cause, while also reporting the plan as slowed and delayed and operators as investing, so the outcome cannot be scored as a result of the plan.
- INT-MKD-0512 leads to MKD-GOV-2026-003-0012: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The sole bid and the extended deadline of 12 January 2026 come only from machine-translated regulator reporting, and the Commission confirms only that a new operator took part in a tender. No item shows an award or any effect on competition, so no result can be judged from the evidence held.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-GOV-2025-004-0035 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0084: The ICT strategy says fixed broadband coverage of 77% of households is in line with an EU average of 78%, while the Commission's 2025 report says fixed broadband was available to 79.6% of households at the end of 2023, below an EU average of 93.1%.
- MKD-EU-2026-007-0110 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0131: The 2025 report gave 87.29% fast fixed broadband coverage for 2024 (same office); 75% for 2025 is a fall that the text does not explain. Reported, not resolved.
