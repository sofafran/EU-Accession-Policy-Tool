# Fact pack RI-TEC-007: Online access for service providers and regulated professions incomplete

The point of single contact does not give service providers full online information and procedures as the Services Directive requires, and there is no transparent electronic register of regulated professions and qualifications.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The requirement that the point of single contact be upgraded to meet the Services Directive is unchanged in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and the register of regulated professions moves from unfinished data validation (2020, 2021) to 'yet to be put in place' (2024, 2025). The contact point itself did advance, from early stage in 2021 to a platform with 312 online services in 2025, so the two limbs of this issue point different ways. No Chapter 3 scale rating is linked to the issue, and the scale would be context only. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0171;MKD-EU-2021-001-0126;MKD-EU-2022-001-0073;MKD-EU-2022-001-0077;MKD-EU-2023-001-0103;MKD-EU-2024-001-0071;MKD-EU-2024-001-0073;MKD-EU-2025-003-0123;MKD-EU-2025-003-0120;MKD-EU-2025-001-0112
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). A contact point platform exists and is proposed as implemented, but the Commission finds it not fully in line with the acquis and the list and register still absent; these statuses are themselves proposals. Sources conflict on which body is the contact point (Central Register or uslugi.gov.mk), and the 238 (2023) and 124 (2024) service counts are not explained. What remains is full online information and procedures under the Services Directive, especially on licences, and a transparent electronic register of all regulated professions. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0124;MKD-EU-2025-003-0121;MKD-EU-2025-003-0123;MKD-EU-2025-001-0113;MKD-EU-2025-001-0206;MKD-EU-2025-003-0120;MKD-EU-2023-001-0104;MKD-EU-2024-001-0072
- priority: Medium (validated). The point of single contact was a key recommendation only in 2021, 2022 and 2023, with the 2021 item flagged as doubtful for the theme, and later Commission items on it and the 2025 item on the register of professions are in body text, so the requirement is repeated. It is a sector portal under the Services Directive and is not central to the digital government theme, which is the same reading applied to taxpayer e-services (RI-TEC-005) and e-procurement (RI-TEC-006). Its importance to Chapter 3 is not the test for a technology issue, and no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition names it. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0124;MKD-EU-2022-001-0074;MKD-EU-2023-001-0102;MKD-EU-2024-001-0071;MKD-EU-2025-001-0113;MKD-EU-2025-001-0206;MKD-EU-2025-003-0125
- tractability: Medium (validated). The platform is in place and service numbers have been added, which favours progress on the contact point. Against that, sectoral laws must be aligned, the responsible body is unclear between sources, and a register of all professions needs input from many regulators. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0112;MKD-EU-2023-001-0102;MKD-EU-2025-003-0121;MKD-EU-2025-003-0123;MKD-EU-2025-001-0206

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0053 Law on Central Registry: adopted. North Macedonia cites the law at the screening as part of the framework for companies. There is no independent evidence of application in these items.
- INT-MKD-0059 Law no. 215/2021 One Stop Shop: adopted. North Macedonia cites Law no. 215/2021 at the screening. It is one reported mention, with no evidence of application.
- INT-MKD-0126 Point of Single Contact: implemented. The Commission reports the platform upgraded and reaching 312 online services by September 2025. It gives no evidence of use, and it asked as late as 2023 for upgrading to meet the Services Directive, so I do not rate it in practice.
- INT-MKD-0225 2024-2025 action plan on the Services Directive: insufficient_evidence. The only item is a Commission request of November 2023 to adopt the plan, whose period has now ended. No later item says whether it was adopted or carried out.
- INT-MKD-0305 New portal of the Central Register: implemented. The screening report says the new portal has simplified procedures, on the country's account. The Commission separately describes online Central Registry filing, but not this portal by name, so in_practice is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0328 Upgrade of the e-services portal for issuing licences to business activities: insufficient_evidence. The only item says in 2021 that this upgrade was underway. No later source says whether this specific upgrade was finished, so its status five years on cannot be determined.
- INT-MKD-0377 Central Register as Point of Single Contact: implemented. The screening report gives the country's account that the Central Register is the Point of Single Contact, while other Commission items name the uslugi.gov.mk platform and say it is not fully in line with the acquis. The sources differ on which body fills the role, though both show something in place. I have not resolved this, and it needs the analyst's view. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0305 leads to MKD-EU-2025-003-0122: success insufficient_evidence, attribution insufficient_evidence. The claim that the new Central Register portal simplified procedures comes only from the country presentation, and government reporting alone cannot show success. The Commission reports one-day online registration without naming this portal, and also finds simplification underexploited for business registration, so independent evidence does not settle it.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-GOV-2024-001-0092 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0072: The Reform Agenda gives 264 e-services as the 2024 baseline, while the Commission report gives 124 services accessible online by September 2024.
- MKD-EU-2024-009-0014 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0072: Commission documents of June and October 2024 give 246 services available online and 124 services accessible online by September 2024.
- MKD-EU-2025-003-0121 vs MKD-EU-2025-003-0123: In the same screening report North Macedonia says the Central Register operates as the Point of Single Contact, while the Commission identifies the Point of Single Contact as www.uslugi.gov.mk.
