# Fact pack RI-TEC-036: National e-services portal limited in range and use

The national e-services portal offers too few transactional services, is little used, or does not serve all users.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). From 2020 to 2026 the Commission repeats the same shortfall: too few fully transactional services, low use and below-expectation results, ending with 'quite underdeveloped' in 2026, and the world index rank falls from 72 to 80 to 84 (the last from a machine-translated strategy table, capped at Medium confidence). The 2025 report does record a real change, with most-demanded services and some certificates now online, so the case for 'improving' is open but rests on one report that the 2026 finding does not bear out. The Chapter 10 scale (Some, Limited, Limited, Some progress in 2020, 2023, 2024, 2025) is context only and was not decisive, and service counts were not used for trend because their definitions differ. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0015;MKD-EU-2020-001-0103;MKD-EU-2021-001-0011;MKD-EU-2022-001-0015;MKD-EU-2022-001-0046;MKD-EU-2023-001-0012;MKD-EU-2023-001-0115;MKD-EU-2024-001-0012;MKD-EU-2024-001-0022;MKD-EU-2025-001-0020;MKD-EU-2025-001-0135;MKD-EU-2025-001-0136;MKD-EU-2026-003-0007;MKD-IO-2022-002-0059;MKD-IO-2024-001-0034;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0092
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The portal is proposed as in practice and its upgrade as implemented, while the Reform Agenda reform is proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals, and the upgrade status sits uneasily with the Commission's 2024 note that the 135 services were not yet configured. Progress against the 50% target cannot be fixed, because the 264 baseline conflicts with the Commission's 392 and 124 figures, and 246 also conflicts with 124, so sources are cited both ways. What remains is a much wider set of fully transactional, commonly requested services that people actually use, on an agreed count, and equal access, where the Commission's statement that the portal caters for special needs conflicts with the audit's finding (machine translated, capped at Medium confidence) that persons with disabilities cannot use e-services. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0015;MKD-EU-2026-003-0007;MKD-EU-2025-001-0135;MKD-EU-2024-001-0023;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0092;MKD-EU-2024-008-0015;MKD-EU-2024-001-0072;MKD-EU-2024-009-0014;MKD-EU-2024-001-0092;MKD-EU-2024-001-0020;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0136
- priority: High (validated). The e-services increase is a Reform Agenda payment condition, and widening the portal's services is a recommendation repeated across three or more reports, including 'treat as a priority' in the 2025 screening. It is central to the digital government theme and Chapter 10. The rating rests on these, not on the large number of national audit and strategy items. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0015;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0017;MKD-EU-2022-001-0045;MKD-EU-2023-003-0037;MKD-EU-2023-007-0003;MKD-EU-2024-009-0011;MKD-EU-2025-004-0103
- tractability: Medium (validated). A single platform, EU funding and a dated payment step favour progress, and the Commission reports new services in 2025. Against that, the Commission's own wording shows the 135-service upgrade slipping for years and fragmented ownership, and the State Audit Office (machine translated, capped at Medium confidence) reports about 140 laws unaligned and 101 of 242 posts unfilled, which means many institutions must act. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0135;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0017;MKD-EU-2024-001-0023;MKD-EU-2023-003-0045;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0002;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0076

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0036 Law on the Central Population Register: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019, and the audit and government sources describe an operating register that is used as a basis for electronic identification. The evidence of use is descriptive and mostly from government, and the 2025 strategy says the data set must be expanded, so I stop short of in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0038 2019 digitalisation law package: implemented. The package was adopted in 2019 and measures followed, including the portal and the coordination groups. The State Audit Office found about 140 laws still not aligned in March 2024, long after the deadline of 25 August 2020, so implementation is partial and the 2025 strategy repeats that finding.
- INT-MKD-0047 Law on Archival Material and Archival Activity 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms adoption on 27 June 2025 and publication on 4 July 2025, and treats the central document management system as still to be completed. A digital strategy row on preparing a new law is a plan row and does not contradict adoption. The link between the earlier Reform Agenda items and this law is inferred, as the entity note says.
- INT-MKD-0052 Law on General Administrative Procedures: adopted. The law has been in force for about nine years, but the Commission finds it is not systematically implemented and the Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio. These findings are preferred to a claim of application, so nothing higher than adopted is supported, although some partial application is likely.
- INT-MKD-0078 National long-term ICT Strategy (unadopted drafts): insufficient_evidence. The drafts themselves were never adopted, but the Commission reported in November 2025 that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan had been adopted, recorded under SMART/MK: 2030 (INT-MKD-0079). No source says whether these drafts became that strategy or were withdrawn, so the analyst should decide whether to merge the entities.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: adopted. The Commission reports in November 2025 that the country adopted a long-term ICT strategy and action plan, and the strategy text says some activities began before formal adoption. The goals in the text are targets, so they do not show delivery. The link between the Commission's sentence and SMART/MK is inferred from timing and is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0085 Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028: adopted. The Government adopted the strategy on 21 January 2025, which the Commission confirms; a February 2025 Commission item still planning adoption is out of date. The only item saying more is the Agency for Electronic Communications' one-line status that the strategy 'is applied', which is government reporting without specifics, comes from a body other than the lead ministry, and which I have not accepted, so the reviewer should decide. The new cyber security law is one delivered measure, but no item reports wider delivery or the half-yearly reports the strategy promises. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0088 Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030: adopted. The State Audit Office records the strategy and action plan as adopted. The measures in these items are stated as aims, and no item shows them carried out as a whole.
- INT-MKD-0089 Public Administration Reform Strategy and Action Plan 2018-2022: implemented. The strategy's period ended in 2022, and a government source credits it with the 2019 digitalisation laws. The Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030 followed, but no item says it formally replaced this one, so I do not use reversed.
- INT-MKD-0098 Reform Agenda of North Macedonia: implemented. The Commission assessed the Reform Agenda steps in October 2025 and found four of sixteen achieved, and the 2026 assessment reports on-site checks of delivered equipment. Delivery is real but partial, and the Commission's findings are preferred to the Agenda's own text.
- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0110 Digital identity (eID): in_practice. The State Audit Office, reporting after its audit, describes eID as issued on registration and used to log in to the national portal and other portals. It also found that some portals require separate profiles and that high-level eID conditions did not raise use, so use is real but limited.
- INT-MKD-0119 National Council for Digital Transformation: unclear. The Council was established in September 2022, and the 2025 strategy describes it as formed to plan and coordinate digitalisation. The State Audit Office could not confirm any activities in 2023 and reports an absence of activities and results, so a named body exists but is said not to function. I give the audit more weight than the government's description, and there is no later evidence of activity. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0123 National Broadband Competence Office: in_practice. The office was set up in 2019, and the Commission says it publishes biannual reports on broadband development, which is specific evidence of it working.
- INT-MKD-0124 National e-Services Portal (uslugi.gov.mk): in_practice. The State Audit Office, an independent source, confirms the portal has been in use since 2019, but finds low use (0.556 services per registered user), and the 2025 strategy reports 314 services and about 13 of 100 adults registered. Use is real but limited. The Commission and the audit differ on access for people with disabilities, which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0125 Upgrade of the National e-Services Portal (EU-funded project): implemented. The Commission states the portal has been upgraded and its offer widened, though in 2024 it said only a few new services had been added against a target of 135 or 139. Earlier reports describe the work as both ongoing and done, and whether the target was met is not stated. The State Audit Office's low-use findings concern the portal as a whole, so use of the upgrade is not shown.
- INT-MKD-0127 Central Population Register: in_practice. The Commission reported the register operational in 2020 and used to crosscheck the 2021 census data, and the State Audit Office describes it as an integrated working database. Later government text that services will draw on it obligatorily shows full use is still to come.
- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0131 Moj Termin eHealth system: in_practice. The Commission says the system was introduced in 2013 and has reduced waiting times, the World Bank describes it in use for appointments, and a mobile application and new modules have been added. Both also report limits: referrals still on paper in 2024, and no interoperability with Health Insurance Fund data as of 2025. Those limits qualify the use but do not contradict it.
- INT-MKD-0141 Unified document management system: implemented. Commission technical checks confirm the system is partially operational in several institutions, and the government reports a pilot in 22 of about 140 central institutions using a small part of its potential. This is partial roll-out, so I do not rate it in practice for the unified system; the target of all central institutions is December 2026.
- INT-MKD-0164 E-Authorisation register: proposed. The latest source lists the register among applications developed or to be developed and describes it in the future tense. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0170 Digital public register of charges: proposed. The register is a Reform Agenda step with a methodology due by the end of 2025. No source reports the register or the methodology as delivered.
- INT-MKD-0171 Digitalisation of para-fiscal charges: implemented. The Commission's desk review in October 2025 used a list of 15 digitalised charges with online links, against a commitment covering all 28. Measures are therefore in place for part of the set, and no item shows their use.
- INT-MKD-0172 Government data centre: proposed. Every source describes the data centre as a future step, the latest being the October 2025 digital strategy, and the Reform Agenda step begins with a feasibility study. No source shows it built.
- INT-MKD-0202 Cross-border services under the Western Balkan initiative: proposed. The screening report said in 2023 that no cross-border services were available and that some were planned. There is no later evidence.
- INT-MKD-0243 National Strategy on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities 2023-2030: proposed. The 2023 strategy describes this strategy as in preparation. There is no later evidence, so adoption is not shown.
- INT-MKD-0250 Government conclusion on digitalising legal procedures (79th session, June 2021): adopted. The State Audit Office records the Conclusion of 15 June 2021 obliging ministries to prepare amendments within 60 days. A later audit found about 140 laws still not aligned, so delivery is not shown.
- INT-MKD-0268 Catalogue of Services: implemented. The catalogue was added to the e-services portal, but the State Audit Office found outdated user lists, only one training session and unpublished services, giving inaccurate and incomplete information. I rely on the audit, so this is not in_practice. The audit reports poor data and does not say that the system does not function, so unclear does not apply.
- INT-MKD-0273 Digital assistants: proposed. The strategy provides for digital assistants as a measure. No item shows them in place.
- INT-MKD-0277 E-Reminder: proposed. The digital strategy describes this tool in the future tense.
- INT-MKD-0288 Integration of separate portals into the National e-Services Portal (A 4.2.2.2): proposed. This is an action plan row in the 2023 strategy. The State Audit Office's 2024 recommendation shows access through the national portal was still to be arranged, and no later source reports completion.
- INT-MKD-0292 Lockdown moving permit e-service: implemented. The Commission reports that the service was offered during the COVID-19 curfew in 2020. It was tied to the curfew, and no source says whether it continued or was withdrawn.
- INT-MKD-0317 Single Sign-On System: unclear. The State Audit Office describes registration and eID on the national portal, but finds that citizens' access to e-services does not go through the Single Sign-On System and recommends using it. A named system exists but is said not to work as intended, and there is no evidence after March 2024.
- INT-MKD-0319 Super App: insufficient_evidence. The strategy lists the Super App among applications developed or to be developed, without saying which applies to it. It does not establish whether the app exists.
- INT-MKD-0328 Upgrade of the e-services portal for issuing licences to business activities: insufficient_evidence. The only item says in 2021 that this upgrade was underway. No later source says whether this specific upgrade was finished, so its status five years on cannot be determined.
- INT-MKD-0352 Analyses on digitalising municipalities: implemented. The PAR Strategy says the analyses were prepared with UNDP support. The status covers preparing the analyses, not any action on them, and it is from the government only.
- INT-MKD-0355 DigInfo centres: proposed. The Ministry says it will carry out an analysis for establishing these centres. No source shows that this happened.

## Outcomes


## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-GOV-2024-001-0092 vs MKD-EU-2024-008-0015: For the same Reform Agenda target on e-services in October 2024, the Agenda gives a 2024 baseline of 264 while the Commission assessment gives a current figure of 392.
- MKD-GOV-2024-001-0092 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0072: The Reform Agenda gives 264 e-services as the 2024 baseline, while the Commission report gives 124 services accessible online by September 2024.
- MKD-EU-2024-009-0014 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0072: Commission documents of June and October 2024 give 246 services available online and 124 services accessible online by September 2024.
- MKD-EU-2024-001-0088 vs MKD-GOV-2024-007-0055: The Commission states in 2024 that most administrative services are offered online and offline, while the State Audit Office in 2024 records 1,120 services available only in person.
- MKD-EU-2023-003-0032 vs MKD-GOV-2024-007-0026: North Macedonia told the 2023 screening that the platform allows access to people with disabilities, while the State Audit Office finds that visually impaired persons cannot use the portal e-services.
- MKD-EU-2024-001-0020 vs MKD-GOV-2024-007-0136: The Commission states in 2024 that the portal caters to people with special needs, while the State Audit Office concludes in 2024 that persons with disabilities cannot use e-services.
- MKD-EU-2023-001-0138 vs MKD-GOV-2023-002-0016: The 2023 report says eIDAS alignment was further delayed, while the 2023 public administration reform strategy says the 2019 law on electronic documents, identification and trust services is aligned with European legislation on digitalisation.
