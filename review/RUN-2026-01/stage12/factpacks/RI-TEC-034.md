# Fact pack RI-TEC-034: Digital single market rules not aligned

Legislation is not aligned with EU rules on online platforms and services, such as the Digital Services Act, the Digital Markets Act, the platform-to-business and geo-blocking regulations, and e-commerce is developing slowly.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The ask to align with the Digital Services Act and Digital Markets Act recurs with little change in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, and e-commerce was described as slow in 2021, 2022 and 2023. The chapter scale is context only and was not decisive. Growth figures for online transactions are country-reported, partly repeated and on differing bases, so they were not read as closing the issue. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0139;MKD-EU-2024-001-0092;MKD-EU-2025-001-0138;MKD-EU-2021-001-0108;MKD-EU-2022-006-0028;MKD-EU-2023-006-0029;MKD-EU-2021-001-0107;MKD-EU-2022-006-0027
- priority: Medium (validated). The requirement is repeated in three reports and relates to binding acquis, but it sits in Chapter 10 body text, not in the 2024 or 2025 key recommendations, and is not named as a benchmark or payment condition in the input. It is therefore a repeated EU requirement that is not central under the criteria. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0139;MKD-EU-2024-001-0092;MKD-EU-2025-001-0138;MKD-EU-2024-001-0015;MKD-EU-2025-001-0022
- tractability: Medium (validated). The work is mainly legislative with EU texts as templates, a government timetable and a request for expert group participation, which depends partly on the Commission. No draft is reported and past progress is limited to training, and applying platform rules will need capacity that the input does not evidence. Items: MKD-EU-2025-004-0028;MKD-EU-2025-004-0030;MKD-EU-2025-004-0101;MKD-EU-2025-004-0108;MKD-EU-2024-001-0094
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2026 report still finds the country only partially aligned on digital services and asks for regulation of online platforms in line with the Digital Services Act and the Digital Markets Act, with digital services now named in the Chapter 10 key recommendation, and it adds a requirement to build the institutional capacity and legal basis to enforce both acts from accession, as set out at the 2026 TAIEX workshops. The responding alignment measures have national targets of 2027 and 2028, only training has been delivered and no 2026 item for the country reports a draft. What remains is the alignment itself for these acts and the Geo-blocking Regulation, full alignment on electronic commerce, and the enforcement capacity and legal basis; the country's report of being largely aligned with the e-Commerce Directive and the Commission's call to align fully differ in degree and speaker and were not treated as closure. Items: MKD-EU-2026-007-0097;MKD-EU-2026-007-0102;MKD-EU-2026-007-0103;MKD-EU-2026-007-0015;MKD-EU-2025-001-0138;MKD-EU-2025-004-0065;MKD-EU-2025-004-0028;MKD-EU-2025-004-0030;MKD-EU-2025-004-0032;MKD-EU-2024-001-0094;MKD-EU-2025-004-0027

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0018 Strategy for the Implementation of the Right to Personal Data Protection 2025-2030: implemented. The Agency reports the strategy was adopted in 2024 and implementation began in 2025. The Commission's finding that budget and consultation problems negatively affected implementation is preferred to the Agency's account on how far it has gone, but it also treats implementation as under way.
- INT-MKD-0185 Accelerated integration: DMA, DSA, P2B and geo-blocking groups: proposed. North Macedonia requested participation and the Commission said it would further explore it. No item shows participation agreed.
- INT-MKD-0194 Alignment with the Digital Services Act and Digital Markets Act: proposed. North Macedonia says it is not aligned and plans to align by 2028, and the Commission confirms in November 2025 that it still needs to do so.
- INT-MKD-0229 Employment Action Plan 2021-2023: insufficient_evidence. The items are only rows of the 2021-2023 plan, each worded as an action to be done, and the plan's period has ended. No item states that the plan was adopted or says what was delivered.
- INT-MKD-0427 Alignment with eIDAS 2.0, the Digital Services Act and the Digital Markets Act: proposed. The 2025 strategy states this as a plan. The eIDAS 2.0 part has since been legislated, but the Commission says alignment with it is not full and the Digital Services Act and Digital Markets Act are still to be aligned.
- INT-MKD-0428 Alignment with the Geo-blocking Regulation (EU) 2018/302: proposed. The screening report says the authorities aim to align by 2027, and no item shows alignment done.
- INT-MKD-0430 Alignment with the P2B Regulation (EU) 2019/1150: proposed. The screening report says the authorities plan alignment by 2027. No source shows an adopted text.
- INT-MKD-0472 Harmonisation of legislation in the context of digitisation processes: proposed. The Personal Data Protection Strategy sets this as a task and an indicator from 2025, and the Agency was still calling for transposition in 2026. No source reports laws adopted for this purpose.
- INT-MKD-0530 Towards a Feasible Implementation of the Digital Services Act in the Western Balkans (study): implemented. The civil society organisation reports that it carried out this research in March and April 2024. This is its own report on a study, not a policy measure.
- INT-MKD-0531 Trainings from European services on the DSA: implemented. The Commission states that authorities received these trainings in June 2024. No source reports what followed.
- INT-MKD-0533 Transposition of acts resulting from the EU digital strategy: proposed. The Agency lists promoting transposition as an aim in its strategy and again in its 2026 report. No transposing act is reported.
