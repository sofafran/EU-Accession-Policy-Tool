# Fact pack RI-FMW-009: National social security law not aligned with the coordination regulations

National pension, health and other social security laws are only partly aligned with the EU coordination regulations, for example through citizenship conditions, and the administrative measures for applying them at accession are not identified or in place.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was context only, and the 2025 ratings have nothing earlier to compare with. The recommendation on administrative measures recurs from 2020 to 2025, and the shift from identifying measures to putting them in place reflects wording, not a reported step. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0077;MKD-EU-2021-001-0061;MKD-EU-2022-001-0064;MKD-EU-2023-001-0091;MKD-EU-2024-001-0009;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014;MKD-EU-2025-003-0090;MKD-EU-2025-003-0104
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). What remains is removing citizenship conditions from the pension and health insurance laws, aligning with the regulations and putting administrative measures in place by accession. The Commission finds the framework partly aligned, but the plan to amend the legislation is only proposed within five years (a status that is itself a proposal). Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0093;MKD-EU-2025-003-0104;MKD-EU-2025-003-0094;MKD-EU-2025-003-0107;MKD-EU-2025-003-0097;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014
- priority: High (validated). Administrative measures for coordination are in the key recommendation in each report from 2020 to 2025, and alignment with the regulations is central to this strand of the chapter. The screening report requires alignment, including on nationality conditions, by accession. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0077;MKD-EU-2021-001-0061;MKD-EU-2022-001-0064;MKD-EU-2023-001-0091;MKD-EU-2024-001-0009;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014;MKD-EU-2025-003-0107
- tractability: Medium (validated). The named legal changes are within national control, and the register law adopted in 2023 shows some related legislative activity. However, it spans the pension and health systems, needs prior analysis and has a plan of up to five years. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0094;MKD-EU-2025-003-0097;MKD-EU-2024-001-0067

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0055 Law on the register of mandatory social insurance: adopted. The Commission reports the law adopted in October 2023. No item shows the register established.
- INT-MKD-0060 Law on Employment and Insurance in Case of Unemployment: adopted. The screening report describes the law's content from the country's own presentation. It says nothing about how it is carried out.
- INT-MKD-0064 Law on Health Insurance: adopted. The Commission describes the law as in place and says its citizenship conditions will need amending. The only description of how it works comes from the country presentation, so there is no specific evidence on its application.
- INT-MKD-0068 Law on Pension and Disability Insurance: adopted. The Commission treats the law as in force, with citizenship conditions that will need amending. The items concern only that legal gap, so they say nothing about how it is carried out.
- INT-MKD-0509 Preparations for EESSI access points: proposed. The country plans to define the access points within the coming five years. The Commission still asks in 2025 for measures to be put in place for electronic exchange.
- INT-MKD-0547 Agreement on the regular exchange of information between the Pension and Disability Insurance Fund and the Austrian pension insurance authority: adopted. The Commission reports that the agreement was signed in June 2026. No item reports information exchanged under it.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0198 leads to MKD-EU-2025-003-0090: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission itself rates preparation on coordination as good and gives the experience of the agreements as the reason, so this is not government reporting alone. The result is narrow: it concerns readiness, and the same report still finds the law only partly aligned.
