# Fact pack RI-FMW-006: Alignment with the European Labour Authority regulation

National law is not aligned with the regulation establishing the European Labour Authority, including the arrangements for cooperating with it.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was context only. The composite recommendation naming the European Labour Authority is nearly unchanged in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, and the only direct findings, in the screening report, say national law is not aligned. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0007;MKD-EU-2025-001-0012;MKD-EU-2025-003-0076;MKD-EU-2025-003-0079
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is aligning national law with the regulation and preparing the State Labour Inspectorate for coordinated tasks. Both responses are only proposed for the medium term, and the implemented training does not align the law; these statuses are proposals. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0076;MKD-EU-2025-003-0079;MKD-EU-2025-003-0077;MKD-EU-2025-003-0078;MKD-EU-2023-001-0096
- priority: Medium (validated). The authority is named in a key recommendation in three reports, but only as part of a sentence mainly about the employment service. The direct support is one source and the issue is not central to the chapter. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0007;MKD-EU-2025-001-0012;MKD-EU-2025-003-0079
- tractability: Medium (validated). Harmonisation is within national control and plans exist, but the plans only run in the medium term, with full implementation at accession. They also need institutional and procedural preparation by the labour inspectorate as well as legal change. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0077;MKD-EU-2025-003-0078;MKD-EU-2025-003-0080

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0200 Capacity building on EURES and the European Labour Authority: implemented. The Commission reports that capacity building for civil servants on EURES and the European Labour Authority took place. No source shows the knowledge being used, and the Commission says EURES amendments would be implemented only on accession.
- INT-MKD-0384 Preparations of the State Labour Inspectorate for European Labour Authority tasks: proposed. The Commission's screening report gives these as mid-term plans. No item shows the preparations carried out.
- INT-MKD-0473 Harmonisation with the regulation establishing the European Labour Authority: proposed. The country states medium-term plans to harmonise, with full implementation upon accession, and the Commission still asks for further alignment in November 2025.
