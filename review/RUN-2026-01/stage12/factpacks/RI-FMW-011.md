# Fact pack RI-FMW-011: Access to healthcare for EU citizens through the European Health Insurance Card

EU citizens can use the European Health Insurance Card or access medical services only where a bilateral arrangement exists, so access is not yet available to citizens of all Member States.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was context only. Nine agreements or nine Member States are recorded in each report from 2020 to 2024, and no development in 2024 and 2025. The 14 Member States in the 2025 report is a noted difference of scope, not growth, because the five added have agreements that do not include EHIC. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0087;MKD-EU-2021-001-0071;MKD-EU-2022-001-0072;MKD-EU-2023-001-0100;MKD-EU-2024-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0108;MKD-EU-2025-001-0109;MKD-EU-2025-003-0198
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). What remains is access through EHIC for citizens of the other Member States. Use with nine Member States is proposed as in practice (a status that is itself a proposal), and the Commission finds technical alignment satisfactory, but the card's use rests on separate arrangements and not on general entitlement. Items: MKD-EU-2024-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-003-0100;MKD-EU-2025-003-0102;MKD-EU-2025-001-0108
- priority: Medium (validated). This is a repeated finding across six reports, but the EHIC is not named in the key recommendation, which refers only to healthcare insurance agreements. The Commission treats technical alignment as satisfactory and it overlaps with RI-FMW-008. Items: MKD-EU-2024-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0108;MKD-EU-2025-003-0102;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014
- tractability: Medium (validated). The sources describe the basis differently: the country says unilateral use and the Commission says bilateral agreements, which matters because the first would be within national control and the second depends on others. The country reports that agreements could soon be reached with three more states, but no new agreement or development has been recorded. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0100;MKD-EU-2025-003-0102;MKD-EU-2025-001-0108

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0197 European Health Insurance Card (EHIC): in_practice. The Commission states in 2022 and 2023 that the card can be used with nine Member States and is recognised by contracted healthcare institutions, and the 2025 screening report repeats the nine countries. Use is on a unilateral basis and limited to those states.
- INT-MKD-0198 Bilateral social security agreements: implemented. The ministry's list states that the agreements are in force and applied, and the screening report repeats this from the country's own presentation. This is government reporting alone, so I do not use the stated in_practice.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0198 leads to MKD-EU-2025-003-0102: success mixed, attribution Medium. The Commission finds technical alignment satisfactory and says the agreements allow EHIC use, but only nine Member States are covered and the country reports that data cannot be processed electronically. The Commission asks for stepped-up capacity to be ready at accession.
