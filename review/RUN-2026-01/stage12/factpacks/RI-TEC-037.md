# Fact pack RI-TEC-037: Sector and local online public services limited

Online public services run by sector bodies or municipalities, outside the national portal, are limited in range, use or quality.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). No repeated Commission wording or comparable series exists: EU items are isolated additions (2024, 2025), and the Chapter 10 scale is context only and was not decisive. The other signals are partial and mixed, since e-Rabota's share of employer registrations rose from 60% to 69% but fell from 67% to 62% for agencies in government reports, and a civil society study finds general slow progress at municipal level. Government and civil society items here are Low or Medium confidence, and the Macedonian ones are machine translated. Items: MKD-EU-2024-001-0113;MKD-EU-2025-001-0075;MKD-GOV-2022-002-0010;MKD-GOV-2025-003-0009;MKD-GOV-2022-002-0011;MKD-GOV-2025-003-0010;MKD-CSO-2025-001-0003
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Sector services exist, and One Stop Shops are proposed as implemented, while the analysis on e-services there, the mobile offices and the employment plan are proposed or at insufficient evidence; these statuses are themselves proposals, and the national items are machine translated, capped at Medium confidence. The Commission's requirement is general, not specific. What remains is a wider range and use of sector and local services, including municipal ones, with published statistics on delivery. Items: MKD-EU-2025-004-0132;MKD-EU-2025-001-0156;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0089;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0027;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0083;MKD-CSO-2025-001-0003
- priority: Low (validated). The Commission mentions sector services only briefly, and most of the concern is raised by the national audit (machine translated, capped at Medium confidence), national plans and civil society, so the issue meets the test of being mainly raised by others. The Reform Agenda target falls on the national portal under the G5 split, and item volume was not counted. Items: MKD-EU-2025-004-0132;MKD-EU-2025-001-0156;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0014;MKD-CSO-2025-001-0002
- tractability: Medium (validated). Individual sector bodies can and do make progress, as e-Rabota and the Cadastre Office show. Across many institutions and municipalities, however, the audit and ICT strategy (machine translated, capped at Medium confidence) describe parallel systems built without clear guidance, and no single actor has control. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0076;MKD-GOV-2025-003-0009;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0090;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0080;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0199

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0002 Law Amending and Supplementing the Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners 2021: adopted. The Assembly adopted the amendments on 15 July 2021 and the 2025 amending law cites them as part of the law in force. Commission reports on work permits describe the parent law, and no source shows these amendments' registration procedures in use, so nothing beyond adoption is shown.
- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0110 Digital identity (eID): in_practice. The State Audit Office, reporting after its audit, describes eID as issued on registration and used to log in to the national portal and other portals. It also found that some portals require separate profiles and that high-level eID conditions did not raise use, so use is real but limited.
- INT-MKD-0118 National ICT council: insufficient_evidence. The only evidence is two passages in one ITU profile of February 2022, describing a council monitoring projects. Sources do not say whether it is the National Cyber Security Council, and no later item mentions it. The difference with the audit's absent body was noted at G5 as different objects, so it is not treated as a conflict here.
- INT-MKD-0124 National e-Services Portal (uslugi.gov.mk): in_practice. The State Audit Office, an independent source, confirms the portal has been in use since 2019, but finds low use (0.556 services per registered user), and the 2025 strategy reports 314 services and about 13 of 100 adults registered. Use is real but limited. The Commission and the audit differ on access for people with disabilities, which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0144 Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS): proposed. North Macedonia reported that documentation had been prepared in 2023, and the Reform Agenda calls the IFMIS reform ongoing in 2024. No item shows the system in operation.
- INT-MKD-0145 Integrated tax information system (ITIS): proposed. The 2022-2024 Economic Reform Programme foresaw the system and the Reform Agenda calls the work ongoing, while the Commission in 2025 still asks for a new integrated system. The World Bank wording suggests an ITIS already exists to be expanded; I follow the Commission and flag the difference. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0156 Open Finance Portal: implemented. The Commission reports that the portal went live and gives the public transaction data, and the ITU profile lists it. No item measures use, and the last evidence is from 2022.
- INT-MKD-0157 National open data portal (data.gov.mk): unclear. The country's presentation to the Commission in February 2025 describes the portal as in line with best practice, but the State Audit Office says it has not functioned since the beginning of 2025 because of procurement problems. The audit is given more weight because it is specific and independent. No later source says it was restored. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0159 Employment Service Agency web portal: implemented. An international report says the IT system allowed online delivery of services, and the Agency and the screening report describe the portal. None of these gives independent evidence of how it is used, so the stated in_practice is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0201 One Stop Shop offices: implemented. The State Audit Office confirms five offices were set up, so measures are in place. The same audit finds some offices outside Skopje not used effectively, so in_practice across the network is not shown.
- INT-MKD-0229 Employment Action Plan 2021-2023: insufficient_evidence. The items are only rows of the 2021-2023 plan, each worded as an action to be done, and the plan's period has ended. No item states that the plan was adopted or says what was delivered.
- INT-MKD-0231 One Stop Shop e-services analysis and action plan (A 4.2.3.4): proposed. This is an action plan row in the 2023 strategy. No later source reports it as carried out.
- INT-MKD-0233 Youth Guarantee implementation plan: insufficient_evidence. The 2021 employment strategy mentions the plan only as listing capacity measures. No source states its formal status or later delivery.
- INT-MKD-0242 National Strategy for e-Services at the Local Level (proposed): proposed. A civil society organisation proposed this strategy for the Government's 2025 work programme. No source shows the Government taking it up.
- INT-MKD-0263 Agency electronic services for submitting requests and delivering documents: implemented. The Agency reports in two successive years that it offers these services and is encouraging their use. This is its own reporting and gives no use figures, so in_practice is not shown.
- INT-MKD-0280 EXIM system: implemented. The screening report describes the system on the country's account. That is not independent evidence of use, so the stated in_practice is lowered.
- INT-MKD-0296 Moj Red: implemented. The public administration strategy reports over 1,000 citizens using the platform in the second half of 2022. That is specific but is government reporting alone, so it does not reach in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0304 New digital services of the Cadastre Office: implemented. The Commission says the Cadastre Office launched new digital services and reduced unresolved cases by 20%. It does not measure use of the services directly, so I stop at implemented, and the fall in cases is not assessed here as a result of them.
- INT-MKD-0308 One Stop Shop information portal: implemented. The State Audit Office reports that a separate portal was set up. It is one statement with no usage data.
- INT-MKD-0311 Online registration of asylum applications: insufficient_evidence. The only item is a 2022 reported statement about the Covid-19 emergency, flagged as a possible misread. It does not show whether online registration continues.
- INT-MKD-0331 Zemi Brojche: implemented. The PAR Strategy reports over 1,000 citizens using the booking platform in late 2022. That is specific but comes only from the government, so it does not meet the test for in practice.
- INT-MKD-0333 e-Rabota electronic system: implemented. The Agency's annual reports describe e-Rabota services and report 128,468 registrations through it in 2024, and the Commission records a deployed upgrade in 2026. The stated in_practice status rests on the Agency's own reporting, which is not enough without independent evidence of use.
- INT-MKD-0337 ePension Portal: implemented. The World Bank report lists the portal among initiatives launched by 2022. It gives no usage data and there is no later evidence.
- INT-MKD-0338 ePersonal Income Tax Portal: implemented. The source lists the portal among initiatives launched by 2022. It gives no use figures.
- INT-MKD-0341 gradezna-dozvola.mk building permit portal: insufficient_evidence. The portal is named only as an example within a sentence about an energy transition roadmap. The item does not state its status.
- INT-MKD-0348 Electronic services of the energy regulator, pharmaceutical chamber and pension insurance fund: implemented. The Commission reports these electronic services were developed. It does not report their use.
- INT-MKD-0352 Analyses on digitalising municipalities: implemented. The PAR Strategy says the analyses were prepared with UNDP support. The status covers preparing the analyses, not any action on them, and it is from the government only.
- INT-MKD-0463 Energy transition roadmap: proposed. The screening report says the roadmap is being crafted with EU support, and no source shows it completed.
- INT-MKD-0501 Mobile one-stop shops: proposed. The country told the Commission in 2023 that it is discussing this option. No later source mentions it.
- INT-MKD-0534 Two e-portals for requesters and holders of public information: implemented. The public administration strategy says the Agency implemented the two portals and will promote them. This is government reporting alone, with no usage data.
- INT-MKD-0539 Digital Agenda Observatory (proposed): proposed. A civil society group proposed this to the Government, and no item shows the Government taking it up.
- INT-MKD-0550 Digitalised procedure for nostrification of foreign educational diplomas: implemented. The Commission reports that the Ministry of Education and Science fully digitalised the procedure for nostrification of foreign diplomas. No item reports its use.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0304 leads to MKD-EU-2025-001-0076: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission reports measured results: unresolved cases down 20% compared with 2024 and the backlog down 10%. This is one period only, and the figures' origin is not stated in the sentence.
