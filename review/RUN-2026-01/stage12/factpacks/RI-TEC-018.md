# Fact pack RI-TEC-018: Data protection authority lacks independence and resources

The data protection supervisory authority depends on government for staffing and funding, has capacity below its tasks, and can be left without a director.

## Validated issue judgements

- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The amendments meant to secure the Agency's autonomy are proposed only, and the 2025-2030 strategy's proposed status of implemented means only that work has begun, with the Commission finding it was held back; these statuses are themselves proposals for G6. What remains is autonomy in hiring and spending, and funding and staff that match the Agency's tasks. Statements that the Agency is independent describe its legal status, while the Commission and the Agency's own reports describe its operating dependence, so this is a noted difference and not a conflict. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0067;MKD-EU-2025-001-0069;MKD-EU-2025-001-0211;MKD-EU-2024-001-0040;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0037;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0015;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0085;MKD-EU-2023-003-0065;MKD-EU-2023-001-0037
- priority: Medium (validated). The Commission has raised the Agency's dependence and capacity from 2021 to 2025 and an independent authority is central to the data theme, but the requirement appears only in body text, in 2023 and 2025 and in the 2025 communication in general terms. It is in no key recommendation, benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition, and the main findings do not mention it, so it is a central requirement with thin support, on the same footing as RI-TEC-019. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0032;MKD-EU-2022-001-0023;MKD-EU-2023-001-0028;MKD-EU-2024-001-0040;MKD-EU-2025-001-0067;MKD-EU-2025-001-0211;MKD-EU-2025-002-0002
- tractability: Medium (validated). Only a few domestic actors are needed, a draft has existed since April 2022 and the budget is small. Past progress is poor, however: the draft has not moved, the Ministry of Finance repeatedly refused funds or returned the employment plan, and the director post took most of 2025 to fill. The Agency's reports are machine translated and capped at Medium confidence, and they are a party's account of its own constraints. Items: MKD-GOV-2026-002-0032;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0037;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0083;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0017;MKD-GOV-2025-010-0012;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0016;MKD-EU-2025-001-0204
- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). From 2021 to 2025 the Commission recorded a worsening, from dependence on ministries to falling staff and no director in 2025, with reported staff of 21, 20, 18 and 17 at the end of 2025 (the last two the Agency's own, machine translated and capped at Medium), while the 2026 report finds that the Agency's capacities have not improved and that it remains critically understaffed and underfunded, but records no further fall and a director appointed in December 2025. Its count of seven staff directly enforcing the law measures a different group from the totals, so it is not read as a further decline, and the chapter scale was not decisive. The 2025 report's finding of no director since January 2025 conflicts on its face with the 2026 report's appointment in December 2025, and both are cited; the Agency dates the appointment to 5 December 2025, after the 2025 report, which would explain the difference, but the conflict is reported and not resolved. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0013 Draft Law amending the Law on Personal Data Protection: proposed. The Agency drafted the amendments in April 2022, and its 2026 report says the process of adopting them had still not started during 2025. No source reports adoption.
- INT-MKD-0017 Law transposing the EU Law Enforcement Directive (planned): proposed. The Agency's 2024 strategy calls for this law by early 2026, but its 2026 report says transposition had still not started during 2025. No source reports a law being adopted.
- INT-MKD-0018 Strategy for the Implementation of the Right to Personal Data Protection 2025-2030: implemented. The Agency reports the strategy was adopted in 2024 and implementation began in 2025. The Commission's finding that budget and consultation problems negatively affected implementation is preferred to the Agency's account on how far it has gone, but it also treats implementation as under way.
- INT-MKD-0418 Agency observer status at the European Data Protection Board: implemented. The Agency reports in three of its own documents that it holds observer status in the Board. No independent item confirms its participation, so government reporting alone does not support in practice.
- INT-MKD-0502 National Programme for the Adoption of the Acquis: implemented. The Agency reports activities it carries out under the programme, such as supervisions and by-laws. This is the Agency's own reporting and it covers one chapter only, so it does not support in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0508 Personal Data Protection Agency oversight of security services under the Law on Interception of Communications: implemented. North Macedonia reports that the Agency inspected the two security bodies in 2021-2022. This is specific, but it is reported by the country at screening with no independent confirmation, so I stop short of in practice.
- INT-MKD-0528 TAIEX support for the Strategy: implemented. The Agency says TAIEX supported the process. It is the Agency's own report and the item has an OCR flag.
