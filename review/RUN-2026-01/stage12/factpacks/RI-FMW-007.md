# Fact pack RI-FMW-007: Information and support for mobile workers on their rights

There is no designated body supporting EU mobile workers in their rights and the system for informing them of their rights is weak.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The chapter scale was context only, and the issue's EU items come from one report, the 2025 screening report, so there is no sequence to read. The 2026 items are regional, concern migrant workers generally and cannot alone support a finding about the country. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0029;MKD-EU-2025-003-0034;MKD-IO-2026-001-0016;MKD-IO-2026-001-0018
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is designating an institution to support EU mobile workers and improving the system that informs them of their rights. No responding intervention is linked, so there is no status to compare. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0029;MKD-EU-2025-003-0034
- priority: Low (validated). The requirement appears in one report, in repeated sentences, and is not named in a key recommendation. The regional items do not raise this, as they concern other workers. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0029;MKD-EU-2025-003-0034;MKD-IO-2026-001-0018
- tractability: High (validated). Designating an institution is a single national decision, with no dependence on others and few actors. There is no evidence of past progress, so this rests on the nature of the task and is less certain for the wider information system. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0034;MKD-EU-2025-003-0029
