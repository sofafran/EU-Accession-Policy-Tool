# Fact pack RI-TEC-029: Customs IT systems not aligned with EU electronic customs

National customs IT systems are not yet aligned with the EU electronic customs strategic plan, and upgrades such as the New Computerised Transit System phases, export and import control systems are delayed or understaffed.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). NCTS Phase 5 preparation started by 2021, delays were reported in 2023 and 2024, and the system launched in January 2025, with the Commission noting 'good progress' on IT systems in 2025. The Commission's recommendation to consolidate systems repeats each year, but the stated result changed. The sub-area wording matches this issue, yet the chapter scale was context only and not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0118;MKD-EU-2023-001-0171;MKD-EU-2024-001-0108;MKD-EU-2025-001-0031;MKD-EU-2025-001-0191;MKD-EU-2025-001-0033
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). NCTS Phase 5 is live, but the 2025 screening rates alignment between low and partial, and Phase 6, the export system and ICS2 are proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals. What remains is development of the remaining systems under the strategic plan, costed at about EUR 34.5 million, with full alignment planned for 2026 to 2030. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0031;MKD-EU-2025-004-0155;MKD-EU-2025-004-0160;MKD-EU-2025-004-0161;MKD-EU-2025-001-0034;MKD-EU-2024-007-0020;MKD-EU-2024-007-0021
- priority: High (validated). E-customs steps appear in the Reform Agenda with a stated payment provision for Phase 6, and the customs IT recommendation recurs from 2021 to 2025, in the main findings in 2024 and 2025. The Commission calls alignment on accession critical to EU financial interests and security, so the issue is central to Chapter 29. Some Reform Agenda items carry a theme_doubtful flag because their wording stresses efficiency, not connection to the EU. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0019;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0023;MKD-EU-2021-001-0116;MKD-EU-2023-001-0173;MKD-EU-2024-001-0019;MKD-EU-2025-001-0033;MKD-EU-2025-002-0010
- tractability: High (validated). One capable agency leads, and it has delivered NCTS Phase 5 after delay and kept Phase 4 running satisfactorily. The limits are IT staffing, the planned 15 experts not yet shown as hired, cost, and an EU-driven timetable to 2030. Items: MKD-EU-2024-001-0107;MKD-EU-2025-001-0031;MKD-EU-2025-001-0167;MKD-EU-2025-004-0161;MKD-EU-2025-004-0162

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0056 Law on Single Window for Trade (draft): proposed. An international organisation's 2024 report recommends adopting this draft law. No source reports its adoption.
- INT-MKD-0058 Customs Law: adopted. The screening report lists the Customs Law as part of the legislation in force, on the country's account. No source in this set reports specifically on its application.
- INT-MKD-0091 Customs ICT development strategy 2021-2025: implemented. The Commission reports the customs ICT strategy adopted in 2022 and states in each report from 2023 to 2025 that it was implemented. No source gives usage or results, and the systems it covers are tracked separately.
- INT-MKD-0104 Reform 4.1.6 Expand the use of E-Customs services: proposed. The items set out a Reform Agenda reform and describe what should follow. No item reports the expanded use of e-customs services under this reform.
- INT-MKD-0148 New Computerised Transit System (NCTS): in_practice. The Commission stated in 2021 that the system is operational and functioning without interruptions. This is specific, though it is the only evidence and it is five years old.
- INT-MKD-0149 NCTS Phase 4: reversed. The Commission reports that its successor, NCTS Phase 5 (INT-MKD-0150), was launched in January 2025. No source says Phase 4 was formally withdrawn, so reversed is used by analogy with a replaced law, and it does not imply a policy reversal.
- INT-MKD-0150 NCTS Phase 5: implemented. The Commission states that Phase 5 was completed and put into operation in January 2025. No source gives usage data, so in_practice is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0151 NCTS Phase 6: proposed. Phase 6 is a Reform Agenda step, and the Commission's November 2025 report still recommends upgrading to it. Phase 5 only went live in January 2025.
- INT-MKD-0152 Automated Export System: proposed. The new export system is a Reform Agenda step with a June 2027 target. No item reports it implemented.
- INT-MKD-0153 Import Control System 2 (ICS2): proposed. ICS2 appears only as a Reform Agenda step with a December 2027 target and is described in the future tense. No source reports that work is finished.
- INT-MKD-0154 Customs Declarations and Excises processing system: proposed. The Reform Agenda says the customs administration still has to establish this system, and no item shows it built or in development.
- INT-MKD-0281 Economic Operator Registration and Identification (EORI) system: implemented. The Commission reports that the pilot phase of the system was launched. A pilot in operation is partial roll-out, and no item reports its use.
- INT-MKD-0286 Integrated Tariff Environment (ITE): implemented. The Commission reports that maintenance and further development of the system were secured in 2025. It gives no usage evidence.
- INT-MKD-0302 National Single Window: proposed. In 2024 the World Bank was still recommending adoption of the draft law needed to allow the new system. No item shows the law adopted or the system built.
- INT-MKD-0323 Uniform User Management and Digital Signature (UUM&DS) system: implemented. The Commission reports that the pilot phase of the system was launched. A pilot in operation is partial roll-out, and no item reports its use.
- INT-MKD-0329 Upgrade of the national module of the IT system for management of economic operators (MEOS): proposed. The screening report gives an upgrade planned for 2024 and full alignment in 2028 to 2030. No item confirms the 2024 upgrade took place.
- INT-MKD-0370 Recruitment of 15 additional IT experts for the e-customs group: proposed. The Commission reports it as a plan to employ 15 more experts. No item shows the recruitment done.
- INT-MKD-0448 Customs IT development milestones 2024-2026 (NCTS phase five, Single Window System, Integrated Tariff Environment, Control and Investigation Management System): proposed. The screening report records these as planned milestones. Phase 5 was later delivered in January 2025, but the other milestones are not reported as met.
