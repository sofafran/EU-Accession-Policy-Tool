# Fact pack RI-FMW-001: Nationality discrimination in access to employment

The law governing access to the labour market does not give EU workers equal treatment with nationals, for example by treating them as any other foreigner or reserving private sector posts for nationals.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Sub-area wording on access to the labour market, which matches this issue, says no progress in 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024, and the 2025 report says there were no legislative developments. The 2025 screening finding of partial alignment is a first assessment of that kind and does not show a change. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0079;MKD-EU-2021-001-0062;MKD-EU-2022-001-0065;MKD-EU-2023-001-0092;MKD-EU-2024-001-0057;MKD-EU-2025-001-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0031
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is equal treatment of EU workers in the law on access to the labour market, including an end to posts in private companies reserved for nationals. The foreigners law is itself the barrier, the 2025 amendment only renames bodies and the further amendments are only proposed with unknown content (all statuses are proposals; the two government items are machine translated, so capped at Medium), while the country's general ban on nationality discrimination is its own account and does not close the gap. The Commission's statement that the law makes no EU distinction conflicts with the country's account on quotas, which is reported and partly explained under RI-FMW-002. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0013;MKD-EU-2025-003-0033;MKD-EU-2025-001-0097;MKD-EU-2025-001-0101;MKD-GOV-2025-001-0002;MKD-GOV-2026-001-0007;MKD-EU-2025-003-0019;MKD-EU-2025-003-0020;MKD-EU-2024-001-0058;MKD-EU-2025-003-0010
- priority: High (validated). A key recommendation on non-discrimination against EU workers is repeated in the 2020 to 2025 reports, and equal access is central to the chapter. No benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition under this issue was found in the evidence. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0076;MKD-EU-2021-001-0060;MKD-EU-2022-001-0063;MKD-EU-2023-001-0090;MKD-EU-2024-001-0008;MKD-EU-2025-001-0013;MKD-EU-2025-003-0033
- tractability: Medium (validated). The change is legal and within the government's control, and the same law was amended in 2021 and 2025. Against that, six reports record no alignment and the requirement runs to the time of accession, so there is no evidence of past progress. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0025;MKD-GOV-2021-001-0001;MKD-GOV-2025-001-0001

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0001 Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners: in_practice. The law was adopted in 2015 and remains in force as amended. The Commission states that the Agency issues work permits under its quotas, and the Agency's 2021 report gives the opinions issued, so the evidence is not only a description of the law.
- INT-MKD-0002 Law Amending and Supplementing the Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners 2021: adopted. The Assembly adopted the amendments on 15 July 2021 and the 2025 amending law cites them as part of the law in force. Commission reports on work permits describe the parent law, and no source shows these amendments' registration procedures in use, so nothing beyond adoption is shown.
- INT-MKD-0003 Law Amending the Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners 2025: adopted. The Assembly adopted the law on 22 April 2025, and it enters into force eight days after publication. Its provisions only rename ministries and bodies, and no item shows any further step.
- INT-MKD-0004 Proposed amendments on the employment of foreigners: proposed. The Employment Agency's reports for 2024 and 2025 both list these only as proposed amendments. The sources do not say whether this is the law adopted in April 2025, so no adoption is assumed.
- INT-MKD-0066 Law on Labour Relations: adopted. North Macedonia described the law at the screening as the general labour law containing a non-discrimination rule. No independent item shows how it is applied, so I stop at adopted.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2024-001-0058 vs MKD-EU-2025-003-0010: The Commission states that the law does not distinguish between EU citizens and other foreigners, while North Macedonia states that under the same law the quotas do not apply to EU citizens following a reciprocity agreement with the EU.
