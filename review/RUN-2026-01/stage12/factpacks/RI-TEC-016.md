# Fact pack RI-TEC-016: Open data and re-use of public sector information incomplete

Institutions do not regularly publish and update datasets on the open data portal, and legislation is only partly aligned with the Open Data Directive and related EU data rules.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Commission items ask for regular data updates in 2020 and more proactive disclosure in 2022, while amendments for Open Data Directive alignment were in preparation in 2023 and still with a working group in 2025; the 2020 sub-area rating of some progress concerns transparency more widely and, like the chapter scale, was not decisive. The State Audit Office reports that the portal has not functioned since early 2025, in machine-translated text capped at Medium confidence, and the country's published screening presentation describes the portal favourably without addressing this. That outage rests on one source with no later status, so it does not yet support worsening, though confirmation would. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0001;MKD-EU-2020-001-0013;MKD-EU-2022-001-0011;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0012;MKD-EU-2025-004-0084;MKD-EU-2025-004-0083;MKD-EU-2025-004-0109;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0030
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). A Law on the Use of Public Sector Data is adopted and 580 datasets were reported by 2022, but the amendments and the Directive alignment action are proposed only, and these statuses are themselves proposals for G6. What remains is alignment with the Open Data Directive and regular publication by institutions on a functioning portal. Sources conflict, since the October 2025 strategy says the country is not aligned while the screening report's country presentation says partially aligned, so both are the country's own statements (the conflict register attributes the second to the Commission), and the Commission's own finding is only that further efforts are needed; the machine-translated items are capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-EU-2025-004-0109;MKD-EU-2025-004-0086;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0087;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0011;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0198;MKD-EU-2025-004-0084;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0031
- priority: Medium (validated). The Commission raises the matter in 2020, 2022 and 2025, but not in its main findings, so it is a repeated requirement that is not central. Most items here come from one national audit and one national strategy, and that volume was not treated as importance. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0013;MKD-EU-2022-001-0011;MKD-EU-2025-004-0109;MKD-EU-2025-005-0013
- tractability: High (validated). One ministry leads, the portal and 74 publishing institutions already existed by 2022, and the stated cause of the outage is a procurement problem rather than a structural one. Progress does not depend on the EU, although the amendments have been slow since 2023. This view leans on machine-translated items capped at Medium confidence, and government reporting on datasets does not itself show success. Items: MKD-GOV-2023-002-0009;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0011;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0031;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0012;MKD-EU-2025-004-0084

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0040 Law on the Use of Public Sector Data: adopted. An international report and the screening report treat the law as in force, and the screening report calls it partially aligned with the EU directive. No source reports how it is applied.
- INT-MKD-0041 Amendments to the Law on the Use of Public Sector Data: proposed. A working group was formed to prepare the amendments, and the digital strategy of October 2025 still lists alignment with the directive as a measure. No source reports adoption.
- INT-MKD-0042 Law on Security of Network and Information Systems 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms the law in force from January 2026 and aligned with NIS2, though the Personal Data Protection Agency's opinion on it was disregarded. The Commission records a government computer security incident response team and a National Cybersecurity Council, but attributes them to the cybersecurity strategy and does not say they are the bodies the law requires; no item reports the by-laws or the lists of entities.
- INT-MKD-0062 Law on Free Access to Public Information: adopted. The source names the law as an enabler of digital government, which shows it exists. Its application is not covered.
- INT-MKD-0067 Law on National Spatial Data Infrastructure: adopted. The country reports the law and two regulations adopted. No item shows the infrastructure put in place.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0090 2019-2021 Transparency Strategy: implemented. The Commission says access to public information improved thanks to the strategy adopted in November 2019, which suggests it was carried out. The evidence is one 2020 passage, and the strategy's period ended in 2021 with no item showing an extension.
- INT-MKD-0156 Open Finance Portal: implemented. The Commission reports that the portal went live and gives the public transaction data, and the ITU profile lists it. No item measures use, and the last evidence is from 2022.
- INT-MKD-0157 National open data portal (data.gov.mk): unclear. The country's presentation to the Commission in February 2025 describes the portal as in line with best practice, but the State Audit Office says it has not functioned since the beginning of 2025 because of procurement problems. The audit is given more weight because it is specific and independent. No later source says it was restored. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0215 Regulation on Metadata Standards: adopted. The screening report records the regulation as adopted, on the country's account. No evidence of application is given.
- INT-MKD-0216 Regulation on Network Services: adopted. North Macedonia reports the Regulation as adopted at the screening. It is one reported mention, with no evidence of application.
- INT-MKD-0244 Open Data Strategy 2018-2020: adopted. The 2025 strategy says the Open Data Strategy has expired and its principles are carried into SMART/MK. Expiry is not repeal, so I do not use reversed, and the item does not show what was carried out.
- INT-MKD-0295 Ministry of Finance open data portal for treasury operations: implemented. The State Audit Office says the Ministry of Finance has established the portal. It gives no evidence of use.
- INT-MKD-0303 National geoportal: implemented. The screening report says the geoportal has been in place since 2015, but this comes from the country presentation, and no source shows its use.
- INT-MKD-0337 ePension Portal: implemented. The World Bank report lists the portal among initiatives launched by 2022. It gives no usage data and there is no later evidence.
- INT-MKD-0338 ePersonal Income Tax Portal: implemented. The source lists the portal among initiatives launched by 2022. It gives no use figures.
- INT-MKD-0422 Alignment with Directive (EU) 2019/1024 (A 3.2.7.1): proposed. This is an action plan row in the SMART/MK action plan, so it is a plan however worded. No item shows delivery.
- INT-MKD-0426 Alignment with Regulation (EU) 2022/868 on European Data Governance: proposed. The screening report says the authorities plan alignment by 2026. No source shows an adopted text.
- INT-MKD-0427 Alignment with eIDAS 2.0, the Digital Services Act and the Digital Markets Act: proposed. The 2025 strategy states this as a plan. The eIDAS 2.0 part has since been legislated, but the Commission says alignment with it is not full and the Digital Services Act and Digital Markets Act are still to be aligned.
- INT-MKD-0445 Connection of the open data portal with the interoperability platform: proposed. The strategy says an analysis will be carried out on making this connection. No source shows it done.
- INT-MKD-0475 INSPIRE Directive alignment: adopted. North Macedonia says it is at an advanced stage of alignment and that a law and two regulations have been adopted. This is self-reported and unconfirmed by independent evidence, and no item shows the framework applied.
- INT-MKD-0483 Legal framework for free access to non-personal data (A 3.2.7.8): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row on drafting a framework, with no evidence of delivery.
- INT-MKD-0487 M 3.5.1 open data: proposed. The 2023 strategy describes this as work that will be done. There is no later evidence.
- INT-MKD-0503 National Spatial Data Infrastructure tri-partite governance agreement: implemented. The screening report describes this agreement, on the country's account. That is not independent evidence of use, so the stated in_practice is lowered.
- INT-MKD-0504 Nomination of a contact person and Open Data Maturity questionnaire: implemented. The State Audit Office records that the Ministry nominated a person and completed the questionnaire on 15 April 2025.
- INT-MKD-0507 Open Data: proposed. The digital strategy says the ministry will identify priority data for publication, which is a plan. The open data portal itself is rated under INT-MKD-0157.
- INT-MKD-0513 Publication of machine-readable data (A 3.2.7.7): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0519 Request to include the country in the Open Data Maturity assessment: implemented. The State Audit Office reports the minister's cabinet sent this request on 17 December 2024. That shows the request was made, not that the country was included.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0090 leads to MKD-EU-2020-001-0012: success mixed, attribution Medium. The Commission, not the Government, states that access to public information improved thanks to the strategy, which set up the open data portal. The State Audit Office reports that the portal has not functioned since early 2025, in machine-translated text capped at Medium confidence, so the 2020 result is not shown to have lasted.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-GOV-2025-004-0087 vs MKD-EU-2025-004-0086: The SMART/MK strategy of October 2025 states that the country is not aligned with the Open Data Directive, while the Commission's screening report published in February 2025 states that the Law on the Use of Public Sector Data is partially aligned with it.
- MKD-IO-2022-002-0058 vs MKD-EU-2022-001-0098: The ITU profile of February 2022 lists the Law on the Security of Network and Information Systems as an existing enabler, while the Commission's 2022 report says the draft of that law has not yet been adopted.
