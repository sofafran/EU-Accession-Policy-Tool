# Fact pack RI-FMW-012: Supplementary pension rights of mobile workers

National law is not aligned with the EU directives on supplementary pension rights, such as the acquisition and preservation of rights for workers who move between Member States.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). All items come from one report, the 2025 screening report, so there is no sequence to read and the chapter scale cannot help. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0044;MKD-EU-2025-003-0047
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is alignment with Directives 98/49/EC and 2014/50/EU, apart from cross-border membership and equal treatment in voluntary funds. Those two exceptions are provided in law but enter into force only at accession, and no responding intervention is linked, so adoption does not count as closure. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0044;MKD-EU-2025-003-0045;MKD-EU-2025-003-0047
- priority: Low (validated). The requirement is raised only in the screening report and in no key recommendation, and supplementary pensions are peripheral to the chapter compared with its main strands. It remains a stated acquis gap, so Low does not mean it can wait past accession. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0044;MKD-EU-2025-003-0047
- tractability: Medium (validated). Legal alignment is within national control and part of the framework is already drafted into law. However, the responsible unit is reported to have only one person and the Commission calls for thorough training. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0045;MKD-EU-2025-003-0043;MKD-EU-2025-003-0046

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0070 Law on Voluntary Fully Funded Pension Insurance: adopted. The screening report describes the law as setting the legal framework, on the country's account. No evidence of its application is given.
