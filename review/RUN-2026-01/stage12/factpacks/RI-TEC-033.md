# Fact pack RI-TEC-033: Low digital adoption by businesses

Digitalisation of the economy and adoption of digital technologies by small businesses remain low compared with the EU and undermine competitiveness, and EU programmes and digital innovation hubs are little used.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission's wording is close to unchanged across reports: advancing but low in 2020 to 2023, advancing slowly in 2024 and 2025, and low in 2026, with one 2025 note that SME adoption has accelerated but stays uneven and basic. The chapter scale is context only and was not decisive. The 54.5% website figure repeats across reports and was not treated as new evidence, and the 3% and 19.7% online sales figures use different populations, so they show no trend. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0070;MKD-EU-2021-001-0039;MKD-EU-2022-001-0050;MKD-EU-2023-001-0068;MKD-EU-2024-001-0050;MKD-EU-2025-001-0082;MKD-EU-2025-001-0085;MKD-EU-2026-003-0003;MKD-EU-2021-001-0043;MKD-EU-2023-001-0071;MKD-IO-2022-002-0079;MKD-GOV-2024-010-0003
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The latest EU asks concern support to companies through the innovation hub and active use of Digital Europe calls; the hub is set up and the association agreement signed, while vouchers, the support portal and the grant mechanism are proposed, and these statuses are themselves proposals. What remains is actual use of these channels and SME adoption beyond basic technologies, as the Commission still reported low digitalisation in 2026. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0029;MKD-EU-2025-014-0005;MKD-EU-2025-001-0157;MKD-EU-2023-001-0143;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0240;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0239;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0159;MKD-EU-2025-001-0085;MKD-EU-2026-003-0003
- priority: Medium (validated). The finding is repeated in most years, largely in economic governance documents, but it has few specific recommendations; the only key recommendation found is the 2025 hub item, and no benchmark or payment condition names it in the input. It is therefore a repeated EU concern that is not central to accession steps. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0029;MKD-EU-2025-014-0005;MKD-EU-2024-009-0003;MKD-EU-2025-012-0003;MKD-EU-2026-003-0003
- tractability: Medium (validated). Government can fund and steer support, and EU programmes and a first hub already exist, but results depend on many small firms' demand, skills and finance. Past effort is small and not evaluated: EUR 0.5 million budgeted in 2023, and the strategy itself says there has been no support mechanism for projects of greater national importance; that source is machine translated and capped at Medium. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0157;MKD-EU-2023-001-0143;MKD-IO-2024-001-0103;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0158;MKD-EU-2025-004-0127;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0084

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: adopted. The Commission reports in November 2025 that the country adopted a long-term ICT strategy and action plan, and the strategy text says some activities began before formal adoption. The goals in the text are targets, so they do not show delivery. The link between the Commission's sentence and SMART/MK is inferred from timing and is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0093 Smart Specialisation Strategy S3-MK 2024-2027: adopted. The Commission reports adoption of the strategy and its action plan in December 2023. Later items only cite the strategy or restate plans, so no source shows measures carried out.
- INT-MKD-0105 Association Agreement to the Digital Europe Programme: adopted. The agreement was signed on 30 June 2023 and the country joined the programme. No item shows actual participation in calls, and in January 2025 the Commission was still urging active participation.
- INT-MKD-0106 Digital Innovation Hubs (national): proposed. The Reform Agenda says two hubs are in early development, and the 2025 strategy still speaks of forming hubs in the future. No source shows a hub operating.
- INT-MKD-0180 Support and subsidy system for digital transformation of agriculture: proposed. The strategy calls for analysis and development of this system. No source reports it being set up.
- INT-MKD-0227 Action Plan of the Industrial Strategy 2022-2023 (programme for digital and green transitions): adopted. The action plan exists with 34 measures, and a budget allocation for 2023 is reported by the country itself. An allocation does not show that measures were carried out, and the plan's period has ended.
- INT-MKD-0353 Competitiveness for Jobs and Economic Transformation (C-JET) grant: insufficient_evidence. The grant appears once, in a list of World Bank engagement, with no status, dates or results. That is too thin to say whether the assessment was done.
- INT-MKD-0359 Financial support and grant mechanism for digital transformation: proposed. The strategy provides for this mechanism, with no evidence it has been set up.
- INT-MKD-0453 Digital service vouchers for SMEs (A 4.1.1.2): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0493 Measure 4.1.3 Capacity to use the Digital Europe programme: proposed. This is an action plan row in the SMART/MK action plan, so it is a plan however worded. No item shows delivery.
- INT-MKD-0510 Project to measure the digitalisation level of construction companies: proposed. North Macedonia says the project will measure digitalisation. No item shows it delivered.
- INT-MKD-0543 Digital support portal for SMEs (A 4.1.1.1): proposed. This is an action plan row in the 2025 strategy. No item shows the portal developed.
