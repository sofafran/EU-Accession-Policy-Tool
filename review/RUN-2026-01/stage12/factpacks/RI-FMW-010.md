# Fact pack RI-FMW-010: Readiness for electronic exchange of social security information

The institutions lack the IT and human capacity to exchange social security information electronically with Member States (the EESSI system) and to use electronic health insurance data.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The sub-area rating 'still at an early stage' matches this issue but appears only in the 2025 screening report, and the annual key recommendation adds electronic exchange only in 2025. The word 'still' suggests the issue is not new, so I do not call it new, and a single year cannot show a trend. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0098;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014;MKD-EU-2024-001-0009
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is the human and IT capacity to exchange social security data through EESSI and to process EHIC data electronically. The registry and the EESSI preparations are only proposed (statuses that are themselves proposals), and the country reports no EHIC reader in its healthcare facilities. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0092;MKD-EU-2025-003-0108;MKD-EU-2025-003-0103;MKD-EU-2025-003-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0097;MKD-EU-2025-003-0101
- priority: Medium (validated). Electronic exchange is central to applying coordination at accession, and the Commission asks for stepped-up effort. The support is thin, however, as it comes from the screening report and one annual key recommendation. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0098;MKD-EU-2025-003-0108;MKD-EU-2025-001-0014
- tractability: Medium (validated). The planned registry has a stated World Bank loan and two-year horizon, but it rests on the country's own account. Several institutions are involved, EESSI access points follow later analysis over five years, and connection also involves the EU side, with no past progress recorded. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0097;MKD-EU-2025-003-0092

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0143 Single unified social insurance registry: proposed. Both sources describe the registry as a plan, with the screening report placing it in the coming two years through a World Bank loan. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0509 Preparations for EESSI access points: proposed. The country plans to define the access points within the coming five years. The Commission still asks in 2025 for measures to be put in place for electronic exchange.
