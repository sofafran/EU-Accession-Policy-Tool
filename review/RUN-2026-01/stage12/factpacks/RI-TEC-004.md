# Fact pack RI-TEC-004: Core e-government building blocks missing

Shared systems that every institution needs to work digitally, such as a single document management system, legal and technical arrangements for electronic archiving, a digital mailbox and shared administrative information systems, are missing or little used.

## Validated issue judgements

- priority: High (validated). The archiving law, the storage platform and the single document system are named Reform Agenda steps with payments attached, and these shared systems are central to the digital government theme. The rating rests on those EU conditions, not on the many national plan items. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0011;MKD-EU-2024-007-0012;MKD-EU-2024-007-0013;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0014;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0015;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0016
- tractability: Medium (validated). The tasks are bounded, led by one ministry and backed by payment incentives, and the legal step was achieved by its 2025 deadline. Roll-out is harder: it needs hardware, licences, training and change of working habits in many institutions, and earlier piloting left 22 institutions using a small part of the system. The national evidence on use is machine translated and capped at Medium. Items: MKD-GOV-2024-002-0014;MKD-EU-2025-011-0039;MKD-EU-2025-011-0045;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0069;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0033;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0076
- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). The public administration rating is context only, but the Commission attributes its some progress in 2026 partly to the upgraded document management system, and it records a central document management system launched and in use by 44 institutions by the end of June 2026, which is a change in use and not only in law. Earlier figures were 21 institutions in 2022 and 22 in 2025, from machine-translated national sources capped at Medium, and the 2026 report calls the system new, so the counts may not measure the same system; the direction is clear but its size is uncertain. The Reform Agenda requires the system across all central institutions, so this is improving and not largely resolved. Items: MKD-EU-2026-007-0001;MKD-EU-2026-007-0002;MKD-EU-2026-007-0024;MKD-EU-2026-007-0025;MKD-EU-2026-007-0100;MKD-EU-2023-003-0044;MKD-EU-2025-001-0047;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0062;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0076;MKD-EU-2025-011-0046;MKD-EU-2024-007-0013
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Of the three Reform Agenda requirements, the archiving law is adopted and applies from June 2026, and the Commission reports a central document management system in use by 44 institutions by the end of June 2026, which is short of the single system across all central institutions that the Reform Agenda requires. No 2026 item for the country reports the storage and record-keeping platform, the digital mailbox or the by-laws, or says whether the 2026 deadlines were met. What remains is the platform, the digital mailbox, the by-laws and extending the document system to all central institutions. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0047 Law on Archival Material and Archival Activity 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms adoption on 27 June 2025 and publication on 4 July 2025, and treats the central document management system as still to be completed. A digital strategy row on preparing a new law is a plan row and does not contradict adoption. The link between the earlier Reform Agenda items and this law is inferred, as the entity note says.
- INT-MKD-0048 Bylaws to the Law on Archival Material and Archival Activity 2025: proposed. The Commission says the law applies from 1 June 2026, leaving time to complete the bylaws. No source reports them adopted.
- INT-MKD-0049 Amendment of the law on archival activity (A 4.1.3.6): insufficient_evidence. The only item is a 2023 action plan row to amend the law. A new Law on Archival Material was adopted in June 2025 (INT-MKD-0047), but no source says it delivers this row, so the reviewer may wish to link them.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0088 Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030: adopted. The State Audit Office records the strategy and action plan as adopted. The measures in these items are stated as aims, and no item shows them carried out as a whole.
- INT-MKD-0100 Reform 2.2.2.1 Secure Digital infrastructures roll-out: proposed. The reform as a whole is a Reform Agenda plan for infrastructure, 5G and related law. A new Law on Electronic Communications was adopted in June 2025 as one part, but no item shows the secure infrastructure rolled out.
- INT-MKD-0125 Upgrade of the National e-Services Portal (EU-funded project): implemented. The Commission states the portal has been upgraded and its offer widened, though in 2024 it said only a few new services had been added against a target of 135 or 139. Earlier reports describe the work as both ongoing and done, and whether the target was met is not stated. The State Audit Office's low-use findings concern the portal as a whole, so use of the upgrade is not shown.
- INT-MKD-0141 Unified document management system: implemented. The Commission reports a central document management system launched and in use by 44 institutions by the end of June 2026, against the 2025 pilot in 22 of about 140 central institutions; the two counts may not measure the same system. Use is still partial against the target of all central institutions by December 2026, so in practice is not rated.
- INT-MKD-0142 Human Resources Management Information System: in_practice. The Commission reports in 2020 and 2023 that use of the system by institutions has increased. The Reform Agenda plans a further update, which is separate from the system already in use.
- INT-MKD-0143 Single unified social insurance registry: proposed. Both sources describe the registry as a plan, with the screening report placing it in the coming two years through a World Bank loan. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0163 Electronic platform supporting the EU integration process: proposed. North Macedonia reported the platform as being developed at the 2023 screening. The evidence is reported only and has not been updated since July 2023, so the status at the freeze date is the last known one.
- INT-MKD-0167 E-platform for records and archival work: proposed. The platform was a Reform Agenda step due by June 2026, and the enabling archival law was adopted in June 2025. No item shows the platform established, and the October 2025 strategy says the infrastructure is missing.
- INT-MKD-0172 Government data centre: proposed. Every source describes the data centre as a future step, the latest being the October 2025 digital strategy, and the Reform Agenda step begins with a feasibility study. No source shows it built.
- INT-MKD-0269 Central Electronic Registry: proposed. The Reform Agenda describes the registry as envisioned. No item shows it established.
- INT-MKD-0272 Digital archives: proposed. These items come from a Reform Agenda annex setting out a support need and expected deliverables. No source reports any tool or standard delivered.
- INT-MKD-0283 Electronic signing by officials: proposed. The 2023 strategy says officials will have to sign electronically once legislation and the document system are in place. No source reports this as done.
- INT-MKD-0284 Government cloud platform: proposed. The strategy describes the platform in the future tense. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0315 Repository and catalogue of digital solutions (A 3.1.7.4): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row, with no evidence of delivery.
- INT-MKD-0325 Upgrade for interoperability of the Government electronic platform, NPAA platform, ENER, e-Parliament and e-Official Gazette: proposed. North Macedonia reported the upgrade as initiated at the 2023 screening. The evidence is reported only and has not been updated since July 2023, so this is the last known status.
- INT-MKD-0335 e-government electronic intranet system for strategic planning: implemented. The only evidence is the country's own account in the screening report. That is not independent evidence of use, so the stated in_practice is lowered.
- INT-MKD-0354 Connection of government institutions: proposed. The digital strategy describes this in the future tense.
- INT-MKD-0443 Cloud-first legal solution (A 1.2.3.1): proposed. This is an action plan row in the SMART/MK action plan, so it is a plan however worded. No item shows delivery.
- INT-MKD-0457 E-Mailbox (E-Sandache): proposed. The strategy says the ministry will develop the mailbox, and no item shows it built.
- INT-MKD-0458 E-Mailbox legal framework (A 3.2.5.2): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0462 Electronic document storage solution (A 3.1.3.4): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0469 General e-government framework: insufficient_evidence. The only item is one general 2020 sentence that preparations continued. It does not identify the framework or say what followed.
- INT-MKD-0522 Revision of by-laws for fully electronic working (A 3.1.2.4): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy, and it applies only if necessary.
