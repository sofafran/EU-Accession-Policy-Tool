# Fact pack RI-FMW-013: Weak corruption prevention in the administration of worker mobility

The bodies administering free movement of workers, notably the employment service, lack a specific integrity policy, internal controls and audit capacity to prevent corruption.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The sub-area rating 'still at an early stage' matches this issue but appears only in 2025, so it is used with the issue's own items. The integrity policy is outstanding in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and the framework is called weak in 2024 and again in the screening report. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0101;MKD-EU-2024-001-0070;MKD-EU-2025-001-0110;MKD-EU-2024-001-0069;MKD-EU-2025-003-0111;MKD-EU-2025-003-0117
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is a signed integrity policy, a person to implement it, a reinforced internal audit department and controls in the institutions concerned. No responding intervention is linked, so there is no status to compare, and the country's stated plan to sign has not been shown to be carried out. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0116;MKD-EU-2025-003-0118;MKD-EU-2025-003-0113;MKD-EU-2025-001-0110
- priority: Medium (validated). The requirement is repeated by the Commission in three reports, but it is not in a key recommendation for the chapter or central to it. The issue's scope is limited to free movement of workers, and general anti-corruption policy is outside it. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0101;MKD-EU-2024-001-0070;MKD-EU-2025-001-0110;MKD-EU-2025-003-0114
- tractability: Medium (validated). Signing the policy and naming a person are small steps for one agency, but no report shows them done, though the agency reportedly planned to do so. Reinforcing audit staff, ministry capacity and controls across institutions is wider. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0113;MKD-EU-2025-003-0116;MKD-EU-2025-003-0118;MKD-EU-2025-001-0110
