# Fact pack RI-FMW-003: Public sector posts closed to EU citizens beyond the public authority exception

EU citizens are barred from public sector posts other than a short list of exemptions, rather than only from posts involving the exercise of public authority and safeguarding the general interests of the State.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since this is part of the chapter and no sub-area wording matches. The finding that EU citizens cannot take public administration posts, apart from some in health and education, is unchanged in each report from 2020 to 2025. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0080;MKD-EU-2021-001-0064;MKD-EU-2022-001-0066;MKD-EU-2023-001-0094;MKD-EU-2024-001-0060;MKD-EU-2025-001-0100
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is limiting the restriction to posts involving public authority and the general interest of the State, in line with case law of the Court of Justice. No responding intervention is linked, so there is no status to compare, and the existing exemptions for health and higher education are the country's account and are narrower than the requirement. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0026;MKD-EU-2025-003-0035;MKD-EU-2025-001-0100;MKD-EU-2025-003-0021;MKD-EU-2025-003-0023
- priority: Medium (validated). This is a repeated finding central to the chapter, but public sector posts are named as a requirement only in the screening report and not in an annual key recommendation. If the reviewers read 'in all sectors' in the 2023 to 2025 key recommendation as covering them, this would be High. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0026;MKD-EU-2025-003-0035;MKD-EU-2023-001-0090;MKD-EU-2024-001-0008;MKD-EU-2025-001-0013
- tractability: Medium (validated). The work is national and depends on no outside party, but it requires a careful review of civil servant categories across the public service against case law. There is no evidence of past progress and no responding intervention. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0027;MKD-EU-2025-003-0035;MKD-EU-2025-001-0100
