# Fact pack RI-FMW-014: Access and rights of third-country workers

Workers from outside the EU face barriers to accessing the labour market, or their rights after admission are not protected or monitored.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The evidence is from international organisations, with one research study, and partly regional, and no measure of this issue repeats over time for the country. The doubling of permits is regional and cannot alone support a finding here, and the Commission's permit counts have unclear bases. The chapter scale was not decisive, as it concerns EU workers' access. Items: MKD-IO-2024-001-0098;MKD-IO-2026-001-0001;MKD-IO-2026-001-0017;MKD-EU-2022-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0104;MKD-RES-2023-001-0036
- unresolved_gap: insufficient_evidence (validated). No EU requirement item exists for this issue, so the comparison the gap requires cannot be made and no responding intervention is linked. The international organisations report, for North Macedonia, regulatory barriers, passport retention, wage deductions, underreporting and overlapping laws, which is what remains on their account; other items are regional. A regional claim that laws are EU-aligned does not name the country and sits uneasily with the Commission's finding of partial alignment for EU workers. Items: MKD-IO-2024-001-0098;MKD-IO-2026-001-0012;MKD-IO-2026-001-0017;MKD-IO-2026-001-0021;MKD-IO-2026-001-0023;MKD-EU-2025-003-0031
- priority: Low (validated). The issue is mainly raised by others: the evidence is from international organisations and partly regional, with no Commission recommendation on third-country workers in Chapter 2. The labour shortage it describes may matter for the country, but that is not the test for importance to accession. Items: MKD-IO-2024-001-0003;MKD-IO-2024-001-0075;MKD-IO-2026-001-0004;MKD-IO-2026-001-0005;MKD-IO-2026-001-0013
- tractability: Medium (validated). The rules and inspection are national matters, without dependence on the EU, but the international organisation evidence, partly regional, points to several institutions, under-resourced inspection and permits tied to one employer. No item shows past progress for the country, so this is a provisional view. Items: MKD-IO-2026-001-0011;MKD-IO-2026-001-0015;MKD-IO-2026-001-0021;MKD-IO-2024-001-0075
