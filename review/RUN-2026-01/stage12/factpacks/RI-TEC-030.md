# Fact pack RI-TEC-030: Not ready for EU justice, home affairs and border information systems

The country lacks the systems, technical adaptations and funding to connect to EU justice and home affairs systems such as ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The requirements on ECRIS, the Visa Information System and Eurodac come essentially from one 2023 screening report, so there is no repeated or changed wording to compare. The 2021, 2022 and 2024 items concern a Frontex biometric plan and do not show whether readiness moved. The chapter scale was context only and not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0122;MKD-EU-2022-001-0044;MKD-EU-2023-003-0091;MKD-EU-2023-003-0095;MKD-EU-2023-003-0096;MKD-EU-2024-001-0049
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). Responding interventions are proposed or lack a clear status, and these statuses are themselves proposals; part of the requirement falls due only at accession, and Eurodac access before accession has no legal basis, so 'open' does not by itself mean late. What remains is a functional criminal records system with ECRIS analysis done, the technical adaptations for the Visa Information System, an IT structure compatible with Eurodac on accession, and the funding for them. Items: MKD-EU-2023-003-0090;MKD-EU-2023-003-0091;MKD-EU-2023-003-0095;MKD-EU-2023-003-0096;MKD-EU-2023-003-0099;MKD-EU-2023-003-0097;MKD-EU-2023-003-0103;MKD-EU-2023-003-0104
- priority: Medium (validated). The systems matter for Chapter 24, but support is thin: one screening report states the requirements and no item shows a benchmark, Reform Agenda condition or repeated key recommendation. This is central with thin support. Items: MKD-EU-2023-003-0095;MKD-EU-2023-003-0096;MKD-EU-2023-003-0099;MKD-EU-2024-001-0049
- tractability: Low (validated). Progress depends on the EU: the Commission says EU law gives candidates no Eurodac access, and the country reports it cannot build to Eurodac without the technical specifications and struggles to secure funding. The Frontex plan offers a route for border systems, but no item shows it being carried out. Items: MKD-EU-2023-003-0101;MKD-EU-2023-003-0103;MKD-EU-2023-003-0136;MKD-EU-2023-003-0095;MKD-EU-2024-001-0049

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0181 Frontex Masterplan for IT systems for mixed migration flows: proposed. Frontex delivered the Masterplan document by 2022, which is why some items state implemented, but it is a plan for developing IT systems. No source shows the planned systems being built, and the Commission still describes it as a plan in 2024.
- INT-MKD-0264 Biometric registration system that follows the Eurodac model: insufficient_evidence. The only item, from 2021, refers to the eventual establishment of such a system with Frontex, and nothing later says what happened. That is too thin to give a status at the freeze date.
- INT-MKD-0267 CT SIENA: implemented. The Commission reports that CT SIENA was rolled out to the country and increased the opportunities for exchange. It does not show actual exchanges, and the item dates from 2020.
- INT-MKD-0346 secure communication link for information exchange (SIENA): implemented. The Commission reported in 2020 that the link was established. No later item shows how it is used.
- INT-MKD-0431 Alignment with the entry-exit system and ETIAS: proposed. The screening report says this alignment will be done upon accession.
- INT-MKD-0518 Request for information exchange and access to Eurodac before accession (accelerated integration): proposed. North Macedonia made the request, and the Commission's answer in the same report is that the Eurodac Regulation does not provide for third-country participation and no change is being considered. The request therefore stands unmet.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0267 leads to MKD-EU-2020-001-0060: success worked, attribution High. The Commission, independent of the government, states the roll-out increased opportunities for direct and secure counter-terrorism exchange, and a later report records 387 messages exchanged. The outcome is narrow: it concerns one channel and says nothing about readiness for EU large-scale systems.
