# Fact pack RI-TEC-010: Digital communication with courts and justice system connections incomplete

Digital communication with courts is incomplete, or justice system IT does not connect between institutions.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Commission calls for digital court communication and connected IT recur in 2020, 2023, 2024 and 2025, with identical wording in the last two, and the single 2026 item on the administrative courts cannot alone show a change of direction. Support is thin (eight items, none for 2021 or 2022, most flagged theme_doubtful), and no chapter scale was decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0030;MKD-EU-2023-003-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0035;MKD-EU-2025-001-0058;MKD-EU-2026-006-0002
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The administrative courts' interconnection is proposed as implemented and the Commission calls it a key advancement, but no status is recorded for the judicial IT strategies and council, and Luris is at insufficient evidence; these statuses are themselves proposals. What remains is digital communication with courts generally, videoconferencing for hearings, and connections between other justice institutions, including the Ministry of Justice and the prosecution. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0058;MKD-EU-2026-006-0002;MKD-EU-2023-003-0130
- priority: Medium (validated). The recommendation is repeated, including in the 2024 and 2025 reports, within the important Chapter 23 area, but digital communication is a supporting part of judicial reform and no benchmark or payment condition on it appears in the input. Evidence is thin and most items are flagged theme_doubtful. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0030;MKD-EU-2024-001-0035;MKD-EU-2025-001-0058;MKD-EU-2023-003-0089
- tractability: Medium (validated). One interconnection shows that progress is possible, but several independent justice institutions must act together, and the Commission points to weak coordination. The evidence on actors and resources is thin, so this rating is tentative. Items: MKD-EU-2026-006-0002;MKD-EU-2023-003-0089;MKD-EU-2023-003-0130;MKD-EU-2020-001-0030

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0274 Digital interconnection between the Administrative Court, the Higher Administrative Court and other public bodies: implemented. The Commission's 2026 Rule of Law report describes the interconnection as a key advancement that enables electronic exchange. It is one statement and gives no usage data.
- INT-MKD-0293 Luris software: insufficient_evidence. The only evidence is a 2023 statement in the country's presentation that the software needs connecting between two bodies. It does not say whether the software is working or whether the connection was made.
