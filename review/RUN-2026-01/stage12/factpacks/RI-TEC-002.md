# Fact pack RI-TEC-002: Digital transformation strategy and governance weak

There is no adopted long-term strategy for digital transformation aligned with EU targets, or the ministry or agency responsible lacks the capacity, staff and inter-institutional coordination to lead and monitor it.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The only movement is that the key recommendation to finalise and adopt the ICT strategy, unchanged from 2020 to 2024, ended in 2025 because the strategy was adopted, and adoption is not improvement in governance. The Commission's wording that the ministry needs stronger capacity is unchanged across 2022 to 2025, which sets the default to persistent, and the 2025 report adds a call for more IT staff. The chapter scale (some progress 2020, limited 2021 to 2024, some 2025) is context only and was not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0105;MKD-EU-2021-001-0083;MKD-EU-2022-001-0099;MKD-EU-2023-001-0149;MKD-EU-2024-001-0015;MKD-EU-2024-001-0080;MKD-EU-2025-001-0052;MKD-EU-2025-001-0128
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The latest EU items report the strategy adopted but still ask for stronger ministry capacity, more IT staff and the digital agency, which remains proposed and which the country has said it will reassess; these statuses are themselves proposals, and that of the National Council is unclear because the 2024 audit found no 2023 activity while the strategy cites its formation. What remains is applying the strategy, building coordinating capacity and IT staffing, and settling the agency question. The Commission's well-structured set-up and the national vacuum are a noted difference of scope, not a conflict. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0127;MKD-EU-2025-001-0128;MKD-EU-2025-001-0052;MKD-EU-2025-014-0004;MKD-EU-2025-004-0004;MKD-GOV-2024-007-0070;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0167;MKD-EU-2025-004-0005;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0012
- priority: High (validated). The ICT strategy was a Chapter 10 key recommendation in five successive reports, and the ministry capacity ask has recurred in four reports to 2025, on an issue central to the digital transformation theme. The key recommendation itself left the list after adoption in 2025 and the capacity ask sits in body text, so High rests on treating adoption as the start rather than the end of the requirement. National plans and audits were not counted towards this rating. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0105;MKD-EU-2021-001-0083;MKD-EU-2022-001-0087;MKD-EU-2023-001-0119;MKD-EU-2024-001-0015;MKD-EU-2022-001-0099;MKD-EU-2023-001-0149;MKD-EU-2024-001-0080;MKD-EU-2025-001-0128;MKD-EU-2025-001-0022
- tractability: Medium (validated). A new ministry and an adopted strategy show that institutional steps can be taken, but the strategy took over five years and coordination depends on many institutions that have offered limited cooperation. IT posts are about half filled and public pay rules limit competition with the private sector, which the ministry cannot change alone. The staffing and working group evidence is machine translated and capped at Medium. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0039 2025 digitalisation law package: adopted. The Commission reports the government adopting several laws in May 2025 and Parliament adopting four in June 2025. This is a package term, and each law is recorded separately.
- INT-MKD-0044 Draft Law on Security of Network and Information Systems and Digital Transformation 2023: insufficient_evidence. A Law on Security of Network and Information Systems (INT-MKD-0042) was adopted in June 2025, but no source says it is this draft or that this draft was withdrawn. The draft has no stated status after February 2025.
- INT-MKD-0046 Agency for Security of Network and Information Systems and Digital Transformation: insufficient_evidence. The draft that provided for this agency has no stated outcome, and the 2025 law names the Ministry and the Agency for Electronic Communications as the competent authorities. No source says the agency was formally dropped.
- INT-MKD-0050 Law on the Organisation and Operation of the State Administration Bodies: implemented. The law gave the Ministry of Digital Transformation its competences, and that allocation is in place. Audit and strategy evidence shows gaps in carrying them out: no accountability line with the Fund for Innovation and Technology Development, and no central register of systems. I therefore do not rate it in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0051 Amendments to the Law on the Organisation and Work of State Administration Bodies 2024: implemented. The amendments were adopted on 10 June 2024 and the Commission confirms the new Ministry of Digital Transformation was set up. The items show the institution in place, not how well it works.
- INT-MKD-0078 National long-term ICT Strategy (unadopted drafts): insufficient_evidence. The drafts themselves were never adopted, but the Commission reported in November 2025 that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan had been adopted, recorded under SMART/MK: 2030 (INT-MKD-0079). No source says whether these drafts became that strategy or were withdrawn, so the analyst should decide whether to merge the entities.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0080 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030 Action Plan 2025-2027: adopted. The Commission reported in November 2025 that the strategy and its action plan were adopted, though the link to SMART/MK is inferred from timing. The strategy's own statement that some activities had begun before adoption is general and unverified, so implemented is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0081 National digital transformation strategy (draft): proposed. The State Audit Office refers to it as a draft in June 2025. It may be the SMART/MK draft, but the source does not say so.
- INT-MKD-0082 Strategy for development of digital skills (part of the ICT strategy): insufficient_evidence. The Commission said in 2022 that the skills strategy was prepared as part of the ICT strategy, with no later report on it. Whether it was carried into the strategy adopted in 2025 is not stated.
- INT-MKD-0083 National Cybersecurity Strategy 2018-2022: reversed. The strategy ran out at the end of 2022 and its successor is the Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028 (INT-MKD-0085). No source says it was repealed, so reversed is used by analogy with a replaced law because the scale has no value for expiry. The Government's later strategy says implementation was not fully achieved.
- INT-MKD-0085 Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028: implemented. The Commission now reports that implementation of the strategy continued and that, under it, a National Cybersecurity Council, a Cybersecurity Department and a government incident response centre were set up, so the Commission and the regulator's earlier statement that the strategy is applied now agree. No item reports the half-yearly reports the strategy promises or measures use of the new bodies.
- INT-MKD-0087 Digital Transformation Roadmap 2024-2030: adopted. The Government adopted the Roadmap by conclusion, as the State Audit Office records. The 2025 strategy says its principles lack enforcement mechanisms, and no item shows the Roadmap itself carried out. Sources differ on the date (late 2023 and January 2024), which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0088 Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030: adopted. The State Audit Office records the strategy and action plan as adopted. The measures in these items are stated as aims, and no item shows them carried out as a whole.
- INT-MKD-0089 Public Administration Reform Strategy and Action Plan 2018-2022: implemented. The strategy's period ended in 2022, and a government source credits it with the 2019 digitalisation laws. The Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030 followed, but no item says it formally replaced this one, so I do not use reversed.
- INT-MKD-0098 Reform Agenda of North Macedonia: implemented. The Commission assessed the Reform Agenda steps in October 2025 and found four of sixteen achieved, and the 2026 assessment reports on-site checks of delivered equipment. Delivery is real but partial, and the Commission's findings are preferred to the Agenda's own text.
- INT-MKD-0118 National ICT council: insufficient_evidence. The only evidence is two passages in one ITU profile of February 2022, describing a council monitoring projects. Sources do not say whether it is the National Cyber Security Council, and no later item mentions it. The difference with the audit's absent body was noted at G5 as different objects, so it is not treated as a conflict here.
- INT-MKD-0119 National Council for Digital Transformation: unclear. The Council was established in September 2022, and the 2025 strategy describes it as formed to plan and coordinate digitalisation. The State Audit Office could not confirm any activities in 2023 and reports an absence of activities and results, so a named body exists but is said not to function. I give the audit more weight than the government's description, and there is no later evidence of activity. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0121 Planned national digital agency: proposed. The agency was planned from at least 2021 but no source shows it was created. In February 2025 the country reported it would reassess the need, since the Ministry of Digital Transformation holds the policy role, while the Commission encouraged setting it up; the stated adopted status on that item concerns the organisation law, not the agency.
- INT-MKD-0123 National Broadband Competence Office: in_practice. The office was set up in 2019, and the Commission says it publishes biannual reports on broadband development, which is specific evidence of it working.
- INT-MKD-0124 National e-Services Portal (uslugi.gov.mk): in_practice. The State Audit Office, an independent source, confirms the portal has been in use since 2019, but finds low use (0.556 services per registered user), and the 2025 strategy reports 314 services and about 13 of 100 adults registered. Use is real but limited. The Commission and the audit differ on access for people with disabilities, which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0127 Central Population Register: in_practice. The Commission reported the register operational in 2020 and used to crosscheck the 2021 census data, and the State Audit Office describes it as an integrated working database. Later government text that services will draw on it obligatorily shows full use is still to come.
- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0236 DESI strategic goal 2023-2027: proposed. This is a stated goal to reach the EU DESI average by 2027 and no more than a target.
- INT-MKD-0238 Digitalisation roadmap: proposed. The 2023 strategy says the roadmap is planned. SMART/MK later refers to a Digital Transformation Roadmap, but no source states this one was published.
- INT-MKD-0239 Good governance policy plan 2023, goal 17: adopted. The State Audit Office cites this plan goal. It is one mention, and no item shows the goal carried out.
- INT-MKD-0242 National Strategy for e-Services at the Local Level (proposed): proposed. A civil society organisation proposed this strategy for the Government's 2025 work programme. No source shows the Government taking it up.
- INT-MKD-0246 Strategic Plan for 2019–2021: adopted. The World Bank report says the ministry launched the plan and describes its programme areas. Its period ended in 2021 and no item shows what was delivered.
- INT-MKD-0251 Government conclusion, 87th session 2021: adopted. The State Audit Office reports the conclusion and its instruction on a working group and deadlines. No item shows that the deadline was met.
- INT-MKD-0252 Government conclusions of the 107th session on consolidating ICT budgets: adopted. The State Audit Office records the obligation set by the conclusions. No item shows ICT budgets actually consolidated.
- INT-MKD-0255 Working group conclusions of 22 May 2022: adopted. The State Audit Office reports the working group conclusions, which tell institutions to prepare plans and budgets. No item shows that institutions did so.
- INT-MKD-0317 Single Sign-On System: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that Single Sign-On was integrated across service providers. This follows the State Audit Office's 2024 finding that access to e-services did not go through the system; the two describe different dates, so they are a change over time and not a conflict. No item measures use of the system.
- INT-MKD-0363 Improving digital competences of public sector employees: proposed. The strategy names this as a precondition and a planned area of work. No item shows delivery.
- INT-MKD-0378 Coordination groups on digitalisation: implemented. The State Audit Office records that the Government formed the two groups. It does not cover what they then delivered.
- INT-MKD-0382 Working group on equipment for digitalisation: adopted. The State Audit Office records a decision forming the group. It does not show the group working or the equipment being aligned.
- INT-MKD-0441 Certificates of IT tool knowledge in job systematisation: proposed. The 2023 strategy describes this as an intended condition in job systematisation acts. There is no later evidence.
- INT-MKD-0451 Digital ambassadors status (A2.2.5.1): proposed. This is an action plan row in the SMART/MK action plan, so it is a plan however worded. No item shows delivery.
- INT-MKD-0456 Draft concept for the digital transformation of society: adopted. The State Audit Office reports the draft concept adopted at the Council's founding session. No item shows it carried out, and the audit found no Council activity in 2023.
- INT-MKD-0474 ICT inspection competence: proposed. The 2023 strategy says this competence will be established, and SMART/MK in 2025 says competences for inspection supervision are not clearly established.
- INT-MKD-0520 Retaining ICT staff in the public sector (M 2.2.6): proposed. This is an action plan row in the 2025 strategy. No item shows retention measures in place.
- INT-MKD-0538 legal and strategic framework aligned with the Digital Agenda for the Western Balkans: proposed. The Commission's assessment says North Macedonia will set up this framework. It is a statement of future action with no sign of delivery as a single framework.
- INT-MKD-0539 Digital Agenda Observatory (proposed): proposed. A civil society group proposed this to the Government, and no item shows the Government taking it up.
