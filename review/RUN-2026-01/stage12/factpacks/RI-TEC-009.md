# Fact pack RI-TEC-009: eHealth systems not interoperable or aligned with EU rules

Electronic health systems are not interoperable with health insurance data, referrals remain on paper, and the legislation is not aligned with the EU acquis on eHealth.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Commission wording on extending Moj Termin is the same in 2022 and 2023, and non-interoperability with Health Insurance Fund data recurs in 2024 and 2025, with non-alignment of legislation added in the 2025 screening. Functional upgrades in 2023 and 2025 are real but have not changed the stated shortfall. The default for three unchanged reports is not strictly triggered, and no Chapter 28 scale rating was decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2022-006-0042;MKD-EU-2023-006-0040;MKD-EU-2023-001-0208;MKD-EU-2024-001-0077;MKD-EU-2025-003-0183;MKD-EU-2025-001-0124
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The 2025 report still asks for interoperability, and the responding steps are proposed (population register and wallet connections) or at insufficient evidence (platform for traceability), none naming Health Insurance Fund data; these statuses are themselves proposals and the plan items are machine translated, capped at Medium confidence. The paper-referral finding and the screening's account of a tool holding referrals are a noted difference, not a conflict, since storing data and issuing on paper can both be true, but practice needs checking. What remains is interoperability with Health Insurance Fund data, an end to paper referrals, and alignment with the eHealth acquis. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0018;MKD-EU-2025-001-0124;MKD-EU-2024-001-0077;MKD-EU-2025-003-0176;MKD-EU-2025-003-0183;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0235;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0236;MKD-EU-2023-006-0041
- priority: Medium (validated). The Commission's requirement is repeated in 2024 and 2025 and raised to a recommendation in 2025, but it is a narrow part of Chapter 28 and only recently prominent. The Reform Agenda's e-services step names the health sector as an example, and this was not read as a payment condition on this issue. Items: MKD-EU-2024-001-0077;MKD-EU-2025-001-0018;MKD-EU-2025-003-0183;MKD-EU-2024-007-0015
- tractability: Medium (validated). A central tool and one lead ministry exist and upgrades are reported, but interoperability needs the Health Insurance Fund as well, and alignment with the acquis needs legislation. National plan and World Bank items add that the platform needs upgrading and users need digital literacy; the plan item is machine translated, capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0208;MKD-EU-2025-001-0124;MKD-EU-2025-003-0194;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0156;MKD-IO-2024-001-0089

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0063 Law on Health Care: adopted. The screening report names this law as defining eHealth, on the country's account. No evidence of its application in this area is given.
- INT-MKD-0065 Law on Healthcare Records: adopted. North Macedonia cites the law at the screening as defining eHealth. It is one reported mention, with no evidence of application.
- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0131 Moj Termin eHealth system: in_practice. The Commission says the system was introduced in 2013 and has reduced waiting times, the World Bank describes it in use for appointments, and a mobile application and new modules have been added. Both also report limits: referrals still on paper in 2024, and no interoperability with Health Insurance Fund data as of 2025. Those limits qualify the use but do not contradict it.
- INT-MKD-0297 MojTermin connection with the digital wallet (A 3.2.8.5): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0298 MojTermin connection with the population register (A 3.2.8.1): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row, marked as under way but not shown as completed.
- INT-MKD-0299 MyHealth mobile application: implemented. The Commission reports that the application was launched in 2023 and that the mobile application was upgraded by 2025. Neither item measures its use.
- INT-MKD-0313 Platform for appointments and traceability of health data (ERP measure 4): insufficient_evidence. The 2022 and 2023 Commission assessments only say that a platform is expected. No later item says what became of it, and it is not shown to be the same as the upgraded eHealth system.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0131 leads to MKD-IO-2022-002-0074: success mixed, attribution Low. ITU states that Moj Termin improved patient management, but that item is flagged as a possible misread. Later sources point to limits: the World Bank says the system could be updated to improve efficiency, and the Commission finds that referrals remain on paper and the system is not interoperable.
- INT-MKD-0131 leads to MKD-EU-2022-006-0041: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission states that Moj Termin helped to significantly reduce waiting times, while recommending extension to primary care doctors. No figures are given, and later criticism concerns appointment-system quality and interoperability, not this past effect.
- INT-MKD-0131 leads to MKD-EU-2023-006-0039: success worked, attribution Medium. The 2023 sentence repeats the 2022 wording, so it is not independent support for LNK-000442 and the same limits apply: a Commission statement with no figures.
