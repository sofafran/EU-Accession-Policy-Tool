# Fact pack RI-TEC-035: Digital tools in company law not fully aligned

Company law is not fully aligned with EU rules on the use of digital tools and processes, such as online formation, electronic filing and director checks.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The alignment ask on digital tools in company law recurs in near-identical wording in 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, so the default applies; the chapter scale is context only and was not decisive. The 2025 screening finding of a high level of alignment shows the remaining ask is narrow, but it is not evidence of change over time. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0075;MKD-EU-2022-001-0078;MKD-EU-2023-001-0107;MKD-EU-2024-001-0010;MKD-EU-2025-001-0015;MKD-EU-2025-001-0116;MKD-EU-2025-003-0142
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). Fully online company registration is in practice and the Commission finds high alignment, on a status that is itself a proposal. What remains is further alignment with Directive (EU) 2019/1151, including scrutiny of a company's object and verification of directors' appointment and disqualification. The country's statement that it is aligned and the Commission's statement that further alignment is necessary are a confirmed contradiction, reported here and not resolved. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0115;MKD-EU-2025-003-0142;MKD-EU-2025-003-0149;MKD-EU-2025-003-0133;MKD-EU-2025-001-0116
- priority: Medium (validated). It appears in the key recommendations of three reports, but always bundled with other company law items, and digital tools are a narrow strand on which the Commission already finds high alignment. It is therefore a repeated EU requirement that is not central to the chapter, and it is not a benchmark or payment condition in the input. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0107;MKD-EU-2024-001-0010;MKD-EU-2025-001-0015;MKD-EU-2025-003-0142
- tractability: High (validated). The remaining points are specific legal amendments in a field with few actors and a working one-stop-shop registry, and they depend on no other institution's roll-out. The ask has nonetheless stood since 2021 with no reported draft, which is why the rating depends on the small scope and not on past momentum. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0149;MKD-EU-2025-001-0115;MKD-EU-2025-003-0187;MKD-EU-2021-001-0075

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0053 Law on Central Registry: adopted. North Macedonia cites the law at the screening as part of the framework for companies. There is no independent evidence of application in these items.
- INT-MKD-0069 Law on Trade Companies: adopted. The country presentation in the screening report says the law regulates disclosure and digital tools. Its application is not covered.
- INT-MKD-0161 Central Registry one-stop-shop online company registration: in_practice. The Commission states in its own words, in 2023 and 2025, that company registration and filing can be carried out fully online in a solid and available one-stop shop. It also describes the digitalised registration as efficient, and no source disagrees.
- INT-MKD-0309 Online access to Central Registry company information: implemented. The country presentation says basic company information is available online free of charge. No independent source confirms this, so in_practice is not reached.

- MKD-EU-2025-003-0133 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0116: In 2025 North Macedonia states it is aligned with the acquis on the use of digital tools and processes in company law, while the Commission says further alignment on the use of digital tools and processes is necessary.
