# Fact pack RI-TEC-017: Personal data protection law not aligned with EU rules

The personal data protection framework is not fully aligned with the General Data Protection Regulation, the Law Enforcement Directive and the ePrivacy Directive, rules on international transfers are missing, and sector laws are not harmonised.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission found no concrete progress on the Law Enforcement Directive in 2020, partial alignment and a need for full alignment in 2023, and pending alignment in 2024 and 2025, so the requirement has not changed across three or more reports. The sub-area rating of no progress in 2024 and 2025 covers more than this issue and was not decisive. The 2025 transfer amendment without equivalent safeguards is a further concern but a single event. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0036;MKD-EU-2023-003-0073;MKD-EU-2023-001-0027;MKD-EU-2024-001-0038;MKD-EU-2024-001-0039;MKD-EU-2025-001-0063;MKD-EU-2025-001-0064;MKD-EU-2025-001-0065;MKD-EU-2025-001-0066
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The 2020 law is proposed as in practice, with the Commission confirming full application from August 2021, while the amendments and the Law Enforcement Directive law are proposed only, the earlier draft was withdrawn in 2022, and all these statuses are themselves proposals for G6. What remains is full alignment with the General Data Protection Regulation, a law transposing the Law Enforcement Directive, safeguards for transfers, harmonised sector laws and confirmed ePrivacy alignment. Sources conflict, since the State Audit Office and the Agency's strategy describe the law as aligned while the Commission calls the framework insufficient, and the country claims full ePrivacy alignment while the Commission seeks further efforts; this judgement does not rely on Article 122 of the unofficial translation (MKD-GOV-2020-001-0043), which may be mistranslated and, read literally, would suspend core chapters until accession. - priority: High (overridden). Full alignment with the data protection rules is central to the data theme and is repeated by the Commission in 2023, 2024 and 2025, but none of the 2020 to 2025 reports names it in the key recommendations, and it is in no benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition. The 2024 and 2025 main findings record no progress, but that is a finding and not a key recommendation, so this is a central requirement with thin support under the test. National and agency items were not counted. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0027;MKD-EU-2023-001-0030;MKD-EU-2023-003-0074;MKD-EU-2024-001-0003;MKD-EU-2024-001-0039;MKD-EU-2025-001-0006;MKD-EU-2025-001-0064
- tractability: Medium (validated). Texts already exist: the Agency drafted amendments in April 2022 and a Law Enforcement Directive draft reached Parliament with, as the Agency reports, a positive informal Commission opinion. Yet the Government withdrew that draft in 2022, neither process moved in 2025, and sector laws need several ministries. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0009 Law on Personal Data Protection 2005: reversed. The law was repealed on the entry into force of its successor, the Law on Personal Data Protection 2020 (INT-MKD-0010).
- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0011 Law on Amending the Law on Personal Data Protection 2021: adopted. The Official Gazette text shows adoption on 23 December 2021 of minor wording amendments. No source reports anything further about carrying them out.
- INT-MKD-0012 Law on Personal Data Protection (amendment on transfers to NATO countries): adopted. The Commission and the Agency's report both state that the Assembly amended the law to allow transfers to NATO countries without prior consultation. No item shows transfers made under it.
- INT-MKD-0013 Draft Law amending the Law on Personal Data Protection: proposed. The Agency drafted the amendments in April 2022, and its 2026 report says the process of adopting them had still not started during 2025. No source reports adoption.
- INT-MKD-0014 Recast of the Law on Personal Data Protection: proposed. The recast was reported as being prepared in 2023, and the Commission said it needed to be completed. The Agency reports the related amending proposal still not adopted in 2025; sources do not say the two are the same.
- INT-MKD-0015 Bylaws under the Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: adopted. The Commission reports thirteen bylaws adopted in May 2020. The item mapped here says nothing about their application.
- INT-MKD-0016 Draft Law transposing the Law Enforcement Directive (withdrawn 2022): reversed. The Government withdrew the draft in September 2022 during second reading. No successor law exists, since the Agency reports the transposition process had not started during 2025 even though its strategy targets adoption by early 2026.
- INT-MKD-0017 Law transposing the EU Law Enforcement Directive (planned): proposed. The Agency's 2024 strategy calls for this law by early 2026, but its 2026 report says transposition had still not started during 2025. No source reports a law being adopted.
- INT-MKD-0018 Strategy for the Implementation of the Right to Personal Data Protection 2025-2030: implemented. The Agency reports the strategy was adopted in 2024 and implementation began in 2025. The Commission's finding that budget and consultation problems negatively affected implementation is preferred to the Agency's account on how far it has gone, but it also treats implementation as under way.
- INT-MKD-0019 Law on Electronic Communications (Official Gazette 39/14): reversed. The Law on Electronic Communications 2025 (INT-MKD-0023) says this law ceases to be in force on the day the new law starts to apply, which is 1 June 2026. This is read from the legal text; no source confirms the date was not moved.
- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025 and amended on 29 December 2025; it entered into force on the eighth day after publication on 4 July 2025 and was due to apply from 1 June 2026, but no later item confirms application or reports measures under it, and the security supervision reported for 2025 rests on the earlier law. By-laws were due within 15 months (October 2026) and operators have 18 months to align (January 2027), so that deadline has not yet passed. The Commission's May 2026 finding that the step is not achieved, with a grace period to 30 June 2026 whose outcome is not in the evidence, concerns alignment, and 'draft' or 'proposal' wording in GOV-2025-004-0172 and GOV-2026-002-0100 is historic rather than a conflict.
- INT-MKD-0040 Law on the Use of Public Sector Data: adopted. An international report and the screening report treat the law as in force, and the screening report calls it partially aligned with the EU directive. No source reports how it is applied.
- INT-MKD-0042 Law on Security of Network and Information Systems 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025, published on 4 July 2025, entered into force on the eighth day and applies from 1 January 2026, except that entities in Art. 4(2) points 8 to 16 are covered only from EU accession. No item shows the measures it requires, such as the by-laws (due within six months), the entity lists, or the government team and network (due within twelve months, so by July 2026), and MKD-CIRT predates the law; the regulator's 'applies' statement gives the legal status only. 'Proposal' in GOV-2026-002-0097 comes from an annex of 2025 opinions, so it is historic rather than a conflict, and the Commission's finding that alignment is incomplete concerns content, not status.
- INT-MKD-0062 Law on Free Access to Public Information: adopted. The source names the law as an enabler of digital government, which shows it exists. Its application is not covered.
- INT-MKD-0071 Law on financial companies: adopted. The Commission says recent changes to the law led to more complaints, which shows the changes exist. It gives no detail of measures put in place, so I do not go beyond adopted.
- INT-MKD-0073 Article 18 of the Constitution (protection of personal data): adopted. The country presentation in the screening report says Article 18 guarantees data protection. This shows a constitutional provision in force, and nothing on its application is mapped here.
- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0203 Rulebook on Security of Personal Data Processing: adopted. The Agency adopted the rulebook on 26 December 2024 and says it began to apply on 1 July 2025. Entry into application is not evidence that controllers or the Agency carried it out, and the only source is the Agency's own report, so I have not used the stated implemented.
- INT-MKD-0217 Rulebook on carrying out supervision (amendment): adopted. The Agency reports adopting and publishing the amendment. No item shows it being applied, and the only source is the Agency's own report.
- INT-MKD-0219 Rulebook on notifying personal data breaches (supplement): adopted. The Agency reports it adopted and published the rulebook. No evidence of application is given.
- INT-MKD-0362 Human Rights Protection in the Age of Artificial Intelligence (project): implemented. The implementing NGO reports that the project produced research and advocacy. It is the only source and describes its own work, so the rating covers delivery only.
- INT-MKD-0433 Amendment of sectoral legislation on misdemeanour sanctions: proposed. The Agency's 2024 plan calls for amending sectoral law, with a target of late 2025 to early 2026. Its April 2026 report describes misdemeanour work but no such amendment in the items reviewed.
- INT-MKD-0444 Collaboration with the European Data Protection Board: proposed. This is an aim in the Agency's strategy. No source reports the collaboration taking place.
- INT-MKD-0471 Harmonisation of laws on the transfer of personal data: proposed. The Agency's strategy says harmonisation is essential and proposes new measures. No completed harmonisation is reported.
- INT-MKD-0472 Harmonisation of legislation in the context of digitisation processes: proposed. The Personal Data Protection Strategy sets this as a task and an indicator from 2025, and the Agency was still calling for transposition in 2026. No source reports laws adopted for this purpose.
- INT-MKD-0498 Methodology for Data Transfer Impact Assessment: proposed. The Agency's strategy plans adoption by the second quarter of 2026. No item, including the Agency's 2025 report, shows it adopted.
- INT-MKD-0499 Methodology for Harmonization of Sectoral Legislation with the Law on Personal Data Protection: adopted. The Agency adopted the Methodology by decision (Official Gazette 38/22). Its reports still call for it to be promoted and for ministries to apply it, and no source shows it being used.
- INT-MKD-0502 National Programme for the Adoption of the Acquis: implemented. The Agency reports activities it carries out under the programme, such as supervisions and by-laws. This is the Agency's own reporting and it covers one chapter only, so it does not support in_practice.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0071 leads to MKD-EU-2023-001-0206: success insufficient_evidence, attribution Medium. The Commission states that the changes led to more complaints, but not what the changes were meant to do. More complaints could reflect more misuse or more willingness to complain, so the outcome cannot be scored as a result.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-GOV-2025-011-0156 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0064: In 2025 the State Audit Office says personal data protection in AI projects is regulated by legislation aligned with European standards, especially the GDPR, while the Commission says adoption of legislation to fully align with the GDPR is still pending.
- MKD-GOV-2025-011-0154 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0064: The State Audit Office's June 2025 report states that national data protection legislation is aligned with EU legislation, while the Commission's 2025 Report states that adoption of legislation to fully align with the General Data Protection Regulation and the Law Enforcement Directive is still pending.
- MKD-GOV-2024-006-0005 vs MKD-EU-2025-001-0062: The Agency's 2025-2030 strategy describes a robust and comprehensive legal framework that aligns closely with the acquis, while the Commission's 2025 Report states that the legal framework for the protection of personal data is insufficient.
- MKD-EU-2025-004-0023 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0102: North Macedonia stated in the screening published in February 2025 that the Law for Electronic Communications is fully aligned with the ePrivacy Directive, while the Commission's 2024 Report states that further efforts are needed for alignment with that Directive.
- MKD-GOV-2025-011-0076 vs MKD-EU-2020-001-0034: The State Audit Office report dates the Law on Personal Data Protection to 2018, while the Commission's 2020 Report states that the new law was adopted in February 2020.
- MKD-IO-2022-002-0058 vs MKD-EU-2022-001-0098: The ITU profile of February 2022 lists the Law on the Security of Network and Information Systems as an existing enabler, while the Commission's 2022 report says the draft of that law has not yet been adopted.
