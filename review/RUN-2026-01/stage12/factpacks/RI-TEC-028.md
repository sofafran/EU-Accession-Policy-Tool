# Fact pack RI-TEC-028: Interoperability platform and data exchange underused

Institutions do not use the national interoperability platform regularly, so administrative data are not shared ex officio between institutions, including for checks such as asset declarations and inspections.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Commission wording that few institutions use the platform runs from 2020 through 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024, which sets the persistent default; the 2025 report records only efforts and repeats the requirement. Connection and transaction figures rose (39 institutions in 2021 to 58 or 63 in 2025; 1.5 million transactions in 2022 to 8 million in 2024), and the Commission confirms the increases in 2023 and 2024, but the counts come from government documents, machine translated and capped at Medium, and most institutions remain outside. The 2020 Commission 'slight increase' and the audit's falling traffic are a noted difference of measure and period, and the chapter scale was context only and not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0121;MKD-EU-2021-001-0103;MKD-EU-2022-001-0110;MKD-EU-2023-001-0152;MKD-EU-2023-001-0153;MKD-EU-2024-001-0028;MKD-EU-2025-001-0003;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0030;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0083;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0060;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0064;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0184;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0185
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The platform operates and some institutions, such as customs, exchange data regularly, but the 2025 Commission recommendation still asks to complete data exchange and the responding upgrades and reforms are mainly proposed; these statuses are themselves proposals. There is a confirmed contradiction: the country told the 2023 screening that the once-only principle is applied, while its 2024 Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio. What remains is regular use of the platform by the institutions obliged to use it, so that evidence is obtained ex officio as the law prescribes. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0004;MKD-EU-2024-007-0016;MKD-EU-2025-001-0166;MKD-EU-2023-003-0029;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0097;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0183;MKD-EU-2024-001-0028
- tractability: Medium (validated). The platform exists and the government controls it, and use has risen somewhat, which favours progress. Against that, the audit found about 1,300 obliged institutions with no costed plan, IT pay at less than half the private sector and no coordinating body, findings the 2025 strategy partly repeats (all machine translated, capped at Medium), and the Commission cites missing software and lack of will. ITU's account of a ministers' council is a noted difference over a different body, leaving how far coordination worked in practice uncertain. Items: MKD-GOV-2022-004-0051;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0054;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0065;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0065;MKD-EU-2023-006-0014;MKD-EU-2024-001-0028;MKD-IO-2022-002-0066;MKD-GOV-2022-004-0017
- priority: High (validated). Data exchange and ex officio evidence sit in a Reform Agenda payment condition, and completing data exchange was a recommendation on public administration reform in 2023, 2024 and 2025, in the main findings in the last two. The 2026 key recommendations on public administration do not repeat it, but the payment condition stands and the 2026 report finds that poor interoperability between databases limits the verification of asset declarations, so the issue remains central to the interoperability and data themes. The large number of items, mostly from one national audit, played no part in this rating. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0036 Law on the Central Population Register: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019, and the audit and government sources describe an operating register that is used as a basis for electronic identification. The evidence of use is descriptive and mostly from government, and the 2025 strategy says the data set must be expanded, so I stop short of in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0052 Law on General Administrative Procedures: adopted. The law has been in force for about nine years, but the Commission finds it is not systematically implemented and the Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio. These findings are preferred to a claim of application, so nothing higher than adopted is supported, although some partial application is likely.
- INT-MKD-0078 National long-term ICT Strategy (unadopted drafts): insufficient_evidence. The drafts themselves were never adopted, but the Commission reported in November 2025 that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan had been adopted, recorded under SMART/MK: 2030 (INT-MKD-0079). No source says whether these drafts became that strategy or were withdrawn, so the analyst should decide whether to merge the entities.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0124 National e-Services Portal (uslugi.gov.mk): in_practice. The State Audit Office, an independent source, confirms the portal has been in use since 2019, but finds low use (0.556 services per registered user), and the 2025 strategy reports 314 services and about 13 of 100 adults registered. Use is real but limited. The Commission and the audit differ on access for people with disabilities, which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0127 Central Population Register: in_practice. The Commission reported the register operational in 2020 and used to crosscheck the 2021 census data, and the State Audit Office describes it as an integrated working database. Later government text that services will draw on it obligatorily shows full use is still to come.
- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0129 Upgrade of the interoperability platform: proposed. The country told the Commission a new platform is under development, while the later digital strategy says an analysis is still needed and lists the upgrade as a measure. Both describe unfinished work.
- INT-MKD-0130 Alignment with the European Interoperability Framework: proposed. Alignment remains a stated plan: the 2023 screening and 2025 strategy describe future or ongoing work, and the Commission still asks for it in 2024 and 2025. The State Audit Office found in 2022 no study of alignment behind the ministry's claim to follow EIF 2.0.
- INT-MKD-0158 Asset and interest declaration system: implemented. The input shows only 2023 plans, but the Commission's 2024 report says the electronic filing system has been operational since January 2024. Links to other institutions were still needed for checks, so I do not rate it in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0163 Electronic platform supporting the EU integration process: proposed. North Macedonia reported the platform as being developed at the 2023 screening. The evidence is reported only and has not been updated since July 2023, so the status at the freeze date is the last known one.
- INT-MKD-0165 E-inspector: implemented. The system exists but coverage was only 10.7% of inspection services in 2021, against a target of 100% in 2026, and the 2025 strategy plans a review of who manages it. All of this is government reporting, and no later coverage figure is given.
- INT-MKD-0218 Rulebook on entry in the list of legal persons (Level 1 certificates): adopted. The State Audit Office item refers to a duty on connected institutions to renew certificates by 29 August 2022, which shows the rule in force. It does not show compliance.
- INT-MKD-0223 Anti-corruption action plan 'Action 21': adopted. The Commission states the Government adopted this plan in March 2021. No source reports on carrying it out.
- INT-MKD-0257 Government conclusions of June and September 2021 on connecting to the interoperability platform: adopted. The State Audit Office records the conclusions and the obligations they set. It also records only six requests for new connections despite them, so there is no evidence that the conclusions were carried out as intended.
- INT-MKD-0258 Government conclusions on multi-year platform maintenance procurement 2021-2024: implemented. The State Audit Office says multi-year maintenance procurements followed these conclusions, and it reviewed the contract reports. The evidence ends in 2022, when the period still had two years to run.
- INT-MKD-0261 Agency electronic access to the Central Register: implemented. The Agency reports its supervision sector has had this access since 2024. This is its own report and gives no usage data.
- INT-MKD-0262 Agency electronic access to the real estate cadastre: proposed. The Agency says a procedure was started at the end of 2024. The item has an OCR flag, and no later item shows access established.
- INT-MKD-0270 Central Registry direct electronic access system for register data: implemented. The country reports that the Central Registry developed this access system. There is no independent evidence of use.
- INT-MKD-0274 Digital interconnection between the Administrative Court, the Higher Administrative Court and other public bodies: implemented. The Commission's 2026 Rule of Law report describes the interconnection as a key advancement that enables electronic exchange. It is one statement and gives no usage data.
- INT-MKD-0275 Digitalisation of civil registry records and electronic extracts: implemented. The screening report repeats the country's statement that civil registry records are digitalised and electronic extracts issued. That is government reporting alone, so I do not use the stated in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0287 Integration and connection of existing systems, platforms and applications: proposed. The digital strategy names this as a strategic priority. It is a statement of intent.
- INT-MKD-0306 Node communication client: proposed. The 2023 strategy says using the client will allow easier connection. No item shows it deployed.
- INT-MKD-0317 Single Sign-On System: implemented. The Commission reports in 2026 that Single Sign-On was integrated across service providers. This follows the State Audit Office's 2024 finding that access to e-services did not go through the system; the two describe different dates, so they are a change over time and not a conflict. No item measures use of the system.
- INT-MKD-0325 Upgrade for interoperability of the Government electronic platform, NPAA platform, ENER, e-Parliament and e-Official Gazette: proposed. North Macedonia reported the upgrade as initiated at the 2023 screening. The evidence is reported only and has not been updated since July 2023, so this is the last known status.
- INT-MKD-0374 Training on institutional horizontal systems (A 2.2.1.7): proposed. This is an action plan row in the 2025 strategy. No item shows training delivered.
- INT-MKD-0420 Alignment of the legal framework with the digitalisation law package: proposed. The strategy says the state will focus on this alignment, and the audit's figure of about 140 unaligned laws is the last count available. No source reports completion.
- INT-MKD-0442 Change of the platform traffic topology: insufficient_evidence. The audit says the change was planned for 2022 through a procurement. No later item says whether it was made.
- INT-MKD-0445 Connection of the open data portal with the interoperability platform: proposed. The strategy says an analysis will be carried out on making this connection. No source shows it done.
- INT-MKD-0480 Integration of the National Spatial Data Infrastructure and Geoinformation Register of Building Land with national e-services: proposed. The 2025 strategy states this as the focus of a measure. No item shows integration done.
- INT-MKD-0506 Once-only principle: unclear. The screening report states the principle is applied, but this is the country's own account. The Reform Agenda and SMART/MK say institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio and that no effective mechanism exists to apply the principle; these later findings weigh against it, and the conflict is for the reviewer. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0515 Register of Beneficial Owners (online inquiry and automatic exchange of information): implemented. North Macedonia says at screening that data are accessible with automatic exchange and online inquiry. It is reported only, with no independent evidence of use, so I stop short of in practice.
- INT-MKD-0537 interoperability framework: implemented. The Commission reports the framework's scope increased in 2020 and that institutions were encouraged to use it in 2022. Later platform data, including customs exchanges reported by the Commission, concern the platform and do not show the framework itself in general use.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0257 leads to MKD-GOV-2022-004-0049: success failed, attribution Low. The conclusions aimed to connect about 1,300 institutions, and the audit found only six new requests and no resourcing analysis or timeframe. The only outcome evidence is a State Audit Office report, which the register files under the government family: it is external audit and not government self-reporting, but the reviewers should confirm that reading, and it is machine translated and capped at Medium. Connections rose later, but the Commission still found most institutions not using the platform in 2023, and the audit came about a year after the conclusions. The State Audit Office is treated as independent, as validated at G2.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2023-003-0029 vs MKD-GOV-2024-001-0097: North Macedonia tells the 2023 screening that the once-only principle is applied, while its 2024 Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio as the law prescribes.
