# Fact pack RI-TEC-024: Security of 5G networks not ensured

The EU 5G Cybersecurity Toolbox, including supplier risk assessment and restrictions on high-risk suppliers, is not fully transposed or applied when 5G networks are deployed.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission has named the 5G toolbox in its recommendations in four successive reports (2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025, two of these items being classified under the framework issue), and its November 2025 wording still asks the country to continue, so the default of persistent applies; the chapter scale is context only and was not decisive. Legal steps did occur (2021 amendments, 2022 by-laws, the June 2025 law with supplier risk provisions), but the screening report says not all the 2021 amendments had been implemented. A reviewer could read the 2025 law as the start of improvement, but it began to apply only in June 2026 and no later EU item assesses it. - unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The latest EU items ask the country to continue implementing the toolbox, including assessing suppliers and restricting high-risk ones, and the Reform Agenda requires the security by-law fully implemented by December 2027; the 2025 law is proposed as adopted, the by-law and supplier assessment as proposed, and the 2021 amendments as reversed because the new law replaces them, all of which are proposals for review. What remains is adopting and applying the by-law and actually carrying out supplier risk assessments and any restrictions, since the law gives the power (machine translated, capped at Medium) but no item shows it used. The Reform Agenda's statement that the step is largely achieved sits against its own partial compliance baseline and the Commission's later wording, and it appears to rest mainly on spectrum and legislation, so it is not treated as closure; the Commission's finding that this law's reform step was not achieved concerns the Code and the Gigabit Act and not the toolbox, and the law allows 15 months for by-laws, so the finding does not decide this gap. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0145;MKD-EU-2025-004-0073;MKD-EU-2024-007-0008;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0065;MKD-GOV-2025-008-0022;MKD-GOV-2025-008-0031;MKD-EU-2024-008-0029;MKD-EU-2025-004-0048;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0066;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0067;MKD-EU-2025-011-0021;MKD-EU-2025-011-0017
- priority: High (validated). Toolbox compliance is a Reform Agenda step and has been named in the Commission's key recommendations in four successive reports, and the enlargement communication presents it as a basis of secure digital infrastructure. The issue is narrow, one clause among several in the chapter's key recommendation, so centrality is the weakest part of this rating and a reviewer could reasonably set Medium. The small number of items did not lower the rating, as volume is not a criterion. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0008;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0065;MKD-EU-2023-001-0121;MKD-EU-2024-001-0015;MKD-EU-2025-001-0022;MKD-EU-2022-001-0087;MKD-EU-2025-002-0011;MKD-EU-2024-008-0037
- tractability: Medium (validated). Few actors are needed, mainly the regulator and the Ministry working with operators, the law already provides the power and by-laws under the 2021 amendments were reported adopted by the following year, which all favour progress. However, not all the 2021 amendments were implemented four years on, a supplier assessment planned from 2023 is not shown as done, and operators complained of having no consultation in 2021. The evidence does not show what restrictions would cost operators, so the rating is not set higher. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0020 Amendments to the Law on Electronic Communications (April 2021): reversed. The 2025 Law on Electronic Communications (INT-MKD-0023) repeals the earlier law, listing Official Gazette 92/21 among its amendments, from the day it starts to apply, 1 June 2026. The successor is therefore in application at the freeze date.
- INT-MKD-0022 By-laws implementing the April 2021 amendments to the Law on Electronic Communications: adopted. The Commission reports the regulator adopted the by-laws. Their parent law ceased to apply on 1 June 2026, new by-laws are due, and no item says what happened to these, so the reviewer should note that.
- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The Commission reports that the new law became fully applicable in June 2026, which settles the date of application. No item reports by-laws or measures taken under it, and the Commission's statement that it strengthens consumer protection and competition is a stated outcome, not evidence of application in practice.
- INT-MKD-0100 Reform 2.2.2.1 Secure Digital infrastructures roll-out: proposed. The reform as a whole is a Reform Agenda plan for infrastructure, 5G and related law. A new Law on Electronic Communications was adopted in June 2025 as one part, but no item shows the secure infrastructure rolled out.
- INT-MKD-0189 5G spectrum assignment: in_practice. The Commission reports that approvals for 5G frequencies were granted and that 5G coverage keeps expanding, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. One frequency remained to be tendered in 2024, and that tender is rated separately as INT-MKD-0512.
- INT-MKD-0190 5G roll-out: in_practice. The input items are Reform Agenda plans, but the Commission records 5G reaching Skopje and coverage continuing to expand, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. The Reform Agenda step also covers the security toolbox and one remaining frequency, which are separate and not shown complete.
- INT-MKD-0191 5G security bylaw: proposed. The by-law is a planned measure, with full implementation in the Reform Agenda due by December 2027. No item shows it adopted.
- INT-MKD-0524 Risk profile assessment of network equipment suppliers, manufacturers and operators in annual supervision: proposed. North Macedonia says the assessment is to be included from 2023, and the 2025 law requires periodic assessment, but the Commission still asks for it in November 2025. No item shows an assessment carried out.
