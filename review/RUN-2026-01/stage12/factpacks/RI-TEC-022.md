# Fact pack RI-TEC-022: Cybersecurity legal and strategic framework incomplete

Network and information security law is not fully aligned with the NIS2 Directive and the Cybersecurity Act, the national cybersecurity strategy is late or fragmented in implementation, and responsibilities are split without effective coordination.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). Commission items moved from no further progress on the 2019 draft law in 2021, through repeated calls to adopt the law and strategy in 2022, 2023 and 2024, to adoption of the strategy in January 2025 and the law in June 2025, which the 2025 report counts as some progress. The chapter scale (limited progress 2021 to 2024, some progress 2025) is context only and was not decisive, and the improvement lies in the legal and strategic framework, since no item shows better coordination after the 2023 call, the 2024 audit finding of a Council that met once and the 2025 strategy's own description of coordination as critical. A February 2022 ITU item lists the law as existing while the Commission's 2022 report says it was still a draft, and later Commission and audit items support the draft reading; the differing dates for the Council (2019 or 2020) and the action plan's end (2027 or 2028) do not affect this value. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0095;MKD-EU-2021-001-0089;MKD-EU-2022-001-0098;MKD-EU-2023-001-0128;MKD-EU-2023-001-0148;MKD-EU-2023-001-0146;MKD-EU-2024-001-0079;MKD-EU-2024-001-0095;MKD-EU-2025-011-0002;MKD-EU-2025-011-0008;MKD-EU-2025-001-0020;MKD-GOV-2024-008-0038;MKD-GOV-2024-008-0006;MKD-GOV-2025-005-0027;MKD-IO-2022-002-0058;MKD-EU-2021-001-0036;MKD-GOV-2024-008-0093;MKD-GOV-2025-006-0001;MKD-EU-2025-011-0010
- priority: High (validated). The law and strategy, an operational competent authority and the NIS2 frameworks are each Reform Agenda payment steps, and NIS2 or cybersecurity legislation recurs in the Commission's key recommendations from 2022 to 2025. The issue is the legal base on which the rest of the cybersecurity theme rests. The rating rests on these EU requirements and not on the large number of national plan and audit items. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0002;MKD-EU-2024-007-0004;MKD-EU-2024-007-0005;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0003;MKD-EU-2022-001-0087;MKD-EU-2023-001-0121;MKD-EU-2024-001-0015;MKD-EU-2025-001-0022
- tractability: Medium (validated). Much of what remains is drafting that the Ministry controls, and adoption followed within a year of the Reform Agenda step, which favours progress; against this, the first draft law stalled from 2019 to 2025, the Council barely functioned and no item shows the by-laws adopted after their deadline. Coordination needs many bodies, the strategy itself names political support and budget as key risks, and one reporting obligation to ENISA cannot be met outside the EU. Tractability is therefore higher for legal alignment than for coordination. Items: MKD-EU-2025-011-0004;MKD-GOV-2025-007-0054;MKD-EU-2021-001-0095;MKD-GOV-2024-008-0006;MKD-GOV-2024-008-0038;MKD-GOV-2025-005-0098;MKD-GOV-2025-005-0099;MKD-GOV-2025-005-0030;MKD-EU-2025-004-0094
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The NIS2-aligned network and information security law took effect in January 2026, a National Cybersecurity Council has been set up, and the 2026 report finds that the framework contributed to NIS2 alignment, but the Commission still asks for further alignment with EU cybersecurity rules and finds the law misaligned with NIS2 on personal data protection because the data protection agency's opinion was disregarded. No 2026 item for the country reports the by-laws, the entity lists or the disclosure and crisis frameworks as adopted, and the 2026 Communication's calls on cybersecurity concern all enlargement countries and do not alone support a finding here. What remains is the outstanding NIS2 provisions, including those on personal data, Cybersecurity Act certification alignment, the by-laws and entity lists, the disclosure and crisis frameworks, working coordination and application to the sectors deferred until accession; the law's text is machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The Commission reports that the new law became fully applicable in June 2026, which settles the date of application. No item reports by-laws or measures taken under it, and the Commission's statement that it strengthens consumer protection and competition is a stated outcome, not evidence of application in practice.
- INT-MKD-0031 Law amending the Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services 2025: adopted. Parliament adopted the law on 27 June 2025 and the Commission confirms this, while noting it does not fully align with the EU regulation. Article 27 of the law says it will start to apply only from 1 June 2027, so it cannot be implemented at the freeze date. That provision is mapped in the data to the parent law INT-MKD-0030.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0040 Law on the Use of Public Sector Data: adopted. An international report and the screening report treat the law as in force, and the screening report calls it partially aligned with the EU directive. No source reports how it is applied.
- INT-MKD-0042 Law on Security of Network and Information Systems 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms the law in force from January 2026 and aligned with NIS2, though the Personal Data Protection Agency's opinion on it was disregarded. The Commission records a government computer security incident response team and a National Cybersecurity Council, but attributes them to the cybersecurity strategy and does not say they are the bodies the law requires; no item reports the by-laws or the lists of entities.
- INT-MKD-0043 Draft Law on Security of Networks and Information Systems 2019: insufficient_evidence. The 2019 draft was never adopted and a law on the same subject (INT-MKD-0042) was adopted in June 2025, but no source says this draft was withdrawn or became that law. Its status after April 2024 is not stated.
- INT-MKD-0044 Draft Law on Security of Network and Information Systems and Digital Transformation 2023: insufficient_evidence. A Law on Security of Network and Information Systems (INT-MKD-0042) was adopted in June 2025, but no source says it is this draft or that this draft was withdrawn. The draft has no stated status after February 2025.
- INT-MKD-0045 15 bylaws to the Law on Security of Networks and Information Systems 2025: proposed. The Commission says the 15 bylaws fall under later steps of the reform. No source up to the freeze date reports them as adopted.
- INT-MKD-0046 Agency for Security of Network and Information Systems and Digital Transformation: insufficient_evidence. The draft that provided for this agency has no stated outcome, and the 2025 law names the Ministry and the Agency for Electronic Communications as the competent authorities. No source says the agency was formally dropped.
- INT-MKD-0047 Law on Archival Material and Archival Activity 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms adoption on 27 June 2025 and publication on 4 July 2025, and treats the central document management system as still to be completed. A digital strategy row on preparing a new law is a plan row and does not contradict adoption. The link between the earlier Reform Agenda items and this law is inferred, as the entity note says.
- INT-MKD-0051 Amendments to the Law on the Organisation and Work of State Administration Bodies 2024: implemented. The amendments were adopted on 10 June 2024 and the Commission confirms the new Ministry of Digital Transformation was set up. The items show the institution in place, not how well it works.
- INT-MKD-0062 Law on Free Access to Public Information: adopted. The source names the law as an enabler of digital government, which shows it exists. Its application is not covered.
- INT-MKD-0074 Amendments to the Energy Law 2022: implemented. The State Audit Office records the 2022 amendment giving the regulator a cybersecurity competence, and that the regulator adopted Cybersecurity Rules on 8 June 2023. This is a measure by the responsible body, but no item shows the rules being enforced.
- INT-MKD-0078 National long-term ICT Strategy (unadopted drafts): insufficient_evidence. The drafts themselves were never adopted, but the Commission reported in November 2025 that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan had been adopted, recorded under SMART/MK: 2030 (INT-MKD-0079). No source says whether these drafts became that strategy or were withdrawn, so the analyst should decide whether to merge the entities.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: implemented. The Commission reports that, in line with the long-term ICT strategy and its action plan, the Ministry of Digital Transformation widened participation in data exchange and integrated Single Sign-On across service providers. This shows measures under the strategy being delivered, but no item measures delivery against its targets, and the link between the Commission's 'long-term ICT strategy' and SMART/MK rests on timing.
- INT-MKD-0083 National Cybersecurity Strategy 2018-2022: reversed. The strategy ran out at the end of 2022 and its successor is the Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028 (INT-MKD-0085). No source says it was repealed, so reversed is used by analogy with a replaced law because the scale has no value for expiry. The Government's later strategy says implementation was not fully achieved.
- INT-MKD-0084 Draft National Cybersecurity Strategy 2023-2027: proposed. This draft was published for consultation in December 2023 and no source reports its adoption under this title. A 2025-2028 strategy was adopted instead, but the sources do not say it is the outcome of this draft, so the last stated status is kept.
- INT-MKD-0085 Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028: implemented. The Commission now reports that implementation of the strategy continued and that, under it, a National Cybersecurity Council, a Cybersecurity Department and a government incident response centre were set up, so the Commission and the regulator's earlier statement that the strategy is applied now agree. No item reports the half-yearly reports the strategy promises or measures use of the new bodies.
- INT-MKD-0086 Action Plan 2025-2027 for implementing the Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028: adopted. The plan's own rows are planned activities, but the Commission states that the Government adopted the accompanying action plan on 21 January 2025, so the plan as a whole is adopted (the Commission labels it 2025-2028, the document 2025-2027). Only one row is shown delivered, the cyber security law, adopted in June 2025. No item reports on other rows, including those due by end 2025, so nothing supports implemented.
- INT-MKD-0087 Digital Transformation Roadmap 2024-2030: adopted. The Government adopted the Roadmap by conclusion, as the State Audit Office records. The 2025 strategy says its principles lack enforcement mechanisms, and no item shows the Roadmap itself carried out. Sources differ on the date (late 2023 and January 2024), which does not change the status.
- INT-MKD-0092 National Operational Broadband Plan 2019-2029: implemented. The plan remains valid, and measures have been carried out in part: the Commission records 5G expanding to Skopje and biannual Broadband Competence Office reports. Commission reports for 2021 and 2022 record delay, and the Office's own report finds most 5G goals met but none of the three measures on 100 Mbps subscriptions implemented. These sources describe different parts of the plan, so they do not conflict.
- INT-MKD-0099 Reform 2.2.1.1 Setting up a comprehensive framework for cyber resilience: adopted. The items in the input are plans, but later Commission evidence shows the Law on Security of Networks and Information Systems adopted in June 2025 and the cybersecurity strategy adopted in January 2025, and the step accepted as achieved although alignment is not complete. The 15 by-laws and the operational authority fall under later steps, and no item shows them delivered. The law itself is a separate entity.
- INT-MKD-0114 NIS2 competent authority: adopted. The 2025 law designates the Ministry and the regulator as competent authorities, and the cyber strategy names the Ministry. No item shows the authority staffed or performing supervisory checks, the June 2026 Reform Agenda test.
- INT-MKD-0116 NIS2 CVD and crisis management frameworks: proposed. These frameworks appear as a Reform Agenda step due by December 2026, with a baseline of zero in 2024. No source shows them in place or in use.
- INT-MKD-0117 National Cyber Security Council: unclear. The Council was formally established, but the Commission says it was not active and the State Audit Office finds it met once and calls it non-functioning, while North Macedonia told the 2023 screening it was in place. I give more weight to the audit and say so here; the 2025 cyber strategy proposes merging it into another council. Sources also date its formation differently (2019 and July 2020). Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0118 National ICT council: insufficient_evidence. The only evidence is two passages in one ITU profile of February 2022, describing a council monitoring projects. Sources do not say whether it is the National Cyber Security Council, and no later item mentions it. The difference with the audit's absent body was noted at G5 as different objects, so it is not treated as a conflict here.
- INT-MKD-0119 National Council for Digital Transformation: unclear. The Council was established in September 2022, and the 2025 strategy describes it as formed to plan and coordinate digitalisation. The State Audit Office could not confirm any activities in 2023 and reports an absence of activities and results, so a named body exists but is said not to function. I give the audit more weight than the government's description, and there is no later evidence of activity. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0206 Cybersecurity Rules of the Energy and Water Services Regulatory Commission 2023: adopted. The State Audit Office states the Commission adopted the rules on 8 June 2023. No source reports on their application.
- INT-MKD-0210 National Taxonomy for Cyber Incidents: adopted. The State Audit Office records that the Government adopted the taxonomy in 2020. No item shows it applied in classifying incidents.
- INT-MKD-0220 Rulebook on standards and rules for the security of information systems (repeal): reversed. The State Audit Office says the rulebook was put out of force by the Law on Electronic Governance and Electronic Services, adopted in 2019. That law is the successor named; the audit notes that no replacement rulebook covers security standards.
- INT-MKD-0224 National strategy on building resilience and confronting hybrid threats (2021-2025) and action plan: implemented. The Commission states that the country has initiated, and to a degree put into operation, strategies for countering cyber and hybrid threats. The item does not name this strategy, whose term ran to 2025, so the link rests on the classification, and no item reports specific measures under it.
- INT-MKD-0254 Information with proposed measures to improve information system security 2023: in_practice. The Government adopted the information in February 2023, and the State Audit Office found 13% of institutions had not taken the measures, which implies most had. This is audit evidence and not government reporting.
- INT-MKD-0434 Amendments for coordinated response to large-scale cyber-attacks and crises: proposed. The screening report says these amendments are planned to take effect by 2027, and no item shows them made.
- INT-MKD-0548 MKD-GOV-CSIRT (Government Computer Security Incident Response Team): implemented. The Commission reports that the Ministry of Digital Transformation established the team, invested in an incident response platform and trained staff. No item reports incidents handled, and the Commission still asks for more staff in the national centre.
