# Fact pack RI-TEC-015: Base registers incomplete, unreliable or not digitalised

Key registers, above all the population register, hold data of poor quality or are little used, and many institutional, property and intellectual property registers are not yet digitalised or kept up to date.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The Commission repeated the need to improve population register data in 2021, 2022 and 2023, and the same recommendation to complete the digitalisation of institutions' registers in 2023, 2024 and 2025, with use of the register still limited in 2024. The 2025 mention of efforts does not change the recommendation, so the default of persistent applies. The Chapter 10 scale is context only and was not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2021-001-0106;MKD-EU-2022-001-0007;MKD-EU-2022-001-0112;MKD-EU-2023-001-0002;MKD-EU-2023-001-0155;MKD-EU-2024-001-0002;MKD-EU-2024-001-0026;MKD-EU-2025-001-0003;MKD-EU-2025-001-0004
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The population register is proposed as in practice, but the register platform, the Register of Registers and the wider e-services reform are all proposed only, and these statuses are themselves proposals for G6. What remains is to complete the digitalisation of institutional, municipal property and intellectual property registers and to raise the quality and use of population register data. The fully digital National Real Estate Register and the Commission's analogue property registers are a noted difference of register and level, not a conflict, and the machine-translated strategy on low use is capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0004;MKD-EU-2024-001-0026;MKD-EU-2025-003-0165;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0030;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0077;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0233;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0067;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0093;MKD-EU-2023-003-0085;MKD-EU-2023-003-0086;MKD-EU-2022-001-0036
- priority: High (validated). The Commission's main findings gave the same key recommendation to complete register digitalisation in 2023, 2024 and 2025, and base registers are central to the digital government theme. The recommendation is one clause of a wider one on administrative procedures and is shared with the data exchange and e-services issues, which reviewers may weigh. The Reform Agenda payment step on a register of charges is flagged as doubtful for this theme and was not relied on. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0002;MKD-EU-2024-001-0002;MKD-EU-2025-001-0004;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0093
- tractability: Medium (validated). The operating population register shows that delivery is possible, and a register platform was begun with EU support. Against that, completion needs many institutions and municipalities to act, and the strategy's baseline of no digital registers in 2022 shows how much remains. The two strategy items are machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0003;MKD-EU-2022-001-0017;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0025;MKD-GOV-2023-002-0069;MKD-EU-2022-001-0036

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0030 Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services: in_practice. This holds for the 2019 framework only, which has applied since 1 September 2020 (electronic documents from 20 December 2019): the Commission's May 2026 assessment records by-laws from 2020, two trust service providers accredited in 2021 and registered, and ministry supervisory inspections. The June 2025 eIDAS 2 amendment, including the digital wallet, is in force but applies only from 1 June 2027, so that part is adopted and not applied at the freeze date, and no item shows its by-laws were adopted. Use is narrow: the Commission found the trusted list step not achieved and the Government still calls for full application of the law.
- INT-MKD-0035 Law on Electronic Management and Electronic Services: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019 and some rulebooks and the interoperability platform are in place, but the State Audit Office found only 2.93% of obliged institutions connected and parent laws not aligned. I rely on the audit over the government's description of the framework, so this is not in_practice. The 2025 strategy still calls for full application.
- INT-MKD-0036 Law on the Central Population Register: implemented. The law was adopted in 2019, and the audit and government sources describe an operating register that is used as a basis for electronic identification. The evidence of use is descriptive and mostly from government, and the 2025 strategy says the data set must be expanded, so I stop short of in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0037 Draft Law amending the Law on the Central Population Register: proposed. The amendment appears as a planned action in 2023 and in the Agency's 2026 list of proposals on which it gave an opinion. No item shows it adopted.
- INT-MKD-0052 Law on General Administrative Procedures: adopted. The law has been in force for about nine years, but the Commission finds it is not systematically implemented and the Reform Agenda says institutions still do not gather evidence ex officio. These findings are preferred to a claim of application, so nothing higher than adopted is supported, although some partial application is likely.
- INT-MKD-0088 Public Administration Reform Strategy 2023-2030: adopted. The State Audit Office records the strategy and action plan as adopted. The measures in these items are stated as aims, and no item shows them carried out as a whole.
- INT-MKD-0101 Reform 2.2.2.3 Widen the offer of public e-services: proposed. This is a Reform Agenda target to raise e-services by 50% by June 2027, and the Commission still described it as a measure in April 2026. No item reports the target met. The stated 2024 baseline differs between sources (392 and 264), which does not affect the status.
- INT-MKD-0127 Central Population Register: in_practice. The Commission reported the register operational in 2020 and used to crosscheck the 2021 census data, and the State Audit Office describes it as an integrated working database. Later government text that services will draw on it obligatorily shows full use is still to come.
- INT-MKD-0128 Interoperability platform (MIM): in_practice. The State Audit Office found the platform operational but used by only a small share of institutions, and the Commission confirms use by customs and few institutions overall. The 2025 strategy gives 63 connected institutions, of which 37 actively exchange data. I rely on the audit and Commission over the screening statements that it simply functions.
- INT-MKD-0164 E-Authorisation register: proposed. The latest source lists the register among applications developed or to be developed and describes it in the future tense. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0168 Platform for digitalising registers: proposed. The 2023 strategy said the platform would come into use during 2023, but no later source confirms it, and in 2025 the Commission still asks for register digitalisation to be completed. A target is not delivery.
- INT-MKD-0169 Register of Registers: proposed. Both items are action plan rows about preparing recommendations and metadata for the register, one from 2023 and one from 2025. No item shows the register established.
- INT-MKD-0170 Digital public register of charges: proposed. The register is a Reform Agenda step with a methodology due by the end of 2025. No source reports the register or the methodology as delivered.
- INT-MKD-0270 Central Registry direct electronic access system for register data: implemented. The country reports that the Central Registry developed this access system. There is no independent evidence of use.
- INT-MKD-0275 Digitalisation of civil registry records and electronic extracts: implemented. The screening report repeats the country's statement that civil registry records are digitalised and electronic extracts issued. That is government reporting alone, so I do not use the stated in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0300 National Real Estate Register (digitalised central database): implemented. The only evidence is the country's own account in the screening report that the register is digitalised and professional users are connected. That is not independent evidence of use, so the stated in_practice is lowered.
- INT-MKD-0324 Unique Electronic Number as primary identifier (A 3.1.5.4): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row on changing the legal framework, with no evidence of delivery.
- INT-MKD-0357 Digitalisation of the State Office for Industrial Property database and online services: proposed. The Commission says the office has made efforts to speed up digitalisation, which is work under way with no stated completion.
- INT-MKD-0365 M 4.2.2 Increasing the number of e-services: proposed. This is a measure in the public administration strategy, worded as an expected effect, and no item reports delivery.
- INT-MKD-0440 Central Population Register upgrade analysis (A 3.1.5.2): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0449 Data standards and data governance (A 3.1.4.5): proposed. This is a SMART/MK action plan row, with no evidence of delivery.
- INT-MKD-0478 Identification of laws to be aligned (A 3.1.1.1): proposed. This is an action plan row in the digital strategy.
- INT-MKD-0515 Register of Beneficial Owners (online inquiry and automatic exchange of information): implemented. North Macedonia says at screening that data are accessible with automatic exchange and online inquiry. It is reported only, with no independent evidence of use, so I stop short of in practice.
