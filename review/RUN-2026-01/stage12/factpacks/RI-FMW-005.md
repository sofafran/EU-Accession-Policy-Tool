# Fact pack RI-FMW-005: Employment service capacity for EURES and labour mobility

The public employment service lacks trained staff or adequate IT and data systems to take part in EU labour mobility networks and vacancy exchange.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was context only. The key recommendation to strengthen the Employment Service Agency is nearly unchanged in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, which sets the default, and the 2025 report says the previous recommendations were not implemented. Training took place in 2023 and the screening report calls capacity satisfactory, but no earlier rating exists to show a rise from. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0007;MKD-EU-2025-001-0012;MKD-EU-2025-001-0011;MKD-EU-2023-001-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0064
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). What remains is updated EURES training after staff turnover, with connection of private employment agencies and later ESCO mapping. The Commission finds the main IT elements in place and capacity satisfactory, and capacity building and IT upgrades are proposed here as implemented while further strengthening is only proposed; all these statuses are proposals. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0012;MKD-EU-2025-003-0065;MKD-EU-2025-003-0070;MKD-EU-2025-003-0055;MKD-EU-2025-003-0063;MKD-EU-2023-001-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0061;MKD-EU-2025-003-0060
- priority: Medium (validated). Agency capacity is named in the key recommendation in three reports, so the requirement is repeated. It is not central now, because the Commission finds capacity satisfactory and the IT elements in place. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0007;MKD-EU-2025-001-0012;MKD-EU-2025-003-0063;MKD-EU-2025-003-0064
- tractability: High (validated). The remaining work rests mainly with one agency, does not depend on other countries and builds on training and IT delivered before. Past capacity building is reported by the Commission, not only by the government. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0096;MKD-EU-2025-003-0063;MKD-EU-2025-003-0064;MKD-EU-2025-003-0065

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0159 Employment Service Agency web portal: implemented. An international report says the IT system allowed online delivery of services, and the Agency and the screening report describe the portal. None of these gives independent evidence of how it is used, so the stated in_practice is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0160 Upgrade of the Employment Service Agency IT system: implemented. The input shows only a 2025 plan, but the Commission's 2026 assessment records an upgrade of the Agency's software deployed for the law on registering work engagement. The upgrade for data from private agencies and EURES exchange in the original plan is not shown, and whether both belong to one entity is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0200 Capacity building on EURES and the European Labour Authority: implemented. The Commission reports that capacity building for civil servants on EURES and the European Labour Authority took place. No source shows the knowledge being used, and the Commission says EURES amendments would be implemented only on accession.
- INT-MKD-0371 Strengthening of Employment Agency capacities: proposed. The screening report lists this among planned steps, from the country's account. No item shows it carried out.
- INT-MKD-0375 Twinning project training on EURES (2014-2015): implemented. The training was carried out in 2014 and 2015, according to the country's account, and it needs updating because of staff turnover. It is outside the evidence window and is recorded as reported.
- INT-MKD-0436 Analysis of harmonisation needs: proposed. The screening report says the plan foresees this analysis. No source reports it done.
- INT-MKD-0497 Memorandum of cooperation on access and exchange of information: adopted. The screening report, from the country's account, says the memorandum regulates access and exchange of information. No item shows it used.
