# Fact pack RI-TEC-019: Data protection authority not consulted and its advice not followed

The data protection authority is not systematically consulted on sector and implementing laws, its recommendations are often not implemented, and parliament does not follow up its reports.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: worsening (validated). The Commission's finding was essentially unchanged from 2020 to 2024, with some progress noted in 2021, and then changed in 2025, when the Government removed the Agency from the mandatory consultation list and Parliament amended the law without consulting it. The sub-area rating of no progress in 2025 matches this issue and the chapter scale was not decisive. The Agency's report confirms the deletion in machine-translated text capped at Medium confidence, and its count of opinions (5 for 2024, 15 for 2025) does not show that consultation improved. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0049;MKD-EU-2021-001-0031;MKD-EU-2022-001-0028;MKD-EU-2023-001-0032;MKD-EU-2024-001-0043;MKD-EU-2024-001-0045;MKD-EU-2025-001-0065;MKD-EU-2025-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0070;MKD-GOV-2025-010-0035;MKD-GOV-2025-010-0036;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0064
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). No intervention is recorded as responding to this issue, and the one recent measure, the removal from the consultation list, has a proposed status of implemented and runs the opposite way; these statuses are themselves proposals for G6. What remains is systematic consultation of the Agency on sector and implementing laws, follow-up of its recommendations and follow-up of its reports in Parliament. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0070;MKD-EU-2024-001-0045;MKD-GOV-2026-002-0063
- priority: Medium (validated). The finding recurs in every report from 2020 to 2025 and the Commission called the 2025 removal concerning, but the 2023 list of areas requiring alignment does not include it and it is not in the main findings. It is a repeated requirement that supports the authority's role rather than being central to the theme, and reviewers may weigh the 2025 reversal differently. Items: MKD-EU-2022-001-0028;MKD-EU-2023-003-0077;MKD-EU-2024-001-0043;MKD-EU-2025-001-0068;MKD-EU-2023-001-0028
- tractability: Medium (validated). Restoring the consultation duty is a change to the Government's own Rules of Procedure, which the Agency identifies precisely and which depends on no one outside the country. Getting ministries to follow advice and Parliament to act on reports is harder, and the Government removed the duty recently. The Agency's items are machine translated and capped at Medium confidence. Items: MKD-GOV-2026-002-0063;MKD-GOV-2025-010-0035;MKD-EU-2025-001-0068;MKD-EU-2024-001-0044;MKD-EU-2024-001-0045

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0012 Law on Personal Data Protection (amendment on transfers to NATO countries): adopted. The Commission and the Agency's report both state that the Assembly amended the law to allow transfers to NATO countries without prior consultation. No item shows transfers made under it.
- INT-MKD-0018 Strategy for the Implementation of the Right to Personal Data Protection 2025-2030: implemented. The Agency reports the strategy was adopted in 2024 and implementation began in 2025. The Commission's finding that budget and consultation problems negatively affected implementation is preferred to the Agency's account on how far it has gone, but it also treats implementation as under way.
- INT-MKD-0042 Law on Security of Network and Information Systems 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms the law in force from January 2026 and aligned with NIS2, though the Personal Data Protection Agency's opinion on it was disregarded. The Commission records a government computer security incident response team and a National Cybersecurity Council, but attributes them to the cybersecurity strategy and does not say they are the bodies the law requires; no item reports the by-laws or the lists of entities.
- INT-MKD-0054 Amendments to the Electoral Code on biometric voter identification: adopted. The amendments were adopted in April 2021, and the Commission notes that the Personal Data Protection Agency was not consulted beforehand. No item shows biometric identification being used at an election.
- INT-MKD-0259 'StopKorona' contact tracing application: insufficient_evidence. The only item says the Agency was consulted on preparing the application in 2020. It does not show the application was launched or used, so the recorded status of implemented is not supported.
- INT-MKD-0517 Removal of the PDPA from the mandatory consultation list: implemented. The Commission reports that the government removed the Agency from the mandatory consultation list, and the Agency's 2026 report repeats this.
