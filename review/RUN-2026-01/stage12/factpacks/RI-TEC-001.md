# Fact pack RI-TEC-001: Artificial intelligence framework not aligned or supported by structures

The rules on artificial intelligence are not aligned with the EU Artificial Intelligence Act, or the enforcement structures and wider capacity needed to apply them and support trustworthy uptake are not in place.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: new (validated). The EU requirement first appears in 2025 (AI Act alignment and enforcement structures) and the only later EU item, in 2026, concerns the AI Factory Antenna, so there is no repeated EU wording to compare; the chapter scale is context only and was not decisive. Separately, national sources show the gap is older than the EU ask: a 2024 government plan mentions AI as a future need, and the 2025 audit traces a stalled strategy to a 2021 working group, but these are one-year snapshots rather than a trend. The value therefore means new as an EU requirement, not new as a national gap. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0149;MKD-EU-2026-005-0002;MKD-GOV-2024-001-0024;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0009
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). Against the single EU alignment ask of 2025, the responding AI legislation and AI strategy are still proposed, and the adopted ICT strategy and signed Antenna grant do not supply alignment or enforcement; these statuses are themselves proposals. What remains is alignment with the AI Act and the enforcement structures it needs, none of which yet exists. National sources confirm the absence (machine-translated audit, capped at Medium), and the audit's statement that data protection law is aligned with the GDPR conflicts with the Commission's statement that full alignment is pending, which is left unresolved. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0149;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0206;MKD-GOV-2025-004-0207;MKD-EU-2025-001-0127;MKD-EU-2026-005-0001;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0089;MKD-RES-2025-001-0010;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0156;MKD-EU-2025-001-0064
- priority: Medium (validated). The rating rests only on the EU layer: one body-text invitation in the 2025 report to align fully with the AI Act, plus a 2026 readout that encourages outreach and does not mention the Act. It is not a benchmark or Reform Agenda payment condition, is absent from the 2025 Chapter 10 key recommendations and is not repeated, so it cannot be High; Medium reflects a requirement central to the AI theme with thin support. The much larger national audit, strategy and research evidence (over 200 of 229 items, mostly from a few documents) describes the gap but was given no weight here. Items: MKD-EU-2025-001-0149;MKD-EU-2026-005-0002;MKD-EU-2025-001-0022;MKD-EU-2025-001-0023
- tractability: Medium (validated). Drafting is within government control, with the EU Act as a template and AI legislation already listed in an adopted action plan, but enforcement structures need a designated authority, specialist staff and funding, across several bodies. The record is weak: one working group meeting since 2021, a digital assistant no longer functioning and no line of accountability between the ministry and the innovation fund, though a data protection law gives some base and an EU-funded Antenna grant is signed. This leans on machine-translated national evidence (capped at Medium), and the audit's finding that no public sector project has been implemented sits against its report of the Buki platform at a state university, a difference of scope it does not explain. Items: MKD-GOV-2025-004-0207;MKD-RES-2025-001-0039;MKD-RES-2025-001-0041;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0009;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0049;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0051;MKD-RES-2025-001-0025;MKD-EU-2026-005-0001;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0093;MKD-GOV-2025-011-0067

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0010 Law on Personal Data Protection 2020: in_practice. The Commission found the Agency executing its new powers once the transition ended, and the Agency's 2025 report gives specifics: 196 supervisions and 121 decisions ordering violations to be removed. Application is real but weak: supervisions found low compliance, other laws are not fully aligned, the Commission records no progress in 2025, a lack of budgetary independence and a NATO transfer amendment, none of which is repeal or suspension. Article 122 in the unofficial translation (provisions 'cease to apply until' accession) contradicts all this evidence, looks like a translation fault and should be checked against the Macedonian text.
- INT-MKD-0018 Strategy for the Implementation of the Right to Personal Data Protection 2025-2030: implemented. The Agency reports the strategy was adopted in 2024 and implementation began in 2025. The Commission's finding that budget and consultation problems negatively affected implementation is preferred to the Agency's account on how far it has gone, but it also treats implementation as under way.
- INT-MKD-0050 Law on the Organisation and Operation of the State Administration Bodies: implemented. The law gave the Ministry of Digital Transformation its competences, and that allocation is in place. Audit and strategy evidence shows gaps in carrying them out: no accountability line with the Fund for Innovation and Technology Development, and no central register of systems. I therefore do not rate it in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0078 National long-term ICT Strategy (unadopted drafts): insufficient_evidence. The drafts themselves were never adopted, but the Commission reported in November 2025 that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan had been adopted, recorded under SMART/MK: 2030 (INT-MKD-0079). No source says whether these drafts became that strategy or were withdrawn, so the analyst should decide whether to merge the entities.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: adopted. The Commission reports in November 2025 that the country adopted a long-term ICT strategy and action plan, and the strategy text says some activities began before formal adoption. The goals in the text are targets, so they do not show delivery. The link between the Commission's sentence and SMART/MK is inferred from timing and is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0081 National digital transformation strategy (draft): proposed. The State Audit Office refers to it as a draft in June 2025. It may be the SMART/MK draft, but the source does not say so.
- INT-MKD-0085 Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028: adopted. The Government adopted the strategy on 21 January 2025, which the Commission confirms; a February 2025 Commission item still planning adoption is out of date. The only item saying more is the Agency for Electronic Communications' one-line status that the strategy 'is applied', which is government reporting without specifics, comes from a body other than the lead ministry, and which I have not accepted, so the reviewer should decide. The new cyber security law is one delivered measure, but no item reports wider delivery or the half-yearly reports the strategy promises. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0092 National Operational Broadband Plan 2019-2029: implemented. The plan remains valid, and measures have been carried out in part: the Commission records 5G expanding to Skopje and biannual Broadband Competence Office reports. Commission reports for 2021 and 2022 record delay, and the Office's own report finds most 5G goals met but none of the three measures on 100 Mbps subscriptions implemented. These sources describe different parts of the plan, so they do not conflict.
- INT-MKD-0093 Smart Specialisation Strategy S3-MK 2024-2027: adopted. The Commission reports adoption of the strategy and its action plan in December 2023. Later items only cite the strategy or restate plans, so no source shows measures carried out.
- INT-MKD-0095 National strategy for AI: proposed. A working group started in 2021, but the State Audit Office finds in 2025 that no AI strategy has been adopted, and the 2025 ICT strategy still lists drafting one as an activity. The working group is a step towards the strategy and not the strategy itself.
- INT-MKD-0178 AI pilot in the 112 emergency service: implemented. The Commission and the State Audit Office both say AI is being piloted in the 112 service for transcription and translation, so a pilot is in place, but no item shows routine use. The entity includes the EENA pilot, which the sources do not explicitly equate with this one, and that is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0228 Action plan for applying AI (A 4.3.4.2): proposed. This is an action plan row in the SMART/MK action plan, so it is a plan however worded.
- INT-MKD-0237 Decision on the Strategic Priorities of the Government 2024-2028: adopted. The State Audit Office cites this as a Government decision setting priorities and listing AI. It is only cited, so nothing shows measures carried out under it.
- INT-MKD-0260 ADA digital assistant: unclear. The State Audit Office says that two years after launch, and after about EUR 150 thousand of spending, ADA is not functioning, and no source says it was formally withdrawn. The item on a CodeWell AI assistant describes a different tool for state aid programmes and does not bear on ADA.
- INT-MKD-0266 Buki AI speech transcription platform: implemented. The State Audit Office says the university officially put the platform into use in 2024. No source gives usage data.
