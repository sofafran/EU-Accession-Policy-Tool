# Fact pack RI-FMW-004: Legal and organisational framework for joining EURES

The country has not set up the legal and organisational arrangements needed to join the European network of employment services, such as a system for appointing EURES members and partners and access for private employment agencies.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). Sub-area wording on EURES, which matches this issue, says no developments or measures in 2020, 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025, and the 2023 key recommendation still asks for alignment. The screening report's 'good level of preparation' sits in tension with these and with its own finding that no appointment system exists; it is the only rating of this kind, so I do not read it as improvement, and I report the tension rather than resolve it. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0083;MKD-EU-2021-001-0067;MKD-EU-2022-001-0069;MKD-EU-2023-001-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0063;MKD-EU-2025-001-0106;MKD-EU-2025-003-0062;MKD-EU-2025-003-0058
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). What remains is a legal system for appointing EURES members and partners and access for private employment agencies. The Commission credits good alignment on IT and structural levels, but every responding intervention is only proposed (these statuses are themselves proposals), with legislative amendments on a 2030 deadline and implementation at accession. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0066;MKD-EU-2025-003-0067;MKD-EU-2025-003-0069;MKD-EU-2025-003-0068;MKD-EU-2025-003-0049;MKD-EU-2025-001-0106
- priority: High (validated). Alignment with EURES is named in the key recommendation in the 2023, 2024 and 2025 reports, and EURES is one of the chapter's main strands. The recommendation is a composite one, so the repetition is of one sentence and not of independent findings. Items: MKD-EU-2023-001-0089;MKD-EU-2024-001-0007;MKD-EU-2025-001-0012;MKD-EU-2025-003-0066
- tractability: Medium (validated). One lead body is planned and the IT and structural base is credited, which helps. However, no legal step has been reported in six years, the country's own timetable runs to 2030 and implementation is tied to accession. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0049;MKD-EU-2025-003-0069;MKD-EU-2025-003-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0106

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0371 Strengthening of Employment Agency capacities: proposed. The screening report lists this among planned steps, from the country's account. No item shows it carried out.
- INT-MKD-0380 Designation of the Employment Service Agency as EURES National Coordination Office: proposed. North Macedonia said at screening that the Agency will act as National Coordination Office, and the Commission reports no EURES developments in November 2025.
- INT-MKD-0435 Amendments to national legislation to comply with EURES standards: proposed. The Commission says these amendments have a deadline of 2030, with implementation on accession.
- INT-MKD-0436 Analysis of harmonisation needs: proposed. The screening report says the plan foresees this analysis. No source reports it done.
