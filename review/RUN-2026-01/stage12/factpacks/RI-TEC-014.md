# Fact pack RI-TEC-014: Broadband planning and investment framework incomplete

National broadband targets, plans and investment are not aligned with EU Digital Decade targets, and EU funding instruments, State aid rules and backhaul studies are not used or in place.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The plan's implementation slowed in 2021 and was further delayed in 2022, a strategic approach to broadband was found lacking in 2022 and 2023, the plan was said only to remain valid in 2024 and 2025, and the 2025 screening asks for its targets to be aligned with the Digital Decade; the 2020 chapter-wide some progress is context and was not decisive. Newer Reform Agenda investment and a feasibility study are plans and do not yet show change, so this is not improving, and no 2026 Commission item addresses the framework. Sources conflict on the plan's period, with ITU and the 2025 report giving 2019-2029 and the 2022 and 2024 reports giving 2019-2023, which affects how far the delays can be measured against targets. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0103;MKD-EU-2021-001-0087;MKD-EU-2022-001-0090;MKD-EU-2022-006-0040;MKD-EU-2023-006-0038;MKD-EU-2024-001-0083;MKD-EU-2025-001-0130;MKD-EU-2025-004-0011;MKD-EU-2024-008-0026;MKD-EU-2025-004-0026;MKD-IO-2022-002-0019
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The latest EU items ask for targets aligned with the Digital Decade, further alignment on aid for broadband and stronger capacity at the public broadcasting enterprise. The compared statuses are proposals for G6: the ICT strategy with its 2030 goals is adopted, but its alignment with the Digital Decade is the government's own statement in machine translation, and target alignment, State aid and the national network are only proposed, while none of the three plan measures towards the 100 Mbps target has been implemented. What remains is updating the plan's targets, settling the aid rules and financing the backhaul network; the reported non-use of the Western Balkans Investment Framework for infrastructure sits beside technical assistance from it for a feasibility study, which appears to be a difference between study and construction but should be checked. - priority: Medium (validated). The requirement is repeated by the EU, in the 2022 and 2023 assessments and the 2025 screening, but it is not named in a benchmark, a Reform Agenda payment step or the chapter's key recommendations. The Reform Agenda plan does call the broadband network the most important digital project, but as an investment, so the issue matters for the theme with thinner support than the legal and regulator issues. Items: MKD-EU-2022-006-0040;MKD-EU-2023-006-0038;MKD-EU-2025-004-0011;MKD-EU-2025-003-0192;MKD-EU-2024-008-0032
- tractability: Medium (validated). Updating targets is within the government's control and a mapping study is the stated first step, but financing the network depends on outside funders, State aid alignment and a public enterprise whose capacity the Commission says needs strengthening. Past progress is weak: the plan was delayed in 2021 and 2022, and the national broadband competence office, reported in machine translation, finds none of three measures implemented. The financing figures come from a table flagged as possibly misread, so they support only the general point that funding is mixed. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025 and amended on 29 December 2025; it entered into force on the eighth day after publication on 4 July 2025 and was due to apply from 1 June 2026, but no later item confirms application or reports measures under it, and the security supervision reported for 2025 rests on the earlier law. By-laws were due within 15 months (October 2026) and operators have 18 months to align (January 2027), so that deadline has not yet passed. The Commission's May 2026 finding that the step is not achieved, with a grace period to 30 June 2026 whose outcome is not in the evidence, concerns alignment, and 'draft' or 'proposal' wording in GOV-2025-004-0172 and GOV-2026-002-0100 is historic rather than a conflict.
- INT-MKD-0078 National long-term ICT Strategy (unadopted drafts): insufficient_evidence. The drafts themselves were never adopted, but the Commission reported in November 2025 that a long-term ICT strategy and action plan had been adopted, recorded under SMART/MK: 2030 (INT-MKD-0079). No source says whether these drafts became that strategy or were withdrawn, so the analyst should decide whether to merge the entities.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: adopted. The Commission reports in November 2025 that the country adopted a long-term ICT strategy and action plan, and the strategy text says some activities began before formal adoption. The goals in the text are targets, so they do not show delivery. The link between the Commission's sentence and SMART/MK is inferred from timing and is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0083 National Cybersecurity Strategy 2018-2022: reversed. The strategy ran out at the end of 2022 and its successor is the Cyber Security Strategy 2025-2028 (INT-MKD-0085). No source says it was repealed, so reversed is used by analogy with a replaced law because the scale has no value for expiry. The Government's later strategy says implementation was not fully achieved.
- INT-MKD-0092 National Operational Broadband Plan 2019-2029: implemented. The plan remains valid, and measures have been carried out in part: the Commission records 5G expanding to Skopje and biannual Broadband Competence Office reports. Commission reports for 2021 and 2022 record delay, and the Office's own report finds most 5G goals met but none of the three measures on 100 Mbps subscriptions implemented. These sources describe different parts of the plan, so they do not conflict.
- INT-MKD-0123 National Broadband Competence Office: in_practice. The office was set up in 2019, and the Commission says it publishes biannual reports on broadband development, which is specific evidence of it working.
- INT-MKD-0174 National broadband infrastructure deployment: proposed. The items describe a planned investment and project. A feasibility study had only started by October 2025, and no item shows the network built.
- INT-MKD-0176 Feasibility study on the national transport optical network: proposed. The October 2025 strategy says the ministry has started the study and also lists preparing it as a measure. No source reports it as finished.
- INT-MKD-0186 Accelerated integration: EU Broadband Competence Offices network: proposed. The country has asked to join the network and the Commission says it will explore this. No source reports membership.
- INT-MKD-0190 5G roll-out: in_practice. The input items are Reform Agenda plans, but the Commission records 5G reaching Skopje and coverage continuing to expand, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. The Reform Agenda step also covers the security toolbox and one remaining frequency, which are separate and not shown complete.
- INT-MKD-0209 Rulebook on the manner of construction of public electronic communications networks and accompanying assets: adopted. The source says the rulebook was published in 2016 and obliges operators to provide data. Its application is not shown, and the item is outside the evidence window.
- INT-MKD-0212 Guidelines for submitting data for newly built electronic communications network and accompanying assets: adopted. The ITU profile describes the guidelines as completing the regulation. It is one 2022 mention, with no evidence of use.
- INT-MKD-0330 Web GIS Collector: insufficient_evidence. A 2022 report says the Agency had been building this system since 2017. No later source says whether it was finished or used.
- INT-MKD-0367 National transport optical network: proposed. The digital strategy treats the network as something to be established, and its feasibility study has only started (INT-MKD-0176).
- INT-MKD-0419 Alignment of national broadband targets with the Digital Compass: proposed. The country's presentation says its targets will be aligned, which is a statement of future intent.
- INT-MKD-0464 Feasibility study for mapping existing and planned broadband coverage: proposed. North Macedonia says the study is in preparation. No item shows it finished.
- INT-MKD-0488 Mandate of the public broadcasting enterprise to build optical infrastructure and provide broadband access: adopted. The Commission says the enterprise was mandated in 2024, and in 2025 says it needs to strengthen its capacities for this role. No item shows infrastructure built under the mandate.
- INT-MKD-0491 Market monitoring and trend analysis mechanisms: proposed. The strategy says these mechanisms are needed, with no evidence they exist.
- INT-MKD-0525 Single project pipeline: implemented. The Commission reported in 2021 that the committee upgraded the pipeline with digital connectivity projects. No later item covers it.
- INT-MKD-0526 State aid for backhaul optical infrastructure and NGA networks in white and grey zones: proposed. The country's presentation says the aid aims to be provided, which is a plan. No aid is reported as granted.
