# Fact pack RI-TEC-011: Electronic communications law not aligned with EU rules

The law on electronic communications is not fully aligned with the European Electronic Communications Code, the Gigabit Infrastructure Act and related rules on cost reduction, spectrum, roaming and open internet access.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: improving (validated). Commission wording that the law needs further alignment repeated from 2020 to 2022, and the matching sub-area wording in 2023 was no progress, but the wording then changed: the 2025 assessment found significant gaps after the new law, and the 2026 assessment found alignment partially achieved after the December 2025 amendments. The chapter-wide limited progress of 2024 is context only and was not decisive. Sources conflict on the Broadband Cost Reduction Directive: the Commission said in 2024 that the country was yet to align, while the country's reported view in the screening is full alignment, and this is not resolved here. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0115;MKD-EU-2021-001-0097;MKD-EU-2022-001-0101;MKD-EU-2023-001-0136;MKD-EU-2024-001-0012;MKD-EU-2024-001-0082;MKD-EU-2025-004-0015;MKD-EU-2025-011-0018;MKD-EU-2025-001-0129;MKD-EU-2026-002-0002;MKD-EU-2026-002-0006
- unresolved_gap: partly_addressed (validated). The Commission's May 2026 assessment considers the Reform Agenda step not achieved and the beneficiary considers it achieved, as it did in 2025, so the two sides conflict and the Commission's finding is the requirement compared here. Responding interventions carry proposed statuses that the reviewers check at G6: the 2025 law and December amendments adopted, the by-laws and 2026 draft amendments only proposed, and the law applicable only from 1 June 2026, with no evidence on the outcome of the grace period ending 30 June 2026. What remains is closing the harmonisation gaps with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act in both laws and adopting and applying the by-laws; the ePrivacy conflict is left to its own issue under the taxonomy. Items: MKD-EU-2026-002-0001;MKD-EU-2026-002-0014;MKD-EU-2025-011-0014;MKD-EU-2025-011-0021;MKD-EU-2026-002-0007;MKD-EU-2026-002-0015;MKD-EU-2026-002-0005;MKD-EU-2026-002-0017;MKD-EU-2026-005-0006;MKD-GOV-2025-008-0032;MKD-GOV-2026-003-0030;MKD-EU-2025-004-0023;MKD-EU-2024-001-0102
- priority: High (validated). Adoption of an aligned law is a Reform Agenda step with a payment provision, and alignment with the Code recurs in the Commission's chapter recommendations from 2022 to 2025. It is central to the digital infrastructure theme because other reforms in the area rest on this legal framework. The roaming and net neutrality parts of the issue are largely positive and do not drive this rating. Items: MKD-EU-2024-007-0006;MKD-GOV-2024-002-0008;MKD-EU-2022-001-0087;MKD-EU-2023-001-0121;MKD-EU-2024-001-0015;MKD-EU-2025-001-0022;MKD-EU-2024-001-0046
- tractability: Medium (validated). The mechanisms are legal alignment and implementing rules, led by one ministry with the regulator and parliament, and a new law and amendments were adopted in 2025, which favours progress. Against that, the Commission assessed the Reform Agenda step as not achieved in both 2025 and 2026, with gaps remaining in two laws, and the by-laws are described as technically demanding and were not yet due. Adoption has been quick but alignment has twice fallen short, which does not support High. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0019 Law on Electronic Communications (Official Gazette 39/14): reversed. The Law on Electronic Communications 2025 (INT-MKD-0023) says this law ceases to be in force on the day the new law starts to apply, which is 1 June 2026. This is read from the legal text; no source confirms the date was not moved.
- INT-MKD-0020 Amendments to the Law on Electronic Communications (April 2021): reversed. The 2025 Law on Electronic Communications (INT-MKD-0023) repeals the earlier law, listing Official Gazette 92/21 among its amendments, from the day it starts to apply, 1 June 2026. The successor is therefore in application at the freeze date.
- INT-MKD-0021 Law on Electronic Communications (amendment for EU roaming rules and the Regional Roaming Agreement): reversed. This amendment formed part of the 2014 law, which ceased to be in force when the Law on Electronic Communications 2025 (INT-MKD-0023) began to apply on 1 June 2026. The successor status of the roaming provisions is not stated, and the repeal covers amendments up to 59/25.
- INT-MKD-0022 By-laws implementing the April 2021 amendments to the Law on Electronic Communications: adopted. The Commission reports the regulator adopted the by-laws. Their parent law ceased to apply on 1 June 2026, new by-laws are due, and no item says what happened to these, so the reviewer should note that.
- INT-MKD-0023 Law on Electronic Communications 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025 and amended on 29 December 2025; it entered into force on the eighth day after publication on 4 July 2025 and was due to apply from 1 June 2026, but no later item confirms application or reports measures under it, and the security supervision reported for 2025 rests on the earlier law. By-laws were due within 15 months (October 2026) and operators have 18 months to align (January 2027), so that deadline has not yet passed. The Commission's May 2026 finding that the step is not achieved, with a grace period to 30 June 2026 whose outcome is not in the evidence, concerns alignment, and 'draft' or 'proposal' wording in GOV-2025-004-0172 and GOV-2026-002-0100 is historic rather than a conflict.
- INT-MKD-0024 Amendments to the Law on Electronic Communications December 2025: adopted. The Commission states the amendments were adopted on 29 December 2025 (Official Gazette 269). They amend a law that applies only from June 2026, with bylaws due within 15 months, and no source reports them carried out.
- INT-MKD-0025 Draft Law amending the Law on Electronic Communications 2026: proposed. The regulator reports that a working group prepared the draft in January 2026 and published it for comment. No item shows it adopted.
- INT-MKD-0026 Bylaws to the Law on Electronic Communications 2025: proposed. The law sets a 15-month deadline for by-laws, and the Commission repeated this in May 2026 without reporting them adopted. The ICT strategy lists preparing them as an activity.
- INT-MKD-0027 Roadmap on the implementation of the Law on Electronic Communications 2025: proposed. The Commission says the beneficiary submitted this roadmap listing bylaws and deadlines, not that it was formally adopted, so the recorded status of adopted is not used. It is a plan of future steps.
- INT-MKD-0028 Law on Construction: adopted. The Commission reports amendments to this law adopted in December 2025 and asks for further amendment to align it. No source reports on how it is applied, so adopted is the highest level shown.
- INT-MKD-0029 Amendments to the Law on Construction December 2025: adopted. The Commission reports the amendments adopted in December 2025 and finds that full alignment with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act has not been achieved. No item shows them being applied.
- INT-MKD-0031 Law amending the Law on Electronic Documents, Electronic Identification and Trust Services 2025: adopted. Parliament adopted the law on 27 June 2025 and the Commission confirms this, while noting it does not fully align with the EU regulation. Article 27 of the law says it will start to apply only from 1 June 2027, so it cannot be implemented at the freeze date. That provision is mapped in the data to the parent law INT-MKD-0030.
- INT-MKD-0042 Law on Security of Network and Information Systems 2025: adopted. The law was adopted on 27 June 2025, published on 4 July 2025, entered into force on the eighth day and applies from 1 January 2026, except that entities in Art. 4(2) points 8 to 16 are covered only from EU accession. No item shows the measures it requires, such as the by-laws (due within six months), the entity lists, or the government team and network (due within twelve months, so by July 2026), and MKD-CIRT predates the law; the regulator's 'applies' statement gives the legal status only. 'Proposal' in GOV-2026-002-0097 comes from an annex of 2025 opinions, so it is historic rather than a conflict, and the Commission's finding that alignment is incomplete concerns content, not status.
- INT-MKD-0047 Law on Archival Material and Archival Activity 2025: adopted. The Commission confirms adoption on 27 June 2025 and publication on 4 July 2025, and treats the central document management system as still to be completed. A digital strategy row on preparing a new law is a plan row and does not contradict adoption. The link between the earlier Reform Agenda items and this law is inferred, as the entity note says.
- INT-MKD-0079 ICT Development Strategy SMART/MK: 2030: adopted. The Commission reports in November 2025 that the country adopted a long-term ICT strategy and action plan, and the strategy text says some activities began before formal adoption. The goals in the text are targets, so they do not show delivery. The link between the Commission's sentence and SMART/MK is inferred from timing and is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0100 Reform 2.2.2.1 Secure Digital infrastructures roll-out: proposed. The reform as a whole is a Reform Agenda plan for infrastructure, 5G and related law. A new Law on Electronic Communications was adopted in June 2025 as one part, but no item shows the secure infrastructure rolled out.
- INT-MKD-0107 Voluntary agreement by telecom operators to lower data roaming charges with the EU: in_practice. The Commission records that the first voluntary reduction came into force on 1 October 2023, and that the agreement was still being implemented in 2024 and 2025. This is specific evidence of application, although no item gives actual prices.
- INT-MKD-0187 Accelerated integration: radio spectrum and 5G legislation: proposed. The country asked to explore phasing in this legislation and the Commission said it would explore this further. No source reports a decision.
- INT-MKD-0189 5G spectrum assignment: in_practice. The Commission reports that approvals for 5G frequencies were granted and that 5G coverage keeps expanding, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. One frequency remained to be tendered in 2024, and that tender is rated separately as INT-MKD-0512.
- INT-MKD-0190 5G roll-out: in_practice. The input items are Reform Agenda plans, but the Commission records 5G reaching Skopje and coverage continuing to expand, and the State Audit Office reports 96.99% population coverage. The Reform Agenda step also covers the security toolbox and one remaining frequency, which are separate and not shown complete.
- INT-MKD-0192 Rulebooks on reducing the cost of deploying high-speed networks: adopted. The Commission reports that the regulator adopted the rulebooks. No source shows them being applied, and the government strategy only restates the Commission's finding.
- INT-MKD-0193 Alignment with the Gigabit Infrastructure Act and European Electronic Communications Code: adopted. Amendments aimed at alignment were adopted on 29 December 2025, but the Commission finds alignment only partial and the step not achieved, while the country considers it achieved. The Commission's assessment is preferred on alignment, and implementing bylaws have a 15-month deadline, so the status goes no higher than adopted. Sources conflict: tier 3 for the reviewers.
- INT-MKD-0195 Plan for the allocation of radio frequency bands 2025: adopted. The Government gave consent on 24 July 2025 and the plan was published on 25 August 2025, according to the regulator's report. The February 2025 screening item predates this plan and so must refer to an earlier one; the entity link should be checked.
- INT-MKD-0213 Guidelines on RLAH and Fair Use Policy: adopted. The screening report records the guidelines as introduced, from the country's own presentation. No item shows them being applied.
- INT-MKD-0221 Rules for the use of radio frequencies without frequency authorisation: adopted. The country's own presentation lists the rules as addressing spectrum harmonisation. No item shows how they are applied.
- INT-MKD-0222 Update on short range devices: proposed. The 2025 screening report says the update was planned for 2024. No source reports that it happened, so the status is only as last stated.
- INT-MKD-0226 Action Plan for alignment with European roaming legislation: proposed. The Government ordered the plan carried out in August 2025 and the gap analyses were submitted in December 2025, but the transposing law, decisions and rulebook were still drafts sent to the Commission for review. The evidence is the regulator's own report and stops in March 2026.
- INT-MKD-0310 Online platform for measuring service quality: proposed. The strategy describes this platform as a component still to be developed. No source shows it in operation.
- INT-MKD-0416 6G spectrum allocation preparations: proposed. The strategy says the country will begin preparing spectrum for 6G, which is a future commitment.

- INT-MKD-0468 GIZ support to the Ministry of Digital Transformation: implemented. The Commission records that the ministry received GIZ support for preparing technical documents, in reports of 2025 and 2026.
- INT-MKD-0470 Glide path for further reductions of roaming charges: adopted. The Commission reports that operators agreed a glide path towards 2028. No item shows any step after the first reduction, which is recorded under the voluntary agreement.
- INT-MKD-0500 Methodology on sustainability of abolishing retail roaming surcharges: adopted. The country's presentation says the methodology was introduced. Its application is not separately shown.
- INT-MKD-0505 Observer participation in the Radio Spectrum Policy Group: implemented. The screening report, from the country's account, says it has taken part as an observer since 2010. That is reported only, so I do not use the stated in_practice.

## Outcomes

- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2020-001-0108: success worked, attribution Medium. The Commission's country report states that the public benefited from decreased roaming prices, and later Commission reports record the roaming-free zone in the country. No price series is in the evidence, so the finding rests on Commission statements.
- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2020-002-0021: success worked, attribution Medium. The reduction is stated in a regional Commission communication that does not name the country, so it relies on the country report for the finding about North Macedonia. The dates differ only because they are phases of one agreement, not a conflict.
- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2021-002-0010: success worked, attribution High. The regional communication reports the elimination of charges under roam like at home, and the country report confirms citizens benefit from the roaming-free zone since 1 July 2021. The regulator's report dates the regime to 1 June 2021 in Low confidence machine translation, and this date conflict is noted but does not change the result.
- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2022-001-0134: success worked, attribution High. The Commission's country report states that the agreement was fully implemented and that the country has benefited from the roaming-free zone since July 2021. This is Commission evidence, not government reporting.
- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2022-002-0032: success worked, attribution High. The regional communication states that the agreement brought about roam like at home from 1 July 2021, and the country report confirms this for North Macedonia. The Commission is here describing a process it helped to secure, so the source is not fully detached.
- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2023-002-0025: success worked, attribution High. This repeats the 2022 statement in nearly the same words, so it is not independent new evidence for the result. The finding about the country again relies on the country report.
- INT-INT-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2025-004-0056: success worked, attribution High. The outcome item is the country's reported view in the screening report, which alone would not support a result. The result rests on the Commission's own 2022 country report, not on this view or on the authorities' report of no consumer complaints.
- INT-INT-0007 leads to MKD-EU-2023-002-0006: success too_early, attribution Low. The Commission reports a first phased reduction from October 2023, but the aim is near-domestic prices by 2027 in the roadmap and by 2028 in the operators' glide path, which differ as ambition and commercial schedule. The items are regional and none shows the effect on prices in North Macedonia, and agreements to extend the zone were still being prepared in 2025.
- INT-MKD-0107 leads to MKD-EU-2023-002-0026: success too_early, attribution Medium. The first reduction came into force on 1 October 2023 and further steps run towards 2028, so only the opening stage can be seen. The items are regional and do not name the country, so they cannot alone support a finding about North Macedonia.

- MKD-EU-2026-002-0001 vs MKD-EU-2026-002-0014: On the same Reform Agenda step in the same 2026 assessment, after the December 2025 amendments, the beneficiary considers it achieved and the Commission considers it not achieved.
- MKD-GOV-2026-003-0033 vs MKD-EU-2021-001-0086: The regulator's 2025 work report dates the start of the roam like at home regime to 1 June 2021, while the Commission dates the regional roaming-free zone to 1 July 2021.
- MKD-EU-2025-004-0023 vs MKD-EU-2024-001-0102: North Macedonia stated in the screening published in February 2025 that the Law for Electronic Communications is fully aligned with the ePrivacy Directive, while the Commission's 2024 Report states that further efforts are needed for alignment with that Directive.
