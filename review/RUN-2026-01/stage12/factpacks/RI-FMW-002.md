# Fact pack RI-FMW-002: Work permits, quotas and residence conditions applied to EU workers

EU workers and their family members are subject to work permits, annual quotas, residence permits or unspecified conditions (for example for seasonal work) that must be lifted or settled by accession.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: persistent (validated). The chapter scale was not decisive, since this is part of the chapter and no sub-area wording matches; Commission items describe annual quotas and work permits unchanged from 2020 to 2025, and the 2025 seasonal work law leaves conditions for EU citizens to be specified. Permit counts (755 in 2021, some 8 900 in 2024) are not used as a trend, because the bases appear to differ: the 2021 figure sits beside 3 242 separate opinions in a machine-translated agency report, capped at Medium. Items: MKD-EU-2020-001-0081;MKD-EU-2021-001-0065;MKD-EU-2022-001-0067;MKD-EU-2023-001-0093;MKD-EU-2024-001-0059;MKD-EU-2025-001-0099;MKD-EU-2025-001-0103;MKD-EU-2022-001-0068;MKD-EU-2025-001-0104;MKD-GOV-2022-002-0004;MKD-GOV-2022-002-0005
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). What remains is access for EU citizens without work permits or residence permits, with their explicit exclusion from quotas, and no responding intervention is linked (statuses are proposals). The confirmed contradiction stands: the Commission says the law does not distinguish EU citizens, while the country says quotas do not apply to them under a reciprocity agreement. Art. 5(2) point 1 of the 2015 law (machine translated, so capped at Medium, and used under LIM-0024) partly explains this, because it does exempt foreigners given equality under such an agreement, but it is conditional, no item identifies a concluded agreement or shows it applied, and the Commission still requires the exclusion. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0025;MKD-EU-2025-003-0028;MKD-EU-2024-001-0058;MKD-EU-2025-001-0097;MKD-EU-2025-003-0010;MKD-GOV-2015-001-0026;MKD-EU-2025-001-0103
- priority: Medium (validated). Permits and quotas are central to the chapter, but they are named as requirements only in the 2024 report and the screening report, not in the annual key recommendation, which is why this is central with thin support. If the reviewers read the key recommendation on non-discrimination as covering permits and quotas, this would be High; the large item count is mostly the law's own text and did not count. Items: MKD-EU-2025-003-0025;MKD-EU-2025-003-0028;MKD-EU-2024-001-0048;MKD-EU-2025-001-0013
- tractability: Medium (validated). The change needs only national law, but it involves two laws and the interior and labour ministries and the Employment Agency, and an international organisation reports overlapping mandates between the laws. The law was amended in 2021 and 2025 without touching this requirement, and the proposed amendments have unknown content. ## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0001 Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners: in_practice. The law was adopted in 2015 and remains in force as amended. The Commission states that the Agency issues work permits under its quotas, and the Agency's 2021 report gives the opinions issued, so the evidence is not only a description of the law.
- INT-MKD-0002 Law Amending and Supplementing the Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners 2021: adopted. The Assembly adopted the amendments on 15 July 2021 and the 2025 amending law cites them as part of the law in force. Commission reports on work permits describe the parent law, and no source shows these amendments' registration procedures in use, so nothing beyond adoption is shown.
- INT-MKD-0003 Law Amending the Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners 2025: adopted. The Assembly adopted the law on 22 April 2025, and it enters into force eight days after publication. Its provisions only rename ministries and bodies, and no item shows any further step.
- INT-MKD-0004 Proposed amendments on the employment of foreigners: proposed. The Employment Agency's reports for 2024 and 2025 both list these only as proposed amendments. The sources do not say whether this is the law adopted in April 2025, so no adoption is assumed.
- INT-MKD-0005 Law on Foreigners: adopted. The law is described as in force, but only in the country presentation in the Commission's screening report. No item mapped here shows how it is applied.
- INT-MKD-0006 2019 amendments to the Law on Foreigners: implemented. The Commission reports that the amendments entered into force in May 2019 and simplified the single application for work permits. This describes the process in law and does not show how it is used, so I do not use the stated in_practice.
- INT-MKD-0007 Single application procedure for work permits: implemented. The Commission says permits may be acquired through a single application to a single authority, last in 2021. This describes the procedure as available, not evidence of how it is used, so the stated in_practice is not adopted.
- INT-MKD-0008 Annual quota of work permits for foreigners: in_practice. The Commission states the country sets maximum quotas each year and that the Agency issues permits following them. The Agency's 2021 report adds counts of opinions based on quota use.
- INT-MKD-0072 Law on the Work Engagement of Individuals: adopted. The Commission reports the law adopted and says the conditions for EU citizens remain to be specified in implementing legislation. A 2026 Commission item records a software upgrade for a law on registering work engagement, which may be this law, but the names differ and this is for the analyst to confirm.
- INT-MKD-0385 secondary legislation under the 2021 amending law: insufficient_evidence. The stated adopted is misleading, because the item is only the law's clause saying the secondary legislation will be adopted within 30 days. No item shows that it was.

## Outcomes

- INT-MKD-0006 leads to MKD-EU-2020-001-0082: success mixed, attribution Medium. The Commission states that the 2019 amendments further simplified the single application, which is not government reporting alone, though no processing data supports it. The World Bank in 2024 still reports regulatory barriers and asks for simpler permits, and quotas are reported regionally to add delay, so the wider result is less clear.

## Confirmed conflicts

- MKD-EU-2024-001-0058 vs MKD-EU-2025-003-0010: The Commission states that the law does not distinguish between EU citizens and other foreigners, while North Macedonia states that under the same law the quotas do not apply to EU citizens following a reciprocity agreement with the EU.
