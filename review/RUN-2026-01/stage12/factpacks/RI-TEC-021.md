# Fact pack RI-TEC-021: Personal data protection in electoral processes weak

The processing of personal data in elections, including biometric data and campaign use, lacks oversight and was introduced without assessing compliance or consulting the data protection authority.

## Validated issue judgements

- direction_of_travel: insufficient_evidence (validated). The Commission used the same wording in three documents, its 2022 and 2023 reports and the 2023 screening report, but no item after 2023 addresses the issue, so the record shows neither that it continues nor that it has eased. The three-report default for persistent was therefore not applied, because the repeats span only two years and the 2024 and 2025 reports are silent. The chapter scale was not decisive. Items: MKD-EU-2022-001-0029;MKD-EU-2023-003-0079;MKD-EU-2023-001-0042;MKD-EU-2023-001-0045
- unresolved_gap: open (validated). The latest requirement, from 2023, is unmet on the record: no intervention is recorded as responding and the Electoral Code's biometric provisions have a proposed status of adopted, and these statuses are themselves proposals for G6. What remains is an assessment of the Code against data protection law and proper oversight of personal data used in campaigns. The State Election Commission addressed some irregularities, but that does not reach these two points, and this finding rests on evidence no newer than 2023. Items: MKD-EU-2023-003-0080;MKD-EU-2023-001-0045;MKD-EU-2023-003-0081;MKD-EU-2023-003-0082
- priority: Low (validated). The Commission raised it only in 2022 and 2023, it is not in the main findings, and it is peripheral to the data theme. Items: MKD-EU-2022-001-0029;MKD-EU-2023-001-0042;MKD-EU-2023-003-0079
- tractability: Medium (validated). Few actors are needed and an assessment of the Code is a limited task, and the State Election Commission has already acted on some irregularities. Oversight of campaign data and any change to the Electoral Code are likely to be harder, and the evidence on this is thin. Items: MKD-EU-2022-001-0031;MKD-EU-2023-003-0080;MKD-EU-2023-001-0045

## Interventions and validated implementation status

- INT-MKD-0054 Amendments to the Electoral Code on biometric voter identification: adopted. The amendments were adopted in April 2021, and the Commission notes that the Personal Data Protection Agency was not consulted beforehand. No item shows biometric identification being used at an election.
